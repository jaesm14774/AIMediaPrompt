👇 **Prompt Template / 指令模板**

> 這個模板捕捉了最具共鳴的夏日體驗——從酷熱煎熬到冷氣救贖的極端情緒轉變！

A relatable 5-second video of **[角色名稱：pink blob creature / Kirby / 圓潤萌物]** discovering the blessed relief of air conditioning on a scorching summer day, with dramatically exaggerated emotional reactions that every human can relate to.

**Phase 1 - The Suffering (0-1.5s):** Character enters frame looking absolutely MISERABLE from heat. **[熱到融化的表現："Body literally drooping and melting like ice cream, leaving a small puddle trail" / "Tongue hanging out comically far, panting like a dog" / "Face completely red/flushed, steam rising from head" / "Moving in slow motion like walking through invisible molasses"]**. Sweat drops fly off dramatically. The room is bathed in harsh, yellow-orange "hot" lighting. Heat wave distortion visible in the air. Character barely makes it to the center of the room, on the verge of collapse.

**Phase 2 - The Discovery (1.5-3s):** Character looks up and notices the AC unit mounted on the wall. **[發現的瞬間："Eyes suddenly SNAP wide open, pupils dilating" / "Dramatic anime-style sparkle effect around the AC" / "Heavenly choir sound implied through golden rays emanating from AC" / "Time freezes as character processes this holy sight"]**. Camera zooms dramatically on the AC unit, which now appears to glow with divine light. Character's expression transforms from despair to hope—the biggest, most desperate puppy-dog eyes imaginable. A single hopeful tear forms.

**Phase 3 - The Blessing (3-5s):** The AC clicks on. A wave of **[冷氣視覺效果："visible cool blue air flowing down like a waterfall of salvation" / "sparkling frost particles cascading" / "refreshing mist with tiny snowflakes"]** descends upon the character. INSTANT transformation: 
- Body re-inflates from melted state
- Color returns from flushed to healthy pink
- Expression shifts to **[極度舒適："pure bliss with eyes closed and the most satisfied smile" / "literally ascending to heaven pose with arms spread" / "melting again but this time from happiness"]**
- Character floats slightly upward in the cool breeze, **[最終姿態："hair/ears flowing majestically in the AC wind like a shampoo commercial" / "striking a dramatic pose with sparkles everywhere" / "curling into a happy ball directly under the vent"]**

Room lighting shifts from harsh warm to cool comfortable blue. The character is now in paradise.

**Visual Style:** Strong color temperature contrast—oppressive warm orange-yellow (Phase 1) transitioning to refreshing cool blue-white (Phase 3). Realistic room setting with visible AC unit, beige walls, curtain in background. Character uses exaggerated squash-stretch and emotion indicators (sweat drops, steam, sparkles, tears of joy). Comedy comes from the relatable over-dramatization of a universal summer experience.

**Technical specs:** 60fps animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format, dramatic lighting shifts with visible color temperature change (5500K→3000K→6500K), motion blur on dramatic movements, lens flare on AC "revelation" moment, particle effects for sweat/steam (Phase 1) and frost/sparkles (Phase 3), comedic timing with extended reaction beats, sound design: oppressive summer cicadas → dramatic revelation chord → satisfying AC hum with wind chimes.

📝 **Example / 指令範例**

> 這個範例展示了一隻有貓耳的粉色圓球在酷熱中發現空調救贖的完整戲劇過程。

A relatable 5-second video of **pink puffball creature with cat-like ears** discovering the blessed relief of air conditioning on a scorching summer day, with dramatically exaggerated emotional reactions.

**Phase 1 - The Suffering (0-1.5s):** Character drags itself into frame from the right, looking absolutely DEVASTATED by the heat. Its normally round pink body is visibly drooping and slightly melting at the edges like soft-serve ice cream. Tongue hangs out comically, panting desperately. The face has turned a flushed red-pink, with cartoon sweat drops launching off in all directions. Steam wisps rise from the top of its head. The room is bathed in harsh, oppressive yellow-orange lighting with visible heat distortion waves. Character barely makes it to the center of the room near the white curtain, stubby legs wobbling, on the absolute verge of dramatic collapse. It lets out a silent exhausted sigh.

**Phase 2 - The Discovery (1.5-3s):** Still slumped over, the character's gaze weakly drifts upward... and LOCKS onto the wall-mounted AC unit visible in the upper corner. Eyes SNAP wide open instantaneously, pupils dilating to maximum size with an audible "ping" implied. Dramatic anime-style sparkle effects burst around the AC unit. Golden rays of holy light emanate from the AC like a religious artifact. Time seems to freeze. Camera executes a dramatic zoom toward the AC, which now glows with divine radiance. Cut back to character: the most desperate, hopeful, beautiful puppy-dog eyes imaginable. A single crystalline tear of hope forms at the corner of one eye. Tiny hands clasp together in prayer position.

**Phase 3 - The Blessing (3-5s):** *Click.* The AC activates. A magnificent waterfall of visible cool blue air cascades down from the unit, carrying sparkling frost particles and tiny snowflakes. The cold wave hits the character like a tsunami of salvation. INSTANT TRANSFORMATION: The melted, droopy body re-inflates with a cartoon "BOING" sound. Flushed color rapidly returns to healthy vibrant pink in a satisfying wave from head to toe. Expression shifts to PURE TRANSCENDENT BLISS—eyes closed in ecstasy, the most peaceful satisfied smile, practically glowing from within. The character slowly floats upward in the cool breeze, arms spread wide in a "take me" pose. Its cat-like ears and any fur flow majestically in the AC wind like a luxury shampoo commercial. Sparkles materialize around the character. The room lighting has completely shifted from harsh orange to comfortable cool blue-white. Paradise achieved. Character opens one eye and gives a satisfied wink to camera.

**Visual Style:** Strong dramatic color temperature contrast—oppressive warm orange (4000K) in suffering phase, cool refreshing blue-white (7000K) in blessed phase. Realistic room environment with visible AC unit, beige textured walls, white curtain providing soft diffused backlight. Exaggerated cartoon emotion indicators integrated into realistic space creates the comedy.

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical (TikTok/Reels optimized), dramatic three-stage lighting shift, lens flare bloom on AC revelation shot, particle systems for sweat/steam (Phase 1) and frost/sparkles (Phase 3), proper squash-stretch principles, comedic timing with held reaction beats, sound design: oppressive summer cicadas and heat buzz → dramatic heavenly revelation chord → deeply satisfying AC hum with gentle wind chimes and a soft "aaahhhh" of relief.

💡 **關鍵參數解析**

- **色溫轉變 (4000K → 7000K)**：色溫從悶熱橙黃到清涼藍白的劇烈轉變，讓觀眾「視覺上」感受到溫度變化——這是這個 Prompt 最關鍵的技術要素
- **三階段情緒曲線**：痛苦→發現→救贖的三階段情緒曲線，完美對應人類經驗中的「問題→轉折→解決」敘事結構
- **空調神聖光效**：將空調處理成散發神聖光芒的救贖象徵，這種誇張手法讓日常物品變成戲劇焦點——幽默來自「認真對待荒謬」
- **鏡頭光暈**：發現空調時的鏡頭光暈強化了「頓悟」時刻的戲劇性，這是電影級的視覺語言
- **粒子效果對比**：Phase 1 的汗水蒸氣 vs Phase 3 的霜花閃光，粒子效果的對比強化了「地獄到天堂」的轉變

🎨 **專家建議**

❌ 讓角色從一開始就在冷氣房裡，失去了「發現」的驚喜和情緒對比

✅ 確保 Phase 1 的「痛苦」表現足夠誇張（身體融化、舌頭外露、蒸氣冒出）——越慘烈的開場，越能讓後面的救贖感動人心！觀眾會因為共鳴而瘋狂分享「這就是我本人」

✨ **延伸挑戰**

👉 創造「季節掙扎」系列：「冬天發現暖氣」、「下雨天找到雨傘」、「餓到不行發現食物」！同樣的三階段情緒結構可以套用到無數「痛苦→發現→救贖」的日常場景。

🔥 **進階挑戰**：讓多個角色一起經歷這個過程——一群小生物擠在空調下面搶位置，或是輪流「升天」的連續反應，群體動態會讓喜感加倍！
