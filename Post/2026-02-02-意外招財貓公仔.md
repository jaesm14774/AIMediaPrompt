🐱💰 **意外招財貓公仔：當憨憨變成財神！ | Accidental Lucky Cat: When Derpy Becomes Divine!**

憨憨小公仔只是在做它的招牌動作，結果被誤認為招財貓——錢越堆越多，它從困惑到擁抱命運的表情變化，笑死！
A derpy little figure was just doing its signature move, but got mistaken for a lucky cat—the money keeps piling up, and watching its expression shift from confusion to embracing destiny is absolutely hilarious!

👇 **Prompt Template / 指令模板**

> 三階段結構：無辜動作 → 身份錯位 → 反轉覺醒。核心笑點來自於角色的「被動招財」和最後的「命運接受」！

A hilarious 5-second video of **[公仔角色：Psyduck / chubby yellow duck figure / round bear plushie / derpy blob creature]** accidentally becoming a money-attracting "lucky cat" fortune charm in a **[場景：cozy retail shop counter / busy ramen restaurant entrance / cluttered home office desk / traditional Asian tea shop]**.

**Phase 1 - Innocent Action (0-1.5s):** A small **[公仔描述："chunky yellow duck figure with perpetually confused expression" / "round derpy creature sitting perfectly still" / "adorable blob with vacant stare and raised paw"]** sits at the edge of a surface, mindlessly doing a simple repetitive motion—**[無辜動作："slowly raising and lowering one stubby arm like scratching its head" / "wobbling side to side with one arm slightly raised" / "rhythmically tilting its head while one hand waves lazily" / "rocking back and forth with arms making a beckoning gesture out of pure habit"]**. Expression is completely vacant and innocent, eyes slightly unfocused, mouth in a peaceful 'o' shape. The movement is completely unintentional, just a quirky habit. Soft natural lighting, cozy indoor ambiance. The figure has NO idea what's about to happen.

**Phase 2 - Mistaken Identity (1.5-3.5s):** A human hand enters frame from the side, **[放錢動作："gently placing a shiny coin on top of the figure's head" / "carefully balancing a folded bill on its raised arm" / "stacking coins in front of it like offerings" / "tucking paper money under its arm like a tip"]**. The figure's eyes **[困惑反應："slowly shift to look at the money without moving its head—just the pupils sliding over" / "blink once in genuine confusion, the waving motion stuttering briefly" / "widen slightly but body continues the same motion out of muscle memory"]**. Beat. Another hand enters—MORE money appears. **[錢越來越多："coins start piling up around its feet" / "bills tucked into every available crevice" / "someone places a small red envelope" / "a piggy bank appears next to it"]**. The figure's expression shifts to **[表情變化："visible '???' energy radiating from it" / "eyebrows slowly furrowing in adorable bewilderment" / "mouth forming a tiny confused frown while still waving"]**. The beckoning motion never stops—it can't help itself.

**Phase 3 - Acceptance & Plot Twist (3.5-5s):** Now COVERED in money—coins balanced on head, bills tucked everywhere, surrounded by offerings like a tiny shrine. The figure **[最終反應："still doing the exact same motion, but now with a tiny resigned smile—'I guess this is my life now'" / "has given up questioning it, eyes now closed in peaceful acceptance, still waving" / "actually starts waving with MORE enthusiasm, leaning into the role" / "one eye opens with a subtle knowing smirk—maybe this isn't so bad"]**. 

**THE TWIST:** A final hand enters—but instead of adding money, it **[反轉瞬間："drops an entire stack of bills, making it rain on the figure" / "places a tiny golden crown on its head, officially coronating it" / "sets down a handwritten sign reading 'Lucky Charm - Please Feed' in front of it" / "gently pets the figure's head, causing all the coins to cascade down in slow-motion like a jackpot"]**. The figure's expression **[爆點反應："FREEZES mid-wave, one eye twitching as it processes this absurdity" / "shifts into the most smug, self-satisfied smirk—it has EMBRACED its destiny" / "tears up slightly—a single sparkling tear of 'I've made it'" / "breaks the fourth wall, looking directly at camera with a knowing 'can you believe this?' expression"]**. Golden-hour lighting intensifies, making everything glow with prosperity. **[最終畫面："money pile sparkles like treasure, figure sits like a tiny wealthy emperor" / "a rainbow lens flare crosses the frame as if blessing this moment" / "confetti of more coins falls from above in slow-motion"]**.

**Visual Style:** Warm, inviting indoor environment with realistic textures—wooden surface, soft ambient lighting, visible everyday clutter suggesting a lived-in space. Figure rendered with smooth plastic/vinyl toy aesthetic—glossy surface catching light, simple geometric features, comically limited expression range that somehow conveys complex emotions. Money rendered realistically with visible coin reflections and paper bill textures. Comedy derives from the contrast between the figure's complete cluelessness and people's earnest belief in its "lucky cat" powers. Aspect ratio should feel intimate, like a candid phone video capturing this absurd moment.

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format (TikTok/Reels/Shorts optimized), warm golden color grading (4500K), shallow depth of field keeping figure sharp while background softly blurred, subtle product-shot lighting making the money glisten, gentle camera sway suggesting handheld authenticity, sound design: soft ambient café/shop sounds, satisfying coin *clink* sounds, subtle comedic timing beats, gentle "hmm?" sound effect when figure notices the first coin.

📝 **Example / 指令範例**

> 以可達鴨為例，展示它在茶飲店意外變成招財神獸的全過程！

A hilarious 5-second video of **Psyduck (the perpetually confused yellow duck Pokémon)** accidentally becoming a money-attracting fortune charm at a **cozy bubble tea shop counter**.

**Phase 1 - Innocent Action (0-1.5s):** A small Psyduck figure sits at the edge of the wooden counter near the cash register. It's doing its signature move—slowly pressing both stubby hands against the sides of its head while swaying slightly, as if perpetually nursing a headache. Expression is the classic vacant Psyduck stare: round eyes slightly unfocused, small bill hanging open, radiating pure "I have no idea what's happening" energy. The head-clutching motion looks exactly like a beckoning cat's wave from certain angles. Soft afternoon light streams through the shop window. Psyduck has absolutely no clue what it's doing or why.

**Phase 2 - Mistaken Identity (1.5-3.5s):** A customer's hand enters frame from the right, gently placing a shiny 50-yuan coin on top of Psyduck's yellow head. The coin balances perfectly between its hands. Psyduck's pupils—and ONLY the pupils—slowly slide to the side to look at the coin. The head-clutching motion continues uninterrupted. A beat of silence. Its expression reads: "...?" Another hand enters—this time tucking a folded red bill under its arm. Psyduck blinks once. Slowly. The motion stutters for just a moment, then resumes. MORE hands appear in quick succession: coins stacking around its feet, someone places a small red lucky envelope, another coin balanced on its bill. Psyduck's eyebrows furrow in adorable genuine confusion. The pile grows. Its tiny mouth shifts into a bewildered frown, but the "beckoning" motion never stops—pure muscle memory.

**Phase 3 - Acceptance & Plot Twist (3.5-5s):** Psyduck is now absolutely COVERED in money—coins balanced precariously on its head between those pressing hands, bills tucked into every available spot, completely surrounded by offerings like a tiny shrine. The headache-clutching pose continues, but now there's something different in its expression. The vacant stare has softened. Eyes close halfway. The confused frown relaxes into what might be... peaceful acceptance? "I guess I'm a lucky charm now."

**THE TWIST:** A final hand enters—but this time, it gently places a tiny handwritten cardboard sign in front of Psyduck that reads: "店之神獸 · 請餵食" (Shop's Sacred Beast · Please Feed). Psyduck's eyes SNAP open. It looks down at the sign. Processes it. Looks at the camera. For a split second, it BREAKS CHARACTER—one eye twitches, the perpetually confused expression cracks into the SMUGGEST, most self-satisfied smirk. It knows EXACTLY what's happening now. And it LOVES it. The headache-clutching motion transforms—no longer confused flailing, but now a deliberate, confident beckoning wave. Golden-hour lighting intensifies dramatically, casting Psyduck in a warm prosperous glow. A single sparkle *ting* appears at the corner of its eye. The coin pile glitters like treasure. In the final frame, Psyduck sits like a tiny wealthy emperor who has fully embraced its accidental destiny—still clutching its head, still swaying, but now with the energy of a creature who has discovered its true calling.

**Visual Style:** Warm bubble tea shop interior with wooden counters, soft Edison bulb lighting, colorful menu boards blurred in background. Psyduck rendered with glossy vinyl toy aesthetic—smooth yellow surface catching warm highlights, simple black bead eyes somehow conveying complex emotional journey. Realistic money with visible coin reflections, crisp bill textures, and lucky red envelopes adding cultural color. Intimate framing like a candid moment caught on someone's phone.

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format (TikTok/Reels optimized), warm golden color grading (4500K) emphasizing the cozy shop atmosphere, shallow depth of field keeping Psyduck pin-sharp against softly blurred background, subtle lens flare from window light, satisfying ASMR-quality coin *clink* sounds for each placement, gentle ambient café chatter and bubble tea shop ambiance, comedic timing beats for each "money placement → reaction" cycle, ending with a soft, satisfied "hmmmm" that somehow sounds like Psyduck accepting its new destiny.

💡 **關鍵參數解析**

- **三階段結構 (Innocent → Mistaken Identity → Plot Twist)**：完美的喜劇節奏——鋪墊、誤會加深、反轉爆點，讓觀眾從頭笑到尾
- **「只動瞳孔」的微反應**：角色身體繼續動作但只有眼珠移動看錢——這種「身體背叛大腦」的喜劇效果超級有梗
- **錢越堆越多的視覺堆疊**：創造滿足感和荒謬感的雙重效果，每一次加錢都是一個小笑點
- **THE TWIST 反轉設計**：從「被動接受」到「主動擁抱」的角色覺醒，是整支影片的記憶點
- **暖金色調 (4500K)**：營造財運滾滾、溫馨繁榮的氛圍，讓錢幣看起來更閃亮

🎨 **專家建議**

❌ 讓角色一開始就意識到發生什麼事——這會破壞「無辜感」

✅ 確保 Phase 1 的動作看起來完全無意識、純粹是習慣。角色的「完全不知情」才是笑點的關鍵！瞳孔滑動、眨眼停頓這些微表情要設計得恰到好處。

✨ **延伸挑戰**

👉 試試不同角色！「卡比揮手被當招財貓」、「皮卡丘揮尾巴被當風水擺設」、「史萊姆抖動被當許願池」！每個角色的「被誤認」方式都能創造獨特笑點。

🔥 **進階挑戰**：結合節慶元素——農曆新年版本放紅包和金幣、情人節版本放巧克力和小熊、聖誕版本放禮物盒——節慶版本的分享率通常高出 3 倍！
