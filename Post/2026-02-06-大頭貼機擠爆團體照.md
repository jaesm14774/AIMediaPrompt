💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **wildly different sizes**: 「極端大小差異」是這個 Prompt 的核心驅動力——最大角色佔 60%、中型佔 25%、最小只有 15%，三種尺寸的比例差創造出空間壓縮的喜劇效果 (Extreme size variation is the core driver—largest at 60%, medium at 25%, tiniest at 15%. This three-tier scale contrast creates spatial compression comedy)

- **varying outline thickness**: 線條粗細隨角色大小變化——大角色用粗線條、小角色用細線條，這個技巧在視覺上強化大小感知的反差，讓觀眾直覺感受到尺寸差異 (Outline thickness varies with character size—thicker for large, thinner for tiny. This technique visually reinforces size perception contrast, making viewers intuitively feel the scale difference)

- **manga-style visual effects**: 漫畫式視覺效果（動態線、汗珠、驚嘆號）為靜態畫面注入動態能量，讓觀眾感覺「這一秒之後一定更混亂」 (Manga-style VFX—motion lines, sweat drops, exclamation marks—inject dynamic energy into static images, making viewers feel "it's about to get even more chaotic")

- **countdown timer showing 3**: 拍貼機倒數計時「3」的細節暗示快門即將按下，為畫面增添緊迫感和故事性——角色們還來不及準備好！ (The countdown timer "3" detail implies the shutter is about to fire, adding urgency and narrative—the characters aren't ready yet!)
