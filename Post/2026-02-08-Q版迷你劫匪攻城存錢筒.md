💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **low dramatic angle at ground level**: 地面低角度拍攝讓存錢筒看起來像山一樣巨大，瞬間放大微型角色的史詩感 (Ground-level camera makes the piggy bank look like a mountain fortress — the single most important technique for "tiny epic" genre)

- **tilt-shift miniature photography effect**: 移軸攝影效果讓桌面場景同時保有微縮感和電影質感，模糊背景強調主角群 (Tilt-shift creates that dreamy miniature world look while keeping cinematic depth — selective focus guides the viewer's eye to the action)

- **4 siege divisions**: 四個攻城分隊讓畫面有豐富的敘事層次，觀眾會反覆觀看發現新細節 (Four assault divisions create narrative density — viewers will zoom in and discover new comedic details every time)

- **desk lamp as battlefield spotlight**: 桌燈當「戰場聚光燈」是天才設定，既合理（桌面場景）又有電影打光效果 (Using a real desk lamp as dramatic lighting is genius — it's both contextually logical and cinematically stunning)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 把所有角色畫成同樣大小、同樣動作，畫面會變得平淡無趣 (Making all characters the same size doing the same thing creates a flat, boring composition)

✅ 給每個分隊不同的「任務困境」— 有人成功、有人失敗、有人累癱，這些反差才是喜劇的來源 (Give each division different "mission problems" — some succeeding, some failing, some exhausted. These contrasts are the source of comedy)
