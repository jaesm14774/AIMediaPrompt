💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **speed ramp (速度漸變)**: 從慢速緊張氛圍（0.5x）到超高速彈飛（4x），這種速度急劇變化是喜劇效果的核心。緩慢的恐怖鋪墊讓最後的爆發更有爆發力 (From slow tension (0.5x) to ultra-fast launch (4x)—this dramatic speed shift IS the comedy. Slow horror build-up makes the explosive payoff exponentially funnier—"speed ramp" is a key technique in viral short-form content)

- **volumetric fog and god-rays (體積霧和丁達爾光)**: 穿過樹冠的月光光束和地面霧氣創造了「吉卜力風」的夢幻森林氛圍，同時也讓暗影生物的出現更有戲劇性 (Moonlight beams through the canopy and ground-level fog create a "Ghibli-esque" dreamy forest atmosphere while making the shadow creature's appearance more dramatic—"volumetric lighting" is a top search term in cinematic AI art)

- **shadow particle system (暗影粒子系統)**: 暗影生物的身體不是實心的——而是由不斷流動的暗色粒子構成，邊緣模糊且持續變形。這讓陰影感覺是「活的」而不是一塊黑色板子 (The shadow creature isn't solid—it's made of constantly flowing dark particles with blurred, shifting edges. This makes the shadow feel "alive" rather than a flat black shape—"shadow particle effect" adds horror sophistication)

- **tonal whiplash (氛圍急轉)**: 前 3.5 秒是正經的恐怖氛圍，最後 1.5 秒突然變成爆笑卡通。這種「認真鋪墊 → 荒謬結局」的反差是短影音最強大的喜劇公式 (First 3.5s play horror STRAIGHT, last 1.5s flip to slapstick cartoon. This "serious setup → absurd punchline" contrast is short-form video's most powerful comedy formula—guaranteed rewatches)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓暗影從一開始就很明顯——如果觀眾和角色同時發現危險，就失去了「觀眾知道但角色不知道」的緊張感（戲劇性反諷） (Making the shadow obvious from the start—if viewers and character discover the danger simultaneously, you lose the "audience knows but character doesn't" tension, known as dramatic irony)

✅ 暗影要「漸進式曝光」：第一幀只有微弱的眼睛光點，第二階段輪廓開始可見，高潮時才完全展露。同時，角色要做「反差極大」的無辜舉動（撿橡果、跟螢火蟲打招呼），讓觀眾的焦慮和角色的天真形成最大對比 (Shadow needs "progressive reveal": first frame shows only faint eye glints, second phase silhouette becomes visible, full reveal only at climax. Meanwhile, the character performs MAXIMALLY innocent actions (picking up acorns, waving at fireflies) to create the biggest contrast between viewer anxiety and character naivety—this contrast drives engagement and comments!)

💡 **SEO 秘訣**: 標題用「POV: 你看得到但他看不到」或「等等…背後那是什麼？！」這種懸念格式，在 TikTok 上點擊率極高 (Use "POV: you can see it but they can't" or "Wait... what's BEHIND them?!" suspense format in titles—these hook formats have extremely high click-through rates on TikTok)
