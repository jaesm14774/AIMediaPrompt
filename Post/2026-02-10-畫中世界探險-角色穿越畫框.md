💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **impasto brushstroke style (厚塗筆觸風格)**: 觸發 AI 生成梵高標誌性的「可見筆觸」質感——角色進入畫中後，整個身體會變成油畫顏料的厚塗堆疊效果，這是「藝術風格轉換」的核心關鍵字 (Triggers Van Gogh's signature "visible brushstroke" texture—the character's body becomes layered oil paint when inside the painting. "Impasto" is THE key term for art-style transformation—trending in AI art communities)

- **dual color grading (雙重調色)**: 在同一部影片中使用兩種完全不同的色彩溫度——博物館暖黃光（3500K）vs 畫中世界的冷調色彩。這種溫差對比讓觀眾直覺感受到「兩個世界」的差異 (Using two completely different color temperatures in one video—warm museum gold vs cool painting palette. This temperature contrast intuitively signals "two different worlds" to viewers—"dual grading" boosts cinematic quality perception)

- **fluid paint simulation (流體顏料模擬)**: 角色從畫中飛出時，身上的顏料噴濺需要有真實的流體物理效果——油畫顏料的黏稠度、飛濺軌跡、地板上的潑灑痕跡 (When the character bursts out, paint splatter needs realistic fluid physics—oil paint viscosity, splash trajectories, floor streaks. "Paint simulation" and "splatter effect" are popular search terms in VFX communities)

- **canvas surface bulging (畫布表面膨脹)**: 角色從畫內推擠畫面時，畫布像彈性薄膜一樣向外凸出——這個「物理不可能但視覺合理」的細節是整部影片最抓眼球的瞬間 (Canvas bulging outward when the character pushes from inside—this "physically impossible but visually logical" detail is the video's most eye-catching moment—unique visual hooks drive saves and shares)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓角色進入畫中後仍然保持原本的 3D 卡通風格——這樣就失去了「風格轉換」的魔力，觀眾不會覺得角色真的「進入」了畫裡 (Keeping the character's original 3D style inside the painting kills the "style transformation" magic—viewers won't feel the character truly "entered" the artwork)

✅ 關鍵是「角色本身變成那幅畫的一部分」——如果是梵高，角色要變成厚塗油畫質感；如果是浮世繪，要變成木版畫線條。進入不同畫作 = 不同的藝術風格轉換，這才是真正的視覺驚喜 (Key: "the character BECOMES part of the painting"—Van Gogh means impasto oil texture, Hokusai means woodblock print lines. Different paintings = different art style transformations. THAT's the real visual surprise—and each variation is a new piece of content!)
