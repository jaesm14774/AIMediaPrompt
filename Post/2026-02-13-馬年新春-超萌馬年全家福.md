💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **at least one character doing something wrong (至少一人出狀況)**: 全家福的喜劇核心——橘子塔快倒、睡著的貓、抱著比自己大的金元寶的倉鼠，這種「真實家庭照」的混亂感引發共鳴 (The comedy core: tangerine tower about to fall, sleeping cat, hamster hugging ingot bigger than itself—this "real family photo" chaos creates resonance)

- **chubby chibi horse with huge eyes (圓潤萌馬)**: 大眼、蘋果腮紅、紅絲帶編辮、小金鈴——馬作為「家庭一員」的萌化設計，是馬年主題的視覺中心 (Big eyes, apple cheeks, braided red ribbons, gold bells—horse as "family member" chibi design is the Horse Year visual center)

- **cozy group arrangement (溫馨群組構圖)**: 左右對稱或環繞式排列，主角騎馬居中，配角分列兩側，創造傳統全家福的儀式感 (Symmetrical or surrounding arrangement, main character on horse center, sidekicks flanking—creates traditional family portrait ceremony feel)

- **Clay-animation Pixar-quality (黏土動畫皮克斯質感)**: 略誇張比例、柔和環境光遮蔽、布料紋理細節，讓 3D 渲染有溫暖的手工感 (Slightly exaggerated proportions, soft ambient occlusion, fabric texture—gives 3D a warm handcrafted feel)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 所有人都完美擺拍——太完美的全家福缺乏「人味」，觀眾不會有「這好像我家」的共鳴 (Everyone posing perfectly—too perfect lacks "human touch", viewers won't feel "this looks like my family")

✅ 「至少一人出狀況」要具體：打瞌睡的、拿不穩的、偷吃餃子的、穿錯衣服的——具體的錯誤比抽象的「有人搞怪」更有喜劇效果 (The "wrong" element must be specific: dozing off, dropping things, sneaking dumplings, wrong outfit—specific mistakes beat abstract "someone being silly")
