💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **half-2D half-3D transition (半2D半3D過渡)**: 馬匹前腿已突破紙面、後腿仍是平面剪紙——這種「正在突破」的瞬間比完全 3D 更有動態張力 (Horse's front legs through paper, back legs still flat—this "mid-breakthrough" moment has more dynamic tension than full 3D)

- **layered depth (層次深度)**: 平面紅底 → 半浮雕裝飾 → 完全 3D 主角，三層結構創造立體書般的視覺驚喜 (Flat red background → semi-raised decor → full 3D characters—three-layer structure creates pop-up book surprise)

- **tilt-shift photography effect (移軸攝影效果)**: 微縮感讓春聯像精緻的立體賀卡，強化「實物感」和分享慾望 (Tilt-shift miniature feel makes the couplet look like a premium 3D greeting card—boosts "tangible" appeal and share desire)

- **paper texture visible (紙張紋理可見)**: 纖維紋理、輕微皺褶、燙金箔——細節讓 AI 輸出更有手工質感 (Fiber grain, crinkle, embossed foil—these details give AI output handcrafted texture)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓角色完全脫離春聯背景——失去「從紙中突破」的連結感，就只是普通的 3D 角色圖 (Characters fully detached from scroll—loses the "breaking out of paper" connection, becomes just a regular 3D character image)

✅ 保留春聯的平面紅底作為對比，讓 3D 突破的區域有明確的「邊界」——金箔飛濺、紙張撕裂邊緣、光從裂縫透出，這些細節是視覺焦點 (Keep flat red scroll as contrast—give 3D breach a clear "boundary": flying gold leaf, torn paper edges, light through cracks. These details are the visual hook)
