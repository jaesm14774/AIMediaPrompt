💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **giant red envelope overflowing (巨型紅包滿溢)**: 紅包大到手拿不住、金幣滿溢——視覺化「馬上有錢」的吉祥寓意，是構圖的核心焦點 (Oversized envelope, coins overflowing—visualizes "Money On Horse" auspicious meaning, the composition's core focus)

- **isometric 3/4 view (等角 3/4 視角)**: 適合展示角色、馬匹、配角隊伍的完整層次，同時保留雲朵、金幣、梅花等背景元素的空間感 (Shows full hierarchy of character, horse, parade—while preserving spatial depth of clouds, coins, plum blossoms)

- **trailing parade of companions (跟隨的配角隊伍)**: 拿橫幅的、打鼓的、抱橘子的——隊伍中「至少一人出狀況」（絆倒、拿不穩）增加喜劇層次 (Banner carriers, drum beaters, tangerine holders—"one tripping" adds comedy layer to the parade)

- **paper-cut style decorations (剪紙風格裝飾)**: 奔馬剪紙、金元寶、梅花——傳統元素與 3D 萌系角色的混搭創造文化辨識度 (Galloping horse paper-cuts, gold ingots, plum blossoms—traditional elements + 3D chibi creates cultural recognition)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 紅包設計成普通大小——失去「滿溢」「巨型」的誇張感，就無法強化「馬上有錢」的視覺衝擊 (Normal-sized envelope—loses the "overflowing" "giant" exaggeration, can't reinforce "Money On Horse" visual impact)

✅ 紅包要「大到不合理」、金幣要「多到灑出來」——這種適度的誇張是吉祥圖的傳統語彙，觀眾會直覺接收祝福感 (Envelope "unreasonably large", coins "spilling out"—this moderate exaggeration is traditional auspicious imagery, viewers intuitively receive the blessing)
