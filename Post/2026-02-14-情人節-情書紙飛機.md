No hundred roses needed. No fireworks show. A tiny character stands on top of a stack of books, launching a paper airplane folded from a pink love letter—the plane trails tiny hearts across the sky, slowly drawing one large heart shape. 80% of the image is sky and negative space. So quiet you can hear the heartbeat.

💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **negative space / generous white space (大量留白)**: 這張圖的核心美學不是「加了什麼」而是「沒加什麼」。角色只佔畫面 15-20%，天空佔了絕大部分——正是這種空曠讓一條小小的愛心軌跡變得無比有力 (The core aesthetic isn't what's ADDED but what's NOT. The character occupies only 15-20% of the frame with sky dominating—this vastness makes one tiny heart trail incredibly powerful. "Negative space illustration" is a premium art search term)

- **ehon style / Japanese picture book illustration (繪本風格)**: 日本繪本插畫的特色是柔和水彩、手繪感墨線、溫暖但不飽和的配色。這種風格天生就帶有「安靜」和「溫柔」的情緒，完美匹配這張圖的詩意氛圍 (Japanese ehon style features soft watercolor, hand-drawn ink lines, warm but muted palette. This style inherently carries "quiet" and "gentle" emotion—perfect for this image's poetic atmosphere)

- **dotted line of tiny hearts (愛心虛線軌跡)**: 紙飛機留下的不是煙霧而是一連串大小不一的小愛心——每顆稍有不同，模擬手繪的不完美感。這比完美對稱的愛心更有溫度和真實感 (The plane trails tiny hearts of varying sizes instead of smoke—each slightly different, simulating hand-drawn imperfection. This is warmer and more authentic than perfectly symmetrical hearts)

- **lipstick kiss mark on wing (機翼上的唇印)**: 一個微小但關鍵的細節——紙飛機翅膀上有一個淡粉色唇印和手寫字。讓觀眾知道這真的是一封情書，不只是普通紙飛機 (A tiny but crucial detail—a faint pink kiss mark and handwriting on the paper airplane's wing. This tells viewers it's truly a love letter, not just any paper plane)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 在天空中塞滿雲朵、星星、彩虹和裝飾——這會破壞「留白即力量」的核心美學，讓紙飛機的愛心軌跡淹沒在雜訊中 (Filling the sky with clouds, stars, rainbows and decorations—this destroys the "negative space IS power" core aesthetic and drowns the paper plane's heart trail in visual noise)

✅ 天空只需要一個乾淨的漸層色（桃色→薰衣草色），最多加 2-3 朵棉花糖小雲。留白不是「空」而是「靜」——讓觀眾的視線自然跟著那條愛心軌跡走。這種克制比堆疊更需要技巧！ (The sky needs only a clean gradient (peach → lavender) with at most 2-3 tiny cotton-candy clouds. Negative space isn't "empty" but "quiet"—letting viewers' eyes naturally follow the heart trail. This restraint requires MORE skill than stacking elements!)

✨ **Try This Variation / 延伸挑戰**

👉 試試「不同高處」版本！站在巨大鉛筆的橡皮擦頂端、歐式屋頂的煙囪上、一朵雲的邊緣——站得越高，天空越大，畫面越有詩意。每個位置都暗示不同的故事背景！

(Try different "heights"! Perched on a giant pencil's eraser tip, on a European rooftop chimney, on the edge of a cloud—the higher the perch, the bigger the sky, the more poetic the image. Each location hints at a different backstory!)

🔥 **進階挑戰**: 做成「對向版」——畫面左下角一個角色投出紙飛機，右上角另一個角色也投出一架。兩架紙飛機的愛心軌跡在天空中交會，形成一個完整的心形。雙人版的浪漫感翻倍！

(Advanced: Make a "two-way" version—character in lower-left launches a plane, another character in upper-right launches one back. Their heart trails meet in the sky to form one complete heart. Double the romance with the duo version!)
