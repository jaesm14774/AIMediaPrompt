💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **heart-shaped power bank (心形行動電源)**: 這是整個畫面的核心道具——將「充電」這個現代行為和「愛心」連結。行動電源必須是心形而非普通長方形，才能讓隱喻一目了然 (The heart-shaped power bank is the core prop—linking the modern act of "charging" with "love". It MUST be heart-shaped, not rectangular, for the metaphor to read instantly)

- **translucent battery indicator UI (半透明電池指示 UI)**: 模仿真實手機充電介面但用愛心取代標準圖示。這個 UI 元素浮在角色頭上，是整張圖的「笑點」——觀眾一看就懂「原來角色就是手機」(Mimics real phone charging UI but with hearts replacing standard icons. This floating UI element IS the punchline—viewers instantly get "the character IS the phone")

- **pixel hearts flowing through cable (像素愛心在線中流動)**: 像資料封包一樣在半透明充電線中流動的小愛心，增添了「科技感」和「魔法感」的有趣混搭 (Tiny hearts flowing through the semi-transparent cable like data packets add a fun blend of "tech feel" and "magic"—a detail that rewards closer inspection)

- **generous white space (大量留白)**: 極簡構圖的關鍵——背景越乾淨，單一隱喻就越有力。不需要其他裝飾元素，充電線和行動電源就是全部 (Key to minimalist composition—the cleaner the background, the more powerful the single metaphor. No decorative clutter needed; the cable and power bank say everything)
