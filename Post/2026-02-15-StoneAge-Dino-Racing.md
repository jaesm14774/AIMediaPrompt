💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **撞翻道具（knocked over）**：塵土飛揚中露出被撞翻的陶器車、訓練假人、商人攤位——製造「闖禍了」的喜劇張力，觀眾會自動腦補前因後果 (Dust cloud reveals knocked-over props — creates "we messed up" comedic tension, viewers imagine before and after)

- **搞怪恐龍（goofy dinosaur）**：迅猛龍像鵝叫、甲龍尾錘像棒棒糖、始祖鳥羽毛閃亮、蛇頸龍寶寶戴荷葉帽——每隻恐龍都有獨特喜感，不是嚴肅寫實風 (Each dino has unique comedy: goose-honking raptor, lollipop-tail ankylosaur, glittery archaeopteryx, lily-pad-hat plesiosaur — not serious realism)

- **觀眾反應（reaction shots）**： cheering 或 face-palming 的史前生物——把「賽道上的混亂」擴大成「全場都在看」的喜劇氛圍 (Cheering or face-palming creatures — expands chaos into "everyone's watching" comedy)

- **Cartoon Network 能量（motion blur + impact stars）**：動態模糊、衝擊星星、汗珠——週六早晨卡通的 freeze-frame 感，不是寫實賽車 (Motion blur, impact stars, sweat drops — Saturday morning cartoon freeze-frame, not realistic racing)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓恐龍跑得很帥或很威風——這不是競速熱血，而是「失控闖禍」的喜劇。恐龍要 goofy、clumsy、honking like a goose (Don't make the dinosaur cool or majestic — this isn't racing drama, it's "out-of-control mess" comedy. Dino should be goofy, clumsy, honking like a goose)
