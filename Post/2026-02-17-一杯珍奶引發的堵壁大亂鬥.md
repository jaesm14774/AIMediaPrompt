💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **每個裝飾元素有獨特「搶法」（each element grabs differently）**：牡丹用花瓣抓、菱格紋翻開伸手、文人探出身體、雲紋當觸手——四種不同的搶食方式讓畫面豐富，不是「全部一起伸手」的單調構圖 (Peonies grab with petals, lattice flips open tiny hands, scholars lean out, cloud spirals extend as tentacles — four distinct "grabbing styles" create rich composition instead of monotonous reaching)

- **外送地址的荒謬細節（absurd delivery address）**：「古厝堵壁中央山水畫，左邊第二位文人，不要加冰」——這個外送地址本身就是笑點，完全符合外送 app 上那種奇怪備註的調性 (The delivery address itself is a joke — perfectly mimics the absurd delivery notes we all see on food delivery apps)

- **菱格紋翻開露出小手（lattice tiles as trap doors）**：這個設定把原本最「無聊」的幾何裝飾變成最搶戲的元素——幾十隻陶瓷小手同時伸出來的畫面，在恐怖和可愛之間完美平衡 (Turning the "boring" geometric pattern into the scene-stealer — dozens of tiny ceramic hands emerging simultaneously, perfectly balanced between creepy and cute)

- **珍奶彈珠台效果（tapioca pachinko effect）**：珍珠在菱格紋的格子間彈跳像彈珠台——把兩個不相關的事物（古蹟幾何圖案×珍珠奶茶）用物理關係連結起來，創造意想不到的視覺效果 (Tapioca pearls bouncing through lattice cells like pachinko — physically connecting two unrelated things into an unexpected visual)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓某一個元素搶到珍奶——沒有人得到才是最好笑的結局！所有人搶→珍奶爆炸→全員被噴→一場空。這種「大家都輸」的結局比「某人贏了」更有社群討論度 (Don't let any single element get the boba — NOBODY winning is the funniest ending! Everyone grabs → tea explodes → everyone gets splashed → all for nothing. "Everyone loses" is more shareable than "someone wins")

✅ 外送員的「無力感」要到位——被百年古蹟集體霸凌的小小外送員，戴好安全帽、舉著空杯子說「配送完成」，這種打工族的職業精神笑點最能引起共感 (The delivery driver's helplessness must land — a tiny worker bullied by century-old heritage art, putting on their helmet, holding up the empty cup saying "delivery complete". This professional dignity comedy resonates hard)
