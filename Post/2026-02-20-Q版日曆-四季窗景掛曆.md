💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **portrait orientation, 2:3 aspect ratio**: 直式 2:3 比例模擬真實 8K 掛曆的紙張尺寸，AI 會自動生成適合印刷的版面而非橫式桌布 (Portrait 2:3 ratio mimics real 8K wall calendar paper size, making AI generate print-ready layouts)

- **full-bleed / edge-to-edge / no white space, no margins**: 三重指令強調「滿版」，避免 AI 生成四周留白的小風景畫。這是解決風景無法填滿畫面的關鍵 (Triple emphasis on "full-bleed" prevents AI from generating a small landscape with white borders—this is the key to filling the entire canvas)

- **semi-transparent frosted white panel**: 毛玻璃面板讓日期格線在豐富的風景背景上依然清晰可讀，同時不完全遮蓋底下的風景 (Frosted glass panel keeps date text readable over busy landscape backgrounds while still letting scenery show through)

- **spiral binding / metal ring holes**: 加入裝訂孔的物理細節讓 AI 理解這是一張掛曆而非一般插畫，會自動調整構圖和版面 (Physical binding details tell AI "this is a wall calendar, not just art"—it adjusts layout accordingly)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 如果把風景和日曆分成上下兩塊（上半插畫、下半白底格線），風景區域太小、白底區域太空，整體會很鬆散 (Splitting illustration/grid into two separate halves makes the landscape area too small and the white grid area too empty—looks loose)

✅ 用「滿版風景 + 毛玻璃面板疊加」的方式，風景鋪滿整張畫布，日曆用半透明面板疊在上方。這樣風景有震撼力、日曆有可讀性，兩全其美 (Use "full-bleed landscape + frosted overlay" approach: scenery fills 100% of the canvas, calendar panel overlays on top. Landscape has impact, calendar stays readable—best of both worlds)

💡 **配色技巧**: 春天用粉綠色面板、夏天用淡藍、秋天用半透明米色、冬天用霧白——讓毛玻璃面板的色調跟季節呼應 (Color tip: spring gets pink-green panel, summer pale blue, autumn translucent cream, winter misty white—match the frosted panel tint to the season)
