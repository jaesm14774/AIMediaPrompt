💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **3:2 aspect ratio / 16-kai desk calendar**: 明確的桌曆比例讓 AI 生成橫式長方形，而非正方形或不規則形狀。加上 "tent-fold stand" 讓 AI 理解這是立式桌曆 (Specific desk calendar ratio generates landscape rectangles. Adding "tent-fold stand" tells AI this is a standing desk calendar)

- **cream-parchment textured background**: 底色用「奶油色羊皮紙質感」而非純白，讓整體有烘焙的溫暖感。這個材質指令會讓 AI 自動加入微妙的紙張纖維紋理 (Cream-parchment texture instead of pure white gives a baked warmth. This material cue triggers AI to add subtle paper fiber textures)

- **not overlapping the date numbers**: 關鍵！告訴 AI 甜點裝飾只能在「邊角」，不能壓到日期數字上。沒有這句，AI 會把蛋糕直接畫在日期上面 (Critical! Telling AI decorations go in "margins only" and must NOT overlap dates. Without this, AI puts cupcakes right on top of date numbers)

- **spiral binding along the top edge**: 螺旋裝訂細節讓 AI 把這張圖理解為「一個物體」（桌曆），而非「一張平面插畫」，構圖會更像真實產品 (Spiral binding makes AI understand this is "an object" (desk calendar), not "a flat illustration"—composition becomes more product-like)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 不要讓甜點裝飾太多太密——日曆的重點是「看日期」，甜點只是調味料。如果整個格線被蛋糕包圍，會變成一張甜點插畫而非日曆 (Don't over-decorate with pastries—the calendar's purpose is "reading dates", pastries are just seasoning. Too many pastries turn it into a food illustration, not a calendar)

✅ 每週配一個小甜點就夠了！讓裝飾有「節制的可愛」——week 1 可頌、week 2 甜甜圈、week 3 蛋糕、week 4 馬卡龍。少即是多 (One small pastry per week is enough! Aim for "restrained cuteness"—croissant week 1, donut week 2, cake week 3, macarons week 4. Less is more)

💡 **色調統一秘訣**: 所有元素都用「烘焙色系」——奶油黃、焦糖棕、玫瑰粉、巧克力深棕。角色也穿同色系的圍裙和帽子，讓整張圖的色彩和諧不跳 (Color unity tip: keep everything in "bakery palette"—butter yellow, caramel, rose pink, chocolate brown. Even the character's apron matches, so nothing feels out of place)
