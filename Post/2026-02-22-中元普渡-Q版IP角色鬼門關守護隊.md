💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **dual-temperature color split（冷暖雙色溫分割）**: 畫面被鬼門一分為二——人間側用溫暖的琥珀金色（3500K），靈界側用冷冽的藍綠紫色（6500K）。這個色溫對比不只是好看，而是視覺上區分「安全/未知」的心理暗示，讓觀眾直覺感受到「門的這邊是家，那邊是未知」(The frame is split by the Ghost Gate — mortal side warm amber-gold (3500K), spirit side cool blue-green-purple (6500K). This temperature contrast isn't just aesthetic — it's a psychological cue separating "safe/unknown", making viewers intuitively feel "this side is home, that side is mystery")

- **ghosts queuing politely with numbered tickets（好兄弟乖乖排隊拿號碼牌）**: 這是整個概念最核心的反差——恐怖的鬼門關變成了有秩序的「服務窗口」，好兄弟不是可怕的存在，而是需要被服務的「顧客」。用現代服務業的概念重新包裝民間信仰，既化解恐懼又保留尊重 (This is the core contrast — the terrifying Ghost Gate becomes an orderly "service window", ghosts aren't scary beings but "customers" needing service. Repackaging folk belief with modern service industry concepts dissolves fear while preserving respect)

- **Kirby inhaling Zhong Kui for transformation（Kirby吸入鍾馗變身）**: 延續 Kirby Copy Ability 的核心機制，但對象換成了中華文化的正義之神。鍾馗是驅邪之神，Kirby 吸入後變成「粉紅正義判官」——兩個IP的核心精神（正義守護）完美結合，不是硬湊而是靈魂共鳴 (Extending Kirby's Copy Ability mechanic, but the target is now a Chinese deity of justice. Zhong Kui is the demon-queller god; Kirby absorbing him creates a "pink justice judge" — both IPs share the core spirit of righteous protection, making this a soul resonance, not a forced mashup)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 把好兄弟畫成恐怖的樣子——如果鬼魂太可怕，就破壞了整個「可愛守護」的概念。好兄弟應該是「穿著床單的小圓球」等級的萌，讓觀眾想摸牠們而不是怕牠們 (Don't make ghosts look scary — if spirits are too frightening, it ruins the "cute guardian" concept. Ghosts should be "small round things in bedsheets" level cute, making viewers want to pet them, not fear them)

✅ 讓守護者的「正義行為」充滿反差萌——Kirby 的判官印章是粉紅色的、Hello Kitty 的法槌是蝴蝶結形的、Gengar 在做心理諮商。「用最可愛的方式執行最嚴肅的工作」才是這個概念的靈魂 (Make the guardians' "justice actions" full of cute contrast — Kirby's judge seal is pink, Hello Kitty's gavel is bow-shaped, Gengar does ghost therapy. "Doing the most serious work in the cutest way possible" is the soul of this concept)
