💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **each character has a role rooted in their original ability（每個角色的龍舟職位都根據原始能力）**: Kirby 當鼓手因為他的吸氣能力可以當渦輪、Pikachu 用電力加速、Totoro 用荷葉當帆——不是隨便分配位置，而是用角色的原始能力製造「合理的荒謬」(Each boat position is assigned based on the character's original ability — Kirby's inhale = turbo engine, Pikachu's electricity = speed boost, Totoro's leaf = sail. Not random assignment, but "logical absurdity" using their real powers)

- **competing teams with contrasting strategies（多隊同時競爭且策略各異）**: 不是一隻船的特寫——而是多支隊伍同時衝刺，每隊風格完全不同。觀眾可以「選邊站」，這種運動漫畫式的多方對決讓畫面充滿動態張力 (Not a single boat close-up — multiple teams racing simultaneously with completely different styles. Viewers can "pick sides", this sports-manga style multi-team clash creates dynamic tension)

- **Qu Yuan as referee on zongzi podium（屈原站在粽子做的裁判台上）**: 把端午節的歷史人物放進裁判角色，而非傷感的投江故事——這是一種「正向重新詮釋」，用可愛和歡樂來致敬文化，同時保留了屈原的存在感和尊嚴 (Putting the historical figure in a referee role instead of the sad river story — this is a "positive reinterpretation" that honors culture with joy and cuteness while preserving Qu Yuan's presence and dignity)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓所有隊伍用一樣的划船方式——如果三隊都只是「正常划船」，就失去了 IP 角色各自特色的意義。每隊的前進方式必須反映角色的核心能力 (Don't let all teams row the same way — if everyone just "rows normally", you lose the point of using IP characters. Each team's propulsion method must reflect their core abilities)

✅ 讓「失敗和混亂」佔據至少一半畫面——Jigglypuff 划到睡著、Snorlax 根本沒在控船、Totoro 太大卡在船裡。完美的競賽不好笑，充滿意外的競賽才讓人笑到肚子痛 (Let "failure and chaos" fill at least half the frame — Jigglypuff falling asleep mid-row, Snorlax not steering at all, Totoro stuck in the boat. A perfect race isn't funny; a race full of accidents is what makes people laugh until they cry)
