💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **Half-flat, half-3D transition（半平面半立體過渡帶）**: 這是整個畫面的靈魂。不是角色「站在」刺繡旁邊，而是角色的下半身**還是**繡線、上半身**已經**活了。那個過渡帶——繡線一根根解開、向上捲曲、重新組成圓潤皮膚——這個「正在變化中」的瞬間比完全變好更有張力 (The soul of the image. Not the character standing beside embroidery, but the body literally transitioning — lower half IS still thread, upper half HAS become alive. The transformation zone is more powerful than the finished result)

- **Fabric-as-landscape（布面即地貌）**: 毛巾圈是山丘、布邊是山脈、皺褶是峽谷。Tilt-shift 鏡頭讓這個微型世界看起來像真正的史詩風景。角色在布面上留下的小腳印更強化了「真的在走」的感覺 (Terry loops as hills, fabric edge as mountain range, wrinkles as valleys. Tilt-shift lens makes this micro world feel epic. Tiny footprints on the cloth prove "really walking")

- **Moonlight vs embroidery-glow（月光 vs 繡光對比）**: 冷色月光＋暖色金線發光＝童話氛圍。這不是白天的清晰世界，是深夜才會發生的秘密魔法 (Cool moonlight + warm golden thread glow = fairy-tale atmosphere. This isn't daytime clarity — it's midnight magic that only happens when no one is watching)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓角色完全從刺繡中跳出來站在旁邊。那就只是「一個角色＋一塊布」，失去了全部的魔法感 (Having the character fully out and standing beside the embroidery. That's just "a character + a cloth" — all the magic is lost)

✅ 保持角色卡在「正在變化」的中間狀態——一半繡線一半真實。這個未完成的瞬間才是讓人屏住呼吸的地方 (Keep the character stuck in the "transforming" in-between state — half thread, half real. This unfinished moment is where people hold their breath)
