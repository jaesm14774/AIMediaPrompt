💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **Chiikawa / Sumikko Gurashi aesthetic（角落生物/吉伊卡哇美學）**: 這兩個 IP 定義了「簡約 Q 版」的黃金標準——粗圓線條、極簡表情、柔和色彩填充。把它們當作風格錨點，AI 就能生成正確的簡約可愛感 (These two IPs define the gold standard for "minimal chibi" — thick rounded lines, minimal expressions, soft color fills. Using them as style anchors helps AI generate the right simple-cute feel)

- **Head-to-body ratio 3:1（頭身比 3:1）**: Q 版角色的核心公式！頭佔身體三倍大，讓角色自動變得圓潤可愛。比例越誇張，角色越有「想捏」的衝動 (The core formula for chibi characters! Head three times bigger than body makes characters automatically round and cute. The more exaggerated, the more "squishable" they feel)

- **Receipt paper like a scarf（收據當圍巾）**: 不是隨便亂放道具——每個小配件都在講故事。價格標在頭上 = 剛被買回來、收據纏身 = 購物現場、硬幣黏額頭 = 混亂中的意外。這些「購物殘留物」讓角色的「商品身份」變得好笑 (Not random props — each accessory tells a story. Price tag on head = just purchased, receipt tangled = shopping chaos, coin on forehead = accidental aftermath. These "shopping remnants" make the character's "merchandise identity" hilarious)

- **Translucent white bag（半透明白色袋子）**: 用半透明材質讓觀眾隱約看到袋內還有更多角色的剪影，暗示「裝了太多」。如果是不透明的袋子，就失去了「數量失控」的視覺暗示 (Translucent material lets viewers glimpse silhouettes of more characters still inside, implying "way too many." An opaque bag would lose the visual hint of quantity overload)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 只畫角色「站在購物袋旁邊」或「從袋子裡探出頭」——這太溫和了，完全沒有「爆炸式逃脫」的戲劇張力和喜感 (Just drawing characters "standing next to a shopping bag" or "peeking out" — too tame, zero explosive breakout drama or comedy)

✅ 關鍵字是「炸開」和「擠壓」：用 "stretched to its absolute limit"、"nearly bursting"、"cheeks squished"、"snapped handle" 建立物理壓力感。讓觀眾感覺「再塞一隻就真的破了」！散落的飲料和收據讓混亂感更真實 (Keywords are "bursting" and "squeeze": use "stretched to limit", "nearly bursting", "cheeks squished", "snapped handle" to build physical tension. Viewers should feel "one more and it really pops!" Scattered drinks and receipts add authentic chaos)

---

✨ **Try This Variation / 延伸挑戰**

👉 試試「不同容器版」：把購物袋換成紙袋、環保購物袋、甚至是日本百貨公司的高級紙提袋——角色從精美包裝裡爆出來的反差更大！或者做「季節限定版」：聖誕紅白袋、萬聖節南瓜袋、情人節心形袋，每個節日一個逃脫主題。

(Try "different container versions": swap the plastic bag for a paper bag, eco tote, or even a fancy Japanese department store gift bag — characters bursting from elegant packaging creates even stronger contrast! Or make "seasonal editions": Christmas red-white bag, Halloween pumpkin bag, Valentine's heart bag — one breakout theme per holiday.)
