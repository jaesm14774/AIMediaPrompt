💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **和洋折衷的核心邏輯（East-meets-West Fusion Logic）**: 不是「和服旁邊放一件禮服」，而是在同一件服裝上讓兩種美學交融。上身是西式馬甲配和服衿（V 領領口），下身是西式裙撐配和布料。帶結是和式技法但做成角色的尾巴形狀。每一個設計元素都要同時有「和」跟「洋」的痕跡 (Not "kimono next to a gown" but both aesthetics fused in ONE garment. Upper body: Western corset with kimono collar. Lower body: Western crinoline with chirimen silk. Obi bow: Japanese technique shaped like the character's tail. Every element must show BOTH Eastern and Western traces)

- **文化衝突萌（Culture Clash Cuteness）**: 這個 template 的靈魂細節！小角色穿上正式的和洋盛裝時的不完美感——袖子太長拖在地上、帶子繞太多圈變得蓬蓬的、頭飾因為頭太圓而微微歪斜。就像小孩在祭典上穿大人的和服一樣，「不合身」本身就是最可愛的地方 (The template's soul detail! The imperfect fit when a small character wears formal East-West wear — sleeves dragging, obi wrapped too many times making it puffy, headpiece tilting on a too-round head. Like a child wearing an adult's kimono at a festival — the "doesn't quite fit" IS the cutest part)

- **和紙質感背景（Washi Paper Background）**: 用和紙質感的米白色背景取代一般的白色背景，搭配傳統雲紋浮水印，讓整張設計圖有「日本傳統 × 現代角色設計」的融合氛圍。左上角的靈感角色用水墨畫風格呈現（而非一般的卡通畫風），強化「古典掛軸」的感覺 (Warm washi-paper textured off-white replaces generic white background, with traditional cloud-pattern watermarks. The inspiration character in the upper corner uses ink-wash painting style instead of cartoon style, reinforcing the "classical hanging scroll" feel)

- **Mon 紋章彩蛋（Mon Crest Easter Egg）**: 穿著者的現代標誌被重新設計成傳統日式家紋——閃電符號變成閃電紋章、星星變成星紋。這個古典化的翻譯增加了「時空融合」的趣味，也是最有分享價值的細節之一 (The wearer's modern symbol redesigned as a traditional Japanese mon family crest — lightning bolt becomes lightning mon, star becomes star mon. This classical translation adds "temporal fusion" fun and high share value)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓和服和禮服各佔一半互不干涉（那是「一半和服一半禮服」的簡單拼接）。真正的和洋折衷是讓兩種元素在同一個部位交融——馬甲配衿口、裙撐配和布、西式鞋跟配漆器質感 (Don't let kimono and gown each take half without interacting. Real fusion means BOTH aesthetics merging at every point — corset with kimono collar, crinoline with chirimen silk, Western heels with lacquer finish)

✅ 利用和服的層疊特性最大化靈感角色的配色展示：三層裙擺由淺到深展示角色的配色漸層，九條拖尾布料對應九尾狐的九條尾巴（每條不同深淺），振袖的內裡和外面用角色的對比色。帶結是配色的焦點——用角色最鮮豔的顏色，搭配金線刺繡，做成角色招牌特徵的形狀。整件服裝就是角色配色板的立體展開 (Maximize the source character's color display through kimono layering: three skirt layers from light to deep show the color gradient, nine train panels match nine tails (each different shade), furisode inner/outer lining uses contrasting character colors. Obi bow is the color focal point — character's most vivid color with gold embroidery, shaped like their signature feature. The entire outfit IS the character's color palette in 3D)
