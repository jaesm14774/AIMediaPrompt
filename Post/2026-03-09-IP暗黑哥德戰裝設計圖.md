⚔️ **可愛角色的暗黑覺醒！IP哥德戰裝設計圖 | Cute Characters Go Dark! IP Gothic Battle-Dress Design Sheet**

把可愛角色塞進暗黑哥德鎧甲禮服裡——鎧甲刻著靈感角色的花紋、裙擺拖著暗影、發光的寶石從縫隙中透出妖豔光芒。但最致命的反差是：裡面那個圓滾滾的可愛傢伙正在非常認真地擺出霸氣姿勢。Gap Moe 拉滿。

Stuff cute characters into dark gothic armor-gowns — armor etched with the inspiration character's patterns, trains trailing shadows, gems glowing eerily through the seams. But the most lethal contrast? The round, adorable creature inside is VERY SERIOUSLY striking a power pose. Gap moe maxed out.

💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **Gap Moe 反差萌（Cute Inside, Dark Outside）**: 這個 prompt 的核心不是「暗黑服裝」本身，而是可愛角色穿暗黑服裝時產生的反差。Kirby 的圓滾身形在尖角鎧甲裡像棉花糖裝進坦克車；認真擺霸氣姿勢但手太短。這個反差才是讓人想分享的關鍵 (The core isn't the dark outfit itself — it's the GAP between cute character and dark outfit. Kirby's round shape in angular armor is a marshmallow in a tank; power-posing with stubby arms. This gap is what makes people want to share)

- **暗化配色邏輯（Darkened Color Logic）**: 靈感角色的原色不能直接用——要全部降 2-3 階暗度。紫色→暗紫、紅色→血紅、藍色→深海藍。但保留一個「發光強調色」（Gengar 的洋紅），讓暗色調中有一個刺眼的亮點，增加視覺焦點 (Source character's colors can't be used directly — darken everything 2-3 stops. Purple→deep purple, red→blood red, blue→deep sea blue. But keep ONE vivid glow accent that pierces through the darkness, creating a visual focal point)

- **鎧甲花紋 = 角色紋路（Armor Engravings = Character Patterns）**: 鎧甲表面的蝕刻花紋是展示靈感角色細節的最佳位置。Gengar 的幽靈漩渦、Charizard 的龍鱗紋、Mewtwo 的能量脈絡——這些紋路被蝕刻在金屬上，用發光琺瑯填充。近看才發現「原來鎧甲上藏了這麼多細節！」 (Armor surface engravings are the perfect place for inspiration character details. Gengar's phantom swirls, Charizard's dragon scales, Mewtwo's energy veins — etched into metal, filled with glowing enamel. Close inspection reveals hidden details everywhere!)

- **表情 Sheet（Expression Sheet）**: callout 區域的迷你表情組圖增加了趣味——穿著暗黑全套的可愛角色分別展示「霸氣戰吼」「困惑臉紅」「打瞌睡」三種表情，每一種都在強化反差萌 (The mini expression sheet adds fun — the cute character in full dark armor showing "fierce battle cry," "confused blush," and "sleepy nod" — each one amplifying the gap moe)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓可愛角色完全變成暗黑角色（失去反差就失去重點）。鎧甲可以很暗黑，但穿著者的臉、體型、招牌特徵必須保持可愛原樣。如果 Kirby 穿上暗黑裝後看起來完全不像 Kirby，那設計就失敗了 (Don't let the cute character become fully dark — losing the gap kills the point. Armor can be dark, but the wearer's face, body shape, and signature features must stay cute. If armored Kirby doesn't look like Kirby anymore, the design fails)

✅ 暗黑背景用深色羊皮紙 + 魔法陣浮水印，配合發光的連接線，創造「黑魔法設計圖」的感覺。武器/權杖是這個 template 獨有的加分項——把靈感角色的力量凝縮成一個可以手持的物件（Gengar 的影球→暗影權杖、Charizard 的火焰→燃燒戰扇），讓穿著者不只是「穿」了角色，更是「駕馭」了角色的力量 (Dark parchment background + arcane circle watermarks + glowing connection lines create a "dark magic design sheet" feel. The weapon/scepter is unique to this template — crystallize the source character's power into a held object, making the wearer not just "wear" the character but "wield" their power)
