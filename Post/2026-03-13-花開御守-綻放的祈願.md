💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **washi paper + blown glass fusion**: 和紙的有機纖維感 + 玻璃的透光性——這個「不可能材質」讓花瓣既有紙的溫暖觸感又有玻璃的光學效果。逆光時和紙纖維可見，正光時玻璃反射閃爍。兩個矛盾的材質融合在一起就是視覺衝擊力 (Washi paper warmth + glass translucency — this "impossible material" gives petals both organic warmth and optical brilliance. Backlit shows paper fibers, front-lit shows glass reflections. The contradiction IS the visual hook)

- **prayer inscriptions visible only when backlit**: 花瓣脈絡裡藏著的祝詞只有透光時才看得見——這是御守「內藏神力」概念的完美視覺化。暗示這朵花的美麗不只是表面的，它的每一條「血管」都是祝福 (Prayer inscriptions visible only through backlight — this perfectly visualizes the omamori concept of "hidden sacred power." Every vein in this flower is literally a blessing)

- **dewdrops as micro-lenses**: 花瓣上的露珠放大了底下的祝詞文字——這是一個「越靠近看越有驚喜」的細節設計。讓觀眾在社群上反覆放大圖片尋找隱藏細節，大幅提升互動時間 (Dewdrops magnifying the prayer inscriptions beneath — a "the closer you look, the more you discover" design. Viewers zoom in repeatedly on social media, dramatically increasing engagement time)

- **character causing the bloom**: 角色不只是「在花裡面」，而是正在「讓花綻放」——舉起雙手，花瓣回應展開。這讓角色不是被動的裝飾，而是場景的推動者。「祈願讓花開」= 情感核心 (The character isn't just "inside the flower" — they're MAKING it bloom. Hands raised, petals responding. This makes the character an active agent, not passive decoration. "A wish makes the flower bloom" = emotional core)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓花完全盛開、花瓣全部打開——看起來只是「角色坐在花裡」，沒有動態感和故事性 (Having the flower fully bloomed with all petals open — it just looks like "character sitting in a flower," no movement, no narrative)

✅ 讓花「正在綻放」——外層已開、中層半開、內層仍包裹著角色。這個「正在進行」的狀態創造了動態張力和故事：祈願正在實現的瞬間。加上散落的花瓣 = 祝福正在散播到世界。整張圖充滿了「即將完成」的美麗懸念 (Keep the flower MID-bloom — outer open, middle half-open, inner still wrapping the character. This "in-progress" state creates dynamic tension and narrative: the moment a prayer is being answered. Plus scattered petals = blessings spreading into the world. The whole image vibrates with beautiful anticipation)
