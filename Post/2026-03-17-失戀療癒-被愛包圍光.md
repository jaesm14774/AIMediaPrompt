💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **gentle and earnest, never pitying（溫柔真誠，從不憐憫）**: 這個描述決定了陪伴角色的情緒基調，不是高高在上的安慰，而是安靜站在你身邊的愛 (This phrase defines the companion's emotional register, creating support that feels equal, grounded, and sincere)

- **Everything glows from within（一切從內部發光）**: 讓畫面的光源來自角色本身，而非外部射燈，傳遞「愛是內在的溫暖」而非表演給人看的 (Internal glow sources create intimacy—love that radiates outward from within, not performed for an audience)

- **No rain, no darkness, no cold（無雨、無黑暗、無寒冷）**: 明確告訴 AI 這不是悲劇場景，引導整體色調往溫暖走，避免 AI 預設的「失戀 = 黑暗雨天」視覺慣性 (Explicit negative instruction prevents AI from defaulting to dark/rainy "heartbreak" aesthetics—critical for healing-focused content)

- **warm peach, amber, and cream（暖桃、琥珀、奶油色）**: 這三色組合比「粉紅色」更有層次感，溫暖但不甜膩，療癒但不幼稚 (This trio is warmer and more sophisticated than plain pink—healing without being saccharine)

- **gentle rim light on both figures（兩個角色都有柔和輪廓光）**: 讓主角與陪伴角色同樣被光包圍，視覺上傳遞「你們是同等重要的存在」 (Rim light on both figures visually equalizes them, showing that the one receiving love matters just as much as the one offering it)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 把陪伴角色的表情描述成「很開心」或過度卡通式燦笑，畫面會變成可愛風，而不是療癒陪伴風 (Making the companion overly cheerful or cartoonishly smiley shifts the image toward cute fantasy instead of emotional healing)

✅ 用「gentle and earnest」、「steady」、「quiet love」這類詞描述陪伴角色的神情，溫柔但不輕浮，這才是真正的陪伴感 (Use "gentle and earnest", "steady", and "quiet love" for the companion's expression to keep the warmth deep rather than shallow)

👉 試試把主角換成動物——縮起來的小貓、縮在角落的小兔，畫面的療癒感更強，也更容易讓人「把自己投射進去」。
也可以把陪伴角色換成光之精靈、毛茸茸守護者、溫柔的大人形象，讓情感投射更自由。
