💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **like the inside of a lantern（像燈籠內部）**: 把金色光雲比喻成燈籠內側，讓 AI 生成的光有「被包裹其中」的溫度感，而不是外部打光 (Comparing clouds to the inside of a lantern gives AI a precise light-quality reference—warm, enclosed, intimate rather than illuminated from outside)

- **present, unhurried, asking nothing（在場、不催促、不索求）**: 三個詞精準定義了療癒式陪伴的本質，不是急著解決問題，而是存在本身就是答案 (These three words define what healing companionship actually means, guiding the companion's posture and emotional presence)

- **pulse faintly, breathing（輕輕脈動，像在呼吸）**: 讓靜態圖像有生命感，雲朵「呼吸」的描述讓整個場景有了體溫 (Adding breath to the clouds gives a static image a living quality—the scene itself breathes with the viewer)

- **No sharp edges anywhere（畫面中沒有任何銳利邊緣）**: 從視覺心理學角度，圓角與柔化邊緣降低威脅感，全圖柔化是療癒型內容的視覺語法 (Psychologically, soft edges signal safety. This single instruction shifts the entire visual grammar toward comfort and healing)

- **soft floating guardian（漂浮的柔光守護者）**: 這種描述保留夢幻感，同時不把角色限制成特定 IP，更適合做多元角色延伸 (This phrase keeps the scene dreamy while avoiding IP lock-in, making the template more reusable across many character styles)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 把背景色設定成白色或藍色——白色太冷漠，藍色會往憂鬱走，都會破壞療癒感 (White backgrounds feel clinical; blue backgrounds pull toward melancholy—both undermine the healing intention)

✅ 背景堅持使用「cream to honey gold」的漸層，這個色組是視覺上最接近「被擁抱感覺」的色調組合 (Stick to "cream to honey gold" gradients—this color range visually approximates the sensation of being held, supported by color psychology research)
