🎬💃✨ **三人同步舞一次到位：AI 短影片節拍教學 | Trio Sync Dance: Beat-Perfect AI Short Video**

三個同款角色從不同遠近走入畫面，格子地板上的同步舞＋最後齊指鏡頭——用結構化分鏡把 6 秒塞滿記憶點，Reels／Shorts 都適用。
Three identical characters enter from different depths, hit a grid-floor sync dance, then point at the camera in unison—structured beats pack six seconds with shareable hooks for Reels and Shorts.

👇 **Prompt Template / 指令模板**

> 提供可重複使用的結構，讓用戶可以自定義。

## Prompt Template

A 6-second video of THREE **[IP角色]** performing a perfectly synchronized dance on a `[地板：neon grid floor / green glowing tile floor / holographic dance floor]`, each entering from a different direction.

**Scene 1 - Three Entrances (0-2.0s)**

- **角色 / Character：** `[IP角色 描述："round, glossy, [COLOR], with tiny stub arms and feet"]` × 3  
  - Lower-left: biggest, closest  
  - Upper-left: medium, mid-distance  
  - Upper-right: smallest, farthest  
- **入場表情 / Entry expression（各擇一）：** `"confident closed-eye grin, one arm swinging"` / `"focused determined expression, chin slightly down"` / `"smug smirk, bouncing as it walks"`  
- **地板效果 / Floor FX：** `[地板效果：concentric green rings spreading from each footstep / neon light pulse following each step]`  
- **背景 / Background：** `[背景：bright lime-green gradient / mint and yellow gradient]` + subtle grid to horizon

**Scene 2 - The Pose Lock (2.0-3.5s)**

All three reach their marks SIMULTANEOUSLY on a beat. FREEZE — same exact pose.

- **舞蹈姿勢 / Dance pose（擇一）：**  
  - `"one arm pulled back in a punch-ready position, the other straight out, leaning slightly forward with fierce expression"`  
  - `"both arms out wide, tiny legs planted, puffed up with pride"`  
  - `"one arm raised diagonally, head tilted, one eye squinting coolly"`  
- Grid floor FLASHES bright on the beat. Camera: low angle, depth perspective with all three in line

**Scene 3 - The Groove (3.5-5.0s)**

Synchronized movement on every beat.

- **節奏動作 / Beat moves（擇一）：**  
  - `"bouncing left-right alternating, arms pumping"`  
  - `"spinning once, then landing back in sync"`  
  - `"each one does a tiny body roll, perfectly timed, rippling front-to-back (closest first, farthest last)"`  
- **節拍特效 / Beat FX：** `[small burst of sparkles on each landing / their footsteps light up the grid tiles in sequence]`  
- Foreground character larger. **互動 / Interaction：** `[smug competitive grins / exchanging nods / winking at camera together]`

**Scene 4 - Point at Camera (5.0-6.0s)**

On final beat, ALL THREE point at camera.

- **指向動作 / Point（擇一）：** `"one stubby arm extended, other on hip, leaning slightly forward"` / `"both arms forward, jazz-hands style"` / `"jumping and pointing mid-air"`  
- **表情 / Expression：** `"biggest grins possible, eyes crinkled closed in joy"` / `"cool confident smirk, absolute swagger"`  
- Background BURSTS: `[特效：star confetti / light rays / color explosion]`. Freeze.

**Visual Style**

Depth-of-field (nearest sharpest, farthest slightly soft). Grid floor as hero stage. Near/far perspective. Color palette: `[配色：bright lime-green and cream / cool mint and white]` with neon accent flashes.

**Technical specs**

9:16 vertical, 4K, 60fps. Camera: low-angle static, perspective shot. Beat-synchronized grid floor effects. Sound: building electronic beat with crisp percussion on sync moments.


💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **三點入場遠近（Lower-left / Upper-left / Upper-right）**: 用「最大最近、中等、最小最遠」建立透視與主角層次，避免三隻貼成一排平面 (Three-depth staging creates parallax and a clear focal character—strong for "sync dance" and "group choreography" search intent)

- **Scene 2 FREEZE on beat**: 全員同拍定格是記憶點，方便演算法辨識「節奏剪輯」與重播 (Simultaneous freeze sells the beat; helps short-form algorithms tag high-retention sync moments)

- **9:16 · 4K · 60fps · low-angle static**: 直式滿版＋低角強化舞台感；60fps 讓踏步與地板光效更乾淨 (Vertical 4K60 matches Shorts/Reels defaults; low static camera reads as "performance stage" in thumbnails)

- **Sound: building beat + crisp percussion**: 聲畫對齊寫進 prompt，降低「動作與節拍脫拍」的失敗率 (Explicit audio brief reduces off-beat motion—useful for tools that accept audio or sync hints)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 三隻角色大小與站位都一樣，畫面變平面團體照 (Same scale and alignment flattens the shot—loses the template's depth trick)

✅ 先鎖定一個 `[IP角色]` 與一組括號內地板／背景，再微調表情與 Scene 2 舞姿；每輪只改一個變因方便對片 (Lock one IP and one bracket set per pass; change one variable at a time when comparing takes—cleaner iteration and clearer SEO filenames like `trio-sync-kirby-neon-grid`)

✨ **Try This Variation / 延伸挑戰**

👉 把「三人」改成同一角色的三種配色或三種小配件（帽子／領結），仍保留遠近層次，觀眾更容易分辨誰是 C 位。英文搜尋可帶 `character variant trio` 類長尾。
(Try three colorways or tiny props on the same character archetype while keeping depth staging—easier to read who leads; long-tail English: "character variant trio".)

🔥 **進階**: 節拍點改為副歌 drop，Scene 4 指向鏡頭時疊一個大字標題 safe area，方便加字幕封面。
(Advanced: align the point-at-camera beat with a chorus drop and leave title-safe space for on-screen text—strong for cover frames.)
