💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **No floating text labels**: 明確禁止 AI 在畫面中生成浮空文字標籤——所有故事敘述必須透過表情、肢體語言和對話泡泡呈現，這是避免 AI 圖像生成失控的關鍵指令。 (Explicitly banning floating text labels forces the AI to rely on visual storytelling — preventing the dreaded "text artifact" failure mode common in comic strip prompts.)

- **4-panel 起承轉結 structure**: 每格對應明確的情緒階段（威嚇→誤解→加深→爆發），讓 AI 在有限格數內完成完整故事弧線，避免每格情緒重複或無變化。 (Each panel maps to a distinct emotional beat — Menace → Misread → Escalation → Eruption — giving AI clear staging instructions that maximize narrative punch per panel.)

- **Diagonal tension composition (Panel ④)**: 要求 Panel ④ 構圖使用「[IP角色] 在左下、對方飛向右上」的對角線張力，這是經典動作漫畫構圖，瞬間傳達力量與衝突感。 (Diagonal composition from bottom-left to top-right is a classic action manga technique that visually conveys force and conflict — critical for making the final "real rage" panel land with maximum impact.)

- **Consistent character scale across all panels**: 指定 4 格中角色比例一致，防止 AI 在不同格中隨意縮放 [IP角色]，破壞連環漫畫的視覺連貫性。 (Consistent scale instruction prevents AI from resizing the character between panels — a common failure in multi-panel comic generation that destroys narrative coherence.)

- **Speech bubbles with tail pointing to speaker**: 明確指定對話泡泡的「尾巴指向說話者」，讓 AI 生成語義清晰的漫畫對話，避免讀者搞不清楚是誰在說話。 (Specifying speech bubble tails pointing to speakers forces AI to generate semantically clear dialogue attribution — essential for multi-character comic strips.)
