💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **85% negative space（85% 留白）**：不是「背景空白」，是主動用空曠製造孤獨感——角色越小，情緒越大 (Extreme negative space doesn't mean "empty", it means intentional solitude amplification — the smaller the character, the heavier the emotion)

- **Two emotional layers: solitude + longing（雙層情緒）**：孤獨是底色，渴望是亮點——少了任何一層都會變成普通可愛插畫 (Solitude as base, longing as highlight. Without both layers, it's just a cute character picture — the tension between the two is what creates emotional depth)

- **Mono no aware（物哀美學）**：日本文化裡「因美好事物的短暫而生的哀愁」——花瓣飄落天生帶有這個情感頻率 (Japanese concept: the bittersweet ache of beautiful, fleeting things. Cherry blossoms are the universal visual symbol — deeply resonant across cultures)

- **0.3x~1.5x 花瓣尺寸範圍**：不均勻的大小讓花瓣有「真實飄落」的層次感，避免圖案化的死板排列 (Varied petal scale creates natural, organic drift — uniform size looks like wallpaper pattern, not falling petals)

- **Character 12~15% frame area（角色佔比控制）**：比你直覺想的更小。這個比例讓角色看起來「被世界包圍」而非「站在場景裡」 (Smaller than your instinct says. This ratio makes the character feel "enveloped by the world" rather than just "placed in a scene")

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 把角色放大到畫面 30% 以上，「感覺主角比較重要」(Making the character larger than 30% because "the main character should feel important")

✅ 越小越孤獨，越孤獨越感動。12~15% 是這個公式的甜蜜點，超過就失去「渺小感」 (Smaller = lonelier = more emotional. 12~15% is the sweet spot — exceed it and you lose the "small in a vast world" feeling that drives the emotional punch)

❌ 加上樹枝、地平線、草地等背景元素，「讓場景更完整」 (Adding background elements like branches or ground to "complete the scene")

✅ 什麼都不加。純白的空曠感本身就是情感的一部分——背景元素只會讓觀眾的視線分散，稀釋那個「差一點」的心疼瞬間 (Add nothing. The white emptiness IS part of the emotion — any background element pulls the viewer's eye away and dilutes that "just barely missing" heartbreak moment)
