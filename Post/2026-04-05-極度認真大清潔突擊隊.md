💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **Over-engineered cleaning uniforms（過規格清潔制服）**: 每件制服配備警示背心、通訊耳機、工具腰帶，視覺資訊過載製造荒謬感，角色外觀即是笑點 (The visual overload of military-grade gear on cartoon characters creates instant absurdist comedy—"over-engineered" and "absurd professional" are high-engagement search terms in animation communities)

- **Sleeping dust speck with face（有睡臉的灰塵）**: 灰塵擁有閉月新月眼與微笑，賦予「被攻擊對象」可愛人格，與突擊隊的嚴肅形成最大反差，製造強烈反差笑點 (Giving the dust a sleeping face creates asymmetric comedy—elite seriousness vs. innocent cluelessness. The "hidden face easter egg" drives rewatch behavior and increases video completion rate)

- **Four-phase narrative structure（四階段敘事結構）**: Target Acquired → Squad Deploy → Total Overkill → The Loop，每個階段都有清晰的情感節奏，5 秒內完成完整喜劇弧線 (A complete comedic arc in 5 seconds: buildup → escalation → payoff → twist loop. - **The Loop ending（宿命循環結尾）**: 結尾揭露另一粒灰塵，四人轉向鏡頭，暗示這是永無止境的宿命，製造哲學性幽默與強烈的迴圈感 (The loop reveal creates a philosophical punchline—Sisyphean destiny coded as cleaning duty. This "endless loop" ending dramatically increases rewatch count, a key metric the algorithm rewards)
