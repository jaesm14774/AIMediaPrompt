💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **tiny silhouette / 渺小剪影**: 角色縮小到只佔一行文字的高度，製造強烈的尺度反差——人類在知識宇宙前的渺小感一眼穿透 (Scaling the character to fit within one line of text creates a visceral sense of scale: a key SEO concept "miniature epic" that resonates with viewers seeking "tiny character big world" visuals)

- **text dissolving into starlight / 文字液化成星光**: 這是整個 Prompt 的核心刺點——文字不是知識的記錄，文字本身就是宇宙的材料。這個視覺雙關讓畫面有哲學深度 (The visual metaphor of "words becoming constellations" is the conceptual core: it creates a second-layer meaning that text and cosmos are made of the same substance — highly shareable philosophical visual)

- **shadow stretches across the star-river / 影子橫跨星河**: 角色的影子連接現實與知識宇宙，這條線是「人與知識之間橋樑」最具體的視覺呈現，也是讓畫面有敘事張力的關鍵細節 (The shadow as a bridge between physical and cosmic realms adds narrative depth; "shadow stretches across galaxy" is a distinctive compositional hook)

- **Studio Ghibli × Craig Mullins**: Ghibli 提供溫暖奇幻的插畫質感，Craig Mullins 提供電影概念藝術的大氣深度——兩者結合確保畫面既溫暖又壯闊 (This style fusion targets two distinct audiences: Ghibli fans seeking whimsical illustration, and concept art enthusiasts seeking epic atmospheric depth)

- **warm ivory white background / 暖象牙白背景**: 極簡背景讓所有書本宇宙像是懸浮在另一個維度，不搶走粒子和光效的主角地位 (Plain ivory white background creates a "floating dimension" effect — the book-universes appear self-contained, amplifying their visual impact)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 不要把背景改成複雜的圖書館或夜空——背景越複雜，書本宇宙的「維度感」越弱，角色的渺小感也會消失 (Avoid complex library or night sky backgrounds — they compete with the book-universes and destroy the "floating dimension" effect that makes this concept work)

✅ 如果想換主角，選擇輪廓清晰的角色（圓潤剪影或細長人形均可）——因為主要視覺元素是「渺小剪影 vs 星河」，輪廓辨識度比細節更重要 (Choose characters with a clear silhouette outline — since the core visual is "tiny figure vs. cosmic river," silhouette readability matters more than fine detail at this scale)
