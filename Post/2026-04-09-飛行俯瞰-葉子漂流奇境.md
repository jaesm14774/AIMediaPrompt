🍃 **當你飛走的那一刻，身後的世界正在消失——葉子漂流奇境 | The World Behind You Is Already Half-Gone — Leaf Drift Fantasy**

你有沒有想過，真正的告別不是回頭看，而是眼角那一瞥——角落裡，某個地方正在沉入水中，而你只能繼續飛。
Have you ever thought that real farewells aren't dramatic glances back — they're the corner-of-the-eye glimpse of something already half-swallowed, as you keep flying forward?

這個 Prompt 不只是一張飛行俯瞰圖。它是一個藏著整個故事的畫面：主角飛向遠方一個若隱若現的光點，身後，那片他離開的世界，已經在慢慢消失。
This prompt isn't just a top-down flight scene. It's a frame hiding an entire story: the character flying toward a distant, barely-visible light — while the world they've left is quietly disappearing behind them.

👇 **Prompt Template / 指令模板**

> 三種飛行道具任選其一，每個都帶有獨特的視覺奇觀與故事語境；三組背景各自藏有「為什麼這一刻重要」的視覺敘事。

`Overhead top-down bird's eye view, camera directly above, looking straight down. **[IP角色]** is lying prone / belly-down on top of [飛行道具 with: 三種道具任選其一，皆帶獨特視覺奇觀 / "a giant translucent maple leaf — leaf veins filled with flowing starlight and tiny constellations, golden-amber glow pulsing like a heartbeat, edges slightly frayed as if it has carried its rider across a thousand skies" / "a torn ancient book page the size of a raft — faded text barely legible, gold-leaf illustrations of extinct creatures at its corners, the trailing edge still smoldering with cold blue fire as if torn from a dying spell" / "the last green leaf in a deep-winter world — impossibly large, vivid emerald against a grey frost landscape below, a single crack of light running down its spine like it is barely holding together"], the character's body filling most of the vessel surface, small hands gripping the edges tightly, clothing and hair streaming backward in fierce wind — vivid motion blur on fabric, hair ribbons trailing like ink brushstrokes.`

`The vast landscape below fills the entire frame: [背景世界 with: 每個背景都藏有一個「為什麼重要」的視覺敘事 / "an endless autumn forest canopy stretching to the horizon — but at the very bottom edge of the frame, barely visible, a cluster of rooftops slowly sinking beneath rising floodwaters, warm amber treetops forming a seamless carpet that swallows everything" / "a boundless fantasy ocean, deep teal water with scattered tiny islands — directly below, one small crescent-shaped island is half-submerged, its lighthouse still blinking faintly, the sea still rising" / "a sprawling misty valley between ancient mountain ridges — far ahead at the horizon, a single warm light glows through violet fog, too distant to identify, as if the whole journey exists only to reach it"], scale contrast emphasizing the character's tininess against the overwhelming, story-charged world.`

`The character looks forward with wide, determined eyes — peaceful yet carrying the weight of departure; behind them, in the far corner of the frame, something small is disappearing into distance or water, already half-gone. Speed rendered through: motion blur on the vessel's trailing edges, a faint luminous contrail dissolving behind, warm golden-hour or cold blue-dusk light cutting diagonally across the frame, soft lens flare. The atmosphere holds both wonder and farewell — "goodbye to everything below, forward toward that one distant light."`

`Studio Ghibli-inspired painterly illustration, soft volumetric lighting, warm-to-cool color gradient from character to horizon, whimsical yet emotionally resonant. 16:9 cinematic composition, ultra-detailed, 8K.`

💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **Overhead top-down bird's eye view**: 正上方垂直俯視構圖是這個 Template 的靈魂——世界鋪滿整個畫面，角色顯得極其渺小，scale contrast 是情感放大器的核心 (Pure top-down perspective eliminates horizon, forcing the entire world to become a carpet beneath the character — the key to making the "tininess vs. vastness" emotional contrast land)

- **Leaf veins filled with flowing starlight**: 葉脈本身流動著星光與星座，道具從「普通楓葉」升級為「飛過千個天空的神器」——這是讓道具從背景配件變成視覺主角的關鍵手法 (Upgrading the vehicle from "leaf" to "leaf with starlight flowing through its veins" gives the prop its own mythology — viewers ask "what IS this thing?" before they even notice the character)

- **Bottom edge of the frame — barely visible, sinking**: 消失中的世界被刻意放到畫面角落、幾乎看不見的位置——這不是粗心，而是構圖策略。觀者發現它的瞬間，情感衝擊比放在中央強十倍 (Placing the disappearing world at the very edge, "barely visible," triggers the discovery effect — the moment viewers find it, the emotional punch hits ten times harder than if it were centered)

- **Warm-to-cool color gradient from character to horizon**: 色溫從主角附近的暖色過渡到地平線的冷色，視覺上強化「告別暖光，飛向未知」的敘事 (Warm tones near the character = safety, memory, what was left behind; cool tones at the horizon = the unknown, the destination — color temperature does the emotional storytelling without a single word)

- **Edges slightly frayed / trailing edge smoldering / crack of light**: 三種道具選項都有一個「邊緣受損」的細節——磨損的楓葉邊、燃燒的古書頁邊緣、快撐不住的裂光。這些細節暗示：這趟旅程本身是脆弱的，不是英雄出征，是孤注一擲 (All three vessel options include a "barely holding together" detail — frayed edges, smoldering fire, a crack of light. This visual language says: the journey itself is fragile. Not a hero's triumph — a desperate leap)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 把「告別感」寫進 Prompt 的形容詞裡（例如加 "emotional", "bittersweet", "longing"）——這樣 AI 不會生成情感，只會生成帶有那些詞的說明文字感畫面 (Adding emotional adjectives like "bittersweet" or "longing" doesn't make the image emotional — it makes it generic. The AI reads these as mood tags, not visual instructions)

✅ 把情感藏進具體的視覺細節：「角落那個快沉進水裡的屋頂」比任何情感詞彙都更有力。讓畫面本身說話，讓觀者自己發現 (Encode emotion into specific visual facts: "a cluster of rooftops slowly sinking, barely visible at the bottom edge" creates the feeling without naming it. Discovery = emotional investment — this is the Ghibli method)

❌ 飛行道具只描述外觀（「大楓葉」、「古書頁」），沒有給它一段歷史或狀態 (Describing a vessel by its appearance alone — "big maple leaf," "old book page" — makes it a prop, not a story object)

✅ 給每個道具一句「它從哪裡來、現在是什麼狀態」的暗示：「edges frayed as if it has carried its rider across a thousand skies」讓楓葉立刻有了傳說感——觀者會問「這片葉子去過哪裡？」 (Give each vessel a hint of origin and current state: "edges frayed as if it has carried its rider across a thousand skies" turns a leaf into a legendary artifact. Viewers will wonder: where has this thing been?)

✨ **Try This Variation / 延伸挑戰**

👉 試試把「沉入水中的屋頂」換成「逐漸縮小的城市燈光」或「正在合攏的森林缺口」，告別的對象不同，情感質地就會完全不同。你的角色在告別什麼？
(Try replacing "sinking rooftops" with "a city's lights slowly shrinking to a single dot" or "a forest canopy closing over a clearing like a wound healing." What is your character saying goodbye to? The answer changes the entire emotional register of the image.)

🔥 **進階挑戰**：嘗試讓三個道具選項和三個背景選項「不對稱搭配」——例如「世界最後一片綠 × 遠方若隱若現的光點」，而不是「最美的道具配最美的背景」。不對稱往往產生更強的情感張力，因為那個組合感覺像是命運，而不是設計。
(Advanced: Try asymmetric pairings — the "last green leaf" with "a single warm light in the fog" rather than the "most visually spectacular" match. Asymmetric combinations feel like fate, not design — and fate hits harder)
