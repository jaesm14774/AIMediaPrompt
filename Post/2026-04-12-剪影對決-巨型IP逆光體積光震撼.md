A tiny black silhouette. An overwhelming wall of cyan light. No face, no detail — only the ancient light behind them defines that they exist at all. And that light is alive.

💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **Pure black silhouette（純黑剪影，零細節）**: 人物不保留任何細節——沒有臉，沒有服裝紋理——完全由背後的光「定義」其存在。這個視覺哲學讓每個觀看者都能投射自己到畫面中，共鳴感更強 (Stripping all detail from the foreground figures creates universal identification — any viewer becomes the silhouette facing the unknown. Zero detail is not laziness; it's the most powerful storytelling choice in this composition)

- **8% frame height（角色佔框8%）**: 嚴格規定人物高度只佔畫面的 8-10%，是製造「被壓倒」感的關鍵數字。不是「人很小」，而是「人小到讓宇宙感降臨」 (Constraining character height to 8-10% of frame is the technical trigger for awe. This is the exact ratio used in epic movie posters — small enough to feel dwarfed, large enough to remain the emotional anchor of the composition)

- **"Living glowing icon"（活的發光圖騰）**: 生物被瀑布/洞窟光源完全融化成輪廓，讓它從「一隻怪獸」升格為「一個神祇的顯現」。輪廓就是全部的資訊——觀看者的想像力補完了其餘的部分 (The creature dissolves into its light source, becoming a glowing icon rather than a rendered beast. The viewer's imagination fills in everything the darkness conceals — always making it more magnificent than any rendering could achieve)

- **No warm tones（零暖色）**: 整幅畫面不允許任何暖色——這個嚴格的限制讓冷藍調成為一種「非人世界」的視覺語言，同時讓地面碎石反射出的細微光芒顯得更加珍貴 (Banning all warm tones is a declaration: this space belongs to something older than human light. The cold monochrome palette physically distances the viewer from normalcy, making the encounter feel genuinely otherworldly)

- **God rays terminating at feet（體積光落點在人物腳邊）**: 體積光柱的終點精確落在人物腳部周圍，創造一個「被光選中」的神聖感——人物不只是站在光裡，而是光「到達」了他們所在的地方 (Specifying that god rays terminate at the characters' feet creates a sacred geometry — the light has traveled from the ancient creature specifically to find these small beings. This detail transforms the composition from illustration into narrative)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓背景生物的細節過於清晰，破壞神秘感——如果觀看者能看見皮膚紋理，就失去了「圖騰感」 (If the creature's surface texture is fully visible, you've lost the icon. Detail destroys the sacred quality — keep the creature dissolving into its own light)

✅ 用「積水倒影」選項將人物的剪影向下鏡像，創造「上下都是剪影，中間是光的世界」的三層構圖——觀看者感覺自己也被夾在其中 (Choose the shallow water reflection option to mirror the silhouettes downward — creating three zones: dark silhouettes above, luminous creature behind, and reflected silhouettes below. The viewer is suspended between worlds)

✅ **體積光的情感密碼**：在提示詞中加入 `"pulsing with slow steady rhythm like breathing"` 讓光有了生命節奏——靜態圖像中感受到生命體的呼吸，是這個模板最強的情感鉤子 (Add "pulsing with slow steady rhythm like breathing" to make a static image feel alive. This is the single detail that elevates the image from illustration to encounter)

✨ **Try This Variation / 延伸挑戰**

👉 試試把「瀑布洞窟」換成「星際蟲洞開口」或「古代廢墟的光柱」——剪影技法在任何發光背景都能運作，而選擇不同的光源故事，會讓整幅畫的情感完全改變。分享作品時標記 #SilhouetteVsGiant 讓更多人看見！
