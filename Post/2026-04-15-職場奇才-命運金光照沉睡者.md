💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **dramatic golden beam of light / 命運金光柱**: 從天花板直射的神聖光柱是視覺錨點，強調「宇宙選中你」的荒謬感，同時製造戲劇化的明暗對比 (The divine spotlight creates an instant "chosen one" narrative — a universally relatable trope that drives shareability and sparks the "this is literally me" reaction)

- **dream bubble in dark desaturated tones / 灰暗夢境泡泡**: 現實金光 vs 夢中陰暗的冷暖對比，是整張圖的情感張力核心——清醒世界給他榮耀，夢裡他卻在噩夢中逃離 (Warm real world vs cold dream interior creates instant visual storytelling — viewers decode the irony in under 2 seconds, maximizing dwell time on the post)

- **tiptoeing colleagues with medals / 踮腳頒獎的同事**: 兩個戰戰兢兢的同事動作製造群體反應的荒謬感，讓觀眾從「被選中者」角度感受那種莫名其妙的加冕時刻 (Supporting characters in mid-action freeze add narrative depth and humor — "why is everyone afraid to wake him?" triggers curiosity and comment engagement)

- **[角色/外觀] 替換變數 / character appearance variable**: 設計為 IP 通用模板，可套入任何 Chibi 風格角色，不限定特定 IP，極大提升模板重用性 (Flexible character slot means the template works for any IP or original character, reducing prompt-engineering overhead for repeat users)

- **exaggerated Chibi proportions + bokeh background / Chibi 誇張比例＋背景散景**: 角色在巨大椅子中顯得更嬌小，加深「小人物被大命運選中」的荒謬反差；散景讓主體突出，符合 Instagram 圖片演算法偏好清晰主體的評分邏輯 (Oversized environment + tiny character amplifies the "big destiny, small body" comedy. Clean bokeh background also signals high-quality composition to Instagram's visual ranking AI)
