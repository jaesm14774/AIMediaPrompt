This template turns "a pet quietly staying through your low moment" into a reusable healing visual.

💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **firefly-like light ring**: 以可視化節律強化安撫感，讓畫面有「被陪著呼吸」的感受 (Creates a visible calming rhythm and makes the scene feel emotionally regulated)
- **only present and protective**: 明確禁止過度動作，避免畫面從療癒跑偏成可愛搞笑 (Prevents playful noise and keeps the emotional tone stable)
- **intimate eye-level framing**: 近距離平視構圖讓觀眾自然代入被陪伴者視角 (Builds immediate empathy and retention)
- **honey gold + warm cream palette**: 暖色低飽和是療育向內容最穩定的視覺語言 (Supports comforting tone and algorithm-friendly visual coherence)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**
❌ 場景塞入太多裝飾與道具，會削弱「人與寵物」核心關係 (Too many props dilute the emotional center)
✅ 保持背景簡潔，讓手、毛髮、呼吸節奏成為主要敘事焦點 (Keep the frame minimal and relationship-first)

✨ **Try This Variation / 延伸挑戰**
👉 把場景換成沙發角落、車內停車場或醫院走廊，測試不同人生低谷情境的共鳴度。
(Try sofa corner, parked car interior, or hospital hallway for different emotional contexts.)
