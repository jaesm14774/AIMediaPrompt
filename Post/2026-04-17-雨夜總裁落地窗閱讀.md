🌃📖 **雨夜總裁落地窗閱讀 | Rainy-Night CEO Window Reading**

小體型 IP 角色坐在超大頂樓辦公室裡閱讀，透過尺寸反差與冷暖對比做出電影級「內心溫度 vs 外界冷冽」氛圍。  
A tiny IP character reads in a vast penthouse office, using scale contrast and warm-cool lighting for cinematic mood.

👇 **Prompt Template / 指令模板**

> 可替換角色、紙鎮小物與書架彩蛋；核心是「小角色 + 大空間 + 雨夜落地窗」。

```
Cinematic wide shot of a rainy-night penthouse office at the top of a skyscraper.
[IP角色] sits on an oversized black leather chair, visibly tiny against grand space.
Wardrobe: minimalist CEO look (black turtleneck, tailored dark pants, thin gold watch).
Action: reading a hardcover book with contemplative posture.
Window reflection can reveal the character's original form (identity contrast metaphor).
Set details: polished floor reflections, designer coffee table, symbolic paperweight, hidden shelf easter egg.
Lighting: warm reading lamp key light + cool city glow fill light.
Lens/look: anamorphic flares, shallow depth, rain bokeh, 2.39:1 cinematic frame, 8K.
```

💡 **Pro Tips / 使用建議**

- 角色在椅子上「偏小」是這張圖的記憶點，請勿放大主角比例  
- 冷暖光交界落在角色輪廓上，質感會大幅提升
