💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **sacred robes designed for someone ten times its size**: 袍子尺寸荒謬化是核心視覺刺點——越大的袍子 × 越小的身體 = 「智慧無從容納」的視覺悖論，讓反差直接被畫面體現，而非只靠文字說明。(Oversized robes create a visual paradox: the smaller the body, the more wisdom overflows its container. The "sea of golden silk pooling at tiny feet" is a renderable detail that makes the philosophical contrast concrete.)

- **the oldest eyes in the softest face**: 整個 Prompt 的記憶點錨句。眼神是靈魂，外觀是反差的彈弓。越嬰兒感的臉 × 越古老深邃的眼 = 情感衝擊越大。(The single sharpest hook. Ancient knowing meets childlike innocence in one face — this visual contradiction is what stops the scroll. The more contrasting the character's exterior, the harder this hits.)

- **fingers open — not grasping, only offering**: 手部姿態的用字精準決定情感方向。「not grasping」代表不強迫，「only offering」代表無條件。這句話讓觀者感受到「被接納」而非「被說教」。(Word choice matters: "not grasping" removes pressure, "only offering" signals unconditional support. This distinction turns a hand gesture into an emotional statement about how the wisest beings give without imposing.)

- **having been there the whole time**: 這是情感引爆點。不是「突然出現的支持」，而是「一直都在但你沒看見」。這個時間層次讓觀者反思自己身邊被忽略的支撐，觸發真實情緒共鳴。(The temporal layer is the emotional detonator. Support that was "already there" implies the viewer missed it — a quietly devastating realization that drives authentic emotional response and share behavior.)

- **[氛圍變體]：神殿 / 雲廊 / 星空**: 三個場景分別放大不同情緒維度——神殿（神聖莊嚴）、雲廊（輕柔如夢）、星空（永恆與無限）。選擇氛圍等於選擇你想讓觀者感受到的情緒質地。(Each atmosphere amplifies a different emotional register: sacred hall = solemn and warm, cloud corridor = soft and dreamy, starfield = timeless and infinite. Match the atmosphere to the emotional tone of your post.)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 角色外觀描述太中性（如「a character」），反差不夠，悖論爆炸不了。(Using neutral character descriptions dilutes the contrast paradox — the entire concept depends on maximum visual incongruity between the exterior and the ancient eyes.)

✅ 角色描述要強調：極度小、極度圓、極度嬰兒感——「barely larger than a teacup」、「head nearly as big as its whole body」是好的描述方向。外型越荒謬可愛，眼神的古老深邃就越有衝擊力。(Push the exterior description to absurdity: "barely larger than a teacup", "head nearly as big as its whole body", "cartoonishly soft and plush-like". The more innocent the exterior, the more shocking the ancient wisdom in the eyes.)

❌ 把「手部動作」和「眼神」分開描述，它們是同一個情感系統的兩端——眼神說「我懂你」，手部說「我不強迫你」。(Treating the hand gesture and eye gaze as separate elements weakens both. They're two ends of the same emotional statement: the eyes say "I understand", the hand says "I'm here but I won't impose".)

✅ 確保手部描述保留「barely reaching」和「fingers open」——正是這個「快到但沒碰到」的懸置感，讓觀者在情感上完成那個觸碰。(Keep "barely reaching" and "fingers open" — the suspension of almost-but-not-quite touching is where the viewer's emotion fills in the gap. Remove this precision and you lose the emotional completion.)

✨ **Try This Variation / 延伸挑戰**

👉 試試改變角色外觀描述：「a tiny ghost-shaped plush」、「a soft round rabbit no bigger than a fist」、「a tiny dragon hatchling」——不同外型的可愛方式不同，古老眼神打出的反差也不同。找到讓你最想說「牠怎麼會懂」的那個外型。(Try different exterior descriptions: "a tiny ghost-shaped plush", "a soft round rabbit no bigger than a fist", "a tiny dragon hatchling". Each exterior creates a different flavor of the paradox — find the one that makes you think "how could *that* understand?".)

🔥 **進階挑戰**: 把氛圍從「神殿」改成某個世俗場景（如「a busy train station platform at 11pm, soft overhead fluorescent light, blurred commuters walking past」）——同樣的聖袍小導師，出現在最日常的疲憊時刻，落差感會讓療癒力度爆表。(Advanced: Change the atmosphere to an everyday exhausted moment — "a quiet late-night convenience store, soft warm fluorescent light, empty aisles". The same sacred-robed mentor appearing in the most mundane modern loneliness creates a different kind of holy encounter.)
