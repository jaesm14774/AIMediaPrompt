**模板重點 / Template Focus**

- 先決定「誰進入誰的宇宙」，不要只做換裝。
- Decide who is invading whose universe. Do not stop at costume-swapping.

- 再決定「這個組合最像哪一幕」，讓畫面有劇情瞬間。
- Then decide which exact iconic moment this mashup should resemble so the frame has drama.

- 最後把角色辨識元素鎖死，例如 Kirby 圓身、Hello Kitty 臉與蝴蝶結、皮卡丘耳朵與電擊。
- Finally, lock the signature traits: Kirby's round body, Hello Kitty's face and bow, Pikachu's ears and electricity.
