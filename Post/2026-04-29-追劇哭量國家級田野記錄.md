💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **TV-blue glow as dominant light source（電視藍光作為主光源）**: 冷色電視光打亮角色臉部，製造戲劇性的冷暖對比陰影，讓「追劇現場感」瞬間成立 (The cold blue TV light vs. warm character palette creates cinematic split-toning—this single detail elevates the scene from "cute illustration" to "you-were-there immersion")

- **tissue pile occupies 30% of frame（衛生紙山佔畫面 30%）**: 精確比例指令確保衛生紙量足夠誇張、形成視覺笑點，而不只是「旁邊有幾張紙」 (Specifying 30% frame coverage forces AI to render a truly absurd tissue mountain—proportional weight is the difference between "charming detail" and "punchline that lands")

- **感動量測站三件套（淚量計 + 衛生紙圖 + 劇情崩壞指數）**: 三個量測指標各自獨立成一個笑點，合起來形成「荒謬但嚴肅」的科學儀器牆——這是整個 Template 的靈魂道具 (Three gauges = three separate punchlines that compound. The "scientific seriousness" applied to emotional chaos is the core comedic tension of this template)

- **screen shows [感人劇情片段]（螢幕劇情填空）**: 螢幕上的字幕選項提供情緒雙層——觀眾在笑的同時被字幕戳到，觸發「這劇我也哭過」的個人記憶 (The subtitle text on screen creates a second emotional layer: viewers laugh at the absurdity AND feel the drama's pull simultaneously—dual-emotion triggers are highly shareable)

- **split-toned shadow / warm-cool palette（冷暖色調分割）**: 固定調色盤（cream / TV blue / soft yellow）讓生成結果色彩穩定一致，避免 AI 隨機選色破壞氛圍 (Locking the 3-color palette to cream/blue/yellow ensures consistent, harmonious output across all character variations—critical for series coherence)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 把螢幕劇情描述得太模糊（如「感人的電視劇」），AI 會生成隨機畫面，失去「字幕 × 觀眾反應」的情緒連結 (Vague screen descriptions like "an emotional drama" give AI no anchor — the subtitle text is what makes the scene resonate; be specific)

✅ 選擇有「一句話殺傷力」的字幕，例如「你要好好的」「我等了你十年」「下輩子再見」——字幕越簡短有力，觀眾代入感越強 (Choose subtitle lines with maximum impact in minimum words: "You have to be okay" / "I waited ten years" / "See you in the next life"—these trigger instant personal memory recall)
