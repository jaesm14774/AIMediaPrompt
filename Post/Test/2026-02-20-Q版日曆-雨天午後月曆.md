💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **3:4 aspect ratio / tear-off wall calendar**: 直式 3:4 比例模擬傳統撕頁日曆的紙張形狀，加上 "perforated tear line" 撕頁虛線讓 AI 理解這是哪種日曆 (Portrait 3:4 ratio mimics traditional tear-off calendar paper shape. Adding "perforated tear line" tells AI exactly which calendar type this is)

- **warm amber vs cool blue-grey**: 整張圖的核心是「冷暖對比」——室內暖色調（琥珀、蜂蜜色）vs 室外冷色調（藍灰雨天）。這個對比讓觀眾本能地感受到「室內很溫暖」 (The core of this image is warm/cool contrast—indoor amber vs outdoor blue-grey rain. This contrast instinctively makes viewers feel "indoors is warm and safe")

- **slightly textured off-white paper surface**: 讓日曆看起來像真實的紙張而非平面設計，AI 會加入微妙的紙張纖維和厚度感，增加「實物感」 (Textured off-white paper makes the calendar look like real paper, not flat graphic design—AI adds subtle fiber textures and thickness)

- **gentle bokeh rain effects**: 景深模糊的雨滴效果讓雨天背景柔和不搶眼，同時保持日曆前景的清晰度——前後景的焦距差就是重點 (Bokeh rain blurs the rainy background softly without stealing attention, maintaining sharp calendar foreground—the depth-of-field difference is key)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 不要讓雨天元素壓過日曆——如果背景的雨太密、窗戶太搶眼，觀眾的視線會被拉到背景而非日期格線 (Don't let rain elements overpower the calendar—if rain is too heavy or the window too prominent, viewer attention shifts to the background instead of dates)

✅ 雨天元素用「暗示」而非「直接展示」：窗戶上的水珠、桌面的微小倒影、夾在日曆頁間的壓花——這些含蓄的細節比大雨傾盆更有氛圍感 (Use rain as "suggestion" not "direct display": water droplets on glass, tiny desk reflections, a pressed flower bookmark—these subtle details create more atmosphere than heavy rain)

💡 **情緒設計**: 角色的「半睡半醒」表情是關鍵！不是開心大笑，不是認真工作，而是那種「好舒服好想繼續躺著」的慵懶滿足感。這就是雨天的情緒 (Character's "half-asleep" expression is key! Not happy-laughing, not serious-working, but that lazy-content "so comfy I don't want to move" feeling. That IS the rainy day mood)
