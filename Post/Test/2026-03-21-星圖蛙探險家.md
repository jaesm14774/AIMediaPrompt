- **connecting star lines extend upward off the paper edge, visually merging with actual glowing stars**: 「地圖延伸到紙外成真實星空」是這個 prompt 的核心視覺機制——沒有這句話，地圖就只是地圖 (This is the conceptual payload of the entire image — the physical boundary between map and sky dissolves. - **helmet slightly askew**: 頭盔歪斜是精準的可愛細節——探險家嚴肅但有點亂的形象 (A slightly tilted helmet communicates "competent but adorably messy" — it humanizes an otherwise serious explorer)
