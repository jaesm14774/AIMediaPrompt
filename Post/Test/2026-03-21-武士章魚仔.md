💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **each arm holds a different miniature weapon**: 「每隻手持不同物品」這個指令強制 AI 生成多樣化道具，而不是複製同一武器八次 (This instruction forces variety — without it AI often repeats the same item across all arms)
- **intensely focused warrior frown that is overwhelmingly cute**: 衝突表情是這個 prompt 的笑點核心——嚴肅的意圖 × 不可能嚴肅的外表 (The contradiction between the intent (fierce warrior) and the result (adorably round octopus) is the entire joke)
- **ink cloud bursting behind as dramatic effect**: 水墨爆炸雲是章魚防禦機制的轉化——既是章魚本能又是武士戰鬥特效，語義雙關 (Ink cloud = both octopus defense and warrior battle effect — semantic pun that adds depth)
- **kabuto sits slightly too large**: 頭盔尺寸略大向下滑是精準的可愛細節，防止 AI 生成完美合身的頭盔 (The too-big helmet slipping down is a precise cuteness directive — perfection kills the comedy)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 武器清單包含抽象概念（如 "courage" 或 "honor"）→ AI 無法視覺化，會生成隨機道具 (Abstract items in the weapon list confuse AI and produce random objects — keep everything concrete and physical)

✅ 武器清單裡混入一個完全不符合武士形象的道具（飯糰、茶杯、書）——荒謬反差就在那一個物品上，是整圖的喜劇點睛之筆 (Sneak one completely non-warrior item into the weapons list — that ONE incongruous item (rice ball, tea cup) becomes the entire comedic punchline)
