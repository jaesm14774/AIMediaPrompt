- **60fps to capture subtle expressions**: 每秒 60 幀能捕捉父母眼神中的溫柔、寶寶臉上的堅定，這些微表情是情感傳達的關鍵 (High frame rate captures the gentle gaze of parents and baby's determined expression—these micro-expressions are crucial for emotional storytelling and viral potential on TikTok/Reels)

- **Gentle focus pulls between parent and baby**: 焦點在父母和寶寶之間溫柔切換，引導觀眾視線，強化「教學 → 失敗 → 鼓勵」的敘事節奏 (Rack focus guides viewer's eye between teaching → adorable failure → gentle encouragement, creating narrative flow that boosts watch time—key for Instagram algorithm)

- **Golden hour glow + soft earth tones**: 金色陽光 + 柔和大地色調營造「溫暖安全」的氛圍，符合「療癒」主題的視覺心理學 (Warm golden lighting + earth tones trigger psychological "warmth and safety" response—scientifically proven to increase emotional engagement and sharing behavior)

- **The magic is in the patience**: 核心概念！父母「從不責罵，只有溫柔引導」+ 寶寶「從不放棄，繼續嘗試」= 純粹的愛與正向價值觀 (Core concept: parent's endless patience + baby's innocent persistence = pure love and positive values.
