👇 **Prompt Template / 指令模板**

```
A kawaii chibi-style illustration of **[角色名：Kirby / Pikachu / 小恐龍 / 自訂角色]** squeezed inside the tiny screen of a [遊戲裝置 with: 復古掌機 / "pastel pink Game Boy with round buttons and a D-pad" / "retro CRT television set with antenna and wood panel frame" / "classic arcade cabinet with colorful side art and joystick"], the character's body is comically too large for the small screen — soft round body pressed flat against the screen glass, cheeks squished against the edges, tiny limbs poking out from the bezels, one arm reaching outside the frame waving at the viewer.

**Composition:** The retro device is centered and takes up 80% of the frame. The character fills the entire screen area and overflows beyond it — body bulging against the screen borders, creating visible pressure and squeeze deformation. Small decorative details on the device: [裝飾細節 with: 遊戲機元素 / "a Poké Ball charm dangling from the corner, star stickers on the shell" / "channel knob and volume dial with tiny heart stickers" / "coin slot glowing softly, player 1 badge"]. **Art Style:** Soft pastel color palette (baby pink, mint green, lavender, cream yellow), thick clean outlines (3-4px), rounded shapes with no sharp edges, chibi proportions (head-to-body ratio 1:1), glossy digital illustration with subtle cel-shading, mobile game UI aesthetic. The device frame is rendered with slightly more detail and texture than the character to create depth contrast.

**Background:** Simple single-color pastel background (matching the device's accent color) with scattered small floating icons — tiny hearts, stars, musical notes, and pixel sparkles. No complex scenery — all attention on the character-device interaction.

**Mood:** Playful, squeezable, nostalgic, irresistibly cute, the visual equivalent of wanting to reach into the screen and poke the squishy character.
```

💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **chibi proportions (head-to-body ratio 1:1)**: 頭身比 1:1 的 Q 版比例，讓角色看起來圓潤可愛到極致，是日式卡哇伊插畫的黃金比例 (Chibi 1:1 head-to-body ratio maximizes cuteness—this is the golden standard for kawaii illustration, trending in 2026 character design)

- **squeeze deformation**: 擠壓變形是這個 Prompt 的靈魂！角色的身體因為空間不夠而被壓扁，創造出物理性的「可愛感」和喜感 (Squeeze deformation is the soul of this prompt—the body physically compressed by lack of space creates tactile cuteness and comedy. Keywords: "squishy character art", "squeeze illustration")

- **thick clean outlines (3-4px)**: 粗線條讓角色在任何尺寸下都清晰可辨，同時增強卡通感和手繪質感 (Thick outlines ensure characters remain crisp at any size while enhancing cartoon feel—critical for Instagram feed visibility and shareability)

- **pastel color palette**: 柔和粉彩色系（粉紅、薄荷綠、薰衣草紫、奶油黃）營造溫暖療癒的視覺氛圍，是 2026 年最受歡迎的卡哇伊配色趨勢 (Soft pastels create warm, healing visual atmosphere—the most popular kawaii color trend in 2026, boosting engagement on visual platforms)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 只寫「角色在螢幕裡」會讓 AI 生成一個正常大小的角色站在螢幕畫面中，完全沒有擠壓感 (Just writing "character inside screen" makes AI generate a normally-sized character standing in a screen image—zero squeeze effect)

✅ 關鍵是描述「身體太大 + 物理變形」：用 "too large for the screen"、"pressed flat against the glass"、"bulging against borders" 這些詞彙，讓 AI 理解角色和空間的衝突關係！ (The key is describing "body too large + physical deformation": use "too large for the screen", "pressed flat against the glass", "bulging against borders" to make AI understand the character-space conflict!)
