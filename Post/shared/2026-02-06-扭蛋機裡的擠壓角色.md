💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **C4D clay render**: Cinema 4D 黏土渲染風格，觸發 AI 生成光滑、圓潤、有質感的 3D 效果，讓角色看起來像真正的黏土公仔 (Cinema 4D clay render triggers AI to generate smooth, rounded, textured 3D effects—making characters look like real clay figurines. "Clay render art" is a top trending keyword in 3D illustration)

- **fish-eye distortion effect**: 魚眼變形效果是關鍵！角色臉貼著弧形玻璃時產生的透鏡變形，讓鼻子和臉頰看起來更圓更大，增添物理真實感和喜感 (Fish-eye distortion is crucial—the lens warping when a face presses against curved glass makes nose and cheeks appear rounder and bigger, adding physical realism and comedy)

- **subsurface scattering**: 次表面散射效果讓角色的皮膚/材質看起來有半透明感，光線穿透表面產生溫暖的內部發光，讓黏土質感更真實 (Subsurface scattering makes character skin/material appear semi-translucent with warm internal glow—essential for realistic clay material rendering)

- **tilt-shift depth of field**: 移軸景深模糊讓場景看起來像微縮模型攝影，上下邊緣的虛化效果強化「玩具感」和「收藏品感」 (Tilt-shift depth of field makes the scene look like miniature model photography—edge blur enhances "toy feel" and "collectible" aesthetic)
