💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **holographic foil shimmer (全息箔片光澤)**: 觸發 AI 生成卡牌標誌性的彩虹反光效果，這是收藏卡視覺識別的核心元素。沒有這個詞，AI 往往生成平面紙質感 (Triggers the iconic rainbow-reflective effect that defines collectible cards. Without this keyword, AI tends to generate flat paper textures—"holographic card effect" is trending in TCG and AI art communities)

- **macro lens perspective (微距鏡頭視角)**: 讓鏡頭極度貼近卡面，可以看到紙張纖維、印刷點和箔片細節，建立卡牌的「真實感」基礎 (Extreme close-up on card surface reveals paper fiber, print dots, and foil details, establishing the "reality" foundation that makes the magical transformation more impactful—"macro photography" drives high engagement on Instagram)

- **2D to 3D transition (2D 到 3D 的轉換)**: 整個影片的核心視覺魔術——角色從平面插畫掙脫到立體空間，然後又回到平面。這種次元轉換創造了極強的視覺衝擊 (The core visual magic—character breaking from flat illustration to 3D space and back. This dimensional shift creates powerful visual impact—"dimensional break" and "art comes alive" are viral-worthy concepts)

- **knowing smirk ending (結尾的會心微笑)**: 結尾角色表情的細微變化是整部影片的「爆點」——暗示卡牌其實一直都是活的，給觀眾留下想像空間 (The subtle expression change at the end is the video's punchline—implying the card was always alive. This narrative hook drives rewatches and comments, boosting algorithm performance)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 直接寫「卡牌上的角色動起來」太模糊，AI 會生成角色從卡牌旁邊出現，而不是從卡面「內部」覺醒 (Writing "the character on the card moves" is too vague—AI generates the character appearing NEXT TO the card instead of awakening FROM WITHIN the artwork)

✅ 關鍵是分階段描述：先強調「within the card border」（在卡面邊框內），再描述「presses against the card surface from inside」（從內部推擠卡面），最後才是突破。這個由內而外的層次感讓 AI 理解次元概念 (Key is staged description: first emphasize "within the card border", then "presses against the card surface from inside", finally the breakthrough. This inside-out progression helps AI understand the dimensional concept—staged prompts consistently produce better video results!)
