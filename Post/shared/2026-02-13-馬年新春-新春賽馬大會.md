💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **golden clouds racetrack (金色雲朵賽道)**: 雲朵賽道結合紅燈籠標竿、金幣與紅色紙屑，創造「天上賽馬」的奇幻年味氛圍。金色雲是中國新年常見的吉祥意象 (Golden cloud track with red lantern pylons and scattered coins creates a "celestial horse race" fantasy—gold clouds are auspicious CNY imagery)

- **Comic relief in last place (搞笑墊底位)**: 第三名設計成「反向騎驢」「掉下馬自己跑」「三隻小雞疊穿外套假裝騎士」等荒謬情境，與領先者的認真形成強烈喜劇對比 (Last place as comic relief—backwards donkey rider, hamster running without horse, three chicks in trenchcoat—creates maximum contrast with fierce leaders)

- **dramatic wide-angle low perspective (低角度廣角)**: 從賽道低處仰拍，讓馬蹄、金幣噴濺、速度線充滿畫面，強化競速動感 (Low-angle wide shot fills frame with hooves, coin spray, speed lines—maximizes racing dynamism)

- **8K resolution, ultra detailed (8K 超細緻)**: 多角色、多裝飾的複雜場景需要高解析度才能呈現每個萌系角色的細節與表情 (Complex multi-character scene needs 8K to render each chibi's details and expressions)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 所有角色都設計成認真競速——沒有「墊底搞笑擔當」會讓畫面缺乏層次，觀眾找不到記憶點 (All characters racing seriously—without a "comic relief" last place, the scene lacks hierarchy and memorable moments)
