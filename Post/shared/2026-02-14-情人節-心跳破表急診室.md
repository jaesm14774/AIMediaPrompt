💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **heart shape ECG pattern (愛心形心電圖波形)**: 整張圖的視覺笑點核心！正常心電圖被愛心取代，這個「醫療儀器失控」的概念一眼就懂。提示 AI 用 "perfect heart shape pattern repeatedly" 確保波形清晰可辨識 (The visual punchline! Normal ECG replaced by hearts—this "medical instrument malfunction" concept is instantly readable. Use "perfect heart shape pattern repeatedly" to ensure AI renders clearly recognizable waveforms)

- **999 BPM in flashing red (999 BPM 紅色閃爍)**: 荒謬的數字讓醫療場景變成喜劇——正常心跳 60-100 BPM，999 是不可能的數字，但在「心動」的語境下完全合理。"Flashing red with warning symbols" 強化了緊急感和荒謬感的對比 (An absurd number turns medical into comedy—normal heartbeat is 60-100 BPM, 999 is impossible, but perfectly logical in the context of "catching feelings". "Flashing red with warning symbols" amplifies the urgent-yet-absurd contrast)

- **pastel candy colors on medical equipment (糖果色醫療設備)**: 粉紅聽診器、薰衣草色剪貼板、薄荷綠護士服——把嚴肅的醫療器材「甜蜜化」，創造「認真場景 × 可愛風格」的核心反差 (Pink stethoscopes, lavender clipboards, mint green scrubs—"sweetifying" serious medical equipment creates the core "serious setting × cute style" contrast that drives the humor)

- **manga-style effect lines (漫畫式效果線)**: 驚嘆號、汗滴、速度線等漫畫效果讓靜態圖片有「動態感」和「緊張感」，讓觀眾感受到護士們的慌張和忙亂 (Exclamation marks, sweat drops, speed lines add implied motion and urgency to a static image—viewers can FEEL the nurses' panic and chaos)
