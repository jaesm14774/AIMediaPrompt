💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **尺寸反差（tiny vs big）**：小小角色騎在大大恐龍背上，短腿晃啊晃的。這種「小人騎大獸」的視覺反差是石器時代騎乘系統最經典的畫面，也是尺度萌的精髓 (Tiny character on big dinosaur, stubby legs dangling — this "small rider on big beast" contrast is StoneAge's most iconic visual, and the essence of scale-based cuteness)

- **旅途小細節（storytelling details）**：便當快掉了、地圖拿反了、腳印開花了——這些小細節把靜態圖變成「有故事的一瞬間」，觀眾會自動腦補前因後果 (Packed lunch about to fall, map held upside-down, flowers blooming in footprints — these details turn a static image into "a story moment", viewers automatically imagine before and after)

- **村莊路牌（village signpost）**：岔路口的原始木牌寫著村莊名——直接喚起石器時代玩家「騎寵跑地圖找村莊」的核心回憶 (Wooden signpost at a fork with village names — directly triggers StoneAge players' core memory of riding pets across the map to find villages)

- **水彩繪本風格（watercolor storybook）**：不用寫實風格，而是選擇溫暖的水彩繪本質感，因為這個畫面要喚起的是「童年感」而非「震撼感」 (Watercolor storybook style instead of realistic — this image should evoke "childhood feeling" not "awe")

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓恐龍跑得很快或場景很危險——這不是動作冒險，而是「悠閒騎寵散步」的日常感。石器時代的騎寵跑圖最美的就是那種無所事事的治癒感 (Don't make the dinosaur running fast or the scene dangerous — this isn't action adventure, it's "leisurely pet riding". The most beautiful part of StoneAge pet riding was that carefree healing feeling)
