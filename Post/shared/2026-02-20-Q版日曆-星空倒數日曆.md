💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **constellation-style connected-dot font**: 用星座連線風格呈現月份名稱，讓文字本身就像星座圖的一部分，與星空主題視覺統一 (Constellation-dot-connected font makes the month name look like part of a star map—visual unity with the starry theme)

- **softly glowing warm-white text**: 日期用「微光暖白」而非純白，讓數字看起來像在發光的星星，而不是硬貼上去的文字 (Softly glowing warm-white makes dates look like luminous stars instead of flat text pasted on a dark background)

- **do NOT interfere with date readability**: 明確告訴 AI 背景星雲和裝飾不能干擾日期的可讀性。沒有這句，AI 會讓星雲蓋住數字 (Explicitly telling AI decorative elements must NOT cover dates. Without this, AI tends to let nebula wisps obscure the numbers)

- **weekend dates glow in warmer gold tone**: 用色溫區分平日和週末，金色週末在藍色星空背景上特別醒目，實用又美觀 (Gold-toned weekends on blue starfield creates natural visual hierarchy—practical and beautiful)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 深色背景上的文字很容易被 AI 弄得太暗或太亮。不要只寫「白色數字在深藍背景」，AI 會給你死白或灰暗的文字 (Text on dark backgrounds is tricky for AI—just saying "white text on navy" gives either harsh white or dim grey)

✅ 用 "softly glowing" + "star-shaped outline" 雙重指令：讓數字自帶光暈效果，既融入星空主題又保持可讀性。這比單純的白色文字有質感太多了 (Use "softly glowing" + "star-shaped outline" double instruction: dates get a glow halo that fits the starry theme while staying readable—way more textured than plain white)

💡 **深色背景黃金法則**: 深色日曆桌布要好看，關鍵是「冷背景 + 暖前景」——深藍星空配暖白發光文字，視覺上舒服又有層次 (Dark calendar wallpaper golden rule: cool background + warm foreground—navy sky with warm-white glowing text creates comfortable contrast with depth)
