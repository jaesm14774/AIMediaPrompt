💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **landscape orientation, 16:9 wide aspect ratio**: 明確指定橫式 16:9 比例，讓 AI 生成標準桌面桌布尺寸，而非隨機正方形。沒有這個指令，AI 很容易生成 1:1 的圖 (Specifying landscape 16:9 forces standard desktop wallpaper dimensions instead of random square output)

- **correctly arranged for the specified month**: 指定「正確排列該月日期」讓 AI 嘗試還原真實日曆的日期排列，而非隨意填數字 (Telling AI to "correctly arrange" dates helps it attempt a real calendar layout rather than random numbers)

- **70-75% of the frame / 20-25% of the frame**: 用明確的百分比控制日曆主體和角色裝飾的構圖比例，確保日曆不會被角色搶走焦點 (Explicit percentage ratios keep the calendar dominant and prevent the character from stealing focus)

- **watercolor-meets-digital**: 混合風格指令讓日曆數字保持清晰銳利（數位感），而花瓣裝飾有手繪水彩的柔和質感，兩者在同一張圖中共存 (This hybrid style keeps date numbers crisp/digital while petals get a soft watercolor texture—both coexisting in one image)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 不要只寫「一張有花的日曆」——AI 會生成花佔滿畫面、日期小到看不見的裝飾插畫 (Don't just say "a calendar with flowers"—AI will generate a flower illustration where dates are tiny and unreadable)

✅ 必須強調日曆是「主體」：用 "occupies 70-75%"、"clearly legible"、"structured grid" 這類字眼讓 AI 知道格線和日期數字是第一優先 (Emphasize the calendar IS the subject: "occupies 70-75%", "clearly legible", "structured grid" tells AI that the grid and date numbers are priority #1)

💡 **延伸玩法**: 可以每個月換不同的花種和 IP 角色！三月櫻花配 Kirby、四月紫藤配 Cinnamoroll、五月向日葵配 Pikachu——做成整年 12 張的系列桌布 (Mix different flowers and characters monthly! March sakura + Kirby, April wisteria + Cinnamoroll, May sunflower + Pikachu—create a 12-month wallpaper series!)
