💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **semi-translucent smoke with soft golden inner glow（半透明煙霧 + 內發金光）**: 這是讓 Q 版神明「既是煙又是角色」的關鍵——不是不透明的公仔放在煙霧前面，而是身體本身由煙霧構成，內部有可見的漩渦紋理和金色光暈。AI 會生成「活的雲朵捏成的可愛角色」而非「貼上去的貼紙」 (This is what makes the chibi gods "both smoke AND character" — not opaque figurines in front of smoke, but bodies MADE of smoke with visible swirling texture and golden glow inside. AI generates "living clouds shaped into adorable forms" instead of "stickers pasted on fog")

- **Ghibli-meets-Nintendo aesthetic**: 這個風格指令讓 AI 找到「可愛但不幼稚」的精確甜蜜點——吉卜力的溫暖手繪質感 + 任天堂的圓潤 Q 版比例，既有藝術性又有辨識度 (This style instruction finds the exact sweet spot of "cute but not childish" — Ghibli's warm hand-drawn texture + Nintendo's rounded chibi proportions, both artistic and instantly recognizable)

- **each deity has specific comedic action（每位神明有獨特的搞笑動作）**: 不是「一群可愛角色站在煙裡」——而是每位神明都在做一個具體的、符合其人設的搞笑行為。媽祖衝浪（海神 → 浪 → 衝浪）、關公用刀自拍（名武器 → 自拍棒）、土地公打瞌睡（慈祥老人 → 午覺）——每個笑點都建立在角色原設定上 (Not "a group of cute characters standing in smoke" — each deity does a specific comedic action rooted in their actual mythology. Mazu surfs (sea goddess → waves → surfing), Guan Yu selfies with his blade (legendary weapon → selfie stick), Tu Di Gong naps (kind old man → napping). Every joke is built on real character lore)

- **the human occupies only 20% of the frame（人類只佔 20%）**: 刻意壓縮信徒的畫面比例，讓神明佔據 80% 的空間——這個反轉（通常寺廟照片以人為主）強化了「這是神明的主場」的訊息，也讓視覺焦點完全在煙霧中的 Q 版世界 (Deliberately compressing human proportion to 20% — this reversal (temple photos usually focus on humans) reinforces "this is the gods' show", keeping visual focus entirely on the chibi smoke-world above)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓所有神明做一樣的動作（都在揮手、都在飛、都在笑）——重複動作會讓畫面變成「表情符號牆」，喪失敘事感。每個角色必須有獨立的「微型故事」在進行 (Don't give all deities the same action — repeated gestures turn the scene into an "emoji wall" with no narrative. Each character must have their own "micro-story" happening simultaneously)

✅ 讓背景神明做「跟主角完全無關的事」——灶君在烤棉花糖、門神在當保鏢、玉皇大帝一臉厭世地看著下面。這種「每個人都在忙自己的事」的群像感，才是讓觀眾反覆看、每次發現新細節的關鍵 (Make background deities do "completely unrelated things" — Kitchen God roasting marshmallows, Door Gods being bouncers, Jade Emperor looking exasperated. This "everyone doing their own thing" ensemble feeling is what makes viewers look again and again, discovering new details each time)
