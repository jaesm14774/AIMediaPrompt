💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **each character's lantern reflects their identity（每個角色的燈籠都反映自身特色）**: Kirby 的燈籠是星之卡比造型但大到扛不動、Pikachu 自己就是燈籠（電力照亮一切）、Totoro 拿橡果燈籠。不是「大家都拿一樣的紅燈籠」，而是燈籠本身成為角色人設的延伸 (Each character's lantern is an extension of their identity — Kirby's is star-shaped but too big to carry, Pikachu IS the lantern (electricity lights everything), Totoro carries an acorn lantern. Not "everyone holds the same red lantern" but the lantern itself extends the character's personality)

- **the riddle '一口吃掉整個宇宙' as meta-humor（燈謎彩蛋：一口吃掉整個宇宙）**: 這個燈謎的答案暗指 Kirby 的吸入能力——是一個觀眾需要「理解 IP 特性」才能 get 到的 meta 笑點。這種跨次元的自我指涉幽默讓作品從「好看」升級到「有趣」(The riddle's answer hints at Kirby's inhale ability — a meta-joke that requires "understanding the IP" to get. This cross-dimensional self-referential humor elevates the work from "pretty" to "clever")

- **canal reflection as a parallel world（水面倒影是平行世界）**: 倒影中的角色做著不同的動作、燈籠是不同顏色、還有一隻只存在於倒影中的月兔——這個設定讓觀眾在同一張圖中「發現雙重世界」，大幅提升觀看時間和分享動機 (Characters in the reflection do different poses, lanterns are different colors, and there's a moon rabbit visible ONLY in the reflection — this setup lets viewers "discover a dual world" in a single image, massively increasing viewing time and share motivation)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 把燈籠光做成冷白色 LED 感——元宵燈會的靈魂是「溫暖的手工感」。2800K 暖黃光 + 紙質半透明燈籠 = 每盞燈都像一顆小太陽。冷光會讓古鎮看起來像商場聖誕節 (Don't make lantern light look like cold-white LEDs — the soul of Lantern Festival is "warm handcraft feel". 2800K warm-yellow + translucent paper lanterns = each lantern like a tiny sun. Cold light makes the ancient town look like a shopping mall Christmas display)

✅ 讓 bokeh 成為你最好的朋友——數百盞燈籠在 f/1.8 淺景深下會變成夢幻的圓形光點陣列，這是元宵夜景最強的視覺武器。前景角色清晰 + 背景全是溫暖的圓形 bokeh = 攝影級氛圍感 (Make bokeh your best friend — hundreds of lanterns at f/1.8 shallow depth become a dreamy array of circular light points, the strongest visual weapon for Lantern Festival nightscapes. Sharp foreground characters + warm circular bokeh background = cinema-grade atmosphere)
