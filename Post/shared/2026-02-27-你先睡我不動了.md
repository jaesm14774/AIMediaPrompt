💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **Cheek squished flat（臉頰壓扁）**: 不是完美可愛地躺著，是真的被壓變形。圓臉壓成扁的、嘴巴被擠開——這種「不完美的睡相」比任何精心設計的姿勢都真實、都溫暖 (Not lying there perfectly cute — the face is genuinely deformed by pressure. Round face flattened, mouth pushed open. This "imperfect sleeping face" is more real and warm than any designed pose)

- **One hand gripping a fistful of shirt hem（手抓著衣角）**: 即使在睡夢中也不放手。這個小動作不用任何文字就能說出：「我不要你走」 (Even in sleep, not letting go. This tiny gesture says "don't leave" without a single word)

- **Frozen in place by love（被愛定住）**: 守護者整個人僵住——不是因為害怕，是因為不捨得動。手機放下了、電視忘了、腿麻了，全部都不重要 (The guardian is frozen — not from fear, but from not wanting to disturb. Phone abandoned, TV forgotten, leg numb. None of it matters)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓睡著的角色看起來完美無瑕——閉眼、微笑、姿勢端正。那是模特兒在拍照，不是真的在睡覺 (Making the sleeping character look flawless — closed eyes, smile, proper posture. That's a model posing, not someone actually sleeping)

✅ 加入真實的睡覺細節：臉被壓歪、嘴巴張開、口水、手抓著旁邊的東西。越「醜」越真實，越真實越溫暖 (Add real sleeping details: squished face, open mouth, drool, hand clutching something nearby. The "uglier" it is, the more real — and the more real, the warmer)

✨ **Try This Variation / 延伸挑戰**

👉 試試「雙向版」：兩個角色都在對方身上睡著了——一個躺在另一個腿上，另一個的頭也歪著靠在沙發上睡了。兩個人都以為自己在守護對方，結果都睡了。
(Try the "mutual version": both characters fell asleep on each other — one on the other's lap, and the other nodded off leaning against the couch. Both thought they were watching over the other. Both lost.)
