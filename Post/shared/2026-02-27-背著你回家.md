你已經睡著了，但他還在走。走得很慢很慢，怕吵醒你。他的肩膀很累，但手很穩。你的臉頰貼著他的背，什麼都不知道，只知道很安全。
You're already asleep, but they're still walking. Slowly, carefully, afraid to wake you. Their shoulders are tired, but their hands are steady. Your cheek pressed against their back, knowing nothing except that you're safe.

💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **Boneless weight of someone who trusts absolutely（完全信任的無骨重量）**: 這是整個 Prompt 最關鍵的一句。睡著的人全身放鬆、癱軟，像一團沒有骨頭的重量——這就是「信任」最純粹的視覺化 (The single most critical phrase. The sleeping person's body is completely limp and heavy — boneless. This IS trust in its purest visual form)

- **Tired but tender half-smile（疲憊但溫柔的微笑）**: 背著你的人不是精力充沛的英雄，他累了一整天，肩膀都在痛。但他在笑。這個反差比任何完美場景都更讓人心疼 (The carrier isn't an energetic hero — they're exhausted, shoulders aching. But they're smiling. This contrast hits harder than any perfect scene)

- **From slightly behind（從背後偏角拍）**: 鏡頭在他們背後，跟著他們走。觀眾看不到睡著的人的臉，只看到那個貼在肩膀上的臉頰。這個角度讓觀眾覺得自己也在默默跟著走 (Camera behind them, following. The viewer can't see the sleeper's face, just the cheek pressed to the shoulder. This angle makes the viewer feel like they're quietly following along)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓背負者看起來輕鬆愉快——如果背著人走路不累，就沒有犧牲感，溫馨感也會打折 (Making the carrier look effortless and cheerful — if carrying someone isn't tiring, there's no sacrifice, and the warmth is diminished)

✅ 讓背負者的疲憊「看得見」：微微駝的肩膀、皺巴巴的襯衫、鬆開的領帶。但同時手很穩、步伐很輕。「累但穩」才是真正的愛的姿態 (Make the carrier's tiredness VISIBLE: slightly stooped shoulders, rumpled shirt, loosened tie. But steady hands and gentle steps. "Tired but steady" is what love actually looks like)

✨ **Try This Variation / 延伸挑戰**

👉 試試「角色互換版」：平常總是被保護的小角色（像 Kirby），反過來努力背著一個比自己大很多的夥伴。小小的身體搖搖晃晃，臉漲得紅紅的，但就是不放手。
(Try the "role reversal": the usually-protected small character (like Kirby) struggling to carry someone much bigger. Tiny body wobbling, face flushed with effort, but absolutely refusing to let go.)
