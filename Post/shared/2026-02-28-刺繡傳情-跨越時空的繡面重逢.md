💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **Faded-to-vibrant color restoration（褪色→復色的時間逆流）**: 這是整個概念的視覺隱喻——時間帶走了顏色，但愛的觸碰讓顏色回來。不是瞬間恢復，而是從指尖觸碰的那一點開始，像水波一樣慢慢向外擴散。最遠處的繡線還是褪色的，最近的已經鮮豔如新。這個漸變本身就是一幅畫 (The visual metaphor for the entire concept — time stole the colors, but a touch of love brings them back. Not instant, but spreading wave by wave from the touch point. Farthest threads still faded, nearest threads vibrant as new. The gradient itself is a painting)

- **"I've been here the whole time"（我一直都在）**: Kirby 的表情不是遊戲裡那個活蹦亂跳的 Kirby，而是一個安靜的、溫柔的版本。這個反差才是催淚點——它等了這麼久，不是為了冒險，只是為了再被看一眼 (Kirby's expression isn't the bouncy game version — it's quiet, gentle. This contrast is the tear-jerker — it waited this long, not for adventure, just to be looked at one more time)

- **Translucent body with visible weave（可透視布紋的半透明身體）**: 角色不是完全實體化的。你可以透過它的身體看到手帕的織紋。這暗示了兩件事：這個重逢是脆弱的、可能是暫時的，同時角色和它的布料原點之間有著永遠無法切斷的聯繫 (The character isn't fully solid — the handkerchief's weave is visible through the body. This implies two things: the reunion is fragile and possibly temporary, AND the character and its fabric origin have an unbreakable bond)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓角色完美嶄新地跳出來，彷彿從沒褪色過。那就失去了「時間」和「記憶」的全部重量 (Having the character pop out perfectly new, as if never faded. That loses all the weight of "time" and "memory")

✅ 保留褪色的痕跡——角色身上的顏色是正在恢復中的，不是已經恢復完畢的。磨損的手帕、斷裂的繡線、泛黃的布面——這些「不完美」才是讓人落淚的真正原因 (Keep the fading — the character's colors are returning, not returned. Worn handkerchief, broken threads, yellowed fabric — these "imperfections" are why people actually cry)

✨ **Try This Variation / 延伸挑戰**

👉 試試「雙向穿越」版本：一張舊照片裡的真人小孩正從照片裡伸出手，而手帕上的刺繡角色也從布面伸出手——兩個不同媒介、不同時空的「記憶」在一張桌子上相遇，指尖即將觸碰。照片裡的光是暖黃色的90年代色調，刺繡的光是金線的魔法光芒。
(Try the "two-way crossing" version: a real child in an old photograph reaches out from the photo surface, while the embroidered character reaches out from the handkerchief — two "memories" from different media and different eras meeting on the same table, fingertips about to touch. The photo emits warm 90s yellowish light, the embroidery emits golden thread magic glow.)
