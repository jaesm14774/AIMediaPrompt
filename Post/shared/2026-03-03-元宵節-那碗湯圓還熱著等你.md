💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **Chipped but beloved old bowl（缺角但珍貴的老碗）**: 不是全新漂亮的餐具，是用了幾十年的家傳老碗。缺角代表時間，時間代表陪伴——這種「不完美」比任何高級餐具都溫暖 (Not a new, pretty dish — it's a family bowl used for decades. The chip represents time, and time represents being together. This "imperfection" is warmer than any luxury tableware)

- **Evidence that someone prepared this and left（有人準備好然後離開的痕跡）**: 這張圖最感人的不是「在場的人」，是「缺席但留下痕跡的人」。眼鏡、毛毯、洗好的碗——每一個物件都是無聲的「我在等你」 (The most moving part isn't the person who's there — it's the person who's absent but left traces. Glasses, blanket, washed bowl — every object is a silent "I was waiting for you")

- **Single warm ceiling lamp（唯一的頭頂暖燈）**: 整個畫面只有一個光源，讓所有注意力集中在餐桌上的那個小圓圈——光的範圍就是愛的範圍，簡單、專注、不需要更多 (One single light source focuses all attention on that small circle at the table — the reach of the light is the reach of love. Simple, focused, needing nothing more)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 畫一家人熱鬧地圍坐吃湯圓——那是「團圓」的標準畫面，但少了故事性和情感衝擊力。人多反而分散了感動 (Drawing a whole family happily eating tangyuan together — that's the standard "reunion" image, but it lacks narrative depth and emotional impact. More people actually dilutes the emotion)

✅ 讓畫面只有一個角色 + 一碗湯圓 + 缺席者留下的痕跡。「不在場的愛」比「在場的愛」更讓人心疼——觀眾會自動腦補那個等到睡著的阿嬤，這種想像空間 = 情感放大器 (Keep the frame to one character + one bowl + traces of the absent person. "Love that's not present" is more heartbreaking than "love that's present" — viewers will automatically imagine the grandmother who waited until she fell asleep. This imagination space = emotion amplifier)
