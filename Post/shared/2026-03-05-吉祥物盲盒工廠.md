💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **Every variant is DIFFERENT（每款都不一樣）**: 盲盒的靈魂就是「不知道會抽到哪一款」。流水線上的每個產品必須是不同造型——不同服裝、不同顏色、不同配件。以 Kirby 為例，每款對應不同的 Copy Ability（冰凍、劍士、火焰），讓畫面五顏六色、充滿收藏慾。如果每個都長一樣，就不是盲盒而是量產品了 (The soul of blind boxes is "not knowing which variant you'll get." Every product on the line MUST be a different design — different costumes, colors, accessories. For Kirby, each matches a different Copy Ability. This makes the scene colorful and collectible. If every one looks the same, it's mass production, not a blind box)

- **Checklist wall with silhouettes（牆上的變體清單剪影）**: 品管站牆上掛著所有款式的剪影圖，有些打勾有些還沒——特別是「隱藏款」旁邊有金色星星標記。這個細節讓觀眾知道「還有更多款式」，激發收藏衝動 (QC station wall shows silhouettes of all variants, some checked off — the "secret rare" has a golden star. This detail tells viewers "there are more variants," triggering collector urge)

- **Ultra-rare REAL SELF as secret variant（真正的自己是隱藏款）**: 最大的 meta 笑點！所有變體都是角色穿不同服裝的版本，但最稀有的隱藏款竟然是「什麼都沒穿的原版自己」——不小心把真正的自己裝進去了。身份的荒謬翻轉：變裝版反而是常見款，素顏版才是稀有的 (The biggest meta joke! All variants are the character in different costumes, but the RAREST secret is "the original self in plain form" — accidentally boxed in. Identity absurdity flip: costumed versions are common, bare original is the rare one)

- **Isometric-lite perspective（微型等距透視）**: 稍微俯視的視角讓整個場景像一個精緻的娃娃屋或模型。比正面平視更能展現工廠的空間佈局和流水線上五顏六色的變體排列，讓觀眾有「窺視微型世界」的沉浸感 (Slightly top-down view makes the scene look like a delicate dollhouse. Shows factory layout and the colorful parade of variants on the belt better than straight-on view, giving viewers "peeking into a tiny world" immersion)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓流水線上的每個產品長一模一樣——這樣就只是「量產工廠」而不是「盲盒工廠」。盲盒的核心魅力是「每款都不同」，流水線必須展示五顏六色、造型各異的變體排列，讓人一眼看到就想收集 (Making every product on the line look identical — that's a "mass production factory" not a "blind box factory." Blind box core appeal is "every variant is different." The line MUST show colorful, diverse variants to trigger instant collector desire)

✅ 用三站結構展現完整的「多樣化生產」流程：Station 1 展示各種不同顏色的布料和配件被縫成不同造型；Station 2 把不同變體裝進外觀一模一樣的盒子（外面看不出裡面是哪款 = 盲盒感）；Station 3 的展示架擺出全系列收藏，讓觀眾看到「原來有這麼多款！」。隱藏款用金色標記暗示稀有度。加上「偷睡覺的穿著半成品服裝」的細節，連休息都在暗示生產的多樣性！ (Use 3-station structure for complete "diversified production" flow: Station 1 shows different fabrics and accessories being sewn into different designs; Station 2 puts different variants into IDENTICAL boxes (can't tell from outside = blind box feel); Station 3 display shelf shows the full collection lineup. Secret rare gets golden star. Even the napping character wears a half-finished costume — diversity hints everywhere!)
