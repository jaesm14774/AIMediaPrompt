💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **Yatai（屋台）**: 日本傳統路邊攤的正式名稱。用這個詞而不是 "food cart" 能讓 AI 生成更道地的日式攤位——木造框架、布帷、紙燈籠、手寫菜單板，一整組日本祭典的視覺語彙 (The proper term for Japanese street food stalls. Using "yatai" instead of "food cart" triggers AI to generate authentic Japanese stall elements — wooden frame, cloth curtains, paper lanterns, handwritten menu boards)

- **Character's face shape taiyaki（角色臉型鯛魚燒）**: 不是普通鯛魚燒——是角色臉部造型的模具。這個設定讓「自己賣自己」的荒謬感具象化：角色一臉認真地烤出自己的臉，打破第四面牆的喜感 (Not regular taiyaki — it's the character's face as a mold. This makes the "selling yourself" absurdity visual: the character seriously grilling its own face, breaking the fourth wall comedically)

- **Wooden step stool（木階凳）**: 一個小但關鍵的細節！角色太矮所以要站在凳子上才能操作攤位。這種「努力不夠高但很認真」的設定自動產生可愛感，比讓角色莫名其妙夠高更有故事 (A small but critical detail! The character is too short, needs a stool to operate the stall. This "not tall enough but trying so hard" setup auto-generates cuteness — much better than the character being inexplicably tall enough)

- **Soft watercolor texture（柔和水彩質感）**: 水彩的暈染效果和暖色調完美搭配食物主題——蒸氣、麵糊、焦糖色都在水彩風格下顯得更溫暖可口 (Watercolor's blending effects and warm tones perfectly complement food themes — steam, batter, caramel colors all look warmer and more appetizing in watercolor style)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 直接畫一個角色站在正常大小的攤位旁邊——失去了「微型世界」的精緻感和尺度反差。正常比例 = 正常無聊 (Drawing a character next to a normal-sized stall — loses the "miniature world" delicacy and scale contrast. Normal proportions = normally boring)

✅ 強調「一切都是迷你的」：攤位只比角色高一點點、凳子是必需品、客人要疊羅漢才看得到。用 "barely taller than the character" 和 "step stool" 確立微型比例，讓整個場景像一個精緻的微縮立體模型！食物要有蒸氣和裂開的截面，讓人「聞到味道」(Emphasize "everything is mini": stall barely taller than character, stool is a necessity, customers stack to see the counter. Use "barely taller" and "step stool" to establish micro-scale, making the whole scene feel like a precious miniature diorama! Food needs steam and cross-sections so viewers can almost "smell" it)
