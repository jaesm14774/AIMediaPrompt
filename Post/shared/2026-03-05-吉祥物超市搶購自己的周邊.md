Imagine a mini supermarket where EVERY product has the same face — tissue boxes, cookie packs, socks, mugs, phone cases, ALL featuring one mascot's face. Another stuck at self-checkout: it scanned its own face instead of the barcode.

💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **Shopping for merchandise featuring THEIR OWN FACE（搶購印著自己臉的商品）**: 打破第四面牆的終極設定——角色不只是「存在」於這個世界，它們「知道」自己是 IP 角色而且對此非常自戀。這種自我意識式幽默讓畫面從「可愛」升級為「可愛又好笑」 (The ultimate fourth-wall-breaking setup — characters don't just "exist" in this world, they "know" they're IP characters and are completely narcissistic about it. This self-aware humor elevates the image from "cute" to "cute AND funny")

- **Body pillow version bigger than actual character（等身抱枕比本人還大）**: 最荒謬的尺度反差！角色抱著比自己還大的自己的抱枕——這個畫面本身就是一個「自戀到扭曲」的視覺笑話 (The most absurd scale contrast! A character hugging a body pillow of itself that's BIGGER than itself — this image alone is a visual joke about "narcissism taken too far")

- **Self-checkout scanning own face（自助結帳掃自己的臉）**: 最精妙的彩蛋！結帳機掃到角色本人的臉而不是商品條碼，產生錯誤——這是「角色即商品」概念的完美點題，同時也是一個讓人會心一笑的科技笑話 (The most clever Easter egg! The checkout scanner reads the character's actual face instead of the product barcode, causing errors — this perfectly punctuates the "character IS the product" concept while also being a tech-humor gag)

- **Bird's-eye slight tilt perspective（微俯鳥瞰視角）**: 超市場景最適合的視角！從上方稍微俯看讓觀眾同時看到兩側貨架和中間走道的所有混亂，像在看一個動態的迷你立體模型 (The ideal angle for supermarket scenes! Slight top-down view lets viewers see both shelf sides and center aisle chaos simultaneously, like watching a dynamic miniature diorama)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 只畫「角色在超市裡購物」——如果貨架上是普通商品就只是「可愛角色買東西」，完全沒有荒謬感。重點是貨架上的商品全部是角色自己的臉！ (Just drawing "character shopping in a supermarket" — if shelves have normal products, it's just "cute character buying things" with zero absurdity. The KEY is that ALL products feature the character's own face!)

✅ 確保「每件商品都印著角色的臉」是畫面最先被注意到的荒謬點。用 "all bearing the character's face" 和具體的商品清單（面紙盒、餅乾、襪子、馬克杯）讓 AI 理解「全部都是同一張臉」的瘋狂程度。加上「品質檢查照鏡子」和「掃臉結帳出錯」兩個彩蛋，讓荒謬感持續升級！ (Make sure "every product featuring the character's face" is the FIRST absurdity noticed. Use "all bearing the character's face" with specific product lists (tissue boxes, cookies, socks, mugs) so AI understands the "ALL the same face" insanity level. Add "quality inspection with mirror" and "face-scan checkout error" Easter eggs for escalating absurdity!)
