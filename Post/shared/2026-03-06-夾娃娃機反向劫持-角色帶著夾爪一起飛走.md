💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **Character is STRONGER than the machine（角色比機器更強）**: 概念核心！不是角色「逃脫」夾爪，而是角色的自然能力讓機器的設計完全失效。夾爪成功夾住了——但沒用，因為飛行 > 夾力。這是「系統 vs 能力」的荒謬碰撞 (The core concept! Not the character "escaping" the claw, but the character's natural ability making the machine design completely irrelevant. The claw grabbed successfully — but it doesn't matter, because flight > grip. An absurd collision of "system vs ability")

- **Cable goes SLACK then pulled UPWARD（鋼索先鬆弛再被往上拉）**: 力量反轉的關鍵視覺瞬間！正常是夾爪拉角色上去（鋼索從上方繃緊），但這裡是角色飛起來把夾爪往上拖（鋼索先鬆→再從下方被拉緊）。這個物理反轉讓觀眾的認知也跟著翻轉 (The key visual moment of power reversal! Normally the claw pulls characters UP (cable taut from above), but here the character flies up and drags the claw upward (cable goes slack → then pulled taut from below). This physics reversal flips the viewer's expectations)

- **Hugging the claw at the end（最後擁抱夾爪）**: 最溫暖的結尾設計！角色不是「打敗」機器，而是把夾爪當成新朋友帶回家。它沒有丟掉夾爪，反而擁抱它——整個敘事從「對抗」變成「友誼」。這個情感反轉讓影片從「搞笑」升級為「暖心」 (The warmest ending design! The character didn't "defeat" the machine — it brought the claw home as a new friend. Instead of discarding it, it HUGS the claw. The narrative shifts from "confrontation" to "friendship." This emotional reversal elevates the video from "funny" to "heartwarming")

- **One plush slowly clapping（一隻娃娃慢慢鼓掌）**: 背景彩蛋！在所有娃娃震驚的表情中，有一隻在角落默默鼓掌——它見證了夾娃娃機歷史上最偉大的一刻。這種「旁觀者致敬」的微小動作讓觀眾忍不住再看一遍找細節 (Background Easter egg! Among all the shocked plushies, one in the corner slowly claps — it witnessed the greatest moment in claw machine history. This tiny "bystander tribute" makes viewers rewatch to catch details)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓角色直接掙脫夾爪再飛走——這就變成普通的「逃脫」劇情了，失去了「反向劫持」的核心笑點。角色必須在夾爪的擁抱下飛走，夾爪始終掛在身上 (Letting the character break free first then fly away — that's just a regular "escape" plot, losing the "reverse heist" core joke. The character MUST fly while still in the claw's grip — claw stays attached the entire time)

✅ 關鍵是「力量反轉的漸進過程」：Phase 1 夾爪成功（觀眾以為正常）→ Phase 2 角色紋風不動 + 馬達掙扎（觀眾開始懷疑）→ 角色膨脹 + 鋼索反向被拉（觀眾驚喜）→ Phase 3 帶著夾爪飛出來（觀眾爆笑）。每個階段的力量對比逐漸翻轉，不是瞬間反轉。最後的擁抱把「搞笑」變成「溫馨」——這才是真正的 S 級結尾 (Key is "gradual power reversal": Phase 1 claw succeeds (viewers expect normal) → Phase 2 character unmoved + motor struggles (viewers start doubting) → character inflates + cable reverses (viewers surprised) → Phase 3 flies out with claw attached (viewers laugh). Power balance gradually flips, not instant. The final hug turns "funny" into "heartwarming" — THAT's a true S-tier ending)
