💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **特徵轉化而非 Cosplay（Feature Translation, Not Cosplay）**: 這不是讓角色「穿上另一個角色的皮」，而是把靈感角色的視覺元素重新設計成真正的時尚單品。鱗片→亮片、尾巴→裙擺、火焰→漸層絲綢。轉化的過程是設計的核心——每個時尚元素都要能追溯回靈感角色的某個特徵 (This isn't dressing as another character — it's redesigning their visual elements into genuine fashion pieces. Scales→sequins, tails→trains, flames→gradient silk. The transformation process IS the design — every fashion element should trace back to a specific source feature)

- **反差萌偷跑（The Charming Reveal）**: 最重要的情感鉤子！穿著者的原始招牌特徵（尾巴、耳朵、身形）必須從華服中可愛地露出來。Pikachu 的閃電尾巴從裙縫跑出來、Kirby 的圓滾身形把禮服撐成可愛的球形——這證明「裡面還是那個角色」，創造反差萌 (The most important emotional hook! The wearer's original signature features MUST peek out adorably — Pikachu's bolt-tail through a skirt slit, Kirby's round shape making the gown ball-shaped. This proves "it's still them inside," creating gap moe)

- **Chibi 互動（Chibi Interaction）**: 靈感角色的小版本不是靜態展示品——它要有動作！坐在穿著者肩上驕傲地看、幫忙整理衣領、模仿同樣的姿勢。這讓設計圖從「靜態展示」變成「有故事的場景」 (The inspiration character's chibi isn't a static display — it needs ACTION! Sitting proudly on the shoulder, adjusting the collar, mimicking the pose. This turns a static design sheet into a scene with story)

- **隱藏彩蛋（Hidden Easter Egg）**: 穿著者自己的標誌藏在設計的隱密處——鞋底、手套內側、裙子口袋裡。這增加了「找到了！」的分享價值，讓觀眾仔細看每個細節 (The wearer's own symbol hidden somewhere secret — shoe sole, inner glove, pocket. This adds "found it!" shareability, making viewers inspect every detail)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 直接讓角色穿上另一個角色的外皮（那是 cosplay，不是服裝設計）。避免字面複製——不要把 Charizard 的翅膀直接黏在禮服上，而是要把翅膀的「形狀語言」轉化為肩部結構設計 (Don't literally paste another character's parts onto a dress — that's cosplay. Avoid literal copying. Don't glue Charizard's wings onto the gown — translate the wing's "shape language" into structural shoulder design)

✅ 用「設計圖思維」處理每個細節：先提取靈感角色的配色板（4-5個主色），再逐一分配到禮服的不同部位（上身主色、裙擺漸層色、配件強調色）。頭飾的設計要從靈感角色的頭部特徵「抽象化」而來，鞋子要呼應尾巴或腳部特徵。callout boxes 是設計圖的靈魂——布料紋理特寫、花紋重複圖案、背面剪影，讓整張圖有「專業設計師手稿」的感覺 (Use "design sheet thinking": extract the inspiration character's color palette (4-5 main colors), then assign each to different gown parts (bodice main, skirt gradient, accessory accent). Headpiece abstracts from head features, shoes echo tail/feet. Callout boxes are the design sheet's soul — fabric close-ups, pattern tiles, back-view silhouette — giving the whole image a "professional designer sketch" feel)
