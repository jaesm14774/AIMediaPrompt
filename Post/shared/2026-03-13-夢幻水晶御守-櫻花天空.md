💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **magatama (勾玉) shape**: 勾玉是日本最古老的守護形狀，繩文時代（12,000年前）就存在。逗號形的弧線讓光線折射出獨特的弧形焦散光，完全不同於長方形御守的直線光影 (The magatama is Japan's oldest protective shape from the Jomon period. Its comma-curve creates unique arc-shaped caustics impossible with rectangular charms — the shape itself IS the visual hook)

- **rounded head opaque → tail transparent**: 勾玉的圓頭較厚不透明，尖尾漸漸透明——這讓角色在圓頭裡清晰可見但被朦朧包裹，尾巴則成為純粹的光線遊戲區 (The graduated opacity creates two visual zones: the head is a cozy visible chamber for the character, the tail becomes a pure light playground)

- **sleeping pose mirrors the magatama curve**: 角色蜷縮的睡姿自然貼合勾玉的弧線——形狀呼應形狀，彷彿勾玉就是為了抱住這個角色而誕生的。這個細節讓整張圖有了「命中注定」的感覺 (The character's sleeping curl matches the magatama's curve — shape echoing shape, as if the jewel was born to cradle this specific character. This detail creates a "destined" feeling)

- **golden aura pooling in the tail like liquid gold**: 角色夢境的金色光暈沿著勾玉弧線流向尾尖——視覺上創造了「夢在流動」的動態感，也暗示角色的平靜就是御守力量的來源 (The golden aura flowing along the curve creates visual movement — "dreams flowing" — and implies the character's peace IS the omamori's power source)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 用傳統長方形布袋造型的御守——形狀單調，光影效果有限，跟市面上所有御守 prompt 沒有差異 (Using the standard rectangular pouch shape — boring light play, zero differentiation from every other omamori prompt)

✅ 選擇有歷史意義的非傳統形狀（勾玉、水滴、花苞、鈴鐺）——每種形狀都會產生完全不同的光線折射和視覺節奏。勾玉的弧線 = 弧形焦散光 + 漸變透明度 + 角色姿態呼應，三層設計合一 (Choose historically meaningful non-traditional shapes (magatama, teardrop, flower, bell) — each creates unique light refraction and visual rhythm.
