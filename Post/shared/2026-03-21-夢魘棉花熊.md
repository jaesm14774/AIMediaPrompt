💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **split perfectly down the middle**: 左右二元對稱是這個設計的核心機制，強制要求 AI 產生視覺對比線，不可模糊 (The hard split line is the visual anchor — fuzzy gradients lose the dramatic tension)
- **fibrous, layered, backlit translucency**: 棉花糖的質感描述指引 AI 產生分層蓬鬆感而非固體球 (This material keyword triggers correct cotton candy texture — without it AI tends to render a solid cloud puff)
- **unaware of the split sky above**: 這句話鎖定了「熊的表情是平靜的」，防止 AI 讓熊顯示出驚恐或察覺表情 (Anchors the bear's expression to peaceful obliviousness — the comedic irony of the entire piece)
- **contrast line down the middle is sharp**: 明確要求邊界要硬，而不是柔化過渡 (Sharp boundary = maximum visual drama; blurred boundary = concept lost)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 讓左右兩側顏色太過相似或邊界模糊，雙面對比就失效了 (If the left-right colors bleed into each other, the entire concept collapses — it becomes just a weird cloud)

✅ 左側主色用暖色（粉/金/橙），右側主色用冷色（深紫/海軍藍/炭黑）——視覺溫度差異讓大腦自動感受到甜/暗對比 (Use warm hues on the dream side, cold-dark on the nightmare side. The brain reads warm=safe, cold=ominous instantly)
