💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **body IS made of soft storm cloud**: 用 IS 而不是 looks like——讓 AI 理解這不是比喻，身體本身的材質就是雲 (The word "IS" vs "looks like" signals literal material transformation — AI reads this as actual cloud-substance body, not cloud-pattern fur)
- **billowing, cottony, three-dimensional cumulus puffs**: 三個形容詞組合描述雲的立體質感——積雲（cumulus）比卷雲或層雲更圓潤，符合幼獅圓滾滾的體型 (Cumulus = round, puffy, three-dimensional. This is the right cloud type for a chibi round body — cirrus clouds would look wispy and flat)
- **tiny thunder rumble lines (like manga speed lines)**: 漫畫式震動線是「雷聲視覺化」的直接表達，讓靜態圖像有動感 (Manga speed lines around the mane add kinetic energy — the mane "crackles" visually even in a still image)
- **delightedly pawing at a tiny raindrop**: 這個動作把「恐怖天氣」變成「好奇幼崽」——情緒反差是整圖的核心 (This action is the emotional inversion: destructive storm energy → curious innocent playfulness)
