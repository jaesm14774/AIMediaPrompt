Sometimes even the happiest creature in the world has an afternoon like this —

💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **untouched cake, frosting slightly melted**: 單一道具細節的故事力——Kirby 不吃食物比任何文字說明都更能傳達「今天心情不好」（Kirby not eating is more emotionally powerful than any caption — it communicates mood through behavior, not description, the mark of great visual storytelling）

- **warm-cool tension between inside comfort and outside melancholy**: 暖黃室內 vs. 灰藍窗外不只是色彩技法，更是「保護」與「憂傷」的視覺隱喻（The warm-cool split maps directly to emotional geography: warm inside = the self you're trying to protect; cool outside = everything weighing on you）

- **low camera angle looking slightly upward**: 仰視角讓 Kirby 在巨大窗口中顯得格外渺小——這個比例差距是「小小存在面對巨大情緒」的視覺語言（The upward angle frames Kirby as tiny within the vast window opening — the scale contrast between small figure and large weather externalizes the feeling of being overwhelmed）

- **"a quiet, companionable sadness"**: 精確命名情緒品質，而非泛稱「悲傷」——這個修飾詞讓 AI 生成的氛圍更接近「下雨天的平靜憂鬱」而非「哭泣式悲傷」（Precise emotional tone descriptors guide the AI toward nuance — "companionable sadness" vs. "grief" produces completely different atmospheric results）

- **misty atmospheric perspective dissolving background details**: 霧化的遠景讓觀眾的視線自然留在 Kirby 身上，同時製造「世界朦朧不清」的孤獨感（Atmospheric haze keeps the viewer's eye on Kirby while creating visual loneliness — the world outside literally becomes unclear, mirroring emotional fog）

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 直接寫 "Kirby looks sad" 或 "Kirby is unhappy" — 命名情緒讓圖片說教而非感染（Naming the emotion directly ("sad Kirby") tells viewers what to feel instead of letting them feel it — always externalize emotion through props, weather, and body language）

✅ 用「身體語言 + 道具 + 天氣」三重層次傳達情緒：托腮姿勢（身體）+ 沒動的蛋糕（道具）+ 薄霧雨天（天氣）= 觀眾自己感受到，而非被告知（Use three layers: body posture + object + weather. The viewer arrives at the emotion themselves — this creates empathy, not instruction）
