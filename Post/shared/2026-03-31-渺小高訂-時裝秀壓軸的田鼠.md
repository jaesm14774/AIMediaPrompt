💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **Deliberate finale pose — chin lifted, one small paw resting lightly on the hip（刻意的壓軸姿態）**: 這不是隨機站姿，而是「刻意的時裝秀壓軸儀式」。`deliberate` 這個字讓 AI 理解這是一個有目的的動作，而非偶然的停頓。`chin lifted` 確保臉部朝向天空，配合仰拍構圖讓輪廓更具震撼力 (The word "deliberate" signals intentional choreography rather than random posture — this prevents AI from generating casual standing poses. Combined with the low-angle shot, it creates the exact "fashion show finale" silhouette that makes this image iconic)

- **Dramatic low-angle upward shot（戲劇性低角度仰拍）**: 這是讓渺小生命顯得偉大的關鍵構圖指令。仰拍視角讓田鼠的輪廓直接對比開闊天空，放大尺度反差的視覺衝擊——若改用平視或俯拍，整個概念的情感核心就消失了 (Low-angle upward shot is the single most important technical instruction — it's what transforms a "cute mouse" into an "epic silhouette against infinity." Without this, AI defaults to eye-level framing and the entire emotional concept collapses)

- **Forest stretches endlessly below, rendered as soft watercolor distance haze（森林化為水彩遠景霧靄）**: 前景主體清晰 + 背景刻意模糊的景深設計。Ghibli 水彩的特質是「越遠越輕柔」，這句指令確保背景不搶奪田鼠的視覺主權，同時賦予畫面「望向無限遠方」的空間詩意 (Atmospheric perspective is core to Ghibli's visual language — close details sharp, distant world soft and fading. This instruction locks in the depth gradient that makes Ghibli backgrounds feel "endless" rather than merely "large")

- **Bioluminescence or catches the last light of a golden sunset（生物發光 / 金色夕陽餘暉）**: 這個「二選一」設計讓 AI 自由選擇最合適的光源方案，同時確保自然地標本身有光，讓田鼠「站在有光的地方」而非被環境淹沒。生物發光感覺神聖，夕陽感覺告別——兩種光源都服務於同一個情感核心：「這是一個特殊的時刻」 (The "or" gives AI compositional flexibility while guaranteeing the platform emits light — this ensures the tiny figure is always backlit or center-lit, never lost in shadow. Both light sources serve "this moment matters" as the emotional through-line)

- **Still and reverent（靜默而莊嚴）**: 這兩個字是整個 prompt 的情感守門員。`still` 防止 AI 生成動態、活潑的畫面；`reverent` 傳遞一種近乎宗教式的敬意氛圍，讓觀者在看到畫面時自動放慢呼吸。這是 Ghibli 電影中最標誌性的情感頻率 ("Still and reverent" are the emotional gatekeepers of the entire prompt. They prevent AI from generating "cute and dynamic" in favor of "small and sacred" — this two-word instruction is responsible for the image's ability to make viewers pause and feel something they can't quite name)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 把地標換成「建築物頂端」（如樓頂、鐘樓）——一旦引入人工建築，整個 Ghibli 森林系的視覺語境就崩解了，自然 × 高訂的反差也消失 (Avoid swapping natural landmarks for man-made structures like rooftops or towers — once you introduce architecture, the Ghibli forest world dissolves and you lose the "nature as runway" contrast that makes this concept unique)

✅ 地標越「生物性」越好：蘑菇、花瓣、葉子、貝殼、松果——這些有機形狀在 Ghibli 水彩中天然好看，且與高訂禮服的精緻工藝形成有趣的質地對比（有機 × 工藝） (The more organic the landmark, the better: mushrooms, petals, leaves, shells, pine cones — organic shapes are native to Ghibli's visual vocabulary and create a beautiful texture contrast with the precision of haute couture craftsmanship)

❌ 省略 `deliberate finale pose` 描述，只寫「standing」——AI 會生成一個普通的站立姿勢，而非時裝秀壓軸的那種自我宣告感 (Never simplify to just "standing" — without the finale pose description, AI generates a casual stance rather than the self-declared "I have arrived" energy that gives this image its emotional power)

✅ 加入季節性地標強化情感層次：**春**選櫻花花瓣（短暫之美）、**夏**選巨型葉片（繁茂生命力）、**秋**選楓葉（盛極而轉）、**冬**選雪覆蘑菇（孤寂壯美）——每個季節賦予田鼠不同的故事 (Add seasonal context to deepen the emotional layer: spring cherry petal (fleeting beauty), summer giant leaf (abundant life), autumn maple leaf (peak before turning), winter snow-capped mushroom (solitary grandeur) — each season gives the mouse a different story)
