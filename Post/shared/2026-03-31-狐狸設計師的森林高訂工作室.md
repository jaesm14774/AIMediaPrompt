💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **體型反差（Size Contrast）**: 小老鼠 vs 長頸鹿、刺蝟 vs 兔新娘——不對等的體型創造出天然的視覺笑點，AI 在構圖時會自動強調這種尺度衝突 (Extreme size disparity is the visual punchline; AI models naturally emphasize scale contrast for comic effect — "absurd size contrast" is the single most important signal in this prompt)

- **荒謬道具視覺化（Absurdist Props — Visual Encoding）**: 用「蠟封合約捲軸」取代寫明文字的合約、「金燙浮雕邀請卡」取代寫明文字的邀請函——避免 AI 在畫面上渲染出亂碼文字，同時保留荒謬感的氛圍 (Replace explicit text labels with visual-only descriptors: "wax crest sealed scroll" instead of writing contract text. This prevents AI text rendering failures while preserving the comedic atmosphere — critical technique for Ghibli-style scenes)

- **Studio Ghibli Watercolor Style**: 半透明水彩疊色（translucent layered washes）+ 可見水彩紙紋（visible watercolor paper texture）+ 手繪背景豐富感（hand-painted background richness）三層指令確保 Ghibli 風格準確復現，而非通用動漫風 (Three-layer Ghibli encoding prevents generic anime output: "translucent washes" = lighting quality, "paper texture" = medium authenticity, "hand-painted richness" = detail density — all three required together)

- **Whimsical yet earnest professional atmosphere**: 這句是整個 prompt 的靈魂指令——「荒謬但嚴肅」的張力就靠這一句撐住。缺少它，AI 可能生成純可愛或純滑稽的圖，而非「嚴肅喜劇」(This single phrase is the tonal anchor. Without it, AI defaults to "cute and funny" rather than "absurdly professional." It signals the comedic register — earnest execution of a ridiculous scenario)

- **Composition directive（構圖指令）**: `Composition emphasizes the absurd size contrast and the designer's hilariously over-serious expression` 明確告知 AI 構圖的視覺重心，避免 AI 自行決定而把焦點放在環境而非角色 (Explicit composition instruction prevents AI from defaulting to environment-focused Ghibli framing — forces character expression and size contrast to be the focal point)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 直接描述道具上的文字（如「牆上貼著寫有 REJET 的紙條」）——AI 會在畫面上渲染出浮空亂碼文字，破壞整體美感 (Never describe text content on props — AI will generate floating, garbled typography that destroys the visual)

✅ 改成描述視覺形狀與氛圍：「牆上貼滿了草率潦草筆跡的碎紙條，下方是散落的草圖」——相同的荒謬感，零文字渲染風險 (Describe the visual appearance of props instead: "scraps covered in frustrated handwritten scrawls." Same comedic implication, zero text rendering risk — key to clean Ghibli-style scenes)

❌ 只用一種反差（都是體型反差）——三個選項全是大 vs 小會讓模板顯得單調 (Don't rely on a single type of contrast — all size-based combos make the template feel repetitive)

✅ 混合不同反差維度：體型反差（老鼠+長頸鹿）× 個性反差（刺蝟+緊張兔新娘）× 行為反差（刺蝟+嘆氣天鵝）——每個選項都有獨立的喜劇邏輯，複用性大幅提升 (Mix contrast dimensions: size contrast × personality contrast × behavioral contrast. Each option should have its own comedic logic — this is what makes templates reusable and shareable)
