💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **雙色調對比設計（Dual Palette Contrast）**: `soft pearl and dusty rose for the Victorian clothing` × `cool neon-lit modern backgrounds` — 這組冷暖對比是讓時空錯位感在第一眼就成立的視覺核心。維多利亞暖色 vs 現代冷色，不需要任何文字說明，觀者立刻感受到「兩個時代同時存在」的張力 (This warm-cool contrast is the visual engine of temporal displacement — warm Victorian tones against cool neon Tokyo creates instant era collision without any text explanation)

- **角色自帶光源（Character-Carried Amber Glow）**: `warm amber glow emanating from the character as if they carry their own era's light` — 這句指令讓角色不依賴場景光源，自帶時代光暈，解決了 Ghibli 柔光與現代霓虹燈可能產生的光影衝突，同時賦予角色一種「異鄉人」的詩意感 (Making the character their own light source solves the Ghibli-vs-neon lighting conflict while adding a poetic "stranger from another time" quality — emotionally resonant and technically clean)

- **Wide establishing shot（寬景構圖指令）**: 強制 AI 採用「全景建立鏡頭」，確保維多利亞服裝的完整輪廓與現代環境同時出現在畫面中——少了這句，AI 可能只特寫角色臉部而失去時空對比的主視覺 (Without this, AI defaults to close-up portrait framing, losing the environment that makes temporal contrast visible — this single instruction preserves the concept's entire visual logic)

- **Mildly bewildered expression（微微困惑的神情）**: 不是誇張驚嚇，不是完全淡定——而是「微微困惑」。這個細微的情緒設定讓角色既有喜劇感又有尊嚴，避免畫面淪為廉價搞笑，維持 Ghibli 式的溫柔幽默 ("Mildly bewildered" is the exact tonal calibration — too shocked becomes slapstick, too calm loses the comedy. This precise description preserves Ghibli's signature gentle humor register)

- **Wistful atmosphere（懷舊惆悵氛圍）**: 這個詞為整幅畫加入情感深度。純喜劇只讓人笑，wistful 讓人笑完之後多想一秒——「他是從哪裡來的？他想回家嗎？」這種餘韻讓圖片值得被儲存和分享，而非只是一笑而過 (This single word adds emotional depth beyond comedy — "wistful" makes the image memorable and saveable, not just funny. It's why people share Ghibli art rather than just scrolling past it)
