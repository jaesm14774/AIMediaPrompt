💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **Void-black seamless background / bomb's pulsing red LED as sole light source**: 雙重設計：背景全黑讓所有注意力集中在角色和炸彈，單一紅色光源讓畫面色調統一且戲劇性十足——電影拍攝「黑箱單光源」技法 (Two-in-one design: black void removes all distractions, single red light source creates total visual drama and color cohesion — a classic cinema "black box single source" lighting technique)

- **At the extreme edge of frame — barely visible — a second pair of gloved hands, motionless**: 這個細節是情感核心：不直接說「隊友死了」，而是讓觀眾自己發現畫面邊緣那雙靜止的手——沉默比任何說明都更有力 (This is the emotional core: never saying "teammate is down" directly, but letting viewers discover the motionless hands at the frame edge — silence is more powerful than any caption)

- **bomb glow reflected as twin burning points in the character's wide eyes**: 讓 AI 渲染眼睛中的光點反射——電影攝影學中「catchlight」的進階應用，一個細節讓角色從插圖變成電影主角 (Rendering bomb glow in the character's eyes is a "catchlight" cinematography technique — this single detail transforms the character from illustration to movie protagonist)

- **Desaturated Saving Private Ryan color grade with selective deep crimson warmth**: 指定具體電影的色彩風格而非說「電影感」，AI 能準確理解：整體去飽和、帶細膩暗部細節、只讓紅色溫暖保留 (Naming a specific film's color grade gives AI a concrete visual reference — "Saving Private Ryan" signals: desaturated overall, detailed shadow tonality, with warm red/amber preservation)

- **Character expression: not fear, not hesitation. Just training**: 表情描述不用形容詞，而是用「排除法」定義——不是恐懼、不是猶豫，而是訓練的本能。讓 AI 渲染出純粹的「專注」而非「緊張」 (Describing expression through negation: "not fear, not hesitation" is more precise than "focused" — it tells AI to exclude panic micro-expressions and render pure trained concentration)
