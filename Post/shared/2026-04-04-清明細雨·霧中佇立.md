💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **umbrella deliberately angled to cover the gravestone — leaving the character fully exposed**: 這是整個畫面的情感核心。不是隨意的姿態，是有意識的選擇——傘的角度決定一切，AI 需要明確方向才能正確渲染這個反差 (The emotional anchor of the entire image. The umbrella's deliberate tilt—shielding the stone, not the character—is the single most important visual statement. Clarity here prevents AI from defaulting to a "normal" umbrella pose)

- **desaturated cool blue-gray palette with only two warm color accents**: 全場冷色調壓制情緒，讓香火的琥珀與油紙傘的深紅各自成為「視覺呼吸點」，引導觀看者視線依序落點 (Full desaturation forces the eye toward the two warm anchors — incense amber at ground level, crimson umbrella held aloft. This controlled warmth creates visual rhythm in an otherwise still scene)

- **each petal holding a single raindrop**: 靜物細節。花瓣上的單顆雨滴是畫面的「靜止時刻」，讓氛圍從哀傷轉為凝視，製造情感延遲 (A still-life micro-detail that slows the viewer down. One raindrop per petal turns grief into contemplation — the scene breathes instead of weeps)

- **visible rain streaks in mid-ground**: 讓雨的存在感具體可渲染，避免 AI 生成「模糊霧氣代替雨」的常見失準 (Specifying rain streaks in mid-ground gives AI a precise spatial instruction, preventing it from rendering rain as formless mist in the foreground or background only)

- **ink-wash photography style + anamorphic lens + fine film grain**: 三者結合讓畫面同時具備東方水墨美感與西方電影感，不是「電影感的東洋風景」，而是兩種語言都在說話的視覺 (The convergence of ink-wash aesthetics with anamorphic cinema language creates a visual that speaks to both East Asian cultural memory and universal cinematic emotion simultaneously)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 只描述角色「站在墓前淋雨」，沒有強調傘的方向——AI 會默認替角色撐傘，完全消解這個畫面的情感核心
(Describing the character "standing in rain by a grave" without specifying umbrella angle causes AI to default to "character sheltering itself" — the exact opposite of the intended emotional statement)

✅ 加入「umbrella deliberately angled to cover the gravestone completely」這個方向限定詞，並搭配「leaving the character fully exposed」補充說明，讓 AI 理解這是有意識的姿態，而不是物理錯誤
(Use directional constraints like "deliberately angled to cover the gravestone — leaving the character fully exposed" to signal intentionality. AI responds better to "deliberate" framing than to passive description alone)
