🪞 **清明鏡界——生死之間，只隔一面水 | The Mirror Between Worlds: Only Still Water Separates the Living and the Gone**

水面這側，你跪拜、燒香、細雨打濕衣袖。
水面那側，先人靜靜回望你——光從靈界升起，從下方照亮你的臉。

生與死，不是隔了一道牆，而是一面水鏡。
這幅畫，兩個世界同在一幀裡，彼此相望。

On your side: kneeling, incense smoke, cold spring drizzle.
On the other side: an ancestor, standing in the reflection, looking back up at you.
The light comes from below — from the spirit realm, upward.
Two worlds. One frame. One still water surface between them.

💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **split-world composition（雙世界構圖）**: 以水面水平分割畫面，讓上下兩個世界同時存在於一幅圖中，形成視覺上的「鏡射敘事」——這是整個 prompt 的核心結構。(Horizontal split creates simultaneous dual-world storytelling in a single frame — the defining visual architecture of this prompt.)

- **volumetric light cascades upward（靈界光線反向向上）**: 通常是生者照亮靈界，這裡反過來——光從靈界升起，從下方照亮活著的人，象徵先人仍在守護。這個反轉是情感的第二層。(Light rising from the spirit realm inverts the usual direction — the ancestor illuminates the living, not the other way. This reversal is the emotional second layer.)

- **desaturated blue-grey vs. warm amber-gold（冷暖色調對比）**: 活人側冷色調象徵現實的重量，靈界側暖色調象徵先人世界的溫暖與豐盛。兩側色溫對比讓邊界感一眼直覺。(Cool-warm contrast makes the boundary immediately readable — grey reality above, golden warmth below.)

- **single incense wisp crossing（唯一跨越兩界的煙線）**: 整幅圖中唯一連通兩個世界的視覺元素，讓靜止的水鏡產生微微的生命感。(The single incense wisp is the only element that crosses the mirror boundary — a delicate visual thread connecting both worlds.)

- **留白 / generous negative space（留白）**: 中國水墨畫傳統技法，天空與邊緣大量留白製造空曠與哀思感，避免畫面過滿壓迫。(Traditional guohua negative space — empty sky and margins create grief, quietude, and breathing room.)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 靈境選項選「先人雙手幾乎觸碰跪拜者的手」，但沒有給予足夠的「幾乎但沒有」距離描述，AI 可能直接讓雙手相觸，失去「被水面隔開」的遺憾感。
(Choosing the "hands almost touching" option without enough separation context may cause AI to render hands fully connected — losing the "separated by water" tension.)

✅ 若選第三個選項（雙手幾乎觸碰），建議在後方補上：`The fingertips are mere millimeters apart, separated by the hyper-still water surface — connection forever just out of reach.` 這樣 AI 才會保留那份「永遠差一點」的情感。
(Add `fingertips mere millimeters apart, separated by the water surface — forever just out of reach` to preserve the unbridgeable grief in that image.)

✨ **Try This Variation / 延伸挑戰**

👉 試試把靈境選項換成：先人手邊放著你小時候的玩具，或先人臉上帶著微笑，但眼中有淚光——用細節去放大「他們知道你來了」的感覺。不同的靈境呈現方式，會帶出截然不同的情感重量。

(Try this: add a detail to the spirit realm — the ancestor holding a childhood toy you once loved, or a smile with a tear on their cheek. Different ancestor appearances create vastly different emotional weights. The more specific the detail, the more piercing the image.)

🔥 **進階構圖挑戰**: 把水鏡邊界從水平改為輕微傾斜（`the mirror surface tilted at a 5-degree angle`），讓兩個世界產生微妙的不穩定感，暗示邊界本來就是脆弱的。
(Advanced: tilt the mirror surface `at a 5-degree angle` — the slight instability implies that the boundary between worlds was never truly solid.)
