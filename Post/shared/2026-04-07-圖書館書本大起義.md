💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **empty book cover hanging loosely in one hand / 手持空封面**: 這是整個 Prompt 最強的敘事細節——空封面代表「起點已被超越」，是[IP角色]成為無意間革命者的物理證據。沒有這個細節，[IP角色]只是旁觀者；有了它，[IP角色]是引爆者。 (The empty cover is the narrative linchpin: it visually proves that **[IP角色]** triggered the uprising. Without it, the character is a bystander; with it, they're the unwitting revolutionary — a single detail that transforms the entire emotional reading of the image)

- **Radiating from [IP角色]'s position like a shockwave / 以角色位置為中心向外輻射**: 起義的擴散方向有明確的空間邏輯——從角色為中心點向外擴散，這讓畫面有清晰的構圖重心，同時視覺上表達「因果關係」 (The shockwave radiation pattern does double duty: it creates a clear compositional focal point AND visually encodes causality — viewers instinctively understand that the character caused the explosion)

- **Empty shelves as geometric backdrop / 空書架作為幾何背景**: 空書架不只是細節，它是整個概念的核心對比——靜默的空架子 vs 上方混亂的知識爆炸。沒有空書架，就沒有「起義成功」的視覺證明 (Empty shelves are not background noise — they are the visual proof that the uprising succeeded. - **Light source from exploding books only / 光源完全來自書本爆炸**: 每本書是獨立的有色光源，這個設定讓畫面色彩豐富但邏輯自洽，且能明確區分不同知識領域的視覺邊界 (Having each book-universe cast its own colored light creates a self-consistent lighting logic: the library's only illumination comes from liberated knowledge — a beautiful metaphor rendered as physics)

- **Studio Ghibli × Moebius graphic novel style**: Ghibli 提供奇幻溫暖的插畫質感，Moebius 提供精細線條與宇宙概念藝術的厚度——兩者組合讓壯觀場景不失溫度 (Ghibli brings warmth and wonder; Moebius brings precision linework and cosmic grandeur. Together they ensure the epic scene never loses its emotional intimacy)
