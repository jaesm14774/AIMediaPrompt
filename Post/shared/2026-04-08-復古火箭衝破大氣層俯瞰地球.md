🚀 **去太空他只帶了一把雨傘，而且毫無悔意 | They Went to Space With a Useless Umbrella — Zero Regrets**

在無邊宇宙的深黑背景前，地球的藍色曲線橫亙畫面下方，一枚亮鉻色的 1950s 復古火箭正以對角線俯衝而上——然後你看到了火箭頂端那個小小的身影，一手插腰，一手撐著一把在真空中完全沒有任何用途的彩色雨傘，悠閒地掃視著腳下那顆星球。

In the infinite black void, Earth's full curvature glows below — and atop a gleaming retro rocket, a tiny figure stands completely unbothered, holding an utterly useless rainbow umbrella in the airless vacuum, casually surveying the planet beneath them. No explanation. No apology.

💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **completely unbothered + gaze sweeping with mild curiosity**：角色的「完全不在意」態度是整個概念的靈魂——這不是壯闊的英雄主義，而是「宇宙規則對我不適用」的輕鬆存在感。AI 會依此渲染出符合這個態度的表情與肢體語言。(The "unbothered" attitude is the conceptual core — it subverts the usual "epic space explorer" trope and creates genuine comedic tension with the grandeur of the scene.)

- **荒謬道具三選項（雨傘 / 熱茶 / 橡皮鴨）**：三個道具各有不同的喜劇質地——雨傘是純粹的視覺荒謬，熱茶是英式冷幽默，橡皮鴨則是角色與道具「同款嚴肅表情」的雙人組合喜劇。選不同的道具，整張圖的笑點方向完全不同。(Each prop creates a different comedic tone — umbrella is visual absurdism, tea is deadpan British humor, rubber duck is a duo-comedy setup with matched solemn expressions.)

- **each material surface catching the Earthglow differently**：道具材質的差異化光影描述（布料 / 陶瓷 / 橡膠反光不同）讓 AI 能精確渲染道具質感，避免生成一個模糊的「彩色物體」。這個細節讓荒謬道具有了真實存在感。(Material-specific lighting cues — fabric, ceramic, rubber — give the AI precise rendering instructions, making the absurd prop feel tangibly real and thus funnier.)

- **1960s vintage NASA concept art / Pixar-quality 3D**：兩個風格選項分別走向不同的視覺美學——復古 NASA 插畫風格有懷舊海報質感，道具的飽和色成為唯一的色彩焦點；Pixar 3D 則讓道具的顏色以誇張的歡樂飽和度對抗深黑宇宙，兩種都有強烈的色彩設計邏輯。(Retro NASA style uses the prop as single saturated accent in an otherwise muted palette — a deliberate color design choice that makes the umbrella/duck/teacup impossible to miss.)

- **anamorphic widescreen 2.39:1 + atmospheric scattering on Earth limb**：電影級寬銀幕比例讓畫面有「大製作感」，地球大氣層邊緣的大氣散射效果則讓地球有真實的宇宙質感，兩者合力創造出「這張圖不像 AI 生成」的視覺高度。(Cinema-grade 2.39:1 ratio combined with physically accurate atmospheric limb scattering creates a production quality that makes the image stand out in any feed.)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 把荒謬道具當成「裝飾品」對待，讓它小到幾乎看不到——這樣就失去了整個概念的爆點 (Treating the absurd prop as decoration and making it too small kills the entire joke — the prop IS the punchline, it needs to be clearly visible)

✅ 使用「the prop rendered with the same loving detail as the rocket」這個指令，強制讓 AI 把道具視為主角之一，確保它在畫面中有足夠的存在感。如果生成結果中雨傘太小或太模糊，可以在 prompt 前段加上「The umbrella is the visual centerpiece of the character's silhouette」來強化 (Use "rendered with the same loving detail" instruction to force the AI to treat the prop as a co-star. If it renders too small, add "The [prop] is the visual centerpiece of the character's silhouette" at the start)

❌ 選了「完全 unbothered」的態度設定，但又同時在描述中加入「excited」或「amazed」等情緒字眼，讓角色態度自相矛盾 (Mixing "completely unbothered" with "excited" or "amazed" in the same prompt creates contradictory character expression signals)

✅ 整份 prompt 從頭到尾貫徹「mild curiosity」而非「awe」的情緒基調——角色看地球的眼神應該是「還不錯嘛」而非「天啊太震撼了」，這個態度反差才是笑點的核心 (Maintain "mild curiosity" tone throughout — the character should look at Earth like it's mildly interesting, not awe-inspiring. That tonal contrast IS the joke.)

✨ **Try This Variation / 延伸挑戰**

👉 試試把三個道具都生成一遍，比較不同喜劇質感的差異：彩色雨傘版（純粹視覺荒謬）、熱茶版（英式冷幽默）、橡皮鴨版（角色與道具同款嚴肅臉）——哪一個讓你笑出來的速度最快？分享你的三連圖！

Character-specific absurdity doubles the impact.)
