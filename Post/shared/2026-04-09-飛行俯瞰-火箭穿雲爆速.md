💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **8-12% of frame（角色佔比）**: 角色刻意渺小——這不是失誤，是讓「尺度反差」成立的關鍵比例。角色愈小，地球愈壯闊，反差張力愈強。 (The tiny character ratio is intentional: it creates the scale-versus-fearlessness tension that makes the image unforgettable. SEO: "epic scale contrast", "tiny character vast environment")

- **Radial speed lines filling 40% of frame（放射速度線）**: 速度線從火箭鼻錐向四方爆射，不是裝飾，是讓靜態圖有「動態爆發感」的核心視覺語言。 (Speed lines radiating from the nose create kinetic energy in a still image—"speed burst illustration", "motion line art" are high-search-intent terms)

- **Three-layer lighting system（三層光源系統）**: 上方直射陽光 + 地球反射藍白光 + 尾焰自發橘金光，三層光源互補，讓機械感與宇宙感同時成立。 (Sunlight + Earthglow + exhaust self-illumination creates cinematic depth without contradictions—"volumetric lighting rocket art")

- **Anamorphic 2.39:1 widescreen（寬銀幕比例）**: 橫幅超寬畫面強化速度感與宇宙壯闊感，是讓作品有「電影感」而非「插圖感」的技術關鍵。 (Anamorphic ratio mimics blockbuster cinema framing, instantly elevating perceived production value—"cinematic AI art")

- **Circular shockwave ripple（圓形衝擊波漣漪）**: 火箭衝穿雲層後，衝擊波向外擴散——這個細節把「穿越瞬間」的物理感做出來，是讓畫面「說故事」的敘事道具。 (The shockwave ripple marks the exact moment of cloud puncture, adding narrative specificity—"cloud burst effect", "rocket breaking through clouds")
