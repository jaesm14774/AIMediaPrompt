💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **Three-color constraint（三色限制）**: 午夜深藍 × 冰藍生物光 × 琥珀金暖光，嚴格的三色限制讓畫面擁有強烈的視覺語言——冷色=超自然的巨物，暖色=脆弱的人類日常，二者的對立製造張力 (A forced three-color palette creates instant visual identity: cold supernatural blue vs. warm human amber. This constraint is what separates cinematic illustration from generic AI output—SEO keywords: "cinematic color palette", "bioluminescent ocean art")

- **Atmospheric perspective（大氣透視）**: 近處清晰、遠處（巨物頂部）溶入藍霧，讓怪獸的高度「消失」在大氣中，視覺上比實際更加巨大 (Dissolving the creature's upper body into storm haze is the single most powerful trick for conveying impossible scale—the viewer's brain fills in what it cannot see, always making it larger than rendered)

- **"Completely unaware"（完全不知情）**: 船員對背後發生的事毫無察覺，這個「不對稱資訊」製造了觀看者的優勢視角，讓觀眾同時感受震撼與靜默的恐懼 (The crew's unawareness creates dramatic irony — the viewer knows what the characters don't, generating a unique tension between awe and dread. This narrative device is why the image stops scrolling)

- **Water surface camera（水面機位）**: 極低角度仰拍讓巨物的比例感爆炸，海面波紋成為前景，把觀看者「拉進」畫面之中 (Placing the camera at water level creates immediate immersion. The ocean surface becomes a mirror-world that doubles the creature's visual presence through reflection)

- **Bioluminescent ghost-form（生物發光鬼魅形體）**: 半透明的發光質感讓怪獸不只是「大」，而是帶有神性的模糊感——不像怪獸電影的實體感，更像神話中的顯現 (Semi-transparency transforms a creature from monster to deity — suggesting it belongs to a different physical reality than the ship. This Ghibli-esque "presence over physicality" is the key to the mythic quality)

🎨 **Pro Tips / 專家建議**

❌ 直接讓巨物完全清晰可見——比例感反而減弱，失去神秘感 (Rendering the creature with 100% clarity actually reduces its perceived scale and removes the mythic quality — full visibility turns deity into decoration)

✅ 讓巨物頂部溶入大氣霧氣，並在其周圍加上「空氣感」的飛行生物剪影，讓觀看者的視線不斷在各層之間移動，延長觀看時間 (Keep the creature's peak dissolving into atmospheric haze. Add spectral flying silhouettes in the sky layer to create visual depth — multiple layers of discovery reward the viewer who lingers)

✅ **三色限制的最強用法**：只把暖色（琥珀金）保留給船艙燈光，整張圖所有其他元素一律冷色。這個單一暖點讓人類的存在感變得極度珍貴——一點點溫暖，在無盡的冷藍中 (Reserve warm amber exclusively for the vessel's cabin lights. A single warm point in a cold blue world makes human presence feel precious and fragile — the entire emotional story told in one color decision)
