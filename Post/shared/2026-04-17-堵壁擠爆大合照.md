🏮🤣 **堵壁擠爆大合照 | Temple Relief Crowd Jam**

一群 IP 角色硬擠進堵壁中央小畫框，裡面混亂爆炸、外框卻優雅完好，形成「內亂外穩」的超強反差笑點。  
Multiple IP characters are crammed into a tiny temple relief panel while the ornamental frame remains pristine, creating a powerful chaos-vs-serenity contrast.

👇 **Prompt Template / 指令模板**

> 可替換角色名單與擠壓細節；關鍵是「中央畫框極度擁擠，外框完全不受影響」。

```
A hyper-detailed comedic image of [IP角色群組] all crammed into the tiny central
landscape panel of a traditional Southern Fujian temple wall relief (堵壁).
Inside the panel: faces squished, bodies compressed, layered pile-up chaos.
Original painting residents are displaced to corners with annoyed reactions.
Outside the panel: peonies, lattice, trims, and cloud motifs remain perfectly pristine.
Material style: mix of modern 3D character look and Koji pottery ceramic texture.
Detail cues: face prints, hand prints, hairline cracks, tiny hidden gags.
Output: 8K, warm afternoon light, miniature diorama feel, zoom-in rich details.
```

💡 **Pro Tips / 使用建議**

- 角色至少 7 個以上，擁擠感才會成立  
- 記得保留「外框完整無損」，這是整張圖最關鍵的笑點
