💼😪 **職場傳說：開會永遠睡最香 | Meeting-Time Sleep Legend**

越高壓、越嚴肅的職場場景，角色卻睡得越安穩，靠反差打造一眼就懂的荒謬喜劇。  
The higher the workplace pressure, the deeper the character sleeps, creating immediate absurd comedy.

👇 **Prompt Template / 指令模板**

> 可替換角色與職場情境；核心是「主角超放鬆、旁人超震驚」。

```
Chibi hand-drawn illustration with clean thick lines and muted pastel tones.
[IP角色] is completely passed out in blissful deep sleep during a tense workplace moment.
Scene options: asleep across conference table / face-planted on keyboard / in CEO chair.
Add key sleepy signals: drool line, floating "Zzz", soft snore wave lines.
Surrounding humans show frozen shock, disbelief, or panic.
Contrast focus: sleeping serenity vs workplace chaos.
Style: Japanese chibi comic vibe, expressive linework, minimal props.
```

💡 **Pro Tips / 使用建議**

- 旁人反應至少 3 種（驚呆、慌張、無語）更有戲  
- Zzz 與口水線建議保留，辨識度最高
