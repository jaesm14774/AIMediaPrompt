💡 **Key Parameters / 關鍵參數解析**

- **massive radio telescope dish**：用「巨大物」建立尺度，浪漫會變得更史詩、更孤獨也更深情 (A massive object sets scale—romance becomes epic and poignant)

- **warm tungsten glow spilling**：冷色夜景 + 暖色窗光，會自然讓視線回到角色與情感 (Cool night + warm window spill guides focus to the characters)

- **no readable text**：避免 AI 生出奇怪字樣破壞真實感 (Prevents bogus signage text from ruining realism)
