# 朱墨千年：俯瞰一幅活著的文明長卷 | Ink of a Thousand Years: A Living Civilization Scroll Seen from Above

## 為什麼這個 Prompt 特別？ | Why This Prompt Is Special

這個 Prompt 的核心震撼來自於「視角的神性」：我們不是站在地面看一座城市，而是從高天俯視一整段文明——洪荒山水在最頂端，現代摩天樓在最底端，五個時代的建築與景觀如詩句排列，由一條朱砂紅綬帶貫穿始終。而最震撼人心的細節：長卷的底端仍在被書寫，最新的墨跡還在宣紙上暈散——歷史沒有終點，它現在就在發生。[IP角色]站在時間中點，白袍的存在讓觀者立刻有了代入感——「我也在這段歷史的某個位置上」。

The core impact of this Prompt comes from a "divine perspective": we are not standing on the ground looking at a city, but looking down from above at an entire civilization — primordial mountains at the top, modern skyscrapers at the bottom, five eras of architecture arranged like lines of poetry, connected by a single crimson ribbon. The most heart-stopping detail: the bottom edge of the scroll is STILL BEING WRITTEN, the freshest ink still spreading on xuan paper — history has no endpoint, it is happening right now. [IP角色] stands at the temporal midpoint, their white robes immediately creating viewer identification — "I too exist somewhere on this timeline."

## Prompt Template

**THE FIRST THING YOU SEE:** A single blazing crimson ribbon (朱砂紅綬帶) cuts vertically through the center of an infinite scroll of black ink and cream paper — it is the only color in the frame, a wound of red through a monochrome world, and it stops the eye IMMEDIATELY. Then the eye adjusts and realizes: the scroll IS the civilization. The black ink IS the city, the mountain, the thousand years. And the tiny white figure standing at the center — that is [IP角色], conducting it all into existence with a raised brush. THIS IS WHAT THE IMAGE IS.

A breathtaking ultra-vertical aerial panorama image of a **living ink-wash cultural scroll map (水墨文化地圖長卷)**, seen from a bird's-eye divine perspective — as if a god is looking straight down at an unfurling handscroll (手卷) that spans [文明時間跨度: "a thousand years of Chinese civilization, from primordial mountains to glass-tower skyline" / "the entire arc of Japanese culture, from Jomon forests to neon-lit Shinjuku" / "the layered history of [自定義城市或文化]"], its surface alive with ink still drying at the newest edge, the entire history of a civilization laid out as a topographic poem that you can read from top to bottom like a sentence.

**THE SCROLL ITSELF (長卷本體):**

Aged xuan paper (宣紙) with visible fiber texture, yellowed at the ancient top and crisp white at the contemporary bottom — the paper ages as it descends, time written into the material. Lacquered wood rollers with jade end-caps (玉軸) at the ancient top. Red wax seals (朱印) at each era transition.

**[IP角色] AS THE AXIS OF TIME (時間之軸):**

[IP角色] appears in flowing white hanfu robes (白袍廣袖) — a blazing white form against the surrounding ink-grey landscape, impossible to miss. Standing at the 40% mark (the temporal mid-point), spine perfectly vertical, head slightly raised, as if the scroll unfurled BECAUSE they stood here. Robes stained with flying ink from both ancient (warm grey) and modern (cool grey) sections — the robes bear the marks of every era. One hand holds a brush raised at shoulder height, its tip touching the crimson ribbon's midpoint. The other hand open, palm upward, receiving from the sky. From [IP角色]'s feet, ink-wash concentric rings radiate outward like stones dropped in water — the act of STANDING HERE has sent ripples through time. From the brush tip, the crimson thread extends in both directions — upward into antiquity, downward into the contemporary future.

**THE CRIMSON NARRATIVE THREAD (朱砂紅敘事線):**

A single continuous ribbon descending in a gently undulating S-curve, changing character with each era:
- Ancient (top): silk trade route (絲路) with camel caravans
- Classical: a canal (運河) with flat-bottomed boats
- Medieval: a city moat (護城河) surrounding a walled capital
- Modern: a light-rail elevated track (輕軌), stations in ink-wash style

**THE FIVE TEMPORAL ZONES (五個時代帶):**

ZONE 1 — PRIMORDIAL (top 15%): [遠古自然場景] Song Dynasty ink wash mountains, Guo Xi style, enormous peaks in deep charcoal, no human presence except a single hermit pavilion, crane pairs in flight, most yellowed paper with authentic foxing marks.

ZONE 2 — CLASSICAL CIVILIZATION (15-35%): [古典聚落] Complete classical city in precision architectural ink painting (界畫 jiehua style) — city walls, market districts, palace complex at center, terraced rice paddies outside, citizen silhouettes at various scales creating depth.

ZONE 3 — TRANSITION (35-55%, where [IP角色] stands): [交匯時代] The crimson ribbon widens to three times its normal width — maximum cultural tension. Ancient ink-wash architecture stands adjacent to clock towers and telegraph poles, both in same ink-wash palette. Ink spatters from [IP角色]'s footstep-rings are largest here, some pure calligraphy marks, others mid-resolution into architectural forms.

ZONE 4 — CONTEMPORARY (55-80%): [現代城市] Glass towers as ink-wash gradients dissolving into mist at their summits, interchange highways as bold ink gestures, harbor, airport. Modern grid becomes simultaneously a technical map and abstract calligraphic composition. Crimson ribbon is a light-rail track.

ZONE 5 — BECOMING (bottom 20%): [生成中] The scroll is STILL BEING WRITTEN IN REAL TIME. Architecture in progressive states of incompletion: full renderings → partial sketches → pure gestural outlines → freshest wet ink marks at the very base, glistening and spreading. The crimson ribbon EXITS the scroll's edge, breaks the frame boundary, continues into the viewer's space — history is being written NOW.

**MARGINALIA (天地眉批):** Period-appropriate annotations in scroll margins, progressively more modern scripts descending, too small to read but creating the feeling of intergenerational study.

**Technical specs:** Ultra-vertical portrait format 1:4 or 1:5 ratio, palette limited to aged-cream (宣紙色), charcoal black spectrum (10 tonal values), and vermillion-crimson (朱砂紅) for ribbon and seals only, ink tones shift from warm-grey (ancient) to cool-grey (modern), mood: witnessing an entire civilization's creative life compressed into a single vertical axis — humbling, majestic, alive.

## 視覺語言解析 | Visual Language Breakdown

**開場鉤子（THE FIRST THING YOU SEE）**
這段描述特意放在 Prompt 最前面，以「命令式」語法告訴 AI 首屏的視覺優先級順序——朱砂紅綬帶最先抓眼，其次才是整體構圖，最後才看到 [IP角色]。確保生成圖的「首屏衝擊」邏輯正確。

**時間軸身份（[IP角色] AS THE AXIS OF TIME）**
- `spine perfectly vertical` — 製造中軸感，讓角色視覺上「支撐」整個構圖
- `ink-wash concentric rings from footsteps` — 「站在此處撼動了時間」的視覺隱喻，極強記憶點
- `palm upward, receiving from the sky` — 「接收」的姿態比「命令」的姿態更有情感深度

**ZONE 5 的「生成中」語法**
- `EXITS the scroll's edge, breaks the frame boundary` — 大寫 + 多個動詞強調「破框」的重要性
- `history is being written NOW` — 最後三個字全大寫，確保 AI 理解這是整個構圖的情感高點

## 最佳化技巧 | Optimization Tips

1. **極端垂直比例不妥協**：1:4 或 1:5，這個比例讓「時間軸由上至下」的敘事感最完整，方形版本會失去 90% 的敘事力量
2. **ZONE 5 的「生成中」概念是傳播核心**：社群文案建議強調「你在哪個 ZONE？」讓粉絲代入自己在文明時間軸上的位置
3. **白色角色在水墨背景中對比最強**：如果 [IP角色] 本身是深色系（如黑色角色），考慮改為「穿著白袍」的設定，不要讓角色融入背景
4. **朱砂紅的面積控制**：紅色綬帶寬度約佔畫面寬度的 3-5%，面積過大會破壞水墨的素雅感
5. **在各 ZONE 交界加入留白**：指定 `zones separated by atmospheric mist (留白), no hard borders between eras` — 讓時代的轉換感覺自然流動而非生硬切割
