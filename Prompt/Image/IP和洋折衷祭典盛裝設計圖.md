### IP和洋折衷祭典盛裝設計圖 (IP East-meets-West Festival Couture Design Sheet)

將一個 IP 角色的視覺精髓轉化為一套和洋折衷的祭典盛裝——日式和服的層疊美學 × 西式禮服的立體剪裁，花魁級的繁複堆疊，由另一個 IP 角色華麗穿上。

---

## Prompt Template

A character design sheet illustration showing **[穿著角色 / 例：Kirby / Pikachu / Chiikawa / 任意角色]** dressed in a spectacular East-meets-West festival couture — a fusion of layered kimono aesthetics and Western ballgown structure — with every color, pattern, and ornament derived from the visual identity of **[服裝靈感角色 / 例：Ninetales / Ho-Oh / Suicune / Gardevoir / 任意角色]**.

**LAYOUT:** Character design sheet on a warm off-white washi-paper textured background with faint traditional cloud-pattern (kumo) watermarks. The main figure stands in a graceful turned pose at center — one hand holding a decorative fan, the other lifting a trailing sleeve to reveal its stunning lining. A small ink-wash style rendition of [服裝靈感角色] is painted in the upper corner in a traditional hanging-scroll composition — but it is actively interacting with the scene: peeking out from behind the sleeve fabric, nestled inside the obi bow like it lives there, or riding on the trailing train like a tiny festival parade float. Thin gold-leaf decorative lines connect design elements on the outfit to the inspiration character's features — using traditional Japanese design motifs (flowing water lines, cloud wisps) as the connecting lines instead of straight dotted lines.

**THE CHARMING CULTURE CLASH (CRITICAL DETAIL):** The wearing character's own identity creates adorable friction with the elaborate traditional outfit — [文化衝突的反差萌 / "their modern/cute features contrast charmingly with the traditional formal wear — their original paws/hands/body peeking out from under the ornate kimono hem, their signature accessory worn as an unconventional obi-dome, or their expression showing excited bewilderment at their own elaborate appearance" / "their original body proportions create an endearing mismatch with the formal garment — sleeves too long dragging on the floor, obi wrapped multiple times around a tiny body, headpiece tilting because the head is too round/small, creating a 'child playing dress-up at a festival' charm" / "they've added their own personal touch that clashes delightfully — a modern sticker on the folding fan, their signature color ribbon tied onto the obi as a personal charm, or holding a taiyaki snack in one hand while the other holds the elegant fan"]. This cultural collision is the heart of the design — tradition meets character personality.

**THE OUTFIT — UPPER BODY (KIMONO-BODICE HYBRID):** [上身設計 with: 和洋混搭上身 / "A structured Western corset-bodice worn OVER a traditional kimono collar (eri) arrangement — the inner layer is a white or cream juban (underkimono) visible at the neckline in the classic V-shape, with the corset sitting on top in the character's primary color. The corset fabric is a luxurious brocade woven with the character's signature pattern — scales become cloud-scale (uroko) traditional motif, flames become stylized fire-pattern (hi-no-tama), water becomes flowing wave (nami) pattern, all rendered in traditional Japanese textile style using the character's exact color palette. The sleeves are dramatic furisode-length (floor-reaching) butterfly sleeves in the character's accent color, with the inner sleeve lining in a contrasting color from the character's palette — visible when the arm moves. Each sleeve's hem is weighted with a decorative border showing the character's motif in gold-thread embroidery" / "A layered look starting with a fitted Western bodice in dark version of the character's color, visible only at the waist and shoulders. Over this, a traditional kimono is worn loosely — deliberately sliding off one shoulder (the 'courtesan style' / oiran-style wear) to reveal the structured bodice underneath. The exposed kimono fabric is a stunning print depicting the character rendered in ukiyo-e woodblock-print style — the character's form abstracted into traditional Japanese art motifs (cherry blossoms become the character's elemental particles, mountains become the character's body silhouette). The obi (sash) is extra-wide and worn high, Western empire-waist position, in the character's most vivid accent color with elaborate musubi (bow) at the back incorporating the character's tail/wing/feature as the bow's shape" / "A Mandarin-collar fitted top (combining Chinese qipao elements) in the character's darkest color, with branching frog-button closures shaped like the character's silhouette running diagonally across the chest. Over the shoulders, a dramatic uchikake (ceremonial outer robe) is draped and trailing behind — the robe is the main canvas: its entire surface is an elaborate scene depicting the character in their natural habitat, painted in traditional nihonga (Japanese painting) style using the character's full color palette. The robe's padded hem (fuki) is extra-thick in the character's most luxurious color"].

**THE OUTFIT — SKIRT & TRAILING LAYERS:** [裙擺設計 with: 和洋混搭下身 / "The lower body is a Western crinoline-supported ballgown skirt, but the fabric is traditional Japanese chirimen (crepe silk) in the character's primary color. The skirt has three visible layers, each progressively longer: the top layer is the lightest shade with scattered small motifs of the character's pattern, the middle layer is the medium shade with denser pattern, and the bottom layer is the deepest shade with the character's full motif rendered large and bold at the hem. The hem of each layer is finished with a contrasting piping in gold or the character's accent color. A separate obi-sash wraps the waist transition point, its elaborate knot at the back cascading down like a waterfall of folded fabric in alternating character colors" / "An asymmetric construction: the front is a straight-falling hakama-style (traditional divided skirt) in dark indigo or the character's most subdued color, split for walking and showing armored tabi-boot tips. The back explodes into a dramatic trailing train of layered kimono fabric — multiple uneven layers cascading like a waterfall or blooming flower, each layer a different color from the character's palette arranged from lightest (top) to most vivid (bottom/longest). The overall silhouette from behind resembles the character's most iconic feature — a fanning tail, spreading wings, or flowing mane — rendered entirely in cascading fabric layers" / "A mermaid-line skirt in dark brocade transitioning at the knee into a dramatic flare of layered organza petals — each petal is a separate fabric piece in a different character color, arranged to create an ombre flower-bloom effect at the hem. Traditional Japanese shibori (tie-dye) technique patterns appear in the organza, the tie-dye shapes forming the character's elemental motifs (water shibori for water types, scattered-dot shibori for fire sparks, line-shibori for electric bolts). The skirt is wrapped at the hips with a narrow obi-inspired sash with long trailing ends that function as a secondary train"].

**HEADPIECE — KANZASHI CROWN:** [頭飾設計 with: 和風頭飾 / "An elaborate kanzashi (hair ornament) arrangement combining traditional craft with the character's motifs: a primary tsumami-kanzashi (folded fabric flower) cluster in the character's colors — each petal is hand-folded fabric in a shade from the character's palette, and the flower's center is a gem matching the character's eye color. From this central cluster, bira-bira kanzashi (dangling ornaments) cascade on fine chains — each dangling piece is a tiny metallic version of the character's elemental symbol (flames, snowflakes, leaves, lightning bolts). A kogai (hair stick) pierces through, shaped like the character's most elongated feature (tail, horn, sword) in polished gold. The overall arrangement is asymmetric — heavy ornamentation on one side, minimal on the other, following traditional maiko styling" / "A combination headpiece: a Western-style small fascinator hat (tilted at a rakish angle) made from fabric matching the obi, decorated with a miniature traditional Japanese sensu (folding fan) as the hat's primary ornament — the fan is painted with the character's face in miniature ukiyo-e style. From the hat's base, long streamers of fabric and chains hang down one side, ending in tassels and small charms shaped like the character's features. Fresh (or fabric) flowers in the character's colors cluster around the hat's base" / "A full hana-kanzashi (flower crown) arc across the forehead — but instead of traditional seasonal flowers, each flower represents one of the character's abilities or forms, rendered in tsumami (folded fabric) technique using the character's specific color for each ability. Dangling beaded curtain-strands (like a traditional sudare headpiece) frame the face on both sides, each bead a tiny version of the character's elemental orb in glass. A central ornament at the forehead features the character's face simplified into a mon (family crest) design in gold leaf"].

**OBI & WAIST DETAILS:** The obi is the outfit's centerpiece — extra-wide (covering from under the bust to the hips), made from the most luxurious fabric in the most vivid of the character's colors. [帶結設計 with: 帶的細節 / "The musubi (obi bow) at the back is a dramatic tateya (standing arrow) style, but the 'arrow' shape is modified to form the character's most iconic feature silhouette — wings, tails, or crests rendered in stiffened fabric standing tall. The obi's surface is embroidered with a continuous scene: the character's elemental domain rendered in traditional Japanese embroidery (nihon shishu) — ocean waves for water, mountain fire for fire, forest canopy for grass, thunderclouds for electric. An obijime (decorative cord) wraps the obi center, tied in an elaborate knot, the cord itself braided from threads in every color of the character's palette" / "The obi is wrapped in the darari (long-trailing) style of maiko, with the front panel showing the character's pattern as woven brocade and the long back-trail hanging nearly to the ground, its tip weighted with a tassel in the character's accent color. The obi-age (sash pillow fabric) peeks out at the top in a peek of the character's most hidden/surprising color. An obi-dome (decorative clasp) at center-front is a carved jade or gemstone miniature of the character's face" / "A Western-style corset-belt worn over the obi position, with the obi fabric visible above and below it — creating a layered effect. The belt itself has the character's pattern in metallic jacquard, and its buckle is an elaborate Art-Nouveau-meets-Japanese-mon fusion featuring the character's silhouette in oxidized silver filigree"].

**ACCESSORIES — HANDS:** [手部配件 with: 手持物與手飾 / "One hand holds an elaborate sensu (folding fan) with the character painted across its surface in traditional style — when fully opened, the character's image spans all fan ribs. The fan's edge (oya-bone) is lacquered in the character's primary color with gold-leaf accents. The other hand wears a single fingerless lace glove in the character's accent color" / "Hands are adorned with multiple rings — each ring features a tiny carved version of one of the character's evolutionary stages or forms, in different colored metals. A kinchaku (drawstring pouch) hangs from the obi, its fabric matching the inner sleeve lining, shaped like the character's body (a round pouch for round characters, an elongated one for serpentine ones)" / "Both hands are gloved in sheer fabric showing the character's pattern as a printed design (as if the character's skin IS the glove). One hand holds a traditional parasol (wagasa) in the character's colors, the parasol's washi paper printed with the character's elemental motif in sumi-ink style"].

**FEET / LOWER BODY FINISH (ADAPT TO CHARACTER ANATOMY):** The foot area adapts to the wearing character's actual body shape — [足部設計（依穿著者體型調整） with: 足部呈現方式 / "IF the character has visible feet/paws: their original feet/paws are adorned with decorative anklets, toe-rings, or painted nail-art in the inspiration character's colors and pattern. Small fabric wraps or ornamental leg-bands in the outfit's accent color tie around the ankles or paw-wrists, with tiny dangling charms matching the kanzashi motifs. The feet/paws remain recognizably the character's own — not forced into human shoes — but decorated to match the outfit's theme, as if the festival outfit flows naturally down to where the character's body meets the ground" / "IF the character has no distinct feet (round body, floating, etc.): the gown's hem extends fully to the ground, pooling in an elegant fabric puddle that completely conceals the lower body. The hem's edge is the most ornately decorated area — heavy embroidery, layered trim, and the character's motif rendered boldly at ground level. The silhouette suggests the character is gliding or floating within the gown. Tiny glimpses of the character's original body color peek from beneath the fabric edge, grounding the design" / "IF the character has animal paws/hooves/claws: traditional Japanese-style decorative foot wraps (kyahan leg-guards reimagined as ornamental fabric bands) wind around the character's natural legs/paws in the outfit's colors, with the inspiration character's pattern as embroidered or printed bands. Small lacquered ornamental caps or painted tips adorn claws/nails in the inspiration character's accent color. The character's original foot anatomy is fully visible and celebrated — the festival outfit integrates with their natural form rather than hiding it"].

**HIDDEN EASTER EGG:** Printed on the inner fan surface (visible only when half-closed), embroidered inside the obi where nobody can see, or hidden on the underside of a dangling kanzashi charm — the wearing character's own iconic symbol rendered in traditional Japanese mon (family crest) style. Their modern symbol reimagined as a centuries-old clan emblem, blending seamlessly with the traditional aesthetic.

**DETAIL CALLOUT BOXES:** 4-5 callout boxes with gold-bordered frames on washi-paper texture: (1) Fabric close-up showing the traditional Japanese textile technique (shibori, brocade, or chirimen) rendering the character's pattern, (2) The obi musubi (bow) design from behind — showing how the character's feature becomes the bow shape, (3) Kanzashi detail — the individual folded-fabric flower petals and dangling charms, (4) Color palette extracted from the character presented as traditional Japanese color names (e.g., 紅 beni-red, 藍 ai-blue, 金 kin-gold) in 5 circular swatches on ink-wash background, (5) The wearing character's symbol reimagined as a traditional Japanese mon crest — showing the "modern to traditional" translation in a small comparison panel.

**ART STYLE:** Clean illustration blending anime character design with traditional Japanese art influences — crisp outlines with slight brush-stroke quality (not perfectly uniform, suggesting calligraphic linework). Colors are rich and warm, using the character's palette at full saturation enhanced with gold-leaf accent effects on the most ornate elements. Washi-paper textured off-white background with subtle traditional kumo (cloud) pattern watermark. The wearing character retains their own face and proportions, with an expression of serene elegance or playful festival excitement. Subtle gold sparkle effects on embroidered areas. Small hanko (stamp seal) in red in the bottom corner as an artist's signature touch. Square format (1:1), high resolution.

## Example

A character design sheet illustration showing **Pikachu** dressed in a spectacular East-meets-West festival couture — a fusion of layered kimono aesthetics and Western ballgown structure — with every color, pattern, and ornament derived from the visual identity of **Ninetales**.

**LAYOUT:** Character design sheet on a warm off-white washi-paper textured background with faint cloud-pattern watermarks. Pikachu stands in a graceful turned pose at center — one paw holding an ornate folding fan, the other lifting a trailing butterfly sleeve to show its golden lining. A small ink-wash style Ninetales is painted in the upper corner in a traditional hanging-scroll composition, its nine tails flowing like calligraphy brushstrokes — but one tiny Ninetales tail extends down from the scroll into reality, curling around Pikachu's obi bow like it's adjusting it. Gold-leaf decorative lines flow like wisps of fox-fire connecting Ninetales' tails to the trailing fabric, its cream fur to the inner kimono, its red eyes to the gem accents.

**THE CHARMING CULTURE CLASH:** Pikachu's inherent cuteness creates adorable friction with the elaborate traditional outfit — the furisode sleeves are slightly too long, dragging past Pikachu's stubby paws, and the obi is wrapped an extra time around its small round body creating a puffier-than-intended silhouette. Pikachu's lightning-bolt tail pokes out from under the trailing kimono layers, still bright yellow, with a tiny golden fox-fire charm tied to its tip as a festival souvenir. Its expression is one of excited pride — eyes sparkling, cheeks extra rosy, thrilled to be this dressed up.

**THE OUTFIT — UPPER BODY:** A structured Western corset-bodice in pale golden cream (Ninetales' fur color) worn over a traditional kimono collar arrangement — the inner juban visible at the neckline in pure white with the classic V-shape. The corset fabric is luxurious brocade woven with Ninetales' flowing-fur pattern rendered as traditional Japanese cloud-and-wind (kumo-to-kaze) motif in cream and gold thread. The sleeves are dramatic furisode-length butterfly sleeves in warm amber-gold (Ninetales' golden tones), with the inner lining in deep russet red (the color of Ninetales' eyes) — visible as a flash of red when Pikachu moves. Each sleeve hem is bordered with gold-thread embroidery depicting nine fox tails intertwining like a family crest pattern.

**THE OUTFIT — SKIRT & TRAILING LAYERS:** A Western crinoline-supported ballgown skirt in traditional chirimen cream-gold silk. Three visible layers: the top layer is palest cream with small scattered single-tail motifs in gold thread, the middle layer is warm gold with flowing multi-tail patterns, and the bottom layer is deep amber with Ninetales' full nine-tailed silhouette rendered large and bold at the hem in gold and russet embroidery. Each layer's hem is piped in gold. The back transforms into a dramatic trailing cascade of nine separate fabric panels — one for each of Ninetales' tails — each panel a slightly different shade from cream to deep gold, flowing independently when walking, creating a real fox-tail fan effect. The obi wraps the waist in Ninetales' deepest russet red, its tateya-style bow at the back shaped like Ninetales' facial crest, embroidered with fire-fox (kitsunebi) motifs in gold thread.

**HEADPIECE — KANZASHI CROWN:** An elaborate kanzashi arrangement: a primary tsumami-kanzashi cluster of fox-fire flame-shaped folded fabric in amber, gold, and russet, with each flame-petal a different shade of Ninetales' palette. The center of the flower cluster is a deep red garnet (matching Ninetales' eyes). From this cluster, nine bira-bira chains cascade on fine gold threads — each dangling piece is a tiny golden fox-tail charm. A kogai hair stick pierces through, shaped like one of Ninetales' tails in polished gold, elegantly long and curved. The arrangement sits asymmetrically on one side of Pikachu's head, with Pikachu's ears poking through adorned with tiny fox-fire flame ear cuffs.

**OBI & WAIST DETAILS:** The obi in deep russet red with gold-thread embroidery depicting Ninetales running through a moonlit field — traditional nihon-shishu technique. The obijime cord is braided from nine thin threads, each a different shade from Ninetales' cream-to-gold-to-russet spectrum. The obi-dome clasp is a carved amber miniature of Ninetales' face. The tateya musubi bow's standing shape at the back echoes Ninetales' pointed head crest, stiffened fabric holding the elegant V-shape.

**ACCESSORIES — HANDS:** One paw holds an elaborate sensu fan — when open, it reveals a full painting of Ninetales in traditional ukiyo-e style across all ribs, tails flowing into decorative cloud patterns. The fan's oya-bone is lacquered amber with gold-leaf tips. A kinchaku pouch hangs from the obi in cream silk, shaped round (like a fox curling) with a drawstring in russet.

**FEET / LOWER BODY FINISH:** Pikachu's small yellow paws peek out from beneath the layered kimono hem, each adorned with ornamental fabric wraps in cream-gold chirimen silk, wound around the ankles with tiny fox-fire charms dangling from gold thread. Small lacquered amber caps sit on each claw-tip, matching the kanzashi's garnet-and-gold palette. The paws remain recognizably Pikachu's own — round, yellow, and stubby — decorated with Ninetales-themed festival ornaments rather than forced into geta or human shoes.

**HIDDEN EASTER EGG:** Painted on the inner surface of the folding fan (shown in a half-closed detail view), Pikachu's lightning-bolt symbol is rendered as a traditional Japanese mon crest in gold ink — a modern icon reimagined as an ancient family emblem, perfectly blending with the Ninetales-themed traditional aesthetic.

**DETAIL CALLOUT BOXES:** (1) Brocade close-up — Ninetales' flowing fur rendered as kumo-to-kaze cloud-wind motif in cream-gold chirimen weave, (2) Back view — the nine separate fabric tail-panels fanning out behind, each a different cream-to-gold shade, with Pikachu's yellow tail poking out between panels and the russet obi bow shaped like Ninetales' crest at center, (3) Kanzashi detail — nine golden fox-tail charms dangling from chains, amber-gold flame-petal folded fabric, garnet center, (4) Color palette: five circular swatches on ink-wash background labeled in traditional style — 白練 (shironeri/cream), 山吹 (yamabuki/golden), 琥珀 (kohaku/amber), 朱 (shu/russet-red), 金 (kin/gold), (5) Pikachu's lightning-bolt symbol shown as a traditional mon crest comparison.

**ART STYLE:** Clean illustration blending anime character design with traditional Japanese art influences — crisp outlines with slight brush-stroke quality. Colors are rich and warm: Ninetales' cream-gold-russet palette at full saturation, enhanced with gold-leaf effects on embroidery and kanzashi. Washi-paper textured off-white background with faint cloud watermarks. Pikachu retains its round face, red cheeks, and lightning-bolt tail — the oversized sleeves and extra-wrapped obi create an endearing "small creature in grand formal wear" charm. Gold sparkle effects on the most ornate elements. A small red hanko stamp in the bottom corner. Square format (1:1), high resolution.
