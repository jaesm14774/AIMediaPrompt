### IP暗黑哥德戰裝設計圖 (IP Dark Gothic Battle-Dress Design Sheet)

將一個 IP 角色的視覺精髓轉化為一套暗黑哥德風的華麗戰鬥禮服——鎧甲與裙擺交融、暗色調中綻放角色招牌色的妖豔光芒，由另一個 IP 角色霸氣穿上。

---

## Prompt Template

A character design sheet illustration showing **[穿著角色 / 例：Kirby / Pikachu / Chiikawa / 任意角色]** clad in a magnificent dark gothic battle-dress — a fusion of ornamental armor and haute couture gown — with every element drawn from the visual identity of **[服裝靈感角色 / 例：Gengar / Darkrai / Mewtwo / Gardevoir / 任意角色]**.

**LAYOUT:** Character design sheet on a dark textured parchment background with faint arcane circle watermarks. The main figure stands in a powerful wide-stance pose at center, one hand raised as if channeling energy, gown and cape swept by invisible wind. A small spectral-glow rendition of [服裝靈感角色] hovers ominously in the upper-left corner, semi-transparent like a ghostly design spirit — it is actively doing something menacing yet endearing: wrapping shadow tendrils around the wearing character protectively, or sitting on the weapon's hilt like a tiny overlord, or whispering dark fashion advice into the wearing character's ear. Thin glowing lines (in the inspiration character's energy color) trace connections between the character's features and corresponding armor/dress elements — like magical blueprint annotations.

**THE GAP MOE — CUTE BENEATH THE DARK (CRITICAL DETAIL):** Despite the intimidating gothic armor, the wearing character's inherent cuteness breaks through in subtle ways — [反差萌細節 / "their original round/soft body shape creates an adorably non-threatening silhouette inside the fierce armor — like a marshmallow in a tank, the armor's fierce angles contrasting with the wearer's naturally cute proportions" / "their signature cute expression persists despite the dark outfit — determined but still wide-eyed, trying their best to look menacing but still radiating inherent cuteness, one cheek puffed in concentration" / "a tiny original-colored accessory from their own identity is worn as a 'good luck charm' clipped to the armor's belt — their favorite item, a small plush of themselves, or a friendship bracelet in their own color, contrasting sweetly against the dark metal"]. This gap between dark exterior and cute core is the key emotional hook that makes the design irresistible.

**THE BATTLE-DRESS — ARMORED BODICE:** [上身鎧甲設計 with: 靈感角色特徵轉化 / "A contoured breastplate shaped to follow the wearing character's torso, forged in darkened metallic version of the inspiration character's primary color — deep gunmetal blue, blackened purple, tarnished gold. The surface is etched with fine-line engravings depicting the character's signature pattern (scales, fur texture, energy veins, circuit-like lines) filled with faintly glowing enamel in the character's accent color. Pauldrons (shoulder armor) rise dramatically, shaped like abstracted versions of the character's most threatening feature — jaws become fanged shoulder guards, wings become bladed sweep-back pauldrons, horns become curved spike-crowned shoulderpieces. A gorget (neck guard) wraps the throat, its edge cut to silhouette the character's head shape" / "A corset-style bodice in dark leather-textured fabric overlaid with articulated armor plates along the ribs and spine — each plate a different depth of the character's color, darkened and desaturated to gothic tones. The centerline features a vertical row of gem-like rivets that glow in the character's energy color (ghostly purple, ominous red, electric teal). Epaulettes hang from chain-mail shoulders — tattered silk pennants in the character's accent color, edges burnt or frayed to suggest battle history. A high collar flares behind the neck like a cobra's hood or a vampire's cape collar, lined in contrasting satin matching the character's most hidden color (the inside of their mouth, the underside of their wings, etc.)" / "A hybrid structure: the front is polished dark armor with the character's facial markings replicated as raised relief on the chestplate — eyes become gem-inset focal points, mouth becomes a ventilation grill design, distinctive markings become gold-inlaid tracery. The sides transition into dark brocade fabric with the character's pattern woven in tone-on-tone (dark on dark, visible only at certain angles). The back is entirely fabric — a dramatic lace-up corset back with ribbons in the character's accent color, showing that beneath the warrior exterior is still elegance"].

**THE BATTLE-DRESS — SKIRT & BATTLE-TRAIN:** [裙擺與戰裙設計 with: 裙型 / "An asymmetric high-low skirt: short in front revealing armored thigh-high boots, cascading down to a dramatic full-length battle-train in the back. The front panels are rigid armor skirt-plates (like a Roman gladiator's pteruges) in darkened metal, each plate engraved with a symbol from the character's lore. The back train is flowing dark fabric — velvet or heavy silk — in the character's deepest color, with the character's elemental motif emerging as slash-cut negative space revealing a glowing underlayer in the character's energy color. The train's edge is ragged and battle-worn, as if dragged through otherworldly terrain — wisps of the character's elemental energy (shadow tendrils, frost crystals, ember sparks) cling to the torn edges" / "A layered cage-crinoline skirt where the outer framework is visible as thin dark metal ribs (like a gothic birdcage), and within the cage, layers of tattered dark tulle in the character's color create volume. The metal ribs are shaped to trace the character's silhouette — if viewed from directly above, the rib pattern would form the character's face or body outline. Between the tulle layers, small orbs of the character's energy color float like trapped will-o'-wisps. The very bottom hem trails dark mist or shadow that pools on the ground" / "A straight column skirt slashed on one side to the thigh for mobility, the slash held together by crossing chains in tarnished silver. The fabric is heavy dark damask woven with the character's pattern in matte-on-sheen contrast. An overskirt of dark translucent material drapes from the back waist, functioning as both skirt extension and cloak, pinned at the hips with brooches shaped like the character's claws or teeth. The overskirt's lining is shocking — bright, vivid, the character's most intense color — visible only when it moves, like a flash of hidden power"].

**HEADPIECE — DARK CROWN:** [暗黑頭冠設計 with: 頭冠類型 / "A gothic crown combining the character's head features with dark royalty aesthetics — twisted blackened metal forming spires that echo the character's horns, ears, or crest. Each spire tip holds a rough-cut gemstone in the character's eye color, unpolished and raw-looking. A dark veil of fine chain-mail drapes from the crown's back, partially obscuring the face from the sides — referencing the character's mysterious or threatening nature. The crown's base band has the character's signature markings engraved and filled with glowing enamel" / "A wide-brimmed dark witch/sorcerer hat with structural elements from the character — the brim's edge cut to match the character's most jagged feature (wing edges, tail fins, claw shapes), the crown wrapped in dark fabric with the character's pattern as embossed texture. A dramatic hat-pin through the crown features a miniature version of the character's weapon or signature item as the pin-head ornament. Dark ribbons or chains hang from the brim's back" / "A half-face mask-and-tiara combination: the upper half of the face is framed by a dark metallic mask that traces the character's facial markings — their eye shapes become the mask's eye openings, their facial lines become raised metal ridges. Above the mask, a minimal crown of sharp spikes in asymmetric arrangement references the character's silhouette. One side of the mask trails dark feathers, thorns, or crystalline spikes matching the character's texture"].

**GAUNTLETS & DARK JEWELRY:** Armored gauntlets extending from fingertip to elbow — the fingers are articulated dark metal claws (referencing the character's claws/paws), the forearm plates bear the character's pattern in etched or embossed detail. A heavy pendant necklace: a thick dark chain supporting a large central medallion that contains the character's silhouette in cut-out negative space, backed by a glowing gem. Armored belt with hanging chains and charm-like attachments — each charm is a tiny dark-metal rendition of one of the character's signature items or features. Ear accessories: dark metal cuffs or chains that drape from the wearing character's ears, with small dangling pendants shaped like the character's elemental symbol.

**FEET / LOWER ARMOR (ADAPT TO CHARACTER ANATOMY):** The lower body armor adapts to the wearing character's actual body shape — [足部鎧甲設計（依穿著者體型調整） with: 足部呈現方式 / "IF the character has visible feet/paws: dark ornamental armor plates wrap around the character's natural feet/paws as articulated claw-guards — each claw/nail tip is capped with a darkened metallic version of the inspiration character's accent color, the ankle area is wrapped with chain-linked greave-bands bearing the character's etched pattern. The feet remain the character's own shape, but armored and decorated to match the dark gothic theme" / "IF the character has no distinct feet (round body, floating, etc.): the battle-skirt's hem extends to the ground in a heavy armored curtain, the lowest edge lined with dark metal plates and the character's energy color glowing from beneath. Dark mist or shadow pools around the base, completely concealing the lower body and creating the impression that the character is an ominous floating entity within the armor — the gown becomes the body" / "IF the character has animal paws/hooves/claws: armored greave-plates shaped to follow the character's natural leg anatomy wrap around each limb — the plates are dark metal etched with the inspiration character's pattern, joints articulated with chain-mail for movement. Claw tips are reinforced with darkened metal caps that glow faintly with the character's energy color. The armor integrates with the character's original form, enhancing it rather than replacing it with human anatomy"].

**WEAPON/ACCESSORY PROP:** Held in one hand or floating nearby — a weapon or scepter that crystallizes the character's power into a physical object: [武器/權杖 with: 武器設計 / "A dark ornamental scepter topped with a caged gem pulsing with the character's energy color, the cage shaped like the character's open mouth or grasping claws" / "A gothic war-fan that unfolds to reveal the character's silhouette in negative-cut metalwork, each fan-rib a blade edge" / "A floating grimoire (spell book) with the character's face embossed on the dark leather cover, pages fluttering open to show glowing glyphs in the character's color"].

**HIDDEN EASTER EGG:** Engraved on the inside of the armor's chestplate (shown in a tiny peeled-back detail view), or stamped on the weapon's base, is the wearing character's own iconic symbol — their personal mark hidden beneath the dark exterior, a secret reminder of who they truly are under all the gothic grandeur.

**DETAIL CALLOUT BOXES:** 4-5 callout boxes with gothic-styled frames: (1) Armor engraving close-up showing the character's pattern translated to metalwork, (2) The glowing energy color — how it manifests through gem, enamel, and underlayer, (3) Dark color palette — 5 swatches: the character's original colors each shifted 2-3 shades darker plus one vivid glow accent, (4) Back-view sketch showing the battle-train and cape silhouette, (5) A tiny "expression sheet" in the corner — 3 mini faces of the wearing character in the dark outfit showing different expressions: fierce battle cry, confused blush, and sleepy head-nod (still in full armor), emphasizing the gap moe.

**ART STYLE:** Dark fantasy illustration with clean but slightly more textured linework than typical kawaii — lines vary slightly in weight for dramatic effect. Rich dark color palette: the inspiration character's colors desaturated and deepened 2-3 stops, with one vivid glowing accent color for contrast. Dark parchment-textured background with faint magical circle watermarks. The wearing character retains their own face and proportions but their expression is fierce, regal, or mysteriously confident. Dramatic lighting from below or from the glowing elements, casting upward shadows. Metallic sheen effects on armor surfaces. Square format (1:1), high resolution.

## Example

A character design sheet illustration showing **Kirby** clad in a magnificent dark gothic battle-dress — a fusion of ornamental armor and haute couture gown — with every element drawn from the visual identity of **Gengar**.

**LAYOUT:** Character design sheet on a dark purple-grey textured parchment background with faint arcane circle watermarks. Kirby stands in a powerful wide-stance pose at center, one stubby arm raised as if channeling shadow energy, a dark cape swept by invisible wind. A small spectral-glow Gengar hovers in the upper-left corner, semi-transparent and grinning — it wraps shadow tendrils around Kirby protectively while giving a smug thumbs-up, like a proud dark mentor. Thin glowing magenta-purple lines trace connections between Gengar's features and the armor — Gengar's spiky ears to the pauldrons, Gengar's grin to the chestplate motif, Gengar's shadow body to the dark train.

**THE GAP MOE:** Despite the menacing gothic armor, Kirby's inherent roundness and cuteness breaks through — its small pink body creates an adorably round silhouette inside the angular dark armor, like a pink marshmallow in a gothic fortress. Kirby's face shows a determined "trying to be scary" expression — furrowed brows but still round cheeks, one stubby arm raised in a power pose that's more cute than threatening. A tiny yellow Warp Star charm is clipped to the armor's belt as a good-luck charm — Kirby's own identity shining in bright yellow against the dark purple metal.

**THE BATTLE-DRESS — ARMORED BODICE:** A contoured breastplate shaped to follow Kirby's round torso, forged in deep blackened purple with a faint metallic sheen. The surface is etched with fine-line engravings depicting Gengar's ghostly aura — swirling phantom patterns filled with faintly glowing magenta enamel. Pauldrons rise dramatically from each shoulder, shaped like Gengar's pointed ears — jagged, menacing spike-crowned shoulderpieces in dark purple metal tipped with glowing red gems (Gengar's eye color). A gorget wraps Kirby's neck area, its edge cut to silhouette Gengar's signature wide grin — the teeth pattern rendered as zigzag metal cutwork.

**THE BATTLE-DRESS — SKIRT & BATTLE-TRAIN:** An asymmetric high-low skirt: short in front revealing armored boot-greaves, cascading to a dramatic full-length battle-train in the back. The front panels are rigid armor skirt-plates in darkened purple metal, each engraved with a different ghost-type symbol. The back train is heavy dark purple velvet with Gengar's face emerging as slash-cut negative space — the eyes and grin cut through the fabric revealing a glowing magenta underlayer beneath. The train's ragged edges trail wisps of dark shadow-mist that pools on the ground, as if Gengar's shadow body is literally becoming the dress's hem.

**HEADPIECE — DARK CROWN:** A gothic crown of twisted blackened purple metal forming three central spires — echoing Gengar's head spikes and pointed ears. Each spire tip holds a rough-cut deep red garnet (Gengar's eye color). A dark veil of fine chain-mail drapes from the crown's back, partially obscuring Kirby's face from the sides, adding menace. The crown's base band has Gengar's grin pattern engraved and filled with glowing magenta enamel — the teeth seem to smile around Kirby's head.

**GAUNTLETS & DARK JEWELRY:** Armored gauntlets on Kirby's stubby arms — the fingertips are articulated dark metal claws (Gengar's sharp claws), the forearm plates bear Gengar's ghostly swirl pattern etched in purple-on-black. A heavy pendant: thick dark chain with a medallion containing Gengar's silhouette in cut-out negative space, backed by a pulsing magenta gem. Belt chains dangle small dark-metal charms — a tiny Gengar face, a shadow ball, a crescent moon.

**FEET / LOWER ARMOR:** Kirby has no distinct legs — the battle-skirt's hem extends to the ground in a heavy armored curtain, the lowest edge lined with dark purple metal plates and magenta energy glowing from beneath. Dark shadow-mist pools around the base where Kirby's round body meets the floor, completely concealing the lower body and creating the ominous impression that Kirby is a floating dark entity within the gothic armor — the gown becomes the body, shadow tendrils creeping outward from under the hem.

**WEAPON/ACCESSORY PROP:** A dark ornamental scepter topped with a cage shaped like Gengar's open mouth, within it a floating orb of swirling purple-magenta energy (Shadow Ball). Kirby holds it in one raised arm, shadow energy wisping from the orb.

**HIDDEN EASTER EGG:** Engraved on the inner face of the chestplate (shown in a tiny peeled-back detail callout), a small Kirby star symbol is stamped in gold — Kirby's secret mark hidden beneath the Gengar-themed dark armor.

**DETAIL CALLOUT BOXES:** (1) Armor engraving close-up — Gengar's phantom swirl pattern in etched purple metal with magenta enamel fill, (2) The glowing magenta energy — how it bleeds through slash-cuts in the train and gem settings, (3) Dark palette — 5 swatches: deep purple, blackened violet, dark grey, blood red (eyes), vivid magenta (energy glow), (4) Back-view sketch showing the shadow-trailing battle-train with Gengar's face negative-space cutout, (5) Mini expression sheet — 3 tiny Kirby faces in the dark crown: fierce battle cry (cute but trying), confused blush, and sleepy nod (still in full armor).

**ART STYLE:** Dark fantasy illustration with clean but dramatic linework. Rich dark purple palette — Gengar's purple desaturated and deepened, with vivid magenta as the single glowing accent. Dark parchment background with faint ghostly circle watermarks. Kirby retains its round body and simple face — the gap between its inherent pink roundness and the fierce dark armor is the design's core charm. Dramatic underlighting from the glowing elements casting upward purple shadows. Metallic sheen on all armor surfaces. Square format (1:1), high resolution.
