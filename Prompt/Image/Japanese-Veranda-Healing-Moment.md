### Japanese Veranda Healing Moment (日式緣側治癒時光)

角色在日式傳統緣側與季節花卉共舞的治癒系場景，Gouache 風格捕捉日常小確幸。

---

## Prompt Template

A heartwarming gouache-style illustration of [主角: Raichu / Totoro / Eevee / Kirby] joyfully [動作: dancing while holding flowers / playing with petals / stretching happily] on a traditional Japanese engawa (veranda), with a smaller companion [配角: Pichu watching curiously from the windowsill / baby version peeking through the sliding door]. The scene is framed against a lush garden backdrop filled with [季節花卉: purple hydrangeas for summer / pink cherry blossoms for spring / red maple leaves for autumn] in full bloom. Three-layered depth composition: foreground character on sunlit stone path, middle ground wooden veranda with warm cream-colored traditional architecture, background dense seasonal foliage with soft bokeh. Rendered in authentic gouache painting technique with visible brushstrokes, reminiscent of Studio Ghibli background art. Natural diffused afternoon sunlight (4500K), subtle rim lighting on flower petals. Character expression radiates pure joy with closed happy eyes. Square composition (1:1), 4K resolution, detailed illustration.
