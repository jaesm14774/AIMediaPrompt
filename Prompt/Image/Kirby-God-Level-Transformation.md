## Examples

1. **【微縮世界】卡比 x 哆啦A夢**: (Hyper-realistic tilt-shift macro photography of Kirby transformed after inhaling Doraemon). Kirby maintains its signature round pink body and determined expressive eyes, now seamlessly integrated with a bright yellow Take-copter. The scene is set in a giant mountain of Dorayaki, featuring Tyndall effect sunlight filtering through a soft afternoon window. Kirby's skin exhibits realistic subsurface scattering (SSS) for a soft yet tangible pink texture. 35mm f/1.8 shallow depth of field. Masterpiece, 8K resolution, ray tracing, ultra-detailed textures.

2. **【暗黑歌德】卡比 x 蝙蝠俠**: (Gothic Chiaroscuro oil painting of Kirby transformed after inhaling Batman). Kirby maintains its signature round pink body and determined expressive eyes, now seamlessly integrated with a tattered black Bat-cape and tiny pointed ears. The scene is set in a gothic cathedral gargoyle at night, featuring heavy rain and a giant pink Bat-Signal projected into the clouds. Kirby's skin exhibits realistic subsurface scattering (SSS). Dramatic rim lighting, Rembrandt style. Masterpiece, 8K, heavy oil paint texture.

3. **【浮世繪】卡比 x 炭治郎**: (Traditional Japanese Ukiyo-e woodblock print of Kirby transformed after inhaling Tanjiro). Kirby maintains its signature round pink body, now seamlessly integrated with a green-and-black checkered Haori and a small Nichirin sword. The scene is set in a vortex of water and cherry blossoms, featuring dynamic ink wash waves and Mount Fuji silhouette. Kirby's skin exhibits realistic texture. Bold outlines, vibrant flat colors, paper texture. Masterpiece, 8K.

4. **【科幻工業】卡比 x 鋼鐵人**: (Industrial Cinematic shot of Kirby transformed after inhaling Iron Man). Masterpiece, 8K, Octane Render style.
