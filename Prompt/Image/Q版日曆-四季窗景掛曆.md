## Example

A usable monthly wall calendar illustration, portrait orientation, 2:3 aspect ratio (resembling a standard 8K-sized printed wall calendar, approximately 380mm x 530mm). The entire canvas is filled edge-to-edge with a full-bleed seasonal landscape illustration — no white space, no margins, the scenery covers 100% of the background: autumn — a wide forest scene carpeted entirely with golden and crimson fallen leaves, warm amber light filtering through a dense maple canopy overhead, a rustic wooden bench and winding path visible among the trees, the sky peeking through in soft orange tones. Overlaid on the lower 45% of this landscape: a semi-transparent frosted white panel (slightly rounded corners, soft drop shadow) containing the functional calendar grid for 2026 October. The grid displays the year and month name at the top, a full 7-column weekly header (SUN through SAT) with all date numbers correctly arranged for October 2026, printed in a clean soft-weight font, dark grey on the frosted white panel, each cell clearly separated by faint gridlines. November's mini preview is shown beside the month label. Tucked into the landscape area (above the calendar panel), small and unobtrusive (roughly 10-15% of the frame): a chibi tiny Totoro sitting on the wooden bench holding a falling maple leaf, blending into the scenery as a charming accent. A spiral binding or two metal ring holes visible at the top edge of the entire piece, reinforcing the wall-calendar look. Soft illustration style with painterly textures for the landscape and clean typography for the calendar, gentle warm color harmony, 4K resolution, ultra detailed.
