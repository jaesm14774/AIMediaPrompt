## Example

A functional and beautiful monthly calendar illustration, landscape orientation, 16:9 wide aspect ratio resembling a desktop wallpaper calendar. The calendar displays 2026 March with the year and month label displayed prominently in the upper-left corner on a small folded page-tab shape in muted sage green. The calendar occupies the dominant center-to-left area of the composition (approximately 70-75% of the frame): a clean monthly grid layout with 7 columns labeled SUN, MON, TUE, WED, THU, FRI, SAT across the top in a soft rounded sans-serif font, and rows of date numbers correctly arranged for March 2026, clearly legible in warm chocolate-brown ink, each date cell evenly spaced in a structured grid. Below the main month, a smaller April preview grid is shown. 3 Mar — delicate cherry blossom petals drifting across the date grid, a few petals resting on individual date cells, soft pink sakura branch extending from the upper-right corner behind the grid. In the right side of the composition, taking up roughly 20-25% of the frame: a small chibi pink round Kirby sitting peacefully beside the calendar grid, leaning slightly against it, with a content expression and rosy cheeks. A tiny teacup and a few extra fallen petals around Kirby. Background: clean soft cream-white with subtle warm light from the left side. Overall aesthetic: gentle watercolor-meets-digital illustration, soft pastel tones, the calendar grid is crisp and readable while the decorative elements have a loose painterly quality. 4K resolution, ultra detailed, warm and serene atmosphere.
