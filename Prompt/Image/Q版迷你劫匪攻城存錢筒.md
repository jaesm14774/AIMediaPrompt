### Q版迷你劫匪攻城存錢筒 (Tiny Bandits vs Giant Piggy Bank)

一群微型Q版劫匪對巨大存錢筒發動史詩級攻城戰，用牙籤搭梯子、橡皮筋彈弓射硬幣、迴紋針鉤爪攀爬，場面像《魔戒》攻城但角色只有硬幣大小。

## Prompt Template

A hyper-detailed kawaii illustration with **epic cinematic composition** depicting a team of **[微型劫匪 with: Q版動物小偷 / "twelve tiny chibi mice in black ski masks and striped shirts, each only the size of a coin, with different colored bandanas (red leader, blue demolition, green scout)" / "a squad of miniature chibi bugs — ladybug commander, ant soldiers, beetle heavy units — all wearing tiny bandit eye masks" / "micro-sized chibi cats in ninja outfits with coin-pouch utility belts"]** launching a full-scale siege assault on a **[目標 with: 巨大存錢筒 / "massive ceramic piggy bank the size of a fortress, glossy pink surface reflecting the tiny attackers, coin slot on top like a castle gate" / "towering gumball machine filled with golden coins instead of gumballs, glass dome like a crystal palace" / "enormous treasure chest overflowing with chocolate gold coins, wooden exterior like a medieval castle wall"]**.

**Epic Battle Layout (Macro Photography Perspective):** The scene is shot from a **low dramatic angle** at ground level, making the piggy bank loom like a mountain fortress. **Siege Division 1 (Ground Assault):** Four bandits push a battering ram made from a **wooden chopstick** mounted on **bottle cap wheels**, charging toward the piggy bank's belly. **Siege Division 2 (Climbing Squad):** Three bandits scale the piggy bank's curved surface using **paperclip grappling hooks** and **dental floss** rope — one has slipped and dangles upside-down by one foot, bandana falling off. **Siege Division 3 (Air Support):** Two bandits ride a **paper airplane** swooping toward the coin slot on top, one pilot and one holding a tiny **rubber band slingshot** loaded with a real coin that's bigger than the bandit. **Siege Division 4 (Tunnel Team):** Behind the piggy bank, two bandits dig under using **plastic spork** shovels, a small pile of excavated cookie crumbs beside them.

**Expressions & Drama:** The red-bandana leader stands on a **thimble watchtower** pointing forward dramatically with a toothpick sword, mouth open in a tiny battle cry. The dangling climber has spiral panic eyes. The paper airplane pilot grins with confident squinted eyes while the co-pilot looks terrified clutching the coin. One digger is exhausted, lying flat with tongue out.

**Art Style:** Kawaii character design meets **war movie cinematography** — dramatic low-angle perspective, volumetric lighting from a desk lamp acting as "battlefield spotlight" casting long shadows. Tilt-shift miniature photography effect with selective focus on the central assault team, soft bokeh on background details. Characters are chibi (head-to-body 2:1) with expressive dot-eyes, but the lighting and composition are cinematic. Warm incandescent color temperature (amber, gold, soft pink), dust particles visible in the lamp light beam.

**Background:** The battlefield is a **desktop surface** — the piggy bank sits on a wooden desk surrounded by scattered real coins (huge relative to the bandits), pencil barricades, eraser bunkers, and a ruler drawbridge. The desk lamp above creates dramatic god-ray lighting. Tiny battle damage: sticky note flags planted by advance scouts, tape-strip roadblocks, and a miniature base camp made from a matchbox.

**Mood:** The visual absurdity of microscopic criminals executing a Hollywood-blockbuster heist on a piggy bank — every inch of the frame packed with comedic military detail.

## Example

A hyper-detailed kawaii illustration with **epic cinematic composition** depicting a team of **twelve tiny chibi mice in black ski masks and striped shirts, each only the size of a coin, with different colored bandanas (red for the leader, blue for demolition, green for scouts)** launching a full-scale siege assault on a **massive ceramic piggy bank the size of a fortress, glossy pink surface reflecting the tiny mouse attackers, coin slot on top like a castle gate**.

**Epic Battle Layout (Macro Photography Perspective):** The scene is shot from a **low dramatic angle** at ground level, making the piggy bank loom like a mountain fortress. **Siege Division 1 (Ground Assault):** Four mice push a battering ram made from a **wooden chopstick** mounted on **bottle cap wheels**, charging toward the piggy bank's belly. **Siege Division 2 (Climbing Squad):** Three mice scale the piggy bank's curved glossy surface using **paperclip grappling hooks** and **dental floss** rope — one mouse has slipped and dangles upside-down by one foot, blue bandana falling off its head. **Siege Division 3 (Air Support):** Two mice ride a **paper airplane** swooping toward the coin slot on top, one pilot mouse grinning and one holding a tiny **rubber band slingshot** loaded with a real coin that's bigger than the mouse itself. **Siege Division 4 (Tunnel Team):** Behind the piggy bank, two green-bandana scout mice dig under using **plastic spork** shovels, a small pile of excavated cookie crumbs beside them.

**Expressions & Drama:** The red-bandana leader mouse stands on a **thimble watchtower** pointing forward dramatically with a toothpick sword, mouth open in a tiny battle cry with visible fang. The dangling climber mouse has spiral dizzy eyes. The paper airplane pilot grins with confident squinted eyes while the co-pilot looks terrified clutching the oversized coin. One digger mouse is exhausted, lying flat face-down with tongue out and little soul ghost floating above.

**Art Style:** Kawaii character design meets **war movie cinematography** — dramatic low-angle perspective, volumetric lighting from a desk lamp acting as "battlefield spotlight" casting long dramatic shadows across the desk. Tilt-shift miniature photography effect with selective focus on the central chopstick battering ram team, soft bokeh on background details. Characters are chibi mice (head-to-body 2:1) with expressive dot-eyes and round ears, but the lighting and composition are cinematic. Warm incandescent color temperature (amber, gold, soft pink), dust particles visible in the desk lamp light beam.

**Background:** The battlefield is a **wooden desktop surface** — the piggy bank sits surrounded by scattered real coins (huge relative to the mice), pencil barricades laid horizontally, eraser bunkers with mice peeking over, and a ruler drawbridge leading to the piggy bank base. The desk lamp above creates dramatic god-ray lighting. Tiny battle details: sticky note flags with skull-and-crossbones drawn in crayon planted by advance scouts, tape-strip roadblocks, and a miniature command tent made from a matchbox with a coin-map spread out inside.

**Mood:** The visual absurdity of microscopic mouse criminals executing a Hollywood-blockbuster siege on a piggy bank — every inch of the frame packed with comedic military detail, tiny heroism, and adorable chaos.
