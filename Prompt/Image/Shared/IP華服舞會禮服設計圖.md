### IP華服舞會禮服設計圖 (IP Fantasy Ballgown Design Sheet)

將一個 IP 角色的視覺精髓——配色、紋路、元素、招牌特徵——全部轉化為一件夢幻舞會禮服，由另一個 IP 角色優雅穿上。角色設計圖格式，細節拉滿。

---

## Prompt Template

A character design sheet illustration showing **[穿著角色 / 例：Pikachu / Kirby / Chiikawa / 任意角色]** wearing a breathtaking fantasy ballgown entirely inspired by the visual identity of **[服裝靈感角色 / 例：Charizard / Ninetales / Rayquaza / Gardevoir / 任意角色]**.

**LAYOUT:** Character design sheet format on a clean light background. The main figure stands in an elegant 3/4 pose at center, showing the full-length gown from head to toe. A small chibi rendition of [服裝靈感角色] sits on the wearing character's shoulder or floats nearby, acting as the design muse — it is actively interacting: adjusting the gown's collar, proudly admiring the outfit with sparkly eyes, or striking the same pose in miniature. Thin decorative dotted lines connect key design elements on the gown back to the inspiration character's corresponding features — tail to train, horns to tiara, color patches to fabric swatches.

**THE CHARMING REVEAL (CRITICAL DETAIL):** The wearing character's OWN signature feature peeks out from the gown in an adorable "I'm still me" moment — [穿著者特徵偷跑 / "their original tail sticks out from a slit in the back of the skirt, still in its original color and shape, contrasting charmingly with the elaborate gown" / "their original ears/horns poke through the headpiece, slightly too big for the tiara, creating a cute mismatch" / "their original body shape creates a distinctively round/angular/tiny silhouette under the gown that makes the haute couture look endearingly imperfect"]. This detail is the emotional hook — proving this is still the same beloved character underneath the fantasy transformation.

**THE GOWN — BODICE:** [上身設計 with: 靈感角色特徵轉化 / "A structured corset bodice with overlapping petal-shaped panels that mimic the inspiration character's most iconic texture — scales become iridescent sequined fish-scale layering, fur becomes cascading ruffled organza wisps, shell becomes a sculpted pearlescent hard-bodice, feathers become delicate quill-shaped boning visible through sheer fabric. The neckline shape echoes the character's silhouette — sweetheart for round characters, high collar for regal ones, off-shoulder draping for flowing serpentine types" / "A wrap-style bodice where the fabric crossing pattern mirrors the character's body markings — stripes become diagonal sash folds, spots become embroidered dot clusters, flame patterns become gradient-dyed panels fading from deep core color to bright edge color. Structured shoulder pieces rise slightly, shaped like abstracted versions of the character's ears, horns, or fins — not literal copies but elegant architectural interpretations in matching metallic fabric" / "A fitted bodice entirely covered in micro-embroidery replicating the character's skin pattern at textile scale — each tiny stitch a different shade creating the character's gradient. The waistline features a sculpted belt shaped like the character's most recognizable accessory, reimagined as a jeweled clasp — a Poke Ball becomes a ruby-and-ivory cameo brooch, a sword becomes a golden rapier-shaped belt buckle, a crown becomes a gem-encrusted waist coronet"].

**THE GOWN — SKIRT & TRAIN:** [裙擺設計 with: 裙型與裙擺拖尾 / "A voluminous A-line skirt with dramatic floor-sweeping train extending 1.5x the character's height behind. The outer layer is translucent organza in the character's lightest accent color, revealing an underskirt in the character's deepest signature color. The train's edge is cut to echo the character's most iconic feature shape — flame-tongue scallops for fire types, wave-ripple curves for water types, jagged lightning-bolt points for electric types, petal-soft rounded edges for grass types. Scattered across the skirt surface, the character's elemental motif appears as embroidered or appliqued accents increasing in density toward the hem" / "A mermaid-silhouette skirt that hugs the figure before flaring dramatically at the knee into cascading fabric waterfalls. Each layer of the flare is a different shade from the character's color palette, creating an ombre waterfall effect. The innermost layer glows faintly with the character's energy color. The train splits into two or three flowing tails that reference the character's tail design — if the character has multiple tails, each train-tail is slightly different in shade and decorated with a unique charm at the tip" / "A structured ballgown skirt with panniers creating wide hip volume, the surface divided into panels like stained-glass windows — each panel depicts a stylized scene or symbol from the inspiration character's lore rendered in fabric applique and embroidery. The hem is finished with a thick border of the character's accent color, and the underskirt peeks through in contrasting fabric when the character moves, showing a hidden pattern — the character's signature attack or ability rendered as an abstract textile motif"].

**HEADPIECE:** [頭飾設計 with: 頭部裝飾 / "A delicate tiara that abstracts the inspiration character's head features into precious metalwork — horns become spiraling golden finials set with gems matching the character's eye color, ears become pointed filigree extensions with dangling crystal drops, crests become a fan-shaped arrangement of metallic feather-like pieces. Hair (or equivalent) is styled to complement — swept up to showcase the tiara if elaborate, flowing down to echo the character's mane if they have one" / "A wide-brimmed fantasy hat with the brim shaped to echo the character's most distinctive head silhouette. The crown of the hat is wrapped in fabric matching the gown, with cascading ribbons or fabric tails hanging from the back that mirror the character's flowing features — long ears, antennae, tendrils, or hair rendered as silk streamers in gradient-dyed colors. A single ornamental pin on the hat front features a miniature stylized version of the character's face as a cameo" / "A floral-and-jewel hair arrangement woven directly into the hair — flowers made from fabric in the character's colors, with centers that are tiny gems matching the character's elemental affinity (rubies for fire, sapphires for water, emeralds for grass, topazes for electric). Delicate chains drape across the forehead with a central pendant shaped like the character's signature symbol. Small floating sparkle effects (decorative stars) surround the headpiece suggesting magical energy"].

**GLOVES & JEWELRY:** Elbow-length gloves in a shade lighter than the gown's primary color, with the character's pattern (scales, stripes, spots, or gradients) rendered as subtle jacquard texture on the fabric. A choker necklace with a central gem cut in the shape of the character's elemental symbol. Layered bracelets on one wrist, each one a thin band in a different color from the character's palette. Drop earrings shaped like tiny abstracted versions of the character's paws, claws, or wings.

**FEET / LOWER FINISH (ADAPT TO CHARACTER ANATOMY):** Visible beneath the gown's hem — the foot area adapts to the wearing character's actual body shape: [足部設計（依穿著者體型調整） with: 足部呈現方式 / "IF the character has visible feet/paws: their original feet/paws peek out from the gown's hem, adorned with decorative anklets or ornamental wraps in the inspiration character's colors and pattern — small gem-tipped toe-caps on claws/nails matching the tiara's gems, fabric ankle-ribbons in the gown's accent color with tiny charm dangles shaped like the inspiration character's features. The feet remain recognizably the character's own, decorated to match rather than hidden in human shoes" / "IF the character has no distinct feet (round body, floating, etc.): the gown's hem extends fully to the floor in an elegant pooling train, the bottom edge becoming the most ornately decorated area — heavy embroidery of the inspiration character's motif, layered ruffled trim, gem-studded hem-weight. The silhouette suggests the character is gliding within the gown. A tiny glimpse of the character's original body color peeks from beneath the fabric edge" / "IF the character has animal paws/hooves: ornamental fabric wraps wind around the character's natural legs in the gown's colors, with the inspiration character's pattern as embroidered bands. Small lacquered decorative caps adorn claw/nail tips in the accent color. The character's original foot anatomy is visible and celebrated — integrated with the outfit rather than concealed"].

**HIDDEN EASTER EGG:** Somewhere in the design — inside the train's lining, on the underside of a dangling kanzashi charm, or as a tiny embroidered detail on the inner glove — the wearing character's OWN iconic symbol is secretly stitched or hidden (e.g., Pikachu's lightning bolt hidden inside the gown's pocket, Kirby's star hidden on the inner belt clasp). This is the "designer's secret signature" that rewards close inspection and adds shareability.

**DETAIL CALLOUT BOXES:** 4-5 small rectangular callout boxes float around the main figure (fashion design sheet style), connected by thin lines to specific features: (1) a close-up of the fabric texture showing how the character's skin/scales/fur translates to textile, (2) the embroidery pattern motif isolated as a repeating tile, (3) the color palette — 4-5 circular swatches extracted directly from the inspiration character with labels, (4) a back-view mini sketch showing how the train looks from behind, (5) a tiny "before vs after" comparison — the wearing character's normal look next to their transformed gown look, side by side in miniature at the bottom.

**ART STYLE:** Clean semi-flat illustration with crisp black outlines of uniform weight, soft cel-shading with minimal gradients, elegant color palette derived entirely from the two characters. Character design sheet presentation — clean white or very light pastel background, reference chibi character in corner, labeled detail callouts. The wearing character retains their own face, expression, and body proportions but is fully transformed by the inspired outfit. Subtle sparkle effects around the most ornate jeweled areas. High resolution, square format (1:1).

## Example

A character design sheet illustration showing **Pikachu** wearing a breathtaking fantasy ballgown entirely inspired by the visual identity of **Charizard**.

**LAYOUT:** Character design sheet format on a clean light background. A bipedal Pikachu stands in an elegant 3/4 pose at center, showing the full-length gown from head to toe. A small chibi Charizard sits proudly on Pikachu's shoulder, tiny arms crossed, admiring the outfit with a smug sparkly-eyed expression — as if saying "my style looks great on you." Thin decorative dotted lines connect the gown's flame-scalloped train back to Charizard's tail flame, the wing-shaped shoulder pieces back to Charizard's wings, and the orange-to-red gradient fabric back to Charizard's belly-to-back coloring.

**THE CHARMING REVEAL:** Pikachu's iconic lightning-bolt tail pokes out from a slit in the back of the ballgown skirt, still bright yellow with its brown base — an adorable contrast against the Charizard-themed orange-red gown. Pikachu's red cheek circles also remain fully visible, untouched by the transformation, grounding the design in Pikachu's identity despite the dramatic outfit change.

**THE GOWN — BODICE:** A structured corset bodice with overlapping petal-shaped panels that mimic Charizard's belly scales — each panel is a warm cream-to-orange gradient, layered like dragon scales in iridescent sequined fish-scale pattern. The neckline is a sweetheart cut with subtle upward points referencing Charizard's horn tips. Structured shoulder pieces rise from each side, shaped like abstracted versions of Charizard's wings — not literal wings but elegant architectural fin-shapes in deep orange metallic fabric, each tipped with a tiny ruby gem.

**THE GOWN — SKIRT & TRAIN:** A voluminous A-line skirt with dramatic floor-sweeping train extending 1.5x Pikachu's height behind. The outer layer is translucent orange organza, revealing an underskirt in Charizard's deep teal-blue (the color of Charizard's inner wing membrane). The train's edge is cut into flame-tongue scallops — each point graduating from deep red at the base to bright gold at the tip, mimicking Charizard's tail flame exactly. Scattered across the skirt surface, small embroidered flame motifs increase in density and size toward the hem, as if the dress is catching fire from the bottom up.

**HEADPIECE:** A delicate golden tiara with two spiraling horn-finials rising from the temples — referencing Charizard's head horns, set with deep orange topazes. Between the horns, a fan-shaped crest of metallic feather-pieces in flame colors fans upward. Pikachu's ears poke through naturally, each adorned with a tiny flame-shaped ear cuff in rose gold.

**GLOVES & JEWELRY:** Elbow-length gloves in warm cream matching Charizard's belly, with a subtle dragon-scale jacquard texture. A choker necklace with a central teardrop ruby that echoes the shape of Charizard's tail flame. Three layered bracelets: one orange, one teal, one gold — Charizard's full palette. Drop earrings shaped like tiny abstracted dragon wings in polished gold.

**FEET / LOWER FINISH:** Pikachu's small yellow paws peek out from beneath the gown's fiery hem, each adorned with tiny ornamental wraps in Charizard's orange-to-red gradient. Small ruby-tipped claw-caps sit on each toe-nail, matching the tiara's gems. Fabric ankle-ribbons in flame-gold wind around each ankle with dangling charms shaped like miniature dragon wings — Pikachu's own paws, recognizably yellow and round, decorated to match the Charizard theme rather than hidden in human shoes.

**HIDDEN EASTER EGG:** On the underside of one of the dangling wing-charms at Pikachu's ankle, a tiny lightning-bolt symbol is engraved in gold — Pikachu's secret designer signature hidden within the Charizard-themed outfit. The callout box points to this with a magnifying-glass icon.

**DETAIL CALLOUT BOXES:** (1) Close-up of the scale-panel bodice texture — overlapping iridescent sequins mimicking Charizard's belly scales, (2) The flame-scallop hem pattern isolated as a repeating motif, (3) Color palette — 5 circular swatches: cream, orange, deep red, teal-blue, gold, (4) A back-view mini sketch showing the dramatic flame-scalloped train cascading behind with Pikachu's yellow tail poking out, (5) A tiny "before/after" comparison at the bottom — normal Pikachu next to gown-Pikachu in miniature.

**ART STYLE:** Clean semi-flat illustration with crisp black outlines of uniform weight, soft cel-shading with minimal gradients, elegant color palette of Charizard's orange-red-teal-gold rendered across every element. Character design sheet presentation — clean white background, chibi Charizard on shoulder, labeled detail callouts. Pikachu retains its round face, red cheeks, and lightning-bolt tail but is fully transformed by the Charizard-inspired haute couture. Subtle sparkle effects around the tiara and choker ruby. High resolution, square format (1:1).
