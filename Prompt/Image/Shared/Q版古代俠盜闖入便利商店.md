### Q版古代俠盜闖入便利商店 (Ancient Bandit Raids Modern Convenience Store)

Q版古代俠盜穿越到現代便利商店打劫，用毛筆寫劫案聲明、被自動門嚇到、把關東煮當寶藏、對條碼掃描器困惑不已，時空反差製造滿滿笑點。

## Prompt Template

A kawaii chibi-style illustration depicting a hilarious **time-displaced robbery scene**: **[古代俠盜角色 with: Q版古裝劫匪 / "a chibi ancient Chinese bandit (圓臉大眼Q版山賊) wearing a traditional brown hemp robe with rope belt, straw sandals, and a cloth mask covering the lower face, carrying an oversized gourd on the back and wielding a wooden staff with a red cloth tied to the end" / "a chibi Edo-period Japanese thief (Q版忍者盜賊) in dark indigo shinobi outfit with mesh armor, tabi boots, and a furoshiki wrapping cloth slung over one shoulder, holding a bamboo blowgun" / "a chibi medieval European rogue (Q版中世紀盜賊) in a tattered green cloak with a feathered cap, leather boots, and a small crossbow made from popsicle sticks"]** who has somehow time-traveled into a **[現代場景 with: 便利商店 / "brightly lit modern Japanese convenience store (konbini) with automatic sliding glass doors, fluorescent ceiling lights, neatly organized shelves of onigiri, bento boxes, and drink coolers, a bewildered part-time worker behind the counter" / "a 24-hour Taiwanese convenience store with tea egg pot steaming on the counter, magazine racks, and a microwave station" / "a colorful Korean convenience store with ramyeon cooking station, kimbap shelf, and K-pop posters on the windows"]**.

**The Comedy of Confusion:** The bandit stands in a dramatic wide-legged pose in the middle of the store, pointing the ancient weapon at the cashier while holding a **hand-written calligraphy scroll** that reads the robbery demand — but the writing is in classical literary Chinese that nobody can read. The **automatic sliding door** behind the bandit keeps opening and closing on the bandit's flowing robe hem, creating a tug-of-war. On the floor, the bandit has knocked over a display of **cup noodles** (mistaking the tall stack for a defensive barricade). Under one arm, the bandit clutches **three rice balls (onigiri)** and a **steaming oden skewer**, clearly distracted by modern snacks mid-robbery.

**Supporting Cast Reactions:** The **part-time worker** (a chibi teenager in store uniform and visor) stares blankly, one hand hovering over the counter bell, the other hand slowly reaching for a phone — not to call police, but to take a photo. A **regular customer** (chibi office worker with briefcase) stands frozen mid-bite of a sandwich, eyes wide. A **store cat** sits on the counter licking its paw, completely indifferent.

**Art Style:** Vibrant kawaii illustration combining **historical costume detail with modern convenience store accuracy**. Chibi proportions (head-to-body 1.5:1) for all characters. The ancient bandit's costume is rendered with warm earthy tones (brown, tan, muted red) and rough fabric texture, contrasting sharply with the store's clean fluorescent lighting and plastic packaging. Thick clean outlines (3px), flat colors with subtle warm shadows. Manga-style reaction symbols: sweat drops on the cashier, question marks around the customer, sparkle eyes on the bandit looking at snacks.

**Background:** The convenience store interior is faithfully detailed — product shelves with tiny readable labels, refrigerator humming glow, floor tiles reflecting fluorescent light, a small TV on the wall showing security camera footage of the bandit's confused entrance. The contrast between the ancient warrior's dramatic pose and the mundane modern store creates the core visual humor.

**Mood:** A fish-out-of-water comedy frozen in one frame — the funniest part is that the bandit is failing at robbery but accidentally discovering the wonders of modern convenience food.

## Example

A kawaii chibi-style illustration depicting a hilarious **time-displaced robbery scene**: **a chibi ancient Chinese bandit (圓臉大眼Q版山賊) wearing a traditional brown hemp robe with rope belt, straw sandals, and a cloth mask covering the lower face, carrying an oversized gourd on the back and wielding a wooden staff with a red cloth tied to the end** who has somehow time-traveled into a **brightly lit modern Japanese convenience store (konbini) with automatic sliding glass doors, fluorescent ceiling lights, neatly organized shelves of onigiri, bento boxes, and drink coolers, a bewildered part-time worker behind the counter**.

**The Comedy of Confusion:** The ancient bandit stands in a dramatic wide-legged kung-fu pose in the middle of the store, pointing the wooden staff at the cashier while holding a **hand-written calligraphy scroll** unfurled in the other hand — the robbery demand written in classical literary Chinese with beautiful brushwork that nobody can read. The **automatic sliding door** behind the bandit keeps opening and closing on the bandit's flowing robe hem, creating a tug-of-war that pulls the bandit backward slightly. On the floor, the bandit has knocked over a pyramid display of **cup noodles** (mistaking the tall stack for a defensive barricade). Under one arm, the bandit clutches **three onigiri** still in plastic wrapping and a **steaming oden skewer with a round fishcake**, clearly distracted by the amazing modern snacks mid-robbery.

**Supporting Cast Reactions:** The **part-time worker** (a chibi teenage girl in konbini uniform with visor) stares blankly with dot eyes, one hand hovering over the counter bell, the other hand slowly reaching for a smartphone — not to call police, but to take a photo for social media. A **regular customer** (chibi salary man with loosened tie and briefcase) stands frozen mid-bite of an egg sandwich, eyes as wide as dinner plates. A **calico store cat** sits on the counter next to the cash register, licking its paw, completely indifferent to the ancient intruder.

**Art Style:** Vibrant kawaii illustration combining **historical Chinese costume detail with modern Japanese convenience store accuracy**. Chibi proportions (head-to-body 1.5:1) for all characters. The ancient bandit's costume is rendered with warm earthy tones (brown, tan, muted red cloth) and rough hemp fabric texture, contrasting sharply with the store's clean fluorescent lighting, glossy plastic packaging, and chrome shelving. Thick clean outlines (3px), flat colors with subtle warm shadows. Manga-style reaction symbols: sweat drops on the cashier, question marks floating around the confused customer, sparkle eyes on the bandit gazing at the oden pot.

**Background:** The konbini interior is faithfully detailed — product shelves with tiny colorful packaging, refrigerator cases with a soft blue-white humming glow, clean white floor tiles reflecting fluorescent light, a small security monitor on the wall showing grainy footage of the bandit's confused entrance (walking into the automatic door before it opened). A knocked-over magazine rack, scattered cup noodles, and the trail of straw sandal footprints on the clean floor tell the story of the chaotic entry. The contrast between the ancient warrior's dramatic martial arts pose and the mundane modern convenience store creates the core visual humor.

**Mood:** A fish-out-of-water comedy frozen in one frame — the funniest part is that the bandit is completely failing at robbery but accidentally discovering the life-changing wonders of modern convenience food.
