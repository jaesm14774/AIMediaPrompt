## Example

A warm bakery-themed monthly desk calendar illustration, landscape orientation, 3:2 aspect ratio (resembling a standard 16-kai desk calendar, approximately 250mm x 175mm, with a triangular tent-fold stand). The calendar displays 2026 September. The calendar is the primary subject, filling the central 70% of the composition: a complete monthly grid on a soft cream-parchment textured background. The year and month are displayed at the top in a warm handwritten-style cursive font in chocolate brown. Below, a neat 7-column grid (SUN–SAT) with date numbers correctly arranged for September 2026, written in a friendly rounded font, dark brown on cream, each date cell evenly spaced with a faint dotted border in light caramel color. A small spiral binding along the top edge of the calendar reinforces the desk-calendar form factor. Tiny watercolor cookie and cupcake illustrations scattered in the margins around the grid — a croissant near week 1, a donut near week 2, a slice of cake near week 3, macarons near week 4, all in soft pastel icing colors, not overlapping the date numbers. In the lower-right corner, small and charming (roughly 15-20% of frame): a chibi adorable Cinnamoroll wearing a tiny chef hat and apron, holding a freshly frosted cupcake, sitting on a stack of recipe books. Flour dusting on cheeks, happy expression. Cinnamoroll is a warm decorative accent that complements the bakery theme without competing with the calendar grid. Warm color palette: butter yellow, cream, rosy pink, chocolate brown. Soft illustration style mixing clean typography with hand-drawn food illustrations, cozy warm lighting feel, 4K resolution, ultra detailed.
