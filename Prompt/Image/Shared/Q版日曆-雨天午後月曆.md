## Example

A cozy rainy-afternoon monthly calendar illustration, portrait orientation, 3:4 aspect ratio (resembling a traditional tear-off wall calendar or daily planner page, approximately 250mm x 340mm). The calendar displays 2026 November. The calendar is the clear main subject, taking up 65-70% of the composition in the upper and center area: a complete monthly grid displayed on a slightly textured off-white paper surface with visible paper edge and a perforated tear line at the top.
