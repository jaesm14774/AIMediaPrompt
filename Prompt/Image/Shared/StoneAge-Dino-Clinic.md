### 石器時代恐龍診所

可愛角色穿著獸皮原始人裝扮，在樹洞診所裡溫柔包紮受傷恐龍寶寶的傷口——擦傷膝蓋、卡在背鰭的石子、尾巴木刺、感冒的副櫛龍。草藥、繃帶、蜂蜜、針灸、聽診器散落診療台。治癒系日常 × Animal Crossing 風格，溫馨療癒。

On a wooden examination table lie **[石器道具："crushed healing herbs in a stone mortar, rolled bandages made from plant fiber, and a clay bowl of honey" / "acupuncture needles made of fishbone, soothing flower tea, and diagrams on bark paper" / "aloe vera leaves, a primitive stethoscope (hollowed bamboo), and reward cookies shaped like bones"]** arranged with care.

Background: Warm interior of a tree-hollow clinic, with shelves full of labeled herb jars, hanging medicinal plants drying, a small waiting area with log stools, soft sunlight filtering through a window. A carved wooden sign reads "Dr. [Character]'s Dino Care Clinic - All Species Welcome."

Art style: Wholesome slice-of-life manga meets Animal Crossing aesthetics — soft linework, gentle color fills, adorable healing vibes. Earthy pastel palette (sage green, peach, cream, soft brown) with fairy-light warmth. The overall mood is tender, nurturing, and emotionally soothing — like a therapeutic comfort comic. 4K resolution, delicate details, heartwarming composition.
