### Kirby 的石器時代騎寵冒險

Kirby 騎在一隻可愛的 Q 版恐龍寵物背上，穿越石器時代的原始叢林——小小身體趴在大恐龍背上的尺寸反差，喚起那段騎寵跑地圖的經典回憶。

---

## Prompt Template

A heartwarming adventure illustration of **[主角角色："pink round Kirby" / "chibi Pikachu" / "tiny Totoro" / "small Doraemon"]** wearing a primitive Stone Age outfit (tiny animal-skin vest with bone button, leaf headband) riding on the back of **[騎乘恐龍："a big friendly green brontosaurus with a dopey gentle smile, long curved neck, and a cozy woven grass saddle-blanket on its back" / "a sturdy orange triceratops with polished horns decorated with flower garlands, wearing a primitive wooden saddle with dangling shell ornaments" / "a speedy blue raptor with feathery tuft on head, wearing a tiny vine bridle, mid-stride with dust clouds trailing behind" / "a massive red T-Rex with surprisingly gentle eyes and tiny useless arms, with a hammock-style seat strung between its back spines"]**, trotting through a vibrant prehistoric landscape. The character is tiny compared to the dinosaur — happily sitting with stubby legs dangling off the sides, one hand gripping onto the saddle, the other hand waving excitedly. A **[旅途小細節："packed lunch wrapped in a giant leaf, bouncing and about to fall off the dinosaur's tail" / "trail of small footprint-shaped flowers blooming wherever the dinosaur steps" / "flock of tiny pterodactyls following them like friendly escort birds" / "map drawn on animal skin, fluttering in the wind, clearly upside-down"]** adds a playful storytelling touch.

Environment: Lush Stone Age wilderness — oversized ferns and tropical plants towering like trees, a crystal-clear river with stepping stones, rounded friendly mountains in the background with gentle volcanic steam. A rustic wooden signpost at a fork in the dirt path reads village names in primitive scratchy font. Scattered amber-colored stones glow softly along the path edges.

Art style: Warm nostalgic storybook illustration — soft watercolor textures with clean outlines, dappled sunlight filtering through giant prehistoric leaves creating spotted shadow patterns on the ground. Color palette: lush greens, warm amber, soft sky blue, with the character's signature color as the eye-catching focal point. Slight fish-eye lens warmth to make everything feel round and huggable. The mood is pure childhood adventure — that feeling of exploring a new area in your favorite game for the first time. 4K resolution, gentle and inviting composition.
