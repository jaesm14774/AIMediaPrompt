### 你先睡我不動了 (You Sleep First, I Won't Move)

角色在你腿上睡著了，你的腿已經麻了但不敢動，怕吵醒他。你低頭看他的睡臉，忍不住微笑。

---

## Prompt Template

A heartwarming close-up illustration of **[在此填入角色名：如 Kirby / Pikachu / a small child / a kitten]** fast asleep on [守護者 with: 讓角色睡在腿上的人 / "a young woman's lap, her hand resting gently on the character's head" / "an older brother's lap, one hand holding a phone above the character trying not to drop it" / "a grandfather's lap, his weathered hand placed protectively over the character's back" / "a best friend's lap on a train, the friend's head also starting to nod off"], curled up and completely surrendered to sleep.

The sleeping character's face is the emotional center — [睡臉細節 with: 角色睡著的樣子 / "cheek squished flat against the person's thigh, mouth slightly open, a tiny bit of drool forming at the corner — not cute-perfect, just real and peaceful" / "face half-buried into the fabric of the person's clothes, one hand gripping a fistful of their shirt hem, even in sleep refusing to let go" / "nuzzled so deeply into the person's lap that only the top of the head and one closed eye are visible, completely burrowed in"]. The body is boneless — no tension anywhere, total weight given over.

The person whose lap is being used looks down at the sleeping face with [守護者反應 with: 看著睡臉的反應 / "a soft, helpless smile — the kind that says 'my leg is completely numb but I will not move for anything in this world'" / "one eyebrow slightly raised in amused tenderness, lips pressed together holding back a quiet laugh at how deeply the character has passed out" / "eyes glistening, chin trembling just slightly, overwhelmed by how small and trusting this sleeping creature is"]. Their body is completely still — frozen in place by love.

Simple setting, minimal detail — [場景 with: 簡單的場所 / "a couch with afternoon light, a forgotten TV remote nearby" / "a park bench with dappled tree shadow" / "a train seat by the window, scenery blurred outside" / "a bed with rumpled sheets, morning light"]. Warm, soft lighting from one side. Background out of focus.

Art style: Soft digital painting, warm palette (peach, honey, cream, soft pink). Close framing — fill the composition with the two figures' physical connection. The point where the sleeping face meets the lap is the soul of the image. Inspired by the intimate quietness of Ghibli character moments. Square format (1:1), 4K resolution, illustration.

## Example

A heartwarming close-up illustration of **Kirby** fast asleep on a young woman's lap, curled up and completely surrendered to sleep. Her hand rests gently on Kirby's round pink head, fingers slightly curled, thumb barely touching Kirby's cheek.

Kirby's face is the emotional center — cheek squished flat against her thigh, distorting Kirby's round shape into a soft pancake, mouth slightly open in a tiny "o", the faintest hint of a content smile even in deep sleep. One tiny hand grips a fistful of her skirt hem, even in sleep refusing to let go. Kirby's body is boneless — no tension anywhere, total weight sunk into her lap like a warm pink water balloon.

The young woman looks down at Kirby's sleeping face with a soft, helpless smile — the kind that says "my leg is completely numb but I will not move for anything in this world." Her other hand hovers near her phone on the couch armrest, abandoned mid-scroll because she can't look away from this sleeping face. Her body is completely still — frozen in place by love.

Simple setting — a couch with afternoon light streaming in from one side, a forgotten TV remote and a half-empty mug on the side table. Warm, soft lighting painting golden edges on Kirby's round silhouette and the woman's hair. Background out of focus — just warm cream and amber.

Art style: Soft digital painting, warm palette (peach, honey, cream, soft pink). Close framing — the composition is filled with Kirby's squished sleeping face against the lap, the woman's gentle hand on Kirby's head, and her tender downward gaze. The point where Kirby's cheek meets her lap is the soul of the image. Inspired by the intimate quietness of Ghibli character moments. Square format (1:1), 4K resolution, illustration.
