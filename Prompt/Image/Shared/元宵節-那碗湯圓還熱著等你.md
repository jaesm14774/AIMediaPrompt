### 那碗湯圓還熱著等你 (The Bowl of Tangyuan Is Still Warm, Waiting for You)

元宵節最溫暖的不是花燈，是有人留了一碗湯圓等你回來。

## Example

A deeply heartwarming illustration in soft gouache painting style — a small kitchen table at night, a single warm ceiling lamp casting a golden pool of light over **Kirby** sitting alone at the table, both tiny pink hands wrapped around a steaming bowl of tangyuan (glutinous rice balls in sweet soup). Kirby's round cheeks are extra flushed from the steam, eyes half-closed in a drowsy, contented smile — the kind of face that says "I waited so long but this warmth was worth it." Kirby's feet dangle from the chair, not reaching the floor.

The bowl is the emotional anchor — a chipped but beloved old bowl — the kind that has been in the family forever — filled with fermented rice sweet soup (酒釀湯圓) with tiny tangyuan floating among sweet osmanthus flowers (桂花), the golden petals dotting the milky surface like scattered stars. Steam curls upward in lazy spirals that catch the lamplight and turn golden, wreathing Kirby's face in warmth.

Evidence that someone prepared this and left — a grandmother's reading glasses folded on the table beside a half-finished crossword puzzle, her chair still warm-looking, a hand-knitted blanket draped over the back — she waited as long as she could before going to bed, but she left everything ready. A small saucer was placed over the bowl to keep it warm, now set aside. The kitchen towel is folded neatly, the stove wiped clean — everything done with quiet, patient love.

The kitchen is small, lived-in, imperfect — old wooden cabinets with mismatched handles, a calendar on the wall, refrigerator covered in family photos and magnets, red paper-cut decorations (窗花) on the window, the faint warm glow of distant lanterns visible through frosted glass. The only bright thing in the frame is the lamp over the table and the steam from the bowl — everything else falls into soft, warm shadow.

Art style: Soft gouache illustration with visible brushstrokes, warm palette dominated by lamp-gold, cream, and gentle shadow. Intimate close composition — the world is just this table, this bowl, Kirby, and the evidence of grandmother's love. The warmth is not grand — it's a single lamp, a single bowl, a single moment of "someone thought of me." Inspired by the quiet domesticity of Ghibli kitchen scenes. Square format (1:1), 4K resolution, illustration.
