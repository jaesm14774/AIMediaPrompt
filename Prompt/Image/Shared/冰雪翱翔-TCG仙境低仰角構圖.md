### 冰雪翱翔・TCG 神降仙境構圖

低仰角仰望——才意識到眼前的不是大鳥，是活的暴風雪本身；翅膀展開的瞬間，整片天空都是牠的氣場。

---

## Prompt Template

Japanese Pokémon TCG card illustration style, clean bold black outline strokes with soft cel-shaded gradient fills, non-realistic anime art. **[IP角色]** — a living blizzard deity — descending through an apocalyptic frozen sky.

Ultra-low worm's eye angle: wings spread so impossibly wide they reach beyond the frame edges, long elegant tail feathers sweeping a vast S-curve that fills the entire vertical composition. The creature is so colossal that the towering ice spires in the foreground are dwarfed to the scale of tiny shards — the blizzard does not surround it, it radiates outward from its wingtips as a living aura. The viewer stands at ground level, realizing they are not watching a soaring bird — they are inside the creature's own storm.

Icy cerulean and misty white color palette — cool gray atmospheric haze blends into the background. Three-layer depth composition: foreground glinting ice crystal shards and powdery snow dust at the viewer's feet, impossibly small against the scale above; midground the deity figure radiating pale cerulean luminous aura, floating sparkle particles and drifting feathers falling like snow; background towering mountain peaks and ice spires dissolving into the creature's own blizzard — indistinguishable from its aura.

[環境氛圜 with: 神降暴風 / "god-scale blizzard descent — wingtip storms shatter ice spires below, dark cumulonimbus crown above the creature's head, lightning frozen mid-strike" / "arctic twilight apparition — deep indigo sky, aurora borealis emanates from the creature's spine, glacier valleys far below like a miniature world" / "silent revelation — crystalline still air broken only by the creature's presence, faint sun halo crowns the head, frozen waterfall shatters mid-cascade from the wingbeat pressure wave"]

Pokémon TCG full-art card illustration quality, delicate sparkle highlight dots, subtle ink texture on outline edges. Vertical 9:16 portrait composition, 4K, soft luminous glow pass, Japanese anime concept art aesthetic.
