### 吉祥物盲盒工廠 (Mascot Blind Box Factory)

## Example

A wonderfully absurd kawaii illustration showing a miniature factory assembly line where **Kirby** is the worker AND the product. A tiny conveyor belt runs diagonally across the scene from upper-left to lower-right. At the start of the belt, raw materials — cotton fluff, felt sheets in pink, and tiny button eyes — sit in small organized bins.

Station 1 (upper-left) — THE VARIANT WORKSHOP: A Kirby wearing a tiny yellow hard hat and safety goggles operates a miniature sewing machine, but the conveyor belt reveals the magic — every single plush Kirby coming off the line is a DIFFERENT Copy Ability variant. A blue Ice Kirby plush with a crystal crown, a green Sword Kirby plush wielding a tiny felt blade, a red Fire Kirby plush with flame-shaped felt pieces on its head, a yellow Beam Kirby plush with a jester hat, and a golden sparkly Star Rod Kirby plush that glitters — the ultra-rare secret variant. The sewing Kirby looks extremely focused, pins held in mouth, stubby arms working the fabric, surrounded by bins of different-colored fabrics, tiny felt swords, miniature crowns, and itty-bitty ability hats.

Station 2 (center) — QUALITY CONTROL & BOXING: Another Kirby stands at a wrapping station with a checklist wall behind it showing silhouettes of ALL the different Copy Ability variants (Ice, Sword, Fire, Beam checked off — the "Star Rod" secret rare silhouette has a golden star next to it). It carefully places each unique variant Kirby into a pastel pink box with a large question mark on the front and Kirby's round silhouette as a watermark, sealed with a holographic 'SECRET?' sticker — each box is identical from outside so you can't tell which Copy Ability is inside. This Kirby wears a tiny clipboard tucked under one arm and has a quality-control stamp hanging from its neck. On the conveyor belt approaching the station, the plush variants ride along in a colorful parade — blue, green, red, yellow — each one distinctly different.

Station 3 (lower-right) — DISPLAY & SHIPPING: A showcase shelf displays one opened example of each Copy Ability variant — Ice Kirby, Sword Kirby, Fire Kirby, Beam Kirby, and Sleep Kirby all standing in a neat collector's display row, each with its signature ability hat and accessory, showing off the full lineup diversity. Next to the display, neatly stacked rows of sealed identical blind boxes on shipping shelves. One Kirby sits atop the highest shelf, legs dangling, having accidentally packaged ITSELF into a box as the ultra-rare "REAL KIRBY" hidden variant — only its actual pink face peeks out from the top, looking mildly confused but content, with a tiny golden crown sticker on the box reading just a star symbol. A "SOLD OUT" sign leans against the bottom shelf. Another Kirby pushes a tiny wooden cart loaded with boxes toward a miniature delivery truck parked at the edge.

Extra details: A poster on the wall shows the full Copy Ability variant lineup as shadowed silhouettes with one spot marked by a golden question mark (the secret rare Star Rod Kirby). Tiny sticky notes with production targets (drawn as tally marks and stars), a coffee mug with Kirby's face on it (half empty), scattered packing peanuts and tiny felt scraps in various colors on the floor, and one Kirby taking a secret nap inside an oversized box in the corner — wearing a half-finished Ninja ability hood, clearly a variant that never got completed.

Art style: Clean flat kawaii illustration with soft pastel fills — palette of baby pink, lavender, mint green, and cream white. Thick uniform rounded outlines, simple dot-and-curve faces, chibi 3:1 head-to-body ratio.
