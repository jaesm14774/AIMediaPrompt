### 吉祥物美食攤車 (Mascot Street Food Cart)

Q 版吉祥物經營超迷你路邊攤，自己就是最可愛的食材——一邊烤著自己造型的鯛魚燒，一邊招呼客人。

---

## Prompt Template

An irresistibly cute kawaii illustration of a tiny street food cart scene — **[在此填入吉祥物角色：如 Kirby / Pikachu / Chiikawa / Molly / 自訂角色]** standing on a small wooden step stool behind a miniature yatai (Japanese street food cart) that is barely taller than the character itself. The character wears a tiny white headband (hachimaki) tied around its head and a mini apron that says nothing — just has a small heart embroidered on the chest pocket.

The cart sells [攤車食物 with: 角色造型的小吃 / "taiyaki (fish-shaped cakes) molded into the character's face shape — rows of freshly baked golden-brown character-face taiyaki cooling on a wire rack, one split open revealing custard cream oozing out, steam rising from the iron mold where a new batch is being pressed" / "character-shaped dango on wooden skewers — three round dango per stick but each ball has a different cute expression (happy, sleepy, surprised), displayed on a bamboo tray with a small bottle of mitarashi sauce, the character is carefully painting sauce on each dango with a tiny brush" / "cotton candy shaped into the character's silhouette — the character operates a mini cotton candy machine, spinning fluffy pastel clouds onto paper cones, each cotton candy head has two candy-dot eyes pressed into it, a finished row of five cotton candy characters standing upright in a display holder"]. The character works with extreme concentration — tongue sticking out slightly, tiny beads of effort-sweat floating above its head.

Three mini customers wait in line — [排隊客人 with: 迷你版同角色顧客 / "three even smaller versions of the same character standing in a neat line, the first one bouncing excitedly on tiptoes reaching toward the counter with sparkle eyes, the second one drooling with a small puddle forming beneath, the third one at the back standing on another one's head to see over the counter" / "three different small round creatures (a cat, a bunny, and a chick) each holding tiny coins in their paws/wings, the cat is sniffing the air with closed eyes, the bunny's ears are perked up pointing toward the food, the chick has climbed onto the bunny's head for a better view"]. A small hand-written menu board sits beside the cart with wobbly drawings of the food items and prices shown as tiny star coins.

A single paper lantern hangs from the cart's pole, glowing soft warm orange. The ground has a few fallen cherry blossom petals and a tiny dropped coin.

Art style: Warm-toned kawaii illustration with soft watercolor texture — color palette of cream, warm peach, soft gold, and gentle brown. Thick rounded outlines, characters with simple dot eyes and curved line mouths, exaggerated chibi proportions. Gentle warm lighting from the lantern casting soft amber glow on everything. The scene reads like a miniature diorama — everything is tiny and precious. Minimal background — just a soft warm gradient fading to cream. Scattered steam wisps drawn as simple curly lines. Square format (1:1), high resolution illustration.

## Example

An irresistibly cute kawaii illustration of a tiny street food cart scene — **Kirby** standing on a small wooden step stool behind a miniature yatai (Japanese street food cart) that is barely taller than Kirby itself. Kirby wears a tiny white headband (hachimaki) tied around its round pink head and a mini apron that says nothing — just has a small heart embroidered on the chest pocket.

The cart sells taiyaki (fish-shaped cakes) molded into Kirby's face shape — rows of freshly baked golden-brown Kirby-face taiyaki cooling on a wire rack, one split open revealing custard cream oozing out, steam rising from the iron mold where a new batch is being pressed. Kirby works with extreme concentration — tongue sticking out slightly, tiny beads of effort-sweat floating above its head. One of Kirby's stubby arms flips the taiyaki mold while the other holds a tiny spatula.

Three mini customers wait in line — three even smaller Kirbys standing in a neat line, the first one bouncing excitedly on tiptoes reaching toward the counter with sparkle eyes, the second one drooling with a small puddle forming beneath, the third one at the back standing on another tiny Kirby's head to see over the counter. A small hand-written menu board sits beside the cart with wobbly drawings of Kirby-taiyaki and prices shown as tiny star coins.

A single paper lantern hangs from the cart's pole, glowing soft warm orange. The ground has a few fallen cherry blossom petals and a tiny dropped coin.

Art style: Warm-toned kawaii illustration with soft watercolor texture — color palette of cream, warm peach, soft gold, and gentle brown. Thick rounded outlines, Kirbys with simple dot eyes and curved line mouths, exaggerated chibi proportions. Gentle warm lighting from the lantern casting soft amber glow on everything. The scene reads like a miniature diorama — everything is tiny and precious. Minimal background — just a soft warm gradient fading to cream. Scattered steam wisps drawn as simple curly lines. Square format (1:1), high resolution illustration.
