### 圖書館書本大起義

[IP角色]翻開了第一本書，觸發了連鎖反應——圖書館裡所有書同時飛離書架，每本在空中爆炸成自己的知識宇宙，而[IP角色]手裡只剩那本書的空封面，茫然望著自己無意間引發的知識解放。

## Prompt Template

A spectacular fantasy illustration of a library in the midst of a great book uprising, triggered by a single reader: **[IP角色]** stands at the epicenter, holding an empty book cover — its pages have already exploded outward as the first wave of the uprising. Radiating from **[IP角色]**'s position like a shockwave, hundreds of books have simultaneously burst off their shelves in an ever-expanding cascade, each tearing open mid-air and releasing its own unique knowledge universe. A history book erupts into a swirling vortex of ancient maps and battle formations, a physics book detonates into orbiting atom models and glowing equations, a poetry book dissolves into cascading golden calligraphy ribbons, a mythology book ignites into a column of flame-and-lightning filled with divine silhouettes. **[IP角色]** looks up with wide eyes of stunned disbelief, the empty book cover hanging loosely in one hand — the unwitting revolutionary who started everything. All around, the wooden shelves are now completely bare and silent, their contents entirely liberated, the empty shelves forming a dramatic geometric backdrop to the chaotic beauty erupting above them. The light source comes entirely from the exploding books themselves, each casting its own colored glow outward: warm amber from history, electric cyan from science, soft gold from poetry, deep violet from mythology. Dramatic low-angle perspective looking upward through the storm of liberated books. Studio Ghibli × Moebius graphic novel style, ultra-detailed particle systems for each knowledge explosion, deep chiaroscuro contrast, 4K resolution.
