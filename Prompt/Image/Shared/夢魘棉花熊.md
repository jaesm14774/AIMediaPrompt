### 夢魘棉花熊 Nightmare Cotton Candy Bear

小熊頭頂上的棉花糖雲同時是甜夢與惡夢——情緒雙面性的貼圖風格插畫。3 個填空：熊毛顏色、棉花雲左右分割、背景色調。

---

## Prompt Template

Adorable chibi sticker illustration of a round sleepy bear sitting cross-legged with eyes half-closed in a dreamy expression. Above its head floats an enormous fluffy cotton candy cloud — split perfectly down the middle: LEFT side is soft pastel dreamland cotton candy (sweet, fluffy, magical), RIGHT side is dark dramatic shadow cotton candy (swirling deep purple-black, with tiny nightmare silhouettes — stars with frowning faces, tiny storm clouds, mini monsters — visible inside the dark fluff). The contrast line down the middle is sharp and dramatic. The bear itself looks perfectly peaceful, unaware of the split sky above.

**[熊毛配色 Bear fur color with: choose your dreamer / "soft honey-gold fur with pink blush cheek marks and a white belly patch" / "cream-white polar bear fur with pale blue shadow tones and snowflake eye markings" / "warm cinnamon-brown fur with caramel highlights and round amber eyes"]**

**[棉花雲分割 Cotton candy cloud split with: choose your dream-nightmare duality / "LEFT: pastel pink clouds with rainbow sprinkles and tiny hearts / RIGHT: deep purple-black with tiny frowning stars and small monster faces peeking out" / "LEFT: mint green clouds with silver butterfly sparkles / RIGHT: stormy charcoal with tiny lightning bolts and sad moon faces" / "LEFT: golden sunrise clouds with tiny sun and chirping birds / RIGHT: midnight navy with shooting nightmare comets and half-eaten moon"]**

Background: [背景色調 Background mood with: choose your twilight / "soft gradient from warm lavender-pink on LEFT to deep cool indigo on RIGHT — reflecting the cloud's duality" / "neutral misty grey that lets the cotton candy contrast do all the visual work" / "clean cream-white background that makes both the sweet and dark sides pop equally"].

Soft painterly kawaii illustration style, clean minimal background, chibi proportions (large round body, tiny limbs), gentle cel-shading with cotton candy texture (fibrous, layered, backlit translucency), dramatic split contrast lighting (warm glow from left, cool shadow from right), sharp clean silhouette suitable for sticker use, sweet-and-dark emotional contrast, professional character design, 4K.
