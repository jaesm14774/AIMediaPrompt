### 情人節 - 心跳破表急診室 (Heart Rate Overload ER)

角色躺在迷你病床上，心電圖螢幕上的心跳波形失控畫出了一個完美的愛心形狀，旁邊的迷你護士角色們驚慌失措地試圖搶救

---

## Prompt Template

A hilarious kawaii illustration of **[主角：如 pink round Kirby / chibi Pikachu / tiny hamster / small round penguin]** lying on a miniature hospital bed with rosy red cheeks and steam coming off their head, connected to a heart rate monitor. The monitor screen is the visual punchline — instead of normal heartbeat waves, the ECG line has gone completely haywire, drawing a **perfect heart shape (♥)** pattern repeatedly across the screen in bright pink, with the BPM reading showing an absurd **"999 BPM"** in flashing red with warning symbols.

**[醫療團隊反應 with: 護士反應 / "two tiny nurse characters in pink scrubs — one frantically pressing buttons on the monitor with sweat drops flying, the other holding up a giant syringe labeled 'Anti-Love Serum' with a determined expression" / "three miniature doctor characters — one checking a clipboard with wide panicked eyes, one trying to unplug the monitor, one on a tiny phone calling for backup while pointing at the heart-shaped ECG" / "a single tiny nurse character standing on a step stool next to the monitor, bonking it with a tiny hammer while a speech bubble shows '!?', and a tiny ambulance toy in the corner with its lights on"]**.

The hospital bed has pink heart-patterned sheets instead of standard hospital white. A small IV drip bag hangs nearby, filled with pink liquid labeled "Love Cure". Small heart-shaped bubbles float up from the patient character. The floor around the bed is littered with scattered medical charts that all show heart symbols instead of data.

Art style: Bright, clean kawaii illustration with bold outlines and flat colors. Medical equipment is rendered with enough detail to be recognizable but in pastel candy colors (pink stethoscopes, lavender clipboards, mint green scrubs). The contrast between serious medical setting and cute Valentine's chaos creates the humor. Manga-style effect lines and exclamation marks around the panicking staff.

Composition: Centered on the bed and monitor — these two elements tell the whole story. The monitor screen should be large enough for the heart-shaped ECG to be clearly visible. Characters are small relative to the equipment, adding to the comedy. Clean light background (hospital white with subtle pink tint). Aspect ratio 4:3 horizontal for maximum monitor visibility.
