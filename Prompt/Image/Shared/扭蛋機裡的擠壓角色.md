## Prompt Template

A delightful 3D clay-render style illustration of **[角色名：Kirby / Totoro / 柴犬 / 自訂角色]** crammed inside a transparent capsule toy (gacha) machine. The main character is way too big for the machine — soft squishy body pressed against the curved glass dome from inside, face and cheeks flattened comically against the transparent surface, creating a "fish-eye" distortion effect where it touches the glass. Around the oversized character, several [迷你角色 with: 同角色縮小版 / "tiny miniature versions of the same character in different colors and poses" / "assorted small round creatures each a different pastel color" / "baby versions of the character sleeping, waving, and tumbling"], each inside their own small colorful capsule balls (red, blue, yellow, green), are packed tightly in the remaining gaps.

**Machine Details:** The gacha machine has a [機器風格 with: 經典日式 / "classic Japanese coin-operated design with a chunky silver turning knob, coin slot, and a small dispensing chute at the bottom" / "candy-colored pastel machine body with heart-shaped coin slot, bow decoration on top, and a tiny prize display window" / "futuristic transparent machine with LED edge lighting, holographic price tag, and chrome details"]. A sign on the machine reads the character's name in cute rounded font.

**Art Style:** High-quality C4D clay render, miniature toy aesthetic with slight tilt-shift depth of field blur at top and bottom edges. Smooth matte materials with subtle subsurface scattering on the character's skin. Rounded geometry everywhere — no sharp edges. Color palette: pastel rainbow with one dominant accent color matching the main character. Volumetric soft-box lighting from above-left, gentle fill light from right, warm bounce light from below.

**Background:** Clean solid pastel gradient background (top: white, bottom: matching accent color at 20% opacity). A few capsule balls scattered on the surface around the machine base. Tiny sparkle particles floating in the air.

**Mood:** Collectible, adorable, tactile — you want to turn the knob and see what comes out, but the main character is clearly not fitting through the chute.

## Example

A delightful 3D clay-render style illustration of **Kirby** crammed inside a transparent capsule toy (gacha) machine. Kirby is way too big for the machine — soft pink squishy body pressed against the curved glass dome from inside, face and cheeks flattened comically against the transparent surface, creating a "fish-eye" distortion effect where Kirby's face touches the glass. Around the oversized Kirby, several **tiny miniature versions of Kirby in different colors and poses** (blue Ice Kirby sleeping, green Sword Kirby waving, yellow Beam Kirby tumbling), each inside their own small colorful capsule balls (red, blue, yellow, green), are packed tightly in the remaining gaps.

**Machine Details:** The gacha machine has a **candy-colored pastel machine body with heart-shaped coin slot, a cute star bow decoration on top, and a tiny prize display window** showing a "Kirby Collection" label. A sign on the machine reads "KIRBY" in cute rounded bubbly font.

**Art Style:** High-quality C4D clay render, miniature toy aesthetic with slight tilt-shift depth of field blur at top and bottom edges. Smooth matte materials with subtle subsurface scattering on Kirby's pink skin. Rounded geometry everywhere — no sharp edges. Color palette: pastel rainbow with dominant pink accent matching Kirby. Volumetric soft-box lighting from above-left, gentle fill light from right, warm bounce light from below.

**Background:** Clean solid pastel gradient background (top: white, bottom: soft pink at 20% opacity). A few opened capsule balls scattered on the surface around the machine base, with one tiny Kirby peeking out of a capsule. Tiny sparkle particles floating in the air.

**Mood:** Collectible, adorable, tactile — you want to turn the knob and see what comes out, but Kirby is clearly not fitting through the tiny chute.
