### Q版IP 文藝復興宮廷肖像

任何角色穿越到文藝復興宮廷，以日系可愛插畫詮釋巴洛克油畫構圖，荒謬莊嚴。

---

## Prompt Template

Chibi hand-drawn illustration, thick clean ink lines, flat colors inspired by old master oil painting palette (deep burgundy, gold, ivory, shadow purple), white background. **[角色/外觀：角色名稱 + 主要外型特徵 / 例："Gengar, chubby purple ghost Pokémon, wide grin, red eyes, stubby arms" / "Kirby, round pink puffball, large cute eyes, rosy cheeks, tiny nub arms" / "Totoro, large round grey forest spirit, wide eyes, pointy ears, white belly markings"]** [宮廷場景 with: 宮廷情境 / "seated on an ornate throne wearing a velvet royal robe and golden crown, one stubby arm resting on the armrest in noble portrait pose, surrounded by miniature versions of the same character as royal attendants" / "posing for a formal portrait, wearing Renaissance collar ruff and doublet, holding a single wilting flower, looking extremely dignified" / "hosting a royal banquet, seated at a long table with tiny servants, dramatically raising a goblet"], chibi art style with baroque-inspired composition and dramatic lighting suggested through shading, rich decorative details on clothing rendered in flat illustration style, single candle or torch light source implied.

---

## Example

Chibi hand-drawn illustration, thick clean ink lines, flat colors inspired by old master oil painting palette (deep burgundy, gold, ivory, shadow purple), white background.
