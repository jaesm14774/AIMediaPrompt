### 春日廢墟秘密花園 — 繁花爆炸廢墟衝擊 (Spring Ruin Secret Garden — Floral Explosion Impact)

四月春季，一座被時間遺棄的廢墟建築，裂縫間爆發出壯麗的春花與藤蔓。破碎的牆面、倒塌的廊柱、碎裂的窗框——每一道傷口都成為花朵的出口。廢棄的死寂與春日的爆炸性生機形成極端對比，一個小小的 [IP角色] 站在廢墟中央，目睹這場自然的壓倒性勝利。

## Example

Wide-angle cinematic photograph of an abandoned Japanese imperial greenhouse — rusted iron framework skeleton still standing, glass panels mostly gone, the steel ribs draped in curtains of weeping cherry blossom branches that pour down from every beam like pink waterfalls, the stone floor buried under fallen petals half a meter deep.

**THE FLORAL EXPLOSION — OVERWHELMING FORCE (CRITICAL):** The collapsed floor section has become a floral crater — where the floor gave way, a garden has detonated upward: peonies the size of human heads, stems as thick as wrists, forcing themselves through broken concrete slabs that have been lifted and tilted like geological plates by root pressure. The flowers have won a physical battle against architecture. Petals in continuous falling motion blur, caught at the moment of impact with the petal floor below.

**[IP角色] — THE WITNESS AT THE CENTER:** [IP角色] stands at the exact center of the frame, dwarfed to near-invisibility by the scale of floral destruction around them — they stand on the petal-buried floor looking up, arms slightly spread, the exact posture of someone trying to take in something too large to comprehend. Their tiny round body is dusted with cherry blossom petals that have landed on them. From above, they look like a single button on a fabric made entirely of flowers.

**VISUAL CONTRAST ENGINE:** A perfectly preserved stone floor mosaic — geometric patterns still mathematically precise — is buried under 15cm of fallen petals. The mosaic pattern is still visible through gaps in the petals, human geometry surrendering to organic chaos. Rusted steel beams overhead are wrapped in living cherry blossom branches so densely the metal is invisible; the blossom-beams cast pink-filtered shadows across everything below.

**LIGHT:** Five distinct light columns enter from the absent glass panels, each illuminating a different flower cluster at peak intensity — the scene reads as a stage performance with multiple spotlights, each flower explosion having its own moment of glory. Dust motes and pollen particles glow gold in each light column. The character stands at the intersection of two light beams, doubly illuminated, the only still point in a scene of overwhelming motion.

**PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE:** Ultra-wide cinematic photography, 16:9 widescreen. Sony A7R V with Sigma 14mm f/1.8 Art lens — foreground peony details in sharp focus, distant rusted iron framework visible through multiple layers of cherry blossom curtains. Warm golden tones for flowers, cool iron-grey for infrastructure. Cinematic color grading, film grain. The feeling: nature's verdict on civilization, rendered in pink. 4K ultra-high resolution.
