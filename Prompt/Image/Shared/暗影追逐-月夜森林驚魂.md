### 暗影追逐 - 月夜森林驚魂

可愛角色在月光下的神秘森林裡開心散步，完全沒注意到背後有一個巨大的暗影生物越來越近——直到最後一秒回頭，被嚇到彈飛！

---

## Prompt Template

A thrilling 5-second video of **[角色描述："a cheerful Pikachu walking carefree" / "a happy Kirby humming along" / "a tiny Totoro bouncing contentedly" / "an oblivious chibi adventurer skipping forward"]** strolling through a moonlit forest, completely unaware that a massive shadow creature is stalking it from behind—building to a hilarious jump-scare moment.

**Phase 1 - Peaceful Walk (0-2s):** A beautiful moonlit forest at night—tall trees with gnarled trunks draped in moss, silver moonlight filtering through the canopy creating dappled light pools on the forest floor. Soft blue-green bioluminescent mushrooms glow along the path. The character walks happily along the dirt path, **[開心動作："swinging its arms with each step, occasionally looking up at fireflies with wonder" / "humming silently with eyes closed, body swaying rhythmically" / "stopping to sniff a glowing flower, then continuing with a satisfied bounce" / "doing little skips, feet barely touching the ground, radiating pure bliss"]**. Expression is pure innocent joy—no sense of danger whatsoever. The forest is enchanting and peaceful. But in the deep background, between the dark tree trunks, barely visible—two enormous glowing eyes open. The viewer notices. The character does NOT. The shadow is massive, dwarfing the trees, but blending perfectly with the darkness.

**Phase 2 - Growing Threat (2-3.5s):** The character continues walking forward, oblivious. But behind it, the shadow creature **[暗影逼近："slides between trees with fluid, liquid-dark movement, its silhouette growing larger with each frame—now visible as a massive grinning entity towering above the canopy" / "creeps closer on all fours, enormous clawed hands reaching forward silently, mouth stretched in an impossibly wide smile" / "rises upward like a dark tide, its body made of swirling shadow particles, eyes locked on the tiny character below" / "materializes from the darkness itself, tree shadows bending and stretching to form its body, reaching one enormous finger toward the character's back"]**. The moonlight flickers as the shadow passes through it. The forest animals flee silently—an owl takes flight, rabbits dart into burrows—but the character notices NOTHING. It even does something extra oblivious: **[反差行為："stops to pick up a pretty rock, examining it closely, completely blocking its peripheral vision" / "starts dancing in a moonbeam, spinning around with eyes closed" / "sits down on a log to rest, sighing contentedly, the shadow looming directly behind the log" / "waves at a firefly, turning its back completely to the approaching darkness"]**. The shadow's grin widens. One enormous dark hand hovers directly above the character. Tension is MAXIMUM.

**Phase 3 - Jump Scare & Payoff (3.5-5s):** Something makes the character pause. A cold breeze. A sound. It slowly turns around and looks up. And UP. And **UP**. The shadow creature fills the entire frame above it—enormous glowing eyes, a crescent-moon grin stretching wide. Beat of pure frozen terror. The character's expression **[驚嚇反應："SHATTERS—eyes bulging to 3x their size, mouth dropping open so far it stretches past its body, every hair/fur standing straight up like static electricity" / "goes through five stages of grief in 0.5 seconds—confusion, recognition, denial, acceptance, ABSOLUTE PANIC" / "turns completely white, outline shaking like a cartoon vibration, soul visibly leaving its body as a tiny ghost" / "literally CRACKS like porcelain—fear so intense the character's face develops visible stress fractures"]**. Then—the character **ROCKETS** upward in panic, launching straight into the sky like a missile, leaving a character-shaped dust cloud and a comedic *WHOOOOSH* trail. The shadow creature blinks, surprised by the extreme reaction. It slowly shrugs. In the final frame, the character is a tiny twinkling star in the night sky, blinking with residual terror. The shadow waves goodbye.

**Visual Style:** Studio Ghibli-meets-horror aesthetic—enchanting moonlit forest rendered in rich painterly watercolor style with deep teals, midnight blues, and silver moonlight. Bioluminescent elements provide warm accent colors. The shadow creature is pure animated darkness—no hard edges, constantly shifting, defined only by negative space and glowing features. Character rendered in cute cel-shaded anime style, creating maximum contrast with the atmospheric horror background. Comedy timing is everything—the threat is played straight until the punchline lands.

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format (TikTok/Reels/Shorts optimized), cool moonlit color grading (7500K blue-silver) with warm bioluminescent accents (3000K amber-green), atmospheric fog and volumetric moonlight rays, particle effects for shadow creature's shifting form, dramatic camera shake on the jump-scare reveal, speed ramp from slow tension to ultra-fast launch, sound design: crickets and gentle forest ambiance, subtle low-frequency rumble building as shadow approaches, dead silence before the turn-around, massive orchestral STING on the reveal, cartoon *ZING* rocket launch sound, distant twinkling *ding* as character becomes a star.

## Example

A thrilling 5-second video of **a cheerful Pikachu** strolling through a moonlit forest, completely unaware that a massive Gengar shadow is stalking it from behind—building to a hilarious jump-scare moment.

**Phase 1 - Peaceful Walk (0-2s):** A breathtaking moonlit forest at night—ancient trees with twisted trunks draped in silver moss, pale moonlight streaming through the canopy in ethereal beams. Soft teal bioluminescent mushrooms dot the forest floor, and tiny fireflies drift like floating lanterns. Pikachu walks happily along the winding dirt path, swinging its stubby arms with each bouncy step, occasionally looking up at passing fireflies with sparkly-eyed wonder. Its red cheek patches glow faintly in the darkness like tiny warm lanterns. Expression is pure innocent bliss—big round eyes full of curiosity, small smile, ears perked up and swaying. The forest feels enchanting and safe. But deep in the background, between two massive tree trunks barely visible in the shadows—two enormous purple-white eyes slowly open. A crescent grin materializes in the darkness. Gengar. The viewer sees it immediately. Pikachu does not. The shadow is ENORMOUS, easily four stories tall, blending with the forest darkness like it's made of night itself.

**Phase 2 - Growing Threat (2-3.5s):** Pikachu continues its cheerful walk, humming silently with closed eyes. Behind it, Gengar's massive shadow form slides between trees with fluid, liquid movement—its silhouette growing larger with each frame, now clearly visible as a colossal grinning phantom towering above the tree canopy. Its body is made of shifting purple-black shadow particles, two enormous hands reaching forward with clawed fingers. The moonlight physically dims as Gengar passes through the beams, shadows bending unnaturally. Forest creatures flee silently—an owl takes flight from a branch, glowing mushrooms dim in fear, fireflies scatter in panic formation. Pikachu notices NOTHING. It stops to pick up a perfectly round acorn, holding it up to examine with one eye closed, completely absorbed. The Lightning Tail casts a tiny shadow that's swallowed by Gengar's massive darkness. Gengar's grin widens impossibly, stretching past the edges of its face. One enormous shadow claw hovers directly above Pikachu's unsuspecting head. Ekans peeks from behind a tree root, sweating nervously at the scene.

**Phase 3 - Jump Scare & Payoff (3.5-5s):** A cold wind ruffles Pikachu's fur the wrong way. Its ears twitch. The acorn drops. Pikachu slowly turns around. Looks up. Gengar's face fills THE ENTIRE SKY—those massive Cheshire eyes and that impossible crescent grin hovering directly above, shadow body stretching across the forest like a dark aurora. One frozen beat. Pikachu's expression DETONATES—eyes bulging to three times their normal size, pupils shrinking to pinpoints, mouth dropping open so wide its jaw stretches past its body. Every strand of yellow fur stands perfectly straight like it's been electrocuted (ironic for an Electric type). Pikachu's cheeks OVERLOAD with panic electricity—sparks flying everywhere, illuminating the entire forest in strobing yellow light. Then Pikachu ROCKETS straight upward like a bottle rocket, leaving a Pikachu-shaped yellow lightning trail and a mushroom cloud of forest debris. *WHOOOOOOSH*. Gengar blinks, genuinely surprised by the velocity. It slowly tilts its massive head, looking up at the retreating yellow streak. Shrugs its enormous shoulders with a "was it something I said?" expression. Final frame: Pikachu is a tiny twinkling yellow star in the night sky between the real stars, still visibly shaking. Gengar waves one massive shadow hand goodbye, grin softer now—almost fond. The acorn rolls to a stop on the empty forest path.

**Visual Style:** Studio Ghibli enchanted forest aesthetic with horror undertones—rich watercolor palette of deep teals, midnight indigos, and silver moonlight, with warm amber bioluminescent accents. Gengar rendered as pure animated darkness—constantly shifting shadow particles, no hard edges, defined only by its glowing purple-white eyes and spectral grin. Pikachu in cute cel-shaded anime style with exaggerated squash-and-stretch on reactions. The tonal whiplash from atmospheric horror to slapstick comedy is the entire joke.

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format, cool moonlit grading (7500K) with warm Pikachu-cheek accent lighting (2800K amber), volumetric fog and god-rays through canopy, Gengar's shadow particle system with turbulence, dramatic Dutch angle and camera shake on jump-scare, speed ramp from 0.5x during tension to 4x on rocket launch, sound design: peaceful cricket ambiance with subtle sub-bass rumble growing, dead silence for the turn-around beat, MASSIVE orchestral brass sting on Gengar reveal, cartoon electric *BZZZT* overload, jet-engine *WHOOOOSH* on launch, gentle twinkling *ding* as Pikachu becomes a star, Gengar's soft ghostly chuckle.
