## Example

A small round character standing before a stone grave in the rain, holding a tiny traditional oil-paper umbrella with both hands, umbrella deliberately angled to cover the gravestone completely — leaving the character fully exposed to rain, visible raindrops beading on its round surface. Moss-covered stone path leading uphill, wet gray stone grave with faint carved inscriptions, scattered white chrysanthemums laid at the base, each petal holding a single raindrop.

Thin sticks of incense burning beside the grave, pale gray smoke rising in thin threads and dissolving into the rain. Overcast sky, layers of white-gray fog drifting through pine and willow branches in the background. The character's silhouette is small against the vast misty hillside, rows of stone graves fading into fog behind.

Cinematic ink-wash photography style, desaturated cool blue-gray palette with only two warm color accents — the amber glow of incense embers at ground level and the deep crimson pattern of the oil-paper umbrella held high, extreme soft diffused lighting from overcast sky, visible rain streaks in mid-ground, shallow depth of field with foreground willow branches gently blurred, 8K resolution, anamorphic lens, fine film grain, wide 2.39:1 cinematic ratio.

Mid-distance shot showing full scene — character tiny against vast misty hillside, both arms raised holding umbrella over grave while its own silhouette stands fully in open rain, fog softening distant rows of graves.
