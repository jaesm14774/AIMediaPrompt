## Example

A split-world ink-wash illustration in the style of Chinese guohua painting meets cinematic fantasy. The composition is divided horizontally by a still water surface that acts as a sacred mirror boundary.

Upper half — the living world: A small round pink character kneels on a mist-covered stone path before a weathered gravestone, burning incense sticks glowing with warm amber light. The environment is rendered in cool desaturated tones — pale grey mist, ink-washed pine trees, soft spring drizzle falling diagonally. The figure is small against the wide mountain landscape, evoking solitude and reverence.

Lower half (reflection world) — the spirit realm: The mirror image is subtly transformed. The stone path becomes a glowing jade walkway. The mist brightens into luminous gold fog. A silhouette of an ancestor figure stands in the reflection, facing upward toward the living world, their outline glowing with soft white light.

The boundary water surface is hyper-still and mirror-perfect, with a single incense wisp crossing from the upper world into the reflection. Volumetric light cascades from the spirit realm upward, illuminating the living figure from below. Desaturated blue-grey palette for the living side; warm amber-gold palette for the spirit side. Heavy ink textures, generous negative space (留白) in the sky and edges. Cinematic 2:1 aspect ratio. 8K resolution, traditional brush stroke texture overlaid on photorealistic rendering.
