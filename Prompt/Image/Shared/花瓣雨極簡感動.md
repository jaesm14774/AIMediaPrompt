### 花瓣雨極簡感動

在純白極簡畫布上，花瓣漫天飄落，渺小的角色在那一刻靜止——簡單乾淨，卻讓人看了鼻酸。

---

## Prompt Template

**[IP角色 with: 建議使用輪廓圓潤、造型簡潔的可愛角色，確保與極簡白底和諧共存 / "a small pink round character" / "a tiny fluffy bear cub" / "a small soft bunny"]** [情感瞬間 with: 決定畫面的敘事張力與情緒層次 / "standing still with one tiny hand gently outstretched, as if trying to catch a falling petal — just barely missing it" / "tilting head upward softly, eyes gently closed, a single petal just landing on the top of their head" / "facing away from the viewer, arms loosely at sides, quietly watching the petals fall alone"], positioned in the lower-center area of the frame,

surrounded by [花瓣種類 with: 決定畫面情緒色調 / "25~35 scattered soft pink cherry blossom petals, various sizes and gentle rotation angles, some translucent" / "30~40 pale white plum blossom petals softly drifting, delicate and sparse" / "20~30 warm golden-pink mixed autumn petals, bittersweet and nostalgic"],

pure white / soft off-white canvas background, absolutely no background elements, vast clean negative space filling 85% of the frame. Flat watercolor illustration style, round soft clean linework, minimal shading. Small light blue-gray drop shadow beneath the character for grounding. Petals scattered irregularly across entire canvas — varying sizes (0.3x~1.5x relative scale), random rotation angles, natural drifting pattern, no petal clusters. Character occupies only 12~15% of total frame area. Two emotional layers: solitude and a quiet moment of wonder or longing. Mono no aware aesthetic — the bittersweet beauty of impermanence, the ache of something beautiful passing. Soft even diffused lighting, no harsh shadows, warm white background glow. Clean digital art with gentle watercolor texture overlay, high quality illustration.
