### 銀河電台-高山天文台誓言 (Milky Way Radio Observatory Vow)

把「遠距攝影」換成更有故事性的「高山天文台」，但仍保留拍攝設備的真實細節：兩位 **[IP角色]** 在巨大射電望遠鏡與控制室燈光之間，透過耳機與對講機同步拍攝流星雨，並在訊號聲與星光下互相許下誓言。氛圍要更「科幻浪漫」且更有空間感。

---

## Prompt Template

Cinematic night scene at a high-altitude observatory during a meteor shower, the Milky Way arcing overhead with crisp star fields. A massive radio telescope dish (parabolic antenna) dominates the background, illuminated by soft safety lights. In the foreground, two **[IP角色：預設用「[IP角色]」，也可填 Kirby / Pikachu / Hello Kitty 等]** stand at an observation deck with a remote-controlled camera rig: a weather-sealed camera, a long telephoto lens **[鏡頭焦段：例如 “400mm f/2.8” / “600mm f/4”]**, a motorized pan-tilt head, cables neatly tied, a small monitor showing a live star-tracker overlay, and a handheld radio transceiver with a glowing LED.

Story action: they coordinate a “remote capture” — one listens on a headset and counts down, the other presses the shutter button; immediately after the shutter click, they clasp hands and whisper a vow under the meteor streaks. The vow should feel sincere and quiet, framed by science equipment and the vast sky.

Atmosphere details: thin frost on metal railings, condensation on the lens barrel, tiny status LEDs, a thermos cup with steam, a folded blanket, warning decals on the deck (no readable text). Meteors: 3–7 streaks with varied brightness, one bright fireball with a subtle flare. Background: distant city lights far below (very faint), low clouds under the observatory, subtle airglow at the horizon.

Lighting and color: cool cyan/indigo moonless night, silver starlight, with gentle warm tungsten glow spilling from the control-room window onto the characters. Composition: wide cinematic 16:9, strong leading lines from deck rails toward the sky, shallow depth of field on the characters, radio telescope slightly softened, meteors sharp. Ultra-detailed, high dynamic range, 4K, filmic grain, no text, no watermark.
