# 長卷敘事地圖 — 朱墨千年一覽無餘

## 核心概念

一幅自空中俯瞰的超長立軸水墨地圖，以「文化時間軸」取代「地理空間軸」——從最頂端的遠古山水，一路沿紅色絲帶向下鋪陳：聚落、古城、宮殿、街市、鐵路、摩天樓，每一個時代的建築與景觀如同詩句排列，由一條貫通全卷的朱砂紅綬帶串聯。[IP角色] 身著白袍，手持毛筆，佇立於長卷中段的「時間交匯點」，既是這幅地圖的創作者，也是這段歷史的見證者。長卷本身是活的——邊緣仍在「書寫中」，最底端的現代城市輪廓尚未完全成形。

## Prompt Template

**THE FIRST THING YOU SEE:** A single blazing crimson ribbon (朱砂紅綬帶) cuts vertically through the center of an infinite scroll of black ink and cream paper — it is the only color in the frame, a wound of red through a monochrome world, and it stops the eye IMMEDIATELY. Then the eye adjusts and realizes: the scroll IS the civilization. The black ink IS the city, the mountain, the thousand years. And the tiny white figure standing at the center — that is [IP角色], conducting it all into existence with a raised brush. THIS IS WHAT THE IMAGE IS.

A breathtaking ultra-vertical aerial panorama image of a **living ink-wash cultural scroll map (水墨文化地圖長卷)**, seen from a bird's-eye divine perspective — as if a god is looking straight down at an unfurling handscroll (手卷) that spans [文明時間跨度: "a thousand years of Chinese civilization, from primordial mountains to glass-tower skyline" / "the entire arc of Japanese culture, from Jomon forests to neon-lit Shinjuku" / "the layered history of [自定義城市或文化]"], its surface alive with ink still drying at the newest edge, the entire history of a civilization laid out as a topographic poem that you can read from top to bottom like a sentence.

**THE SCROLL ITSELF (長卷本體) — filling the entire frame:**

The scroll is physically present as an object: aged xuan paper (宣紙) with visible fiber texture, slightly yellowed at the ancient top section and crisp white at the contemporary bottom section — the paper itself ages as it descends, time written into the material. The scroll edges (卷軸) are visible at left and right — burnished lacquered wood rollers with jade end-caps (玉軸) at the ancient top, transitioning to simple modern paper edges at the bottom (the scroll has not yet been rolled and mounted at its newest section). A scholar's red wax seal (朱印) appears at intervals throughout the scroll wherever a new era begins.

**[IP角色] AS THE AXIS OF TIME (時間之軸) — the most important element in the frame despite being tiny:**

[IP角色] appears in flowing white hanfu robes (白袍廣袖) — a blazing white form against the surrounding ink-grey landscape, the second brightest element in the frame after the crimson ribbon, impossible to miss. They stand at approximately the 40% mark from the top of the scroll — the **temporal mid-point** where ancient and modern are in perfect tension. Despite being dwarfed by the vast civilization spread below them, [IP角色]'s posture radiates absolute authority: spine perfectly vertical, head slightly raised, as if the scroll unfurled BECAUSE they stood here, not the other way around.

Their white robes are visually complex: the fabric catches flying ink droplets from all directions — ink from the ancient sections above (warm grey, aged) and ink from the modern sections below (cool grey, fresh) — so the robes are STAINED with time itself, bearing the marks of every era. Both sleeves hang wide and catch the invisible wind of history. One hand holds a brush raised at shoulder height, its tip touching the crimson ribbon's midpoint — this is the contact point. The other hand is open, palm upward, as if receiving something from the sky. Their expression: not triumphant, not humble — PRESENT, fully alive, the only truly living thing on an ancient scroll.

The most dramatic detail: from [IP角色]'s feet where they stand on the scroll surface, ink-wash footprint rings (like stones dropped in water) radiate outward in concentric circles through the surrounding landscape — the very act of STANDING HERE has sent ripples through time. From their brush tip, the crimson thread extends in both directions — upward into antiquity, downward into the contemporary future — [IP角色] is physically holding the timeline together.

**THE CRIMSON NARRATIVE THREAD (朱砂紅敘事線) — the compositional spine:**

A single continuous crimson ribbon (朱砂紅綬帶) descends through the entire scroll in a gently undulating S-curve — it is simultaneously a river, a road, a wall, a railway line, a light-rail track, an elevated highway, depending on which section of the scroll it passes through. The ribbon is always recognizably the same ribbon — same width proportionally, same crimson hue — but its character shifts with each era:
- **Ancient section (top)**: The ribbon is a silk trade route (絲路), with camel caravans in microscopic ink-wash detail walking its length
- **Classical section (mid-upper)**: A canal (運河) with flat-bottomed boats and willow trees on its banks
- **Medieval section (mid)**: A city moat (護城河) surrounding a walled ancient capital
- **Modern section (lower)**: A light rail line (輕軌) whose stations are rendered in ink-wash style — the technology of the present expressed in the visual language of the past

**THE FIVE TEMPORAL ZONES (五個時代帶) — the scroll's chapters:**

[場景分區 with: 長卷的五個時代段落 / "ZONE 1 — PRIMORDIAL (洪荒時代, top 15%): [遠古自然場景: 'Pure Song Dynasty (宋代) ink wash landscape in the style of Guo Xi (郭熙) — enormous mountains rendered in deep charcoal with卷雲皴 (cloud-scroll texture strokes), waterfalls cascading in pure white ink, pine forests on peaks rendered as black ink gestures. No human presence except a single hermit pavilion barely visible in the mist. Crane pairs (丹頂鶴) visible in flight at the sky-mountain boundary' / '原始森林與巨石群的俯瞰水墨圖，以飛白技法描繪古木樹冠' / '荒野山脈與無名聚落，以濃淡墨色堆疊出海拔落差']. Ink tones: 80% deep charcoal, no red except the single crimson ribbon beginning its journey at this zone's southern edge. The paper grain at this zone is most yellowed and aged, with what appears to be authentic ancient foxing marks (霉斑)" / "ZONE 2 — CLASSICAL CIVILIZATION (古典城邦, 15-35%): [古典聚落場景: 'A complete classical Chinese city (古城) rendered in精細界畫 (precision architectural ink painting) style — city walls (城牆) with corner watchtowers visible at all four compass points, interior grid of streets with labeled market areas (市坊) in Chinese characters, imperial palace complex (宮殿群) at center rendered with maximum architectural detail (individual roof tile rows visible, painted beam-ends in charcoal ink, courtyard trees as ink gestures)' / '江戶時代城下町的俯瞰界畫，武家屋敷與商家町屋密布，護城河環繞' / '[自定義古典聚落]: walled city, temple complex, market district in period-accurate ink-wash architectural style']. Citizens rendered as ink-wash silhouettes creating depth despite the flat map perspective" / "ZONE 3 — TRANSITION & ENCOUNTER (交匯時代, 35-55%, where [IP角色] stands): This is the most visually complex zone — where [IP角色]'s authority as time-conductor is most visible. The crimson ribbon here WIDENS to three times its normal width, becoming a pulsing artery of creative energy at the exact point where [IP角色] stands. Ancient and modern architecture coexist: [交匯時代建築: '書院, 宗廟, 茶館 standing adjacent to clock towers, telegraph poles, early modern western-influenced facades' / '江戶末期黑船時代：武士道場與洋館比鄰而立' / '自定義文化交會期建築']. The ink spatters radiating from [IP角色]'s footstep-rings are largest here — some pure calligraphy marks, others mid-resolution into architectural forms" / "ZONE 4 — CONTEMPORARY (當代城市, 55-80%): [現代城市場景: 'Glass tower skyscrapers whose facades are rendered as ink-wash gradients (darker at base, lighter toward mist-dissolving summits), interchange highways as bold ink lines, a harbor with ink-wash vessels, an airport with planes as tiny abstract ink marks' / '新宿高層建築群的水墨俯瞰，電車路線如界畫細線縱橫' / '[自定義現代城市]: contemporary skyline, infrastructure grid, urban density rendered in ink-wash style']. The modern city grid becomes simultaneously a technical map and an abstract calligraphic composition. The crimson ribbon is now a light-rail elevated track" / "ZONE 5 — BECOMING (生成中, bottom 20%): The most radical zone — here the scroll is STILL BEING WRITTEN IN REAL TIME. Architecture appears in progressive states of incompletion: some buildings are full renderings, others are partial pen sketches, the very bottom shows only freshest ink marks — WET and glistening, actually spreading on the paper fiber as the viewer watches. The crimson ribbon at this section's base EXITS the scroll's edge, breaks the frame boundary, and continues as a physical brushstroke into the viewer's space — history has not ended. It is being written. NOW."]. At each zone boundary, vermillion seals (朱印) mark the transition in archaic script. The zones are separated not by hard borders but by zones of atmospheric mist (留白) — the in-between moments of history that belong to no era.

**MARGINALIA AND ANNOTATIONS (天地眉批):**

In the traditional blank margins (天頭地腳) on left and right of the scroll, notes and annotations appear in different calligraphic hands from different eras — observing the landscape in the main scroll. These annotations use period-appropriate scripts (oracle bone, seal, clerical, regular, running script) getting progressively more modern as they descend. The annotations are too small to read but their presence creates the feeling of an artwork that has been studied and annotated across generations.

**Technical specs:** Ultra-vertical portrait format 1:4 or 1:5 ratio, as if the scroll is 5 meters long viewed from directly above, color palette strictly limited to aged-cream (宣紙色), charcoal black spectrum (墨色 10 tonal values), and vermillion-crimson (朱砂紅) for the ribbon and seals only — NO OTHER COLORS except trace amounts of verdigris (石青) for distant mountain washes, ink tones shift from warm-grey (ancient) to cool-grey (modern) as the scroll progresses, the overall mood is the overwhelming emotion of witnessing an entire civilization's creative life compressed into a single vertical axis — humbling, majestic, and alive.

## 使用說明

1. 將 `[IP角色]` 替換為目標角色（例：Kirby / 龍貓 / 原創 OC / 品牌吉祥物）
2. 將 `[文明時間跨度]` 替換為目標文明範圍（例：「千年中華文明，從洪荒山水到玻璃摩天樓」）
3. 長卷的每個 ZONE 場景描述均有 `[自定義選項]` 佔位符，可指定特定城市或文化（台北版：在 ZONE 2 填入台北古城，ZONE 4 填入台北 101 天際線）
4. 五個時代帶可跨文化套用（日本版：繩文→平安→江戶→明治→現代；韓國版：三國→高麗→朝鮮→現代）
5. `[IP角色]` 的白袍可改為其他純色服裝，但白色在水墨背景中對比最強
6. 紅色綬帶的顏色可調整（金色 = 皇家/神聖感；藍色 = 現代/科技感；白色 = 純粹/空靈感）
7. ZONE 5「生成中」的概念最具傳播力，建議在社群發文時強調「歷史正在被書寫」的文案
