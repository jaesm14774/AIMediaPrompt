### 飛行俯瞰-葉子漂流奇境

縮小的 [IP角色] 趴伏在具有神秘故事感的巨大飛行道具上，鏡頭從正上方俯視，廣闊世界鋪滿畫面——畫面深處隱約可見一個若隱若現的光點或消逝中的地景，角色向前飛去，身後那片世界正在縮小；告別感與前行感並存，觀者會不由自問「他要去哪裡？」

---

## Prompt Template

Overhead top-down bird's eye view, camera directly above, looking straight down. **[IP角色]** is lying prone / belly-down on top of [飛行道具 with: 三種道具任選其一，皆帶獨特視覺奇觀 / "a giant translucent maple leaf — leaf veins filled with flowing starlight and tiny constellations, golden-amber glow pulsing like a heartbeat, edges slightly frayed as if it has carried its rider across a thousand skies" / "a torn ancient book page the size of a raft — faded text barely legible, gold-leaf illustrations of extinct creatures at its corners, the trailing edge still smoldering with cold blue fire as if torn from a dying spell" / "the last green leaf in a deep-winter world — impossibly large, vivid emerald against a grey frost landscape below, a single crack of light running down its spine like it is barely holding together"], the character's body filling most of the vessel surface, small hands gripping the edges tightly, clothing and hair streaming backward in fierce wind — vivid motion blur on fabric, hair ribbons trailing like ink brushstrokes.

The vast landscape below fills the entire frame: [背景世界 with: 每個背景都藏有一個「為什麼重要」的視覺敘事 / "an endless autumn forest canopy stretching to the horizon — but at the very bottom edge of the frame, barely visible, a cluster of rooftops slowly sinking beneath rising floodwaters, warm amber treetops forming a seamless carpet that swallows everything" / "a boundless fantasy ocean, deep teal water with scattered tiny islands — directly below, one small crescent-shaped island is half-submerged, its lighthouse still blinking faintly, the sea still rising" / "a sprawling misty valley between ancient mountain ridges — far ahead at the horizon, a single warm light glows through violet fog, too distant to identify, as if the whole journey exists only to reach it"], scale contrast emphasizing the character's tininess against the overwhelming, story-charged world.

The character looks forward with wide, determined eyes — peaceful yet carrying the weight of departure; behind them, in the far corner of the frame, something small is disappearing into distance or water, already half-gone. Speed rendered through: motion blur on the vessel's trailing edges, a faint luminous contrail dissolving behind, warm golden-hour or cold blue-dusk light cutting diagonally across the frame, soft lens flare. The atmosphere holds both wonder and farewell — "goodbye to everything below, forward toward that one distant light."

Studio Ghibli-inspired painterly illustration, soft volumetric lighting, warm-to-cool color gradient from character to horizon, whimsical yet emotionally resonant. 16:9 cinematic composition, ultra-detailed, 8K.

## Example

Overhead top-down bird's eye view, camera directly above, looking straight down. A small cute chibi character is lying prone / belly-down on top of a giant translucent maple leaf — leaf veins filled with flowing starlight and tiny constellations, golden-amber glow pulsing like a heartbeat, edges slightly frayed as if it has carried its rider across a thousand skies. The character's body fills most of the leaf surface, small hands gripping the edges tightly, clothing and hair streaming backward in fierce wind — vivid motion blur on fabric, hair ribbons trailing like ink brushstrokes.

The vast landscape below fills the entire frame: an endless autumn forest canopy stretching to the horizon, warm amber and orange treetops forming a seamless carpet — but at the very bottom edge of the frame, barely visible, a cluster of rooftops slowly sinking beneath rising floodwaters, the world the character has left already half-swallowed by the rising sea. Scale contrast emphasizes the character's tininess against this overwhelming, story-charged world.

The character looks forward with wide, determined eyes — peaceful yet carrying the weight of departure; in the far corner behind them, the last rooftop disappears beneath water, already half-gone. Speed rendered through: motion blur on the leaf's trailing edges, a faint starlight contrail dissolving behind, warm golden-hour rays cutting diagonally across the frame, soft lens flare. The atmosphere holds both wonder and farewell — "goodbye to everything below, forward toward that one distant light."

Studio Ghibli-inspired painterly illustration, soft volumetric lighting, warm-to-cool color gradient from character to horizon, whimsical yet emotionally resonant. 16:9 cinematic composition, ultra-detailed, 8K.
