### 馬年新春 - 馬上有錢立體春聯

以傳統春聯的構圖為基底，超Q角色和小馬從紅底金字的春聯中「跳出來」，結合「馬上有錢」的吉祥雙關語，立體剪紙與浮雕風格

## Prompt Template

A stunning 3D pop-up paper art Chinese New Year spring couplet (春聯) come to life. The composition is a vertical red scroll with ornate gold borders and traditional cloud-pattern trim. The main golden calligraphy text **[春聯文字："'馬上有錢' (Money Comes Immediately / Literally: Money On Horse)" / "'馬到成功' (Success Upon Horse's Arrival)" / "'萬馬奔騰' (Ten Thousand Horses Galloping)" / "'龍馬精神' (Dragon-Horse Vitality)"]** is written in elegant brushstroke calligraphy—BUT the characters are cracking and bursting open as 3D characters physically EMERGE from the paper surface. Center focal point: **[主角角色："pink round Kirby" / "chibi Pikachu" / "tiny round Totoro" / "adorable chibi Snoopy" / "cute small Doraemon"]** sitting on top of a super cute chibi horse that is leaping OUT of the scroll surface, **[立體姿態："the horse's front hooves have already broken through the paper plane, 3D paper fragments and gold leaf flying everywhere, the character riding triumphantly with arms raised, golden coins and ingots cascading from a treasure bag on the horse's saddle, paper-cut 福 characters fluttering around like butterflies" / "the horse galloping forward through a tear in the scroll, red paper curling back in layers revealing a golden light dimension behind, the character clinging to the mane with excited expression, a trail of red envelope confetti and cherry blossom petals erupting from the breach" / "the horse mid-rear, front legs pawing at the air above the scroll surface while back legs are still flat paper-cut style, creating a half-2D half-3D magical transition, the character balancing on top with a giant calligraphy brush painting '2026' in mid-air with golden ink"]**. Supporting elements: **[配角點綴："tiny paper-cut round companions folding themselves into 3D from flat red paper on the scroll margins, some halfway transformed—flat legs but 3D heads, looking confused" / "miniature origami animals (crane, fish, rabbit) unfolding from the decorative border pattern, hopping around the emerging horse, one origami cat riding a paper airplane made from a red envelope" / "small golden paper-cut coins with square holes stacking themselves into towers along the scroll edges, tiny paper figures in different action poses popping up like pop-up book elements"]**. The scroll background remains traditional flat red with gold accents, creating dramatic contrast with the 3D elements breaking free. Paper texture is visible—fiber grain, slight crinkle, embossed gold foil on calligraphy. Layered depth: flat paper background → semi-raised decorative elements → fully 3D main characters. Dramatic warm lighting from the golden breach, casting paper shadows, gold particle effects and paper confetti floating in air. Mixed-media art style: traditional Chinese paper-cutting meets modern 3D pop-up book illustration, rich red and gold festive palette, tilt-shift photography effect for miniature feel, 8K resolution, ultra detailed.
