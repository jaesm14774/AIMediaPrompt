### 黏土動畫甜點王國

可愛角色在甜點王國的定格黏土動畫風格，萊卡工作室質感

---

## Prompt Template

A stop-motion claymation style illustration of **[在此填入可愛角色：如 round clay cat / squishy clay bear / bumpy clay penguin / lumpy clay frog]** exploring a whimsical dessert kingdom, currently [互動場景 with: 選擇一種與甜點的互動 / "climbing a towering macaron stack like colorful stepping stones, reaching for a cherry on top" / "swimming in a giant pudding pond with cookie crumb islands and wafer stick bridges" / "riding a donut car down a chocolate syrup highway with sprinkle guardrails" / "building a sandcastle-style fortress using sugar cubes and candy cane pillars"], surrounded by **[甜點景觀：如 lollipop trees / gumdrop mountains / cotton candy clouds / waffle cone towers]**.

The character is made of smooth matte clay with visible fingerprint textures and subtle surface imperfections that give authentic handmade feeling. Simple dot eyes and a tiny curved smile. The body has that satisfying squishy quality where you can imagine pressing it and it would slowly bounce back. Tiny stubby limbs that are slightly uneven in the charming way of handcrafted figures.

Environment: The entire dessert kingdom is built from real confectionery reimagined as landscape - frosting becomes snow-capped mountains, chocolate bars form building foundations, cookies pave the roads, hard candies are gemstones. Everything has that miniature diorama quality with slightly exaggerated proportions. Depth achieved through layered set pieces like a theatrical stage.

Art style: Laika Studios / Aardman Animation aesthetic - warm directional lighting creating soft shadows, shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh on background elements. Color palette is candy-bright but not garish: pastel pinks, mint greens, butter yellows, with rich chocolate and caramel accents. The lighting suggests a cozy indoor studio setup.

Technical: Macro photography look with f/2.8 aperture simulation, subtle dust particles catching light, perfect focus on character with gradual falloff. Surface textures are key - the clay should look touchable, the sweets should look edible. 4K resolution capturing every tiny imperfection that makes it feel real and handmade rather than CG.
