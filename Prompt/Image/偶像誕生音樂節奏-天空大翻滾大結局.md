### 偶像誕生音樂節奏 - 天空大翻滾大結局
# Sky Tumble Grand Finale

> **系列 / Series：** 偶像誕生音樂節奏短影片系列 · 將 `[IP角色]` 替換為圓滾滾可愛角色（Kirby / Pikachu / Pusheen 等）。共用規格見同資料夾 `偶像誕生音樂節奏短影片系列.md`。

**概念 / Concept：** 從一個角色變成無數個，在夕陽天空中自由翻滾，然後停下凝視同一個夕陽——獨旅與同行並存。

---

## Prompt Template

A euphoric, bittersweet 7-second finale — **[IP角色]** alone, then EVERYWHERE — tumbling through sunset sky — shared wonder.

**Scene 1 - One in the Sky (0-1.5s)**

SINGLE **[IP角色]** in `[夕陽天空：warm gradient — deep purple-blue top to amber-gold horizon]`. Small figure, slow tumble, arms out.  

**表情 / Expression：** `[peaceful, wistful solitude / content alone]`. Few tiny stars despite sunset. Scale: enormous sky, small character

**Scene 2 - The Multiplication (1.5-3.0s)**

More **[IP角色]** from off-frame — same design. Then more. **出現方式（擇一）：**  
- `"like they were always there, camera just didn't see"`  
- `"materialized from clouds"`  
- `"fell from above, tumbling joyfully down"`  

**數量 / Count：** 5 → 10 → by 3.0s frame DENSE, 15–20 visible. Same character, casual joy, varied poses/rotations

**Scene 3 - The Tumble Party (3.0-5.0s)**

Chaos — joyful. Many **[IP角色]** tumbling in sunset. **翻滾 / Moves（擇一）：**  
- `"spinning lazily / rolling diagonally"`  
- `"two pass close, eye contact mid-tumble"`  
- `"full summersault / face-down float like asleep mid-tumble"`  

**天空演化 / Sky evolution：** deepest amber-gold, purple deepening toward night; small stars overhead. Crowd fills frame with warmth

**Scene 4 - The Pause (5.0-6.0s)**

ON A BEAT — ALL STOP. Every **[IP角色]** rights-up, looks same direction: `[horizon sunset / large glowing star / unspoken agreement]`. Silence beat. **表情：** same soft wonder — one feeling: beauty

**Scene 5 - Final Wide (6.0-7.0s)**

Slow pull-back — widest shot. Sky full of glowing **[IP角色]**, all paused, same gaze — constellation of one character × many. Sunset gold on all.  

Edges inward: gentle fade into sky — not vanish, become sky — until one remains center. Original. Alone again, not lonely.  

**最後：** `[soft wondering smile → brief look at camera — knowing — back to sunset]`. Fade to warm golden light

**Visual Style**

Solitude vs togetherness. Multiplication feels natural, not alarming. Final return to one completes emotional circle. Sunset carries ending-as-beginning feeling.

**Technical specs**

9:16 vertical, 4K, 60fps. Camera: static mid sections, slow pull-back finale. Organic placement (no rigid grid). Sunset: volumetric light. Sound: quiet → choral/orchestral swell as crowd fills → soft single note as reduces to one. The all-stop beat = profound punctuation.
