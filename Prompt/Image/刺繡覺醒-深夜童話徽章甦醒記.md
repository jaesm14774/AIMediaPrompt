### 刺繡覺醒 - 深夜童話徽章甦醒記 (Embroidery Awakens — Midnight Fairy Tale)

深夜時分，精美刺繡徽章上的角色悄悄活了過來，從金色繡框中探出身子，在月光灑落的布面上展開一場微型童話冒險。

## Prompt Template

A magical, heartwarming illustration capturing the exact moment **[在此填入角色名：如 Kirby / Pikachu / Totoro / a tiny fox spirit]** awakens inside an exquisite embroidered patch and begins climbing out of the golden thread border into the real world. The scene is set on [刺繡底布 with: 角色刺繡所在的布料 / "a soft white terry cloth towel draped over a bedside chair" / "a child's beloved cotton handkerchief on a moonlit pillow" / "a grandmother's silk cushion cover on an old rocking chair" / "a vintage denim jacket pocket, patch sewn on with love"].

The character is caught mid-emergence — [覺醒瞬間 with: 從刺繡中甦醒的樣子 / "the lower half still flat embroidery threads in satin stitch, while the upper body has become soft, round, and three-dimensional, tiny hands gripping the gold-thread frame edge, pulling itself out with a sleepy yawn" / "one arm already 3D and reaching beyond the circular patch border, fingers touching the real fabric beneath with wide-eyed wonder, while the rest of the body remains woven in colorful silk threads" / "standing on the embroidered border like a tiny balcony, stretching after a long sleep, one foot still stitched flat to the fabric surface, the other now plump and dangling over the edge"]. The transition zone between flat embroidery and living 3D form is the visual miracle — threads visibly unraveling and re-forming into soft skin, fur, or rounded shapes, with a few loose golden threads trailing behind like fairy-tale breadcrumbs.

Around the awakened character, [微型童話場景 with: 在布面上展開的小冒險 / "tiny embroidered flowers on the surrounding fabric have also begun blooming into 3D — miniature roses unfurling real petals, a stitched butterfly lifting one wing off the cloth, creating a garden path for the character to explore" / "the character has left tiny real footprints on the terry cloth surface, and is now following a trail of glowing golden thread that leads across the towel like a fairy-tale road" / "other small embroidered motifs nearby — stars, clouds, a tiny moon — are also half-awakening, creating a miniature midnight sky above the character as it takes its first steps on the cloth landscape"]. The fabric surface becomes a vast terrain from the character's tiny perspective — towel loops are rolling hills, cloth folds are mountains, wrinkles are valleys.

Lighting: Soft moonlight streaming from a nearby window, casting long blue-silver shadows across the fabric. The character and the golden thread border emit a faint warm inner glow — the magic that brought the embroidery to life. Firefly-like golden sparkles drift upward from where threads are transforming. The contrast between cool moonlight and warm embroidery-glow creates an intimate, fairy-tale atmosphere.

Art style: Hyper-detailed macro photography aesthetic meets storybook illustration. Tilt-shift effect making the fabric world feel vast. Warm golden and cool moonlit blue color palette. The embroidery stitches are photographically precise — you can count individual threads. The 3D character portion has soft Pixar-like rendering. Depth of field focuses on the character at the border, with the surrounding cloth world softly blurred. 4K resolution, 1:1 square format.

## Example

A magical, heartwarming illustration capturing the exact moment **Kirby** awakens inside an exquisite embroidered patch and begins climbing out of the golden thread border into the real world. The scene is set on a soft white terry cloth towel draped over a child's bedside chair, moonlight falling across the fabric.

Kirby is caught mid-emergence — the lower half still flat embroidery threads in beautiful satin stitch, pink silk threads carefully laid in parallel rows forming Kirby's round body, while the upper body has become soft, round, and gloriously three-dimensional. Kirby's tiny hands grip the ornate gold-thread frame edge, pulling itself out with a sleepy yawn, eyes half-closed, mouth open in a round "o" of drowsy wonder. The transition zone is the visual miracle — you can see individual pink embroidery threads unraveling from the flat surface and curling upward, re-forming into Kirby's smooth, plump 3D skin. A few loose golden threads from the border trail behind Kirby like a fairy-tale cape.

Around Kirby, tiny embroidered stars on the surrounding towel fabric have begun popping into 3D — miniature golden stars lifting off the cloth surface and floating upward, spinning slowly. A small embroidered crescent moon nearby is half-flat, half-real, casting its own tiny glow. Kirby has left adorable round footprints pressed into the soft terry cloth as it walked to the edge of the patch. The towel's looped texture forms a vast rolling landscape from Kirby's miniature perspective — each terry loop is a soft white hill, the purple trim edge of the towel is a distant mountain range.

Lighting: Soft moonlight from a bedroom window casts long blue-silver shadows across the towel terrain. Kirby and the golden border emit a faint warm glow — peachy-pink from Kirby's emerging body, rich gold from the thread frame. Tiny firefly-like sparkles of golden light drift upward from where embroidery threads are transforming into life. The contrast between cool moonlight and warm embroidery-magic creates an intimate fairy-tale midnight atmosphere.

Art style: Hyper-detailed macro photography meets storybook illustration. Tilt-shift effect making the towel world feel vast and explorable. You can count individual embroidery stitches in Kirby's still-flat lower half. The 3D upper half has soft Pixar-like rendering with subsurface scattering in Kirby's pink skin. Depth of field focuses on Kirby at the golden border, with the cloth landscape softly blurred into dreamy bokeh. 4K resolution, 1:1 square format.
