### 吉祥物便利袋大逃脫 (Mascot Shopping Bag Breakout)

買太多吉祥物了！購物袋炸開，Q 版角色們爭先恐後地從袋口冒出頭來，擠成一團。

---

## Prompt Template

A charming flat-style kawaii illustration — a bulging convenience store plastic shopping bag sitting on the ground, stretched to its absolute limit and nearly bursting. The bag is translucent white with [購物袋圖案 with: 袋上的可愛設計 / "a simple printed store logo of a smiling star and pastel stripes" / "a cute hand-drawn bear face logo with 'THANK YOU' printed in rounded bubbly font" / "a repeating pattern of tiny hearts and paw prints in light pink ink"]. From the top opening, **[在此填入吉祥物角色：如 Kirby / Pikachu / Chiikawa / Molly / 自訂角色]** and five mini versions of itself are all desperately trying to escape at once — the main character's round squishy face pops out from the bag opening with cheeks squished by two smaller ones pressing from both sides, tiny stubby arms flailing upward. Two more peek out from a small tear in the side of the bag, one winking mischievously. Another has already tumbled out and sits on the ground next to the bag looking dazed with swirly eyes and a content smile.

Each mini character has a slightly different expression and accessory — one wears a tiny price tag still attached to its head, one is tangled in receipt paper like a scarf, one hugs a smaller version of itself (recursion!), one has a coin stuck to its forehead, and one holds a miniature shopping basket. The bag's handles are stretched thin like noodles, one handle has already snapped.

Scattered around the base: crumpled receipts, a loyalty card, loose coins, a squished onigiri, and one lone chopstick. A small puddle of spilled milk tea with boba pearls rolling away adds to the chaos.

Art style: Clean kawaii line art illustration with soft pastel color fills — primarily white, pink, cream, and light yellow. Thick rounded outlines (like Chiikawa or Sumikko Gurashi aesthetic), minimal shading using only soft blush marks on cheeks and simple drop shadows. Characters have dot eyes, tiny curved mouths, and exaggerated squishy proportions (head-to-body ratio 3:1). The composition is centered and vertical, the bag as the focal point with characters spilling outward in all directions. White or very light cream background with scattered small sparkle marks (✦) and tiny floating hearts. Square format (1:1), high resolution illustration.

## Example

A charming flat-style kawaii illustration — a bulging convenience store plastic shopping bag sitting on the ground, stretched to its absolute limit and nearly bursting. The bag is translucent white with a cute hand-drawn bear face logo with 'THANK YOU' printed in rounded bubbly font. From the top opening, **Kirby** and five mini Kirbys are all desperately trying to escape at once — the main Kirby's round pink squishy face pops out from the bag opening with cheeks squished by two smaller Kirbys pressing from both sides, tiny stubby arms flailing upward. Two more mini Kirbys peek out from a small tear in the side of the bag, one winking mischievously. Another mini Kirby has already tumbled out and sits on the ground next to the bag looking dazed with swirly eyes and a content smile.

Each mini Kirby has a slightly different expression and accessory — one wears a tiny price tag still attached to its head, one is tangled in receipt paper like a scarf, one hugs an even tinier Kirby (recursion!), one has a coin stuck to its forehead, and one holds a miniature shopping basket. The bag's handles are stretched thin like noodles, one handle has already snapped.

Scattered around the base: crumpled receipts, a loyalty card, loose coins, a squished onigiri, and one lone chopstick. A small puddle of spilled milk tea with boba pearls rolling away adds to the chaos.

Art style: Clean kawaii line art illustration with soft pastel color fills — primarily white, pink, cream, and light yellow. Thick rounded outlines (like Chiikawa or Sumikko Gurashi aesthetic), minimal shading using only soft blush marks on cheeks and simple drop shadows. Characters have dot eyes, tiny curved mouths, and exaggerated squishy proportions (head-to-body ratio 3:1). The composition is centered and vertical, the bag as the focal point with Kirbys spilling outward in all directions. White or very light cream background with scattered small sparkle marks (✦) and tiny floating hearts. Square format (1:1), high resolution illustration.
