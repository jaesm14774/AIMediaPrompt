### 吉祥物超市搶購自己的周邊 (Mascot Supermarket Self-Merch Scramble)

Q 版吉祥物衝進超市瘋搶印著自己臉的周邊商品——抱著自己造型的枕頭、搶自己圖案的零食、推著堆成山的購物車，完全是自戀式購物狂歡。

---

## Prompt Template

A delightfully absurd kawaii illustration of a miniature supermarket aisle — **[在此填入吉祥物角色：如 Kirby / Pikachu / Chiikawa / Molly / 自訂角色]** in multiple copies, all frantically shopping for merchandise featuring THEIR OWN FACE. The scene captures peak self-obsessed shopping chaos in the cutest possible way.

**The Shopping Frenzy:**

Left side of the aisle — CHARACTER-FACE PRODUCT SHELVES: Neatly organized (but being rapidly depleted) shelves stocked with products all bearing the character's face — [商品種類 with: 角色周邊商品 / "character-face printed tissue boxes, character-shaped cookies in cellophane bags, character-print socks hanging on hooks, a stack of character-face ceramic mugs, and character-pattern tote bags folded on the top shelf" / "character-shaped gummy candy in clear jars, character-print adhesive bandages, character-face ice cream bars in the freezer section below, character-embroidered handkerchiefs, and character-design phone cases on spinning racks" / "character-face instant ramen cups stacked in a pyramid, character-print stationery sets, character-shaped bath bombs in pastel colors, character-design umbrellas in a barrel, and character-face coin purses hanging from display hooks"]. Some shelf spaces are already empty with small 'SOLD OUT' price tags dangling.

Center — THE SHOPPERS: One character pushes an overflowing miniature shopping cart — items piled so high that only its eyes peek over the mountain of merchandise, stubby arms barely reaching the cart handle. Behind it, another character drags a basket across the floor because it's too heavy to carry — filled entirely with plush toys of itself, one plush arm flopping over the edge. A third character rides inside a shopping cart being pushed by a fourth — the rider character hugs an enormous body pillow version of itself that is bigger than the actual character. A fifth character has discovered the last item on a high shelf and is standing on a wobbly tower of stacked products trying to reach it, about to topple.

Right side — THE AFTERMATH: One character sits on the floor surrounded by opened packages, having started a "quality inspection" right in the aisle — comparing a keychain version of itself to its own face using a tiny hand mirror, looking deeply satisfied. Another character is at a self-checkout machine, but the scanner keeps beeping errors because it's scanning the character's own face instead of the product barcode.

Small environmental details: a floor wet-sign shaped like the character, shopping music notes floating in the air (♪♫), a dropped shopping list with items checked off (drawn as tiny character faces), a "BUY 2 GET 1" promotional sign, and a tiny store employee (same character in a vest) looking exhausted leaning against a shelf.

Art style: Vibrant kawaii illustration with flat color fills — cheerful palette of pastel pink, baby blue, soft yellow, mint green, and white. Clean thick outlines with rounded ends, characters in chibi proportions with dot eyes and simple expressive mouths. Bird's-eye slight tilt perspective showing the full aisle from above at a gentle angle. The supermarket shelves create natural frame lines. Bright even lighting like fluorescent store lights — minimal shadows, maximum clarity and readability. Small decorative sparkles, motion blur lines on running characters, and floating emotion marks. Clean white floor with subtle tile grid lines. Square format (1:1), high resolution illustration.

## Example

A delightfully absurd kawaii illustration of a miniature supermarket aisle — **Kirby** in multiple copies, all frantically shopping for merchandise featuring THEIR OWN FACE. The scene captures peak self-obsessed shopping chaos in the cutest possible way.

**The Shopping Frenzy:**

Left side of the aisle — KIRBY-FACE PRODUCT SHELVES: Neatly organized (but being rapidly depleted) shelves stocked with products all bearing Kirby's round pink face — Kirby-face printed tissue boxes, Kirby-shaped cookies in cellophane bags, Kirby-print pink socks hanging on hooks, a stack of Kirby-face ceramic mugs, and Kirby-pattern tote bags folded on the top shelf. Some shelf spaces are already empty with small 'SOLD OUT' price tags dangling.

Center — THE SHOPPERS: One Kirby pushes an overflowing miniature shopping cart — items piled so high that only its dot eyes peek over the mountain of pink merchandise, stubby arms barely reaching the cart handle. Behind it, another Kirby drags a basket across the floor because it's too heavy to carry — filled entirely with plush Kirbys, one plush arm flopping over the edge. A third Kirby rides inside a shopping cart being pushed by a fourth — the rider Kirby hugs an enormous body pillow version of itself that is three times bigger than the actual Kirby. A fifth Kirby has discovered the last Kirby-shaped cookie box on a high shelf and is standing on a wobbly tower of stacked products trying to reach it, about to topple.

Right side — THE AFTERMATH: One Kirby sits on the floor surrounded by opened packages, having started a "quality inspection" right in the aisle — comparing a keychain Kirby to its own face using a tiny hand mirror, looking deeply satisfied with a content blush. Another Kirby is at a self-checkout machine, but the scanner keeps beeping errors because it's scanning Kirby's own pink face instead of the product barcode.

Small environmental details: a floor wet-sign shaped like Kirby, shopping music notes floating in the air (♪♫), a dropped shopping list with items checked off (drawn as tiny Kirby faces), a "BUY 2 GET 1" promotional sign, and a tiny store employee Kirby in a green vest looking exhausted leaning against a shelf.

Art style: Vibrant kawaii illustration with flat color fills — cheerful palette of pastel pink, baby blue, soft yellow, mint green, and white. Clean thick outlines with rounded ends, Kirbys in chibi proportions with dot eyes and simple expressive mouths. Bird's-eye slight tilt perspective showing the full aisle from above at a gentle angle. The supermarket shelves create natural frame lines. Bright even lighting like fluorescent store lights — minimal shadows, maximum clarity and readability. Small decorative sparkles, motion blur lines on running Kirbys, and floating emotion marks. Clean white floor with subtle tile grid lines. Square format (1:1), high resolution illustration.
