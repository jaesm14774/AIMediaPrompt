### 堵壁擠爆大合照

一群 IP 角色全擠進古厝堵壁中央那個小小的山水畫框裡面拍團體照——臉被壓扁貼在畫面表面、互相推擠疊羅漢、有人被夾在角落只露出一隻手。原本畫中的古典文人被擠到邊邊角角、一臉不爽。外面的牡丹花、菱格紋裝飾完全沒被動到，優雅古典如初。

---

## Prompt Template

A hyper-detailed comedic image of **[IP 角色群組："Kirby, Pikachu, Totoro, Doraemon, Snoopy, Hello Kitty, and Mario" / "a chaotic mix of 8-10 different adorable chibi characters" / "a crowd of assorted cute IP characters numbering at least 7"]** ALL crammed inside the tiny central landscape painting panel of a traditional Southern Fujian temple wall relief (堵壁), squishing against each other and pressing against the painting's surface like sardines in a can — while the surrounding ornamental borders remain completely pristine and undisturbed.

**The Central Painting (CHAOS ZONE):** The traditional landscape painting panel — originally depicting a serene mountain scene with scholarly figures — is now PACKED with **[擠爆場景："at least 7-8 IP characters jammed into a space designed for 2-3 painted scholars — faces squished against the invisible 'glass' of the painting surface, cheeks pancaked flat, eyes bulging from pressure, bodies compressed and overlapping" / "characters piled on top of each other in a comedic human(?) pyramid — the ones at the bottom flattened almost completely, the ones on top pressed against the top border of the painting frame, everyone's limbs tangled"]**. The characters are rendered in a MIX of the traditional Koji pottery (交趾陶) ceramic style AND their modern appearance — **[風格混搭："some characters have half-transformed into ceramic glazed versions of themselves while others remain full 3D, creating visual chaos — a glazed ceramic Kirby arm pressed against a fully 3D Pikachu face" / "all characters are partially ceramic-styled from being inside the painting too long — Totoro's belly has developed ceramic crazing patterns, Pikachu's tail has become a glazed ceramic paddle, but their expressions are fully modern and very annoyed"]**.

**Character Details (the squeeze):** Each character reacts differently to being crammed in:
- **[角色A反應："Kirby is pressed flat against the painting's surface facing outward — its round body compressed into an oval, cheeks puffed even more than usual, eyes squeezed into stressed crescents, both stubby arms spread against the 'glass' leaving visible palm prints on the painted surface" / similar squished expression]**
- **[角色B反應："Pikachu is wedged sideways between two larger characters, only its face and one ear visible, cheeks electrically sparking from stress — tiny lightning bolts zapping the neighboring characters who flinch" / similar wedged position]**
- **[角色C反應："Totoro is at the very back, its massive body taking up 40% of the space, inadvertently crushing everyone forward — its expression is an apologetic 'sorry I'm so big' grimace, tiny soot sprites escaping from underneath it" / similar large character taking up too much space]**
- **[角色D反應："one character is visible only as a single hand poking out from the pile, waving desperately for help — impossible to identify which character it is" / "one character has been squeezed OUT of the painting's side border, half its body inside the painting and half dangling outside the frame, kicking its legs"]**

**The Original Painting Residents (NOT HAPPY):** The 2-3 original classical scholarly figures who BELONG in this painting have been **[原住民反應："shoved into the extreme corners of the landscape scene — one scholar is crammed behind the painted mountain with only his annoyed face peeking out, his scroll crushed — another is dangling from the painted pine tree branch to avoid being trampled, robes disheveled — the third has been pushed so far to the edge that half his body has been squeezed OUT of the painting frame, his flat painted legs dangling outside the border, arms crossed, expression of pure 'I've been in this painting for 200 years and this is what I get'" / similar displaced grumpy residents]**. One of them holds a tiny classical calligraphy speech scroll that reads: **[吐槽文字："「這裡只有三個名額。」(This painting only has 3 spots.)" / "「你們有訂位嗎？」(Do you have a reservation?)" / "「我要投訴管理處。」(I'm filing a complaint with management.)"]**.

**The Ornamental Frame (PRISTINE):** In sharp contrast to the interior chaos, the surrounding decorative borders remain COMPLETELY undisturbed — **the glazed ceramic peony flowers (牡丹)** bloom elegantly in pink and purple with green leaves, not a single petal out of place. **The geometric diamond lattice (菱格紋)** in deep teal maintains its perfect geometric grid. **The gold-green-blue decorative trim** with white dot patterns continues its dignified pattern. **The spiral cloud motifs (如意雲紋)** in the corners curl serenely. This ornamental border is the visual equivalent of a calm person watching chaos unfold — ancient, elegant, completely unbothered. One ceramic butterfly perches perfectly on a peony — perhaps watching the interior chaos with painted-on amusement.

**Surface Details:** The painting's surface shows evidence of the chaos — **[表面細節："tiny fingerprint smudges and face-prints on the painting surface from characters pressing against it" / "hairline cracks radiating from the most pressurized spots in the painting" / "one character's breath has fogged up a small patch of the painting surface from the inside, with a tiny finger-drawn sad face in the fog"]**. Some tapioca pearls from a previous delivery incident are still stuck in the lattice. The white plaster wall surrounding the panel is aged and authentic, dark wooden beams above, red brick floor below.

**Technical Specs:** 8K resolution, extreme detail on both the pristine Koji pottery (交趾陶) borders (glossy glaze, crazing patterns, kiln-fire color variations) AND the chaotic interior (individual character expressions, squeeze physics, material mixing between ceramic and modern styles). Warm afternoon lighting, slight tilt-shift miniature effect to enhance the "diorama" feel. The image should reward zooming in — every corner has a comedic detail. The contrast between the serene ornamental frame and the interior pandemonium is the entire joke.

## Example

A hyper-detailed comedic image of **Kirby, Pikachu, Totoro, Doraemon, Snoopy, Hello Kitty, and Mario** ALL crammed inside the tiny central landscape painting panel of a traditional Southern Fujian temple wall relief (堵壁), squishing against each other and pressing against the painting's surface — while the surrounding ornamental borders remain completely pristine.

**The Central Painting (CHAOS ZONE):** The landscape painting panel — originally a serene scene of misty mountains, pine trees, and a winding stream with three classical scholarly figures — is now STUFFED with seven IP characters jammed into a space designed for three painted scholars. The scene is a masterpiece of physical comedy:

**Kirby** is pressed flat against the painting's surface facing outward — its normally round body compressed into a wide oval pancake, pink cheeks puffed to maximum capacity from the pressure behind it, eyes squeezed into stressed little lines, both stubby arms spread against the invisible "glass" of the painting surface leaving tiny palm prints on the painted mountains underneath. Its face is the closest to the viewer — a perfect circle of squished pink distress. Kirby's body has partially taken on Koji pottery ceramic texture from being inside the painting — glazed pink with visible crazing patterns on one cheek, but the other cheek is still modern matte pink and very much alive and suffering.

**Pikachu** is wedged sideways between Totoro's belly and Doraemon's back — only its squished face, one ear, and its lightning-bolt tail are visible. Its red cheeks are sparking involuntarily from the compression — tiny bolts of electricity zapping Doraemon (who flinches) and Snoopy (whose fur stands on end). Pikachu's visible ear is bent at a painful angle against the painting's upper border. Its one visible eye communicates: "I regret everything."

**Totoro** occupies the entire back of the painting like a living wall — its enormous grey belly taking up roughly 40% of the total space, inadvertently squishing everyone else forward. It can't even turn around. Its face peeks over the pile of characters with an apologetic grimace — tiny sweat drops. Three soot sprites (まっくろくろすけ) have been squeezed out from underneath Totoro and are floating around the chaos looking distressed. Totoro's fur has partially glazed over into ceramic texture — grey ceramic with a hint of green Koji pottery glaze.

**Doraemon** is sandwiched vertically between Pikachu and Hello Kitty, its round blue body compressed into an hourglass shape. Its 4D pocket is being pressed against the frame border, accidentally dispensing random gadgets into the painting — a Take-copter stuck in the painted pine tree, a Anywhere Door wedged into the ceramic mountain, a Small Light rolling down the landscape. Doraemon's whiskers are bent in opposite directions from the pressure.

**Snoopy** is somehow ON TOP of everyone — lying flat across the pile of heads as if it's a bed, completely unbothered, sleeping in his classic "on top of the doghouse" pose. Eyes closed, peaceful smile. The only character not stressed. Woodstock is perched on Snoopy's belly, equally zen, surrounded by chaos. Snoopy's white body has remained suspiciously un-ceramic — as if he's too chill to be affected by the painting's rules.

**Hello Kitty** is wedged into the lower-left corner — her bow pressed flat against the border, her face maintaining its signature expressionless calm even under extreme compression. She's holding a tiny ceramic teacup (borrowed from the original painting's still life) and sipping tea as if nothing unusual is happening. One of her ears has turned into glazed red ceramic from pressing against the border too long.

**Mario** is at the bottom of the pile — almost completely flattened, visible only as his red cap, his mustache, and two desperate hands reaching upward from the character pile. One hand holds a tiny white flag of surrender. His cap has been knocked sideways and now bears ceramic glaze marks. A tiny speech bubble near his hand reads: 「Mamma mia.」

**The Original Residents (FURIOUS):** The three classical scholarly figures who actually LIVE in this painting have been displaced:
- The first scholar is crammed behind the painted mountain in the far corner — only his deeply annoyed face visible, peeking out from behind the peak, his crushed scroll dangling. His expression is centuries of accumulated frustration. A calligraphy scroll unfurls from his corner: 「這裡只有三個名額，你們看不懂嗎。」(There are only 3 spots. Can't you read.)
- The second scholar is clinging to the painted pine tree branch overhead — robes disheveled, hat hanging from one ear, dangling above the chaos like a cat refusing to come down. One of his ceramic sandals has fallen off and landed on Kirby's head.
- The third scholar has been pushed so far to the right that HALF his body has been squeezed OUT of the painting frame entirely — his flat painted legs and lower robe dangle outside the ornamental border over the lattice pattern, his upper body still inside the painting with crossed arms and an expression of "I'm calling my union representative."

**The Ornamental Frame (SERENITY):** In magnificent contrast, the surrounding borders are COMPLETELY unbothered: glazed ceramic peony flowers (牡丹) bloom in perfect pink and purple splendor, not a petal displaced. The geometric diamond lattice (菱格紋) in deep teal maintains flawless geometric precision. Gold-green-blue trim bands with white dot patterns continue their elegant rhythm. Corner spiral cloud motifs (如意雲紋) curl peacefully in cobalt blue ceramic. One ceramic butterfly on the upper peony surveys the interior chaos with painted-on aristocratic indifference. The ornamental frame radiates the energy of a perfectly composed person watching absolute mayhem unfold and choosing not to get involved.

**Surface Details:** Tiny face-prints and palm-prints visible on the painting surface from characters pressing outward. A small patch of the surface fogged up from Kirby's heavy breathing — a tiny sad face drawn in the fog with a finger. Hairline cracks radiating from where Totoro's back presses hardest against the painted mountains. Two tapioca pearls from a previous incident are lodged in the lattice (referencing the delivery disaster). The white plaster wall is aged and authentic, dark camphor wood beam above, herringbone red brick floor below.

**Technical Specs:** 8K resolution, extreme detail on pristine Koji pottery (交趾陶) borders (glaze pooling, crazing, kiln-fire variations) AND chaotic interior (individual expressions, squeeze physics, ceramic-modern style mixing). Warm afternoon light (4500K), shallow depth of field with the central chaos in sharp focus and the serene border slightly soft. Tilt-shift miniature effect optional. The image rewards zooming in — every corner contains a comedic detail (soot sprites, dispensed gadgets, ceramic sandal on Kirby's head, sleeping Snoopy, Mario's surrender flag). The pristine-frame-versus-interior-chaos contrast is the entire visual joke.
