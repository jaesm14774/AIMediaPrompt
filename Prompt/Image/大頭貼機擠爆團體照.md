### 大頭貼機擠爆團體照 (Photo Booth Character Pile-Up)

一群大小不一的角色擠進拍貼機的小小取景框中搶拍團體照，大的佔滿畫面、小的被擠到角落，每個角色表情和姿勢都不同，混亂又可愛的瞬間。

---

## Prompt Template

A vibrant kawaii illustration of a chaotic group photo moment: multiple **[角色主題：Kirby 不同型態 / 各種小動物 / 同一角色不同尺寸 / 自訂]** of wildly different sizes all trying to squeeze into one tiny photo booth frame at the same time. The photo strip frame is visible — a classic [拍貼機風格 with: 日式大頭貼 / "Japanese purikura booth with sparkly pink curtain, heart-shaped camera lens, and countdown timer showing 3" / "vintage Polaroid-style instant photo with white bottom border and handwritten date" / "smartphone selfie screen with front camera dot and filter icons on the sides"].

**Size Chaos:** The largest character takes up 60% of the frame — round body pressed against the top and sides, only the face and upper body visible. Two medium-sized characters (40% of the largest) are squeezed on either side, cheeks pressed together, one being pushed up and tilted sideways. Three to four tiny characters (15% of the largest) are scattered in the remaining gaps — one sitting on top of the big character's head looking proud, one wedged in the bottom corner with only eyes and forehead visible peeking up, one clinging to the frame edge about to fall out, one photobombing from behind with only eyes peeking over.

**Expressions & Poses:** Every character has a unique reaction — [表情組合 with: 歡樂混亂 / "the big one has serene closed eyes ignoring the chaos, medium ones are laughing and pushing each other, tiny ones show panic, determination, and mischief" / "big one is yelling with open mouth, medium ones make peace signs and duck faces, tiny ones are crying, sleeping, and doing bunny ears behind others" / "big one is squishing eyes shut from pressure, medium ones are taking selfies with tiny phones, tiny ones are climbing and sliding off each other"]. The overall energy is a split-second snapshot of beautiful chaos.

**Art Style:** Bright saturated kawaii palette with strong outlines (varying thickness: thicker for larger characters, thinner for tiny ones to enhance size perception). Chibi proportions, round expressive eyes, exaggerated blush marks. Clean digital illustration with flat colors and minimal shading — focus is on character expressions and spatial relationships. Small motion lines, sweat drops, and exclamation marks as manga-style visual effects around the struggling characters.

**Background:** The photo booth frame itself is the main compositional device. Outside the frame: [外框裝飾 with: 拍貼裝飾 / "sparkly sticker decorations — stars, hearts, bows, and date stamp in cute font" / "vintage film border with sprocket holes and exposure number" / "phone UI elements: battery icon, time, signal bars"]. The contrast between the orderly frame and the chaotic contents creates visual humor.

**Mood:** Hilarious, relatable, friendship chaos — that one group photo where nobody looks perfect but everyone remembers it forever.

## Example

A vibrant kawaii illustration of a chaotic group photo moment: multiple **Kirby in different Copy Ability forms** of wildly different sizes all trying to squeeze into one tiny photo booth frame at the same time. The photo strip frame is visible — a classic **Japanese purikura booth with sparkly pink curtain, heart-shaped camera lens, and countdown timer showing 3**.

**Size Chaos:** The largest Kirby (normal pink, no ability) takes up 60% of the frame — round body pressed against the top and sides, only the happy face and upper body visible. Two medium-sized Kirbys (Sword Kirby with green hat, and Fire Kirby with flame crown) are squeezed on either side, cheeks pressed together, Sword Kirby being pushed up and tilted sideways by Fire Kirby. Three tiny Kirbys are scattered in the remaining gaps — Ice Kirby sitting on top of big Kirby's head looking proud with crossed arms, Sleep Kirby wedged in the bottom corner with only the nightcap and drowsy eyes visible peeking up, Ninja Kirby clinging to the frame edge about to fall out and throwing a tiny star.

**Expressions & Poses:** Every Kirby has a unique reaction — the big one has serene closed crescent-moon eyes ignoring the chaos entirely, medium Sword Kirby is laughing with mouth wide open while pushing Fire Kirby, Fire Kirby makes an annoyed face with tiny flame puffs. Tiny Ice Kirby sits proudly, Sleep Kirby is half-asleep despite the madness, and Ninja Kirby shows determined eyes with sweat drops. The overall energy is a split-second snapshot of beautiful chaos.

**Art Style:** Bright saturated kawaii palette (pinks, greens, oranges, blues) with strong outlines (varying thickness: thicker for larger Kirbys, thinner for tiny ones to enhance size perception). Chibi proportions, round expressive eyes, exaggerated blush marks on all Kirbys. Clean digital illustration with flat colors and minimal shading — focus is on character expressions and spatial relationships. Small motion lines, sweat drops, and exclamation marks as manga-style visual effects around the struggling Kirbys.

**Background:** The purikura frame itself is the main compositional device. Outside the frame: sparkly sticker decorations — stars, hearts, bows, and a date stamp reading "2026.02.06" in cute rounded font. The contrast between the orderly decorated frame and the chaotic Kirby pile-up inside creates visual humor.

**Mood:** Hilarious, relatable, friendship chaos — that one group photo where nobody is ready but the camera already clicked.
