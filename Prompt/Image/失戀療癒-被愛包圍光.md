### 失戀療癒-被愛包圍光 | Healing Light of Being Loved

一位溫柔的陪伴角色捧著發光的心，把暖暖的光灑在縮起來的主角身上——你以為自己一個人，但愛從未停止照著你。

---

A [陪伴角色描述 with: 療癒角色的外觀與氣質 / "soft celestial guardian made of warm peach light, with kind eyes and open hands" / "fluffy round companion with cream-colored fur, rosy glow, and a calm reassuring smile" / "gentle human-like friend in a loose knitted cardigan, kneeling nearby with caring eyes"] stands close beside [主角狀態 with: 主角的姿態 / "a small figure sitting with knees hugged to chest, head slightly bowed, eyes closed" / "a tiny person curled on a soft cloud, facing downward, expression tired and quiet" / "a small figure sitting alone on a warm wooden floor, chin resting on knees"], gently holding out both arms, cradling a glowing warm heart between them. The heart radiates soft golden-rose light that spills over the figure beside them, wrapping the main character in warmth. The companion's expression is gentle and earnest, never pitying, only steady and full of quiet love. The background is a soft gradient of warm peach, amber, and cream, like the last light of a safe afternoon. Everything glows from within. No rain, no darkness, no cold.
