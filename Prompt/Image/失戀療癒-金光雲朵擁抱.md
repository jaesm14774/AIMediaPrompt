### 失戀療癒-金光雲朵擁抱 | Golden Cloud Embrace

難過的時候，整個世界的溫柔都悄悄來了——一位溫暖的陪伴角色輕輕靠近，讓金色的光把你整個人包起來。

---

## Prompt Template

A [主角描述 with: 主角的外觀 / "small human figure with soft rounded features, sitting cross-legged, expression weary and tender" / "tiny person with disheveled hair, wearing oversized sweater, sitting quietly with eyes half-closed" / "small figure with soft features, hands folded in lap, expression peaceful but faintly sad"] is surrounded by gentle floating clouds of warm golden light, not storm clouds but soft luminous puffs like the inside of a lantern, drifting close and wrapping around the figure like a slow embrace. Nearby is a [陪伴角色描述 with: 療癒角色的形象 / "soft floating guardian shaped from warm light, with a quiet smile and caring eyes" / "fluffy cloud companion with tiny hands and a sleepy gentle expression" / "kind older-sibling figure in soft homewear, leaning in with a calm reassuring presence"], resting one hand or wing softly on the figure's shoulder, present, unhurried, asking nothing. The golden clouds pulse faintly, breathing. The entire scene feels like being held without words. Background: deep soft cream fading to warm honey gold. No sharp edges anywhere. Studio Ghibli lighting aesthetic, soft watercolor wash, warm yellow and cream palette with rose accents, dreamlike bokeh, 8K.
