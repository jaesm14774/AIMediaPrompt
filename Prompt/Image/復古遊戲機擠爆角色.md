### 復古遊戲機擠爆角色 (Retro Console Squeeze)

可愛角色身體太大塞不進復古遊戲機的小螢幕，圓滾滾的身體從螢幕邊框溢出擠壓變形，營造出爆棚的萌感與懷舊氛圍。

---

## Prompt Template

A kawaii chibi-style illustration of **[角色名：Kirby / Pikachu / 小恐龍 / 自訂角色]** squeezed inside the tiny screen of a [遊戲裝置 with: 復古掌機 / "pastel pink Game Boy with round buttons and a D-pad" / "retro CRT television set with antenna and wood panel frame" / "classic arcade cabinet with colorful side art and joystick"], the character's body is comically too large for the small screen — soft round body pressed flat against the screen glass, cheeks squished against the edges, tiny limbs poking out from the bezels, one arm reaching outside the frame waving at the viewer.

**Composition:** The retro device is centered and takes up 80% of the frame. The character fills the entire screen area and overflows beyond it — body bulging against the screen borders, creating visible pressure and squeeze deformation. Small decorative details on the device: [裝飾細節 with: 遊戲機元素 / "a Poké Ball charm dangling from the corner, star stickers on the shell" / "channel knob and volume dial with tiny heart stickers" / "coin slot glowing softly, player 1 badge"]. The character's expression is a mix of happy and slightly struggling — closed smiling eyes, tongue sticking out, rosy cheeks turning even rosier from the squeeze.

**Art Style:** Soft pastel color palette (baby pink, mint green, lavender, cream yellow), thick clean outlines (3-4px), rounded shapes with no sharp edges, chibi proportions (head-to-body ratio 1:1), glossy digital illustration with subtle cel-shading, mobile game UI aesthetic. The device frame is rendered with slightly more detail and texture than the character to create depth contrast.

**Background:** Simple single-color pastel background (matching the device's accent color) with scattered small floating icons — tiny hearts, stars, musical notes, and pixel sparkles. No complex scenery — all attention on the character-device interaction.

**Mood:** Playful, squeezable, nostalgic, irresistibly cute, the visual equivalent of wanting to reach into the screen and poke the squishy character.

## Example

A kawaii chibi-style illustration of **Kirby** squeezed inside the tiny screen of a **pastel pink Game Boy with round buttons and a D-pad**, Kirby's body is comically too large for the small screen — soft round pink body pressed flat against the screen glass, cheeks squished against the edges, tiny stubby arms poking out from the bezels, one arm reaching outside the frame waving at the viewer.

**Composition:** The pastel pink Game Boy is centered and takes up 80% of the frame. Kirby fills the entire screen area and overflows beyond it — round body bulging against the screen borders, creating visible pressure and squeeze deformation. Small decorative details on the Game Boy: a star wand charm dangling from the corner, tiny Warp Star stickers on the shell. Kirby's expression is pure joy despite the squeeze — closed crescent-moon smiling eyes, tongue sticking out playfully, signature blush circles turning even pinker from the compression.

**Art Style:** Soft pastel color palette (baby pink, mint green, lavender, cream yellow), thick clean outlines (3-4px), rounded shapes with no sharp edges, chibi proportions (head-to-body ratio 1:1), glossy digital illustration with subtle cel-shading, mobile game UI aesthetic. The Game Boy frame is rendered with slightly more detail and texture than Kirby to create depth contrast.

**Background:** Simple soft pink background with scattered small floating icons — tiny hearts, stars, musical notes, and pixel sparkles. No complex scenery — all attention on the Kirby-Game Boy interaction.

**Mood:** Playful, squeezable, nostalgic, irresistibly cute, the visual equivalent of wanting to reach into the screen and squish Kirby even more.
