### 情人節 - 情書紙飛機 (Love Letter Paper Plane)

一個小角色站在高處，將粉紅色情書摺成紙飛機擲出，紙飛機在天空中留下由小愛心組成的飛行軌跡，畫出一個大大的心形航線

---

## Prompt Template

A poetic minimalist illustration of **[角色：如 pink round Kirby / chibi Snoopy / tiny white cat / small round bird]** standing on the edge of **[站立位置 with: 高處場景 / "a tall stack of oversized vintage books, the spines showing romantic novel titles" / "a single rooftop chimney of a miniature European-style house, red brick texture" / "a giant pencil standing upright like a tower, the character perched on the eraser end"]**, one arm extended forward in a throwing pose, having just launched a paper airplane folded from a pink love letter.

The paper airplane is mid-flight in the sky, trailing a dotted line of tiny hearts behind it. The heart trail draws a large, visible heart shape in the sky — the plane has already completed one loop of the heart and is now curving to complete the second half. Each trailing heart is slightly different in size, creating a hand-drawn imperfect feel. The paper airplane itself shows visible handwritten text and a lipstick kiss mark on its wing.

The sky is a clean gradient — **[天空色調 with: 天空氛圍 / "soft sunset gradient from warm peach at horizon to lavender at top, a few tiny cotton-candy clouds" / "clear pastel blue with scattered small white clouds shaped vaguely like hearts, golden hour warm light" / "dreamy twilight purple-to-pink gradient with the first evening star visible, the heart trail glowing faintly"]**.

Art style: Soft watercolor-meets-digital with visible brushstroke texture on the sky gradient. The character is small (occupying only 15-20% of the frame) — the sky and the heart trail are the real subjects. Gentle ink outlines, muted warm pastel palette. Inspired by Japanese picture book illustration (ehon style). The overall mood is quiet and hopeful, not loud or busy.

Composition: The character is positioned in the lower-left third. The paper plane and heart trail sweep across the upper two-thirds of the frame in an elegant curve. Generous negative space — the emptiness of the sky makes the single heart trail powerful. Vertical 3:4 ratio for phone wallpaper aesthetic. This image works because of what it DOESN'T include — no clutter, just one gesture, one flight path, one feeling.
