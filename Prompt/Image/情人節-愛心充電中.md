### 情人節 - 愛心充電中 (Love Charging in Progress)

一個角色像手機一樣靠牆充電，愛心能量從心形行動電源緩緩注入，頭頂電量指示從14%漲到100%，現代感十足的浪漫小品

---

## Prompt Template

A cute minimalist illustration of **[角色：如 pink round Kirby / chibi Pikachu / tiny Totoro / fluffy white cat / small Snoopy]** sitting on the floor, leaning against a clean white wall like a phone being charged. A glowing pink cable runs from the character's back to a **heart-shaped power bank** lying on the floor beside them. Above the character's head, a translucent battery indicator UI floats in the air, showing **[充電狀態 with: 充電進度 / "14% with a tiny broken heart icon, energy bar barely filled, the character looking drowsy and wilted" / "58% with a half-heart icon, energy bar half pink, the character perking up slightly with one eye open" / "100% with a full sparkling heart icon, energy bar completely filled with glowing pink, the character beaming with rosy cheeks and tiny hearts floating off their body"]**.

The power bank has a small LED indicator with heart-shaped lights. The charging cable is semi-transparent and you can see tiny pixel hearts flowing through it like data packets, traveling from the power bank into the character. A subtle circular glow radiates from the connection point.

Art style: Clean digital illustration with soft pastel palette — blush pink, warm white, soft lavender. Flat design with subtle gradients, inspired by modern app UI aesthetics mixed with kawaii illustration. The battery indicator looks like a real phone UI element but with heart motifs replacing standard icons. Thin clean outlines, minimal shadows, generous white space.

Composition: Centered subject on clean white/light pink background. The character, cable, and power bank form a simple triangular arrangement. The floating battery UI above acts as the visual punchline. Aspect ratio 3:4 vertical. No clutter — the humor comes from the single visual metaphor.

Lighting: Soft ambient glow from the power bank and cable, warm pink tones. The character emits a faint warm aura when fully charged. Overall feeling: cozy, modern, and instantly relatable — everyone knows the relief of a phone finally charging.
