### Q版IP 情緒災難電影海報

日常小情緒放大成超誇張日系手繪災難片，任何角色一臉崩潰站在荒謬宇宙級災難正中央。

---

## Prompt Template

Chibi hand-drawn illustration, thick expressive ink lines, bold flat colors, vertical cinematic disaster poster composition. **[角色/外觀：角色名稱 + 主要外型特徵 / 例："Gengar, chubby purple ghost Pokémon, tiny stubby arms, enormous red eyes" / "Kirby, round pink puffball, tiny nub arms, big blue eyes" / "Totoro, large round grey forest spirit, wide eyes, white belly markings"]** with sweat drops flying, as the helpless protagonist trapped in an absurdly tiny crisis blown up to apocalyptic scale: [情緒災難 with: 荒謬災難場景 / "standing at the edge of a single-bite eaten piece of cake, staring into the abyss with existential horror, as the remaining cake crumbles like a collapsing cliff — disaster film title at bottom: 最後一口" / "frozen mid-step before a grocery store with 47 different yogurt options, surrounded by a blinding divine light shining from inside, overwhelmed to the point of levitation — title: 選擇困難末日" / "staring at a phone screen showing 3% battery at midnight, entire body engulfed in panic flames, dramatic thunderstorm behind — title: 充電器不見了"], dramatic low-angle shot, bold hand-lettered Japanese sound effect (ガーン！ or ドーン！) in upper corner, limited 3-color palette, exaggerated chibi proportions, no realistic rendering.

---

## Example

Chibi hand-drawn illustration, thick expressive ink lines, bold flat colors, vertical cinematic disaster poster composition. Gengar, chubby purple ghost Pokémon, tiny stubby arms, enormous red eyes, sweat drops flying, as the helpless protagonist trapped in an absurdly tiny crisis blown up to apocalyptic scale: staring at a phone screen showing 3% battery at midnight, entire body engulfed in purple panic flames, dramatic thunderstorm behind — title area: 充電器不見了, dramatic low-angle shot, bold hand-lettered Japanese sound effect ガーン！in upper corner, limited 3-color palette of purple, cream, and electric yellow, exaggerated chibi proportions, no realistic rendering.
