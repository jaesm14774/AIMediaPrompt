### 春節香煙 — Q版神明煙霧大遊行

廟宇香爐升起的裊裊煙霧裡，一整排 Q 版迷你神明正從煙中「走出來」——小小媽祖踩著煙霧衝浪、Q版關公騎著煙做的赤兔馬飛奔、迷你土地公躺在煙雲吊床上打瞌睡。神明們全體出動，在你的香煙裡開趴。

## Prompt Template

A stunning hyper-detailed mixed-reality photograph taken inside a traditional Chinese temple during Lunar New Year. In the foreground, a large bronze tripod incense censer (銅鼎香爐) with verdigris patina and dragon relief carvings sits on the stone floor, dozens of lit joss sticks planted in the grey ash, their glowing orange tips producing thick plumes of white incense smoke that rise upward.

THE MAGICAL CORE — as the smoke rises and curls above the censer, **dozens of tiny chibi deity figures (Q版迷你神明)** are materializing WITHIN the smoke, each one approximately 8-12 cm tall, their bodies semi-translucent and made of condensed luminous smoke with soft golden inner glow — they are NOT solid figurines, they are living smoke-sculptures that move, breathe, and interact with each other while drifting upward with the smoke currents.

**The Chibi Deity Parade (Q版神明遊行隊伍):**

[主要神明組合 with: 選擇你的 Q 版神明陣容 / "chibi Mazu (Q版媽祖) — tiny round face with oversized phoenix crown that keeps tilting because her head is too small for it, surfing on a curling smoke wave like a pro surfer with her ornate robes flowing behind her, one tiny hand adjusting her crown, the other giving a confident peace sign — behind her, two chibi Qianliyan (千里眼) and Shunfenger (順風耳) generals running on the smoke trying to keep up, one tripping over his own oversized battle axe, the other cupping his giant ear to listen to prayers while jogging" / "chibi Guan Yu (Q版關公) — impossibly long beautiful beard that trails behind him like a silk scarf in the wind, riding a miniature Red Hare horse (赤兔馬) made entirely of swirling red-orange smoke with ember-glowing eyes, galloping across the smoke plumes at full speed — his tiny Green Dragon Crescent Blade (青龍偃月刀) is being used as a selfie stick to take a photo with chibi Liu Bei and Zhang Fei who are waving from a lower smoke curl" / "chibi Tu Di Gong (Q版土地公) — the tiniest and chubbiest of all, bald round head with enormous white beard that doubles as a blanket, lounging in a hammock made of two intertwined smoke wisps, fast asleep with a content smile, clutching a miniature gold ingot like a teddy bear — a tiny chibi Tiger Lord (虎爺) curled up like a house cat at his smoke-feet, also sleeping, its smoke-stripes slowly shifting patterns as it breathes"].

**Additional background deities** scattered throughout the rising smoke at various heights: a chibi Kitchen God (灶君) roasting a tiny smoke-marshmallow over a joss stick ember; a chibi Door God (門神) pair standing guard on two parallel smoke columns like tiny angry bouncers with their arms crossed; a chibi Jade Emperor (玉皇大帝) sitting on a throne-shaped smoke formation at the very top, looking down at the chaos below with a resigned "why did I hire these people" expression.

**THE HUMAN ELEMENT:** At the base of the frame, partially visible, [祈禱者反應 with: 信徒看到煙霧中神明的反應 / "a woman in a crimson qipao kneeling before the censer, her eyes widening in disbelief as she notices the tiny parade forming in her incense smoke — her hands still pressed together in prayer position but her mouth slightly open in an amazed smile, caught between reverence and pure delight" / "a small child tugging at their grandmother's sleeve and pointing excitedly at the smoke deities while grandma, eyes closed in prayer, doesn't notice — the chibi Tu Di Gong has woken up just enough to wave sleepily at the child before rolling over in his smoke-hammock" / "a temple attendant in traditional robes frozen mid-step, holding a broom, staring at the smoke parade with the expression of someone who has worked here for 30 years and is seeing this for the first time"]. The human occupies only the bottom 20% of the frame — this is the deities' show.

**Visual Style:** The temple background is rendered in warm, slightly desaturated photorealistic tones — vermillion pillars, dark wooden beams, golden altar decorations, red lanterns casting warm ambient glow. The chibi deities are rendered in a contrasting style: soft luminous cel-shading with a Ghibli-meets-Nintendo aesthetic, their smoke-bodies having visible swirling internal texture like living clouds shaped into adorable forms. Each deity emits a faint golden aura that casts tiny warm highlights on the real bronze censer and surrounding smoke. Volumetric god rays from high temple windows illuminate the smoke, making the chibi deities glow where the light hits them. The contrast between the SACRED realistic temple and the ADORABLE cartoon deities is the entire point — divine beings are cuter than anyone expected.

**Technical specs:** Shot on medium format, 4:5 portrait, f/2.8 for the censer sharp and smoke-deities at various focus planes creating dreamy depth layering, warm color temperature (3800K temple + golden deity glow), 8K resolution, the mood is equal parts sacred and hilarious — a temple where the gods are real, tiny, and having the time of their lives in your incense smoke.
