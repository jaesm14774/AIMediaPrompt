### 水彩治癒系美食枕頭

可愛角色躺在巨大美食上的溫暖治癒系水彩插畫，萌萌的幸福感

---

## Prompt Template

A heartwarming watercolor illustration of **[在此填入可愛角色：如 chubby blue cat / round pink hamster / fluffy white bunny / tiny orange fox]** happily lying on top of [美食類型 with: 選擇一種舒適的美食 / "a golden crispy toast with melting butter dripping down the sides" / "a fluffy pancake stack with maple syrup pooling around" / "a warm cinnamon roll with creamy white icing swirls" / "a soft steamed bun with gentle steam rising"], with **[配菜裝飾：如 sunny-side-up egg / fresh strawberries / whipped cream dollop / crispy bacon strips]** placed beside them on a simple ceramic plate.

The character has an adorably content expression - eyes closed in bliss forming happy curved lines (◠‿◠), tiny pink blush marks on cheeks, a small satisfied smile. Tiny floating hearts and steam wisps drift above, conveying warmth and comfort. The character's body is soft and squishy, slightly melting into the food surface as if it's the coziest bed in the world.

Art style: Soft watercolor painting with visible brush texture and gentle color bleeding at edges. Warm color palette dominated by honey gold, soft peach, creamy white, and pastel accents. Delicate ink outlines that are slightly imperfect and hand-drawn feeling. The food is rendered with appetizing detail - you can almost smell the freshly made breakfast.

Composition: Centered subject on clean white/cream background with minimal elements. Soft drop shadow beneath the plate creating gentle grounding. Empty space around the subject emphasizes the cozy focal point. Aspect ratio 3:4 vertical for phone wallpaper aesthetic.

Technical: High resolution digital watercolor rendering, soft ambient lighting from upper left, no harsh shadows. The overall feeling should be "healing" (治癒系) - the kind of image that makes viewers smile and feel warm inside. Inspired by Korean and Japanese cute food illustration style.
