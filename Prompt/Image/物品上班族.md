### 物品上班族

日常物品在辦公室展現荒謬物理現象，復古卡通風格 × 超寫實攝影

---

## Prompt Template

A hyper-realistic office photography of **[在此填入日常物品：如 coffee mug / alarm clock / desk lamp / stapler / computer mouse]** in a modern tech company office. The object has large expressive vintage 1950s cartoon eyes and mouth with exaggerated panicked expression, wearing a tiny business suit or office accessories.

[荒謬物理現象 with: 物品特性失控場景 / "Coffee mug with anxious eyes as thick black coffee liquid overflows and floods the entire desk like an ink tsunami, drowning spreadsheets and keyboard" / "Alarm clock showing stuck time at 9:00 AM eternally, its cartoon face screaming silently as bell hammer vibrates violently creating motion blur trails" / "Stapler with desperate expression frantically chomping through mountain of papers, metal jaws stretched impossibly wide with staples flying everywhere like confetti"]

The scene uses corporate documentary photography style with desaturated color grading except for the object's vibrant anthropomorphic features and the surreal physics phenomenon. Overhead fluorescent office lighting creating harsh shadows and institutional atmosphere. Shot with 35mm lens f/2.8, shallow depth of field focusing on the object's comically distressed expression. Background shows authentic office environment—cubicles, computers, bland gray walls. The object's original function becomes the source of visual absurdity.

Retro animation aesthetic meets hyper-realistic photography with surreal physics. 8K resolution, film grain, professional color grading. The humor comes from: mundane office object + vintage cartoon expressiveness + impossible physical phenomenon = visual absurdity and metaphor for work-life chaos.
