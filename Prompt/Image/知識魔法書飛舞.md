### 知識魔法書飛舞

[IP角色]渺小如塵，孤身站在一本翻開書頁的邊緣——腳下的書頁文字正在液化成星河，而四周漂浮的每一本書，都是一個正在誕生的宇宙。

---

## Prompt Template

A cinematic miniature-epic illustration: **[IP角色]** stands as a tiny silhouette at the very edge of an enormous open book's page, so small that their entire figure fits within a single line of printed text below their feet. The text on the page is visibly dissolving, letter by letter transforming into glowing starlight — the words becoming a river of constellations flowing across the page-surface like a galaxy seen from above. **[IP角色]**'s shadow stretches dramatically across this star-river, connecting the physical world to the knowledge cosmos. Surrounding the central book, four other open books float in slow orbit, each one a universe mid-explosion: one book erupts with golden rune-fire like a solar flare from its spine, another breathes a cascading emerald forest growing upward from its pages, a third has a translucent solar system rotating just above its open spread, a fourth releases silver sound-wave auroras. All knowledge elements — star-letters, rune sparks, forest spores, orbit arcs, sound waves — slowly drift and intermingle in the air between the books, forming a breathtaking knowledge nebula. The background is a clean, warm ivory white, utterly plain, making the floating book-universes appear to exist in their own dimension. Studio Ghibli × Craig Mullins concept art style, dramatic lighting from within each book-universe casting colored light outward, ultra-fine linework, deep atmospheric depth, 4K.
