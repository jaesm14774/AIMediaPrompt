## Example

A deeply touching illustration of **Kirby** fast asleep on the back of a father in a rumpled work shirt with his tie loosened, being carried home. Kirby's round pink cheek is pressed flat against the father's shoulder, tiny arms draped loosely around his neck, round body completely limp and heavy with deep sleep — the boneless weight of someone who trusts absolutely.

The father walks slowly, carefully, each step placed gently to avoid jostling Kirby. His expression is what makes the scene — a tired but tender half-smile, eyes soft with a warmth that a long day at work cannot erase. One hand supports Kirby's soft round body from below, the other holds a briefcase. His shoulders are tired — you can see the slight stoop — but his grip on Kirby is sure and steady.

The setting is simple: a quiet residential street at dusk, warm light spilling from house windows onto the sidewalk. A neighbor's cat watches from a fence post. The family mailbox is visible a few steps ahead. The world is winding down — the last warm amber of the day fading into soft blue twilight.

Art style: Soft, warm digital painting with slightly muted tones. The focus is on the physical connection — Kirby's cheek squished against the shoulder, the father's careful footstep, the way his loosened tie drapes over Kirby like a tiny blanket. Warm side lighting from the house windows. Shallow depth of field blurring the residential street into soft amber and blue. Close to medium shot from slightly behind, showing both figures on their quiet walk home. Square format (1:1), 4K resolution, illustration.
