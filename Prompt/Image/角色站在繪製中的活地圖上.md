### 角色站在繪製中的活地圖上

整片大地本身就是一幅正在被繪製的手繪地圖——山脊是等高線、河流是藍色墨水、森林是楓葉圖案印章，[IP角色] 渺小站在地圖線條與真實地貌的交界縫隙，分不清腳下是世界還是圖紙。

## Prompt Template

Aerial overhead view of a vast mountain landscape — but the entire terrain is simultaneously a real world AND a hand-drawn cartographic map being rendered in real time. The visual rule: every geographical feature exists in two states at once. Mountain ridgelines are also ink contour lines (topographic brown strokes, slightly imprecise, hand-inked). Rivers are actual flowing water AND blue cartographic annotation lines — the water surface has the flat opacity of map ink, yet still catches light and moves. Forested slopes are covered with tiny repeated stamped maple-leaf symbols (the kind found on antique maps), each stamp also a real autumn tree when examined closely.

A tiny **[IP角色]** stands at the exact visual seam where the cartographic rendering meets raw undrawn terrain — to the character's left, the world is fully "mapped": labeled, outlined, color-coded in aged parchment tones. To the character's right, the terrain exists as pure unrendered reality: vivid autumn color, atmospheric haze, no lines, no labels. The character stands with one foot on each side of this invisible boundary, looking outward.

Camera: straight-down bird's-eye view, the character at dead center, the map/reality boundary line running diagonally through frame. The "mapped" half uses desaturated cartographic palette (sepia, dusty blue, forest green ink). The "real" half uses full saturated autumn warmth (crimson, gold, amber). The contrast is unmistakable, split cleanly by the advancing ink frontier.
