## Example

Cinematic wide-angle establishing shot, a penthouse office at the top floor of a skyscraper at night during heavy rain. Kirby (Nintendo's iconic pink spherical character, small stubby arms, red feet, large round blue eyes with a sharp determined gaze) sitting in an oversized black leather Barcelona chair positioned against floor-to-ceiling panoramic windows — Kirby is comically tiny compared to the massive chair, pink body barely filling one seat cushion, stubby red feet dangling far above the polished concrete floor, emphasizing the absurd scale contrast between the small round creature and the grand CEO environment. Kirby is wearing a slim-fit black turtleneck sweater and tailored dark navy trousers, a thin gold wristwatch catching reflected light — minimalist CEO aesthetic. Kirby holding a hardcover book in one hand resting on the armrest, the other hand supporting the chin in a contemplative pose, eyes locked onto the pages with laser focus, entire body language radiating quiet authority, reflection of the pink figure perfectly mirrored in the rain-streaked glass creating a ghostly double — but the reflection shows Kirby without the CEO outfit, just the original pink sphere, visual metaphor of identity beneath the role. The penthouse features polished concrete floors reflecting the city lights, a single Noguchi coffee table with an untouched whiskey glass and a Warp Star paperweight glowing faintly gold, minimalist bookshelves along one wall with carefully curated hardcovers and a single Meta Knight figurine hidden among the spines. Rain droplets streaming down the massive windows create prismatic light effects, each drop refracting the neon city below into tiny kaleidoscopic points. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting: warm amber reading lamp as key light from one side, cool blue city glow as fill from behind, creating striking color contrast on Kirby's surface where the warm and cool tones meet at the equator of the spherical body. Shot with anamorphic lens creating horizontal flares from city lights, ultra-wide 2.39:1 cinematic aspect ratio, shallow depth of field with Kirby sharp and rain-bokeh background dreamy.
