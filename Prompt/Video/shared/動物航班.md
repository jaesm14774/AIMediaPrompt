### 動物航班

飛機上發生不可思議的事——行李箱打開跑出一堆動物、座位底下鑽出毛茸茸的身影、頭頂行李艙掉下意想不到的乘客。空服員傻眼，乘客驚呆，但動物們一臉淡定，彷彿這趟航班本來就屬於牠們。

**Phase 1 - The Discovery (0-2s):** The scene begins with **[觸發場景："a passenger opening their overhead bin to grab their bag" / "a flight attendant opening the luggage compartment for a passenger" / "someone unzipping their carry-on suitcase" / "a child looking under their seat and gasping" / "the cabin crew doing a routine check"]**. The human reaction: **[人類反應："mouth drops open in disbelief" / "freezes completely, processing what they're seeing" / "looks around to confirm others are seeing this too" / "the flight attendant's professional smile cracks into genuine shock" / "a child points excitedly while parents look horrified"]**.

**Phase 2 - Chaos Unfolds (2-4s):** The animals have now **[動物行為："taken over the aisle, waddling/walking in an orderly line like they own the place" / "claimed various seats, settling in with zero regard for assigned seating" / "spread throughout the cabin—some on seats, some on laps, some investigating the beverage cart" / "formed a pile in the aisle, blocking all movement, completely unbothered"]**. Their expressions are **[動物表情："impossibly calm, as if this is a normal Tuesday" / "mildly curious about their surroundings, zero stress" / "sleepy and content, some already napping" / "looking at humans like THEY'RE the weird ones"]**. Meanwhile: **[連鎖反應："other passengers start filming with phones" / "the pilot's voice comes on: 'uhh... folks, we have a... situation'" / "a flight attendant tries to herd them but gives up immediately" / "one brave passenger tries to pet one and succeeds—it's very soft" / "the seatbelt sign dings on and off repeatedly in confusion"]**. **[額外細節："one [animal] has found the snack cart and is helping itself" / "two [animals] are fighting over the window seat" / "one is wearing someone's sleep mask" / "a [animal] is pressing all the call buttons"]**.

**Phase 3 - New Normal (4-5s):** The cabin has **[最終狀態："reached a strange equilibrium—animals and humans coexisting" / "completely surrendered to the animal takeover" / "achieved unexpected harmony"]**. Final shot: **[結尾場景："a passenger shrugs and shares their snack with the [animal] next to them" / "the flight attendant, defeated but smiling, offers drinks to the animals" / "everyone has accepted their fate—some humans are napping WITH the animals" / "the captain walks out to see the situation and just slowly backs into the cockpit"]**. The animals look **[最後表情："deeply satisfied with how this has turned out" / "like they've been planning this heist for months" / "at the camera with an expression that says 'what are you gonna do about it?'"]**. **[最後一幕："one [animal] in the aisle looks directly at camera and slow-blinks" / "a group photo is somehow being taken" / "the destination sign now reads '[ANIMAL'S NATURAL HABITAT]'" / "a tiny paw reaches up and turns off the reading light—goodnight"]**.

**Visual Style:** Realistic airplane interior with comedic timing—the humor comes from the contrast between mundane airline setting and absurd animal invasion. Animals rendered cute but with "I know exactly what I'm doing" energy. Human reactions should be genuine shock/confusion/eventual acceptance. Lighting is standard airplane lighting but captures the chaos perfectly.

**Technical specs:** 60fps for smooth chaos, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format, natural airplane lighting, dynamic camera following the action, sound design: overhead bin CLICK, dramatic pause, then chaos—animal sounds mixed with passenger gasps, the iconic seatbelt ding, confused intercom sounds.

## Example 1: 水豚行李箱大逃亡

A hilarious 5-second video of **dozens of capybaras** bursting out of an overhead luggage compartment on a commercial flight, creating the most wholesome chaos in aviation history.

**Phase 1 - The Discovery (0-2s):** A tired businessman stands up to grab his laptop bag from the overhead bin. He clicks open the compartment—and IMMEDIATELY regrets it. Capybaras. So many capybaras. They pour out like a furry waterfall, tumbling over each other with their signature unbothered expressions. One, two, five, TEN, FIFTEEN—how is this physically possible?! They just keep coming. The man stands frozen, a capybara balanced on his head, another one sliding down his arm. His face: pure existential crisis. The flight attendant in the background drops her tray. A child in the next row SCREAMS with joy.

**Phase 2 - Chaos Unfolds (2-4s):** Capybaras have spread everywhere. Three have claimed the entire first-class section, lounging like executives. A line of them waddles down the aisle in perfect single-file formation—where are they going? Nobody knows. One has discovered the beverage cart and is drinking directly from the orange juice carton with zero shame. Another is sitting on a passenger's lap, and the passenger has simply accepted this as their life now. A capybara is pressing the flight attendant call button repeatedly—*ding ding ding ding*. The pilot's voice crackles: "Ladies and gentlemen, we are experiencing... unprecedented... wildlife activity. Please remain calm." No one is calm. One capybara is trying on someone's neck pillow. Another has stolen a passport and is examining it.

**Phase 3 - New Normal (4-5s):** Somehow, peace has been achieved. Passengers and capybaras have reached an understanding. A grandmother is feeding crackers to three capybaras around her. A businessman has given up on work and is using a capybara as an armrest—it doesn't seem to mind. The flight attendant has started offering drinks to the capybaras ("Sir? Ma'am? Orange juice?"). The original overhead bin victim is now buried under a pile of sleeping capybaras, only his face visible, expression shifted from horror to acceptance to "actually this is pretty nice." Final shot: the cockpit door opens, the captain steps out, sees the capybara-filled cabin, makes eye contact with one capybara that slow-blinks at him, and he simply... closes the door again. Flight continues.

## Example 2: 企鵝入侵經濟艙

A hilarious 5-second video of **emperor penguins** unexpectedly marching down the airplane aisle like they're boarding the flight, confusing everyone.

**Phase 1 - The Discovery (0-2s):** Mid-flight. A flight attendant pulls back the curtain to the economy section—and FREEZES. A perfect line of emperor penguins is waddling down the aisle. Where did they come from? The rear bathroom? The cargo hold? Brazil?? Nobody knows. They march in impeccable formation, flippers at their sides, chest puffed out, radiating "we belong here" energy. A passenger in an aisle seat does a double-take so hard they spill their entire drink. The penguins do not acknowledge the chaos they're causing. They have places to be.

**Phase 2 - Chaos Unfolds (2-4s):** The penguin parade continues. They stop at row 24. One penguin looks at the seat, looks at the seated passenger, looks back at the seat. The passenger nervously starts gathering their things—"I... I can move..." The penguin settles in the now-vacant seat with immense satisfaction. Others are claiming overhead bins as personal spaces—hopping up with surprising athleticism. One penguin has gotten into a standoff with a toddler over a bag of goldfish crackers; neither is backing down. A penguin is standing at the emergency exit, reading the safety instructions with apparent genuine interest. The pilot makes an announcement but can't finish because a penguin has somehow gotten into the cockpit.

**Phase 3 - New Normal (4-5s):** The penguins have settled. They're scattered throughout economy class like actual passengers—some window seats, some aisle seats, several sharing a row in a huddle (old habits). Flight attendants have given up and are serving fish to the penguin rows. One penguin is watching the in-flight movie very intently. A human child and a baby penguin are now best friends, sharing headphones. Final shot: camera pulls back to reveal that the entire economy section is now about 50% penguin. The overhead announcement dings: "We will be making an unscheduled stop... in Antarctica." The penguins look pleased. The humans look confused but no longer resistant. One penguin turns to camera and does a little bow.

## Example 3: 貓咪座位下伏擊

A hilarious 5-second video of **cats of all breeds** suddenly emerging from under airplane seats like they were hiding there the whole time.

**Phase 1 - The Discovery (0-2s):** A passenger drops their phone under the seat. They reach down to grab it—and make eye contact with a cat. A fluffy orange cat, just... sitting there, staring back. "WHAT THE—" Before they can react, cats start emerging from under EVERY seat. Like a horror movie, but fluffy. A black cat from 12A. A tabby from 12B. A Persian from 12C. They've been here the WHOLE FLIGHT. How did no one notice?? A Siamese stretches dramatically as it emerges, clearly annoyed at being discovered. A passenger screams. Another passenger immediately starts trying to take a selfie with the nearest cat.

**Phase 2 - Chaos Unfolds (2-4s):** Cats are now EVERYWHERE, doing exactly what cats do—which is whatever they want. A cat has claimed the beverage cart as its throne. Two cats are fighting over a sunny spot near the window (sunbeam priority is universal). One cat is kneading a passenger's blanket, making aggressive eye contact. A hairless Sphynx is walking along the seat headrests like a balance beam—passengers duck as it passes. One cat has knocked a phone out of someone's hand. Another is sitting in the aisle, blocking the flight attendant, absolutely refusing to move. The pilot announces: "It appears we have... cats." A cat meows directly into the intercom. Chaos.

**Phase 3 - New Normal (4-5s):** Classic cat behavior: having caused maximum disruption, all cats are now asleep. They've arranged themselves in the most inconvenient spots possible—on keyboards, across armrests, on faces. One passenger has three cats on them and cannot move. They have accepted their fate. A flight attendant pets a sleeping cat and smiles—resistance is futile. Final shot: the original passenger who found the first cat is now buried under a pile of purring cats, phone forgotten, looking at the camera with an expression that says "I guess I have cats now." One cat opens one eye, sees everything is as it should be, and closes it again. Purring fills the cabin.

## Example 4: 柯基從頭頂行李艙滾出來

A hilarious 5-second video of **corgis** rolling out of overhead bins across the entire airplane, their tiny legs flailing adorably.

**Phase 1 - The Discovery (0-2s):** A flight attendant does the pre-landing overhead bin check. She opens the first one—and a corgi ROLLS out, legs wiggling, fluffy butt first. It lands on a passenger's lap with a surprised "BORF!" Before anyone can react, passengers start opening their own bins. EVERY SINGLE ONE has corgis. They roll out like adorable loaves of bread, stubby legs scrambling, tongues out. It's raining corgis. The cabin is filled with surprised screams and delighted laughter. One corgi lands perfectly in the aisle and immediately starts zooming.

**Phase 2 - Chaos Unfolds (2-4s):** Maximum corgi energy. They're zooming down the aisle (it's a sploot-highway now). They're doing that corgi thing where they just STARE at you until you give them attention. One corgi has discovered the snack box and is nose-deep in pretzels. A synchronized group of corgis is herding passengers toward the back of the plane—they can't help it, it's instinct. A corgi is trying to herd the beverage cart. Another corgi is doing the "corgi smile" at a camera phone—this will go viral. A small child is OVERJOYED, lying on the floor surrounded by corgis, living their best life.

**Phase 3 - New Normal (4-5s):** The zoomies have ended. All corgis are now doing the signature corgi sploot—lying flat on the cool airplane floor, legs extended behind them, living their best lives. The entire aisle is a corgi carpet. Flight attendants have to step over them carefully. One corgi is asleep on the safety instruction card it pulled down. Another is splooted across someone's laptop keyboard—important emails will not be sent today. Final shot: the captain walks out, surveys the sploot situation, sighs, and sploots down next to a corgi. He pets it. The corgi's tail wags once. Peace in our time.

## Example 5: 海豹從行李箱滑出

A hilarious 5-second video of **seals** sliding out of luggage and flopping around the airplane cabin like the ocean floor.

**Phase 1 - The Discovery (0-2s):** A woman opens her large suitcase to get her sweater—and a SEAL looks up at her. A full, actual, chonky seal, somehow packed into her luggage. "I... I don't own a seal..." she whispers. The seal makes direct eye contact and lets out a single "AUGH." This triggers other seals. One flops out from under the seat. Another rolls into the aisle. It's unclear where they're all coming from but they just keep appearing—sliding, flopping, rolling into view. A businessman opens his briefcase: seal. A woman opens her purse: small seal. How. WHY.

**Phase 2 - Chaos Unfolds (2-4s):** Seals have no legs and zero shame. They're flopping everywhere, making seal noises (the "AUGH" bark that sounds oddly judgemental). One seal has rolled onto a passenger and is just... lying there, being a weighted blanket. The passenger cannot move. Another seal is doing the "banana pose" in the aisle—pure relaxation, blocking all foot traffic. A seal is slapping the tray table with its flipper repeatedly—*slap slap slap*—absolutely loving the sound. Two seals are having a bark-off, getting louder and louder. A flight attendant slips on a seal and lands on another seal (both are fine, seals are squishy).

**Phase 3 - New Normal (4-5s):** Peak seal chaos has evolved into peak seal chill. They've formed puddles throughout the cabin—just blobs of seal, vibing. One seal is balanced on the armrest, defying physics, doing the classic seal "head raised, eyes closed" pose. Passengers have started using seals as pillows (surprisingly comfortable). A baby seal is asleep in someone's neck pillow. Final shot: an aerial view of the entire cabin—humans and seals intermingled in a bizarre harmony. One seal looks up at camera and does the seal smile. The flight continues to an unknown destination. Nobody seems to mind anymore.
