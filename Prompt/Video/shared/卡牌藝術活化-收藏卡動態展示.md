### 卡牌藝術活化 - 收藏卡動態展示

一張收藏卡牌的精美插畫突然「活過來」，角色從靜態畫面中掙脫，在卡面上自由行動，最後回到原始姿勢——但表情已經完全不一樣了。

---

## Prompt Template

A cinematic 5-second video of a **[收藏卡類型："shiny holographic Pokémon trading card" / "rare Yu-Gi-Oh! card with foil border" / "Magic: The Gathering mythic rare card" / "custom illustrated anime gacha card"]** whose artwork magically comes to life in dramatic fashion.

**Phase 1 - Static Display (0-1.5s):** A beautiful collectible card rests on a **[展示表面："dark velvet display cloth with soft spotlight from above" / "polished mahogany desk with warm desk lamp glow" / "collector's glass case with museum-style lighting" / "weathered wooden table with candlelight flickering"]**. The card features **[卡面角色：a cute creature / a legendary beast / a warrior character / a mythical spirit]** in a detailed illustrated scene—completely static, pristine card condition. Camera slowly pushes in toward the card, focusing on the artwork. Holographic foil shimmers subtly as the light shifts. The card looks like any normal collectible—until a tiny detail moves. A leaf in the artwork sways. A ripple crosses painted water. Something is waking up.

**Phase 2 - Art Comes Alive (1.5-3.5s):** The illustrated character within the card **[甦醒動作："blinks its painted eyes—the 2D illustrated pupils suddenly shift and track the camera" / "stretches its limbs within the card border like waking from a long nap, painted muscles actually flexing" / "shakes its head, sending illustrated particles scattering within the card frame" / "yawns dramatically, the 2D mouth opening with visible depth, breaking the flat illustration plane"]**. The background artwork starts moving too—painted clouds drift, illustrated grass sways, drawn water flows. But everything stays WITHIN the card border, as if the card is a tiny window into a living world. The character looks around with curiosity, discovering it can move for the first time. Expression shifts from confused to delighted. Holographic effects intensify, rainbow light refracting across the card surface as the magic strengthens.

**Phase 3 - Breaking Free & Return (3.5-5s):** The character **[突破瞬間："reaches one hand THROUGH the card border—a 3D hand emerging from a 2D surface, casting a real shadow on the table" / "presses against the card border from inside like pressing against glass, the card surface physically bulging outward" / "leaps upward within the card, head poking ABOVE the card's top edge into the real world, looking around in wonder"]**. The card glows with intense energy. Light particles stream from the card surface. Then suddenly—**FLASH**—the character snaps back into position. The card is static again. But the character's expression has changed: **[最終表情："now wearing a tiny knowing smirk instead of the original neutral expression" / "one eye is now winking at the camera, breaking the fourth wall" / "the pose is slightly different—arms crossed with attitude instead of the original stance"]**. The holographic foil settles. The card looks normal again. But you KNOW it's alive.

**Visual Style:** Hyper-realistic card photography transitioning to magical realism. The physical card rendered with extreme detail—visible card stock texture, edge wear, holographic rainbow patterns, protective sleeve reflections. The animated artwork maintains its original illustration style (cel-shaded / painterly / anime) but with added depth and motion, creating a stunning contrast between 2D art and 3D reality. Lighting shifts from practical (desk lamp / spotlight) to magical (internal card glow, particle effects) and back.

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format (TikTok/Reels/Shorts optimized), dynamic color grading shifting from warm practical (4000K) to cool magical (7000K) and back, extreme shallow depth of field on card edges, macro lens perspective emphasizing card details, holographic light dispersion effects, sound design: ambient room tone, subtle magical chime when eyes first move, building orchestral swell during the "alive" phase, satisfying *snap* when character returns to static pose, pin-drop silence at the final knowing smirk.

## Example

A cinematic 5-second video of a **shiny holographic Pikachu trading card from the Chinese 151 set** whose artwork magically comes to life in dramatic fashion.

**Phase 1 - Static Display (0-1.5s):** A pristine Pikachu card rests on dark velvet cloth under a single warm spotlight. The card features Pikachu in a moonlit forest scene—painted in a rich anime-watercolor style with Gengar lurking as a massive shadow among the trees, Ekans coiled nearby. The artwork is completely still, just a beautiful collectible card. Camera slowly pushes in, the holographic foil shimmering with rainbow light across the forest scene. The "皮卡丘 HP60" text and card border gleam. Everything is static—until a painted leaf in the forest canopy drifts sideways. A ripple crosses the illustrated pond. The moon in the artwork flickers.

**Phase 2 - Art Comes Alive (1.5-3.5s):** Pikachu's painted eyes BLINK. The 2D illustrated pupils shift and lock onto the camera with startled recognition. It looks down at its own painted hands, flexes its illustrated fingers. The entire card artwork awakens—painted trees sway in an imaginary wind, Gengar's shadow eyes glow with animated purple light, illustrated fireflies begin drifting through the forest scene. Pikachu takes a cautious step within the card, its movements still in the watercolor art style but now fully animated. It notices Gengar's shadow looming and its ears flatten—the painted expression shifting to genuine fear. The holographic surface erupts with intensified rainbow refractions as the magic strengthens, light dancing across the velvet surface.

**Phase 3 - Breaking Free & Return (3.5-5s):** Pikachu scrambles away from Gengar within the card, running toward the camera. It reaches the card border and presses both hands against it from inside—the card surface physically BULGES outward, the border bending in 3D space. Real light spills from the cracks. Pikachu pushes harder, cheeks sparking with electricity that ARCS out of the card surface, leaving tiny scorch marks on the velvet. For one breathtaking moment, Pikachu's face pokes through—a 3D head emerging from a 2D surface, real shadows cast on the table, looking directly at the viewer with wide amazed eyes. Then—**FLASH**—a burst of electric light, and Pikachu snaps back into the card. Static again. But now, instead of the original scared expression, Pikachu wears the SMUGGEST little grin. One eye winks. Its tail is positioned slightly differently—pointing upward in a victory pose. Gengar in the background looks confused. The holographic foil settles into calm shimmer. The card looks normal. But that wink says everything.

**Visual Style:** Hyper-realistic macro card photography meeting magical realism. Physical card rendered with extreme fidelity—visible linen card stock texture, crisp Japanese text, holographic constellation pattern, protective top-loader sleeve catching light. Pikachu's animation maintains the original watercolor-anime illustration style but adds dimensional depth. The moonlit forest palette (deep blues, silver moonlight, purple shadows) contrasts beautifully with the warm practical spotlight on the real-world velvet surface.

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format, color grading shifting from warm amber (3800K) during static phase to cool moonlit blue (8000K) during alive phase, macro lens with f/1.4 shallow depth of field, holographic chromatic aberration effects, electric spark particle system for Pikachu's cheek discharge, sound design: quiet room ambiance, subtle card-stock creak when surface bulges, Pikachu's soft "Pika?" whisper, building magical chime, electric crackle, dramatic silence at the final wink.
