### 小動物親子時光

Baby 小動物跟著爸媽學技能但總是做錯，爸媽溫柔糾正的瞬間讓人心都融化了。

---

## Prompt Template

A heartwarming 5-second video capturing precious parent-child moments in the animal kingdom, where baby animals adorably fail at copying their parents. The scene features **[親子組合：baby penguin with parent penguin / kitten with cat mom / puppy with dog dad / baby otter with otter parent]** as the baby attempts to mimic **[學習技能：walking like parent but keeps wobbling and falling / grooming itself but licking the wrong paw / howling but only squeaking / swimming but just floating belly-up]**.

[溫馨互動 with: 讓人心融化的親子瞬間 / "Parent gently nuzzles baby back upright after it tumbles, baby shakes off and tries again with determined tiny face" / "Mom cat patiently demonstrates paw-licking, kitten watches intently then licks its own ear instead, mom gives gentle head-bump of encouragement" / "Dad dog howls beautifully, puppy tries but produces tiny squeak, dad looks down with proud eyes anyway"]

Camera: Medium shot capturing both parent and baby in frame, natural outdoor/habitat lighting with soft golden hour glow. The focus shifts between parent's patient teaching and baby's clumsy attempts, creating emotional connection.

Technical execution: Shot at 60fps to capture subtle expressions and movements. Warm, natural color grading emphasizing golden sunlight and soft earth tones. Shallow depth of field (f/2.8) with gentle focus pulls between parent and baby. The video radiates pure wholesomeness—no sadness, only gentle encouragement and unconditional love. Optional sound: soft animal sounds, nature ambiance, gentle music.

The magic is in the patience: parent never scolds, only gently guides. Baby never gives up, keeps trying with innocent determination. Pure, uncomplicated love.
