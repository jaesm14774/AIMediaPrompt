### 畫中世界探險 - 角色穿越畫框

Q版可愛角色站在美術館巨幅名畫前，好奇地觸碰畫面——結果整個人被吸進畫裡！在畫中世界短暫冒險後，驚慌地從畫面中彈出來，身上沾滿顏料。

---

## Prompt Template

A whimsical 5-second video of **[角色描述："a tiny round Kirby figure" / "a small chibi cat character" / "a pudgy hamster adventurer" / "a miniature blob creature with a backpack"]** accidentally getting sucked into a famous painting at an art museum, having a wild adventure inside, and popping back out covered in paint.

**Phase 1 - Museum Curiosity (0-1.5s):** Inside a grand art museum with polished marble floors and warm gallery lighting, the small character stands before an enormous framed painting—**[名畫風格："Van Gogh's Starry Night with swirling cobalt skies" / "Monet's Water Lilies with dreamy pastel reflections" / "Hokusai's Great Wave with towering indigo surf" / "Vermeer's Girl with a Pearl Earring in soft golden light"]**. The character tilts its head, captivated by the artwork. It slowly raises one stubby hand toward the canvas surface. Gallery is quiet, elegant, other paintings visible on walls in soft focus. The character's tiny finger makes contact with the paint surface—and a ripple spreads across the canvas like touching water.

**Phase 2 - Sucked In (1.5-3s):** The painting PULLS the character in. The character's expression shifts to pure shock as the canvas surface stretches like a membrane, wrapping around it. With a cartoonish *SCHLORP*, the character disappears into the painting entirely. The canvas ripples settle. Inside the painting world, we see the character tumbling through the artwork in the painting's own art style—**[畫中冒險："rolling down Van Gogh's swirling hills, hair and body painted in thick impasto brushstrokes, stars spiraling around it" / "splashing into Monet's lily pond, emerging covered in pastel smudges, frogs made of brushstrokes leaping away" / "surfing down Hokusai's Great Wave, screaming silently in woodblock print style, fish-shaped ink splashes everywhere" / "standing frozen next to the Girl, accidentally wearing the pearl earring, both staring at camera in identical poses"]**. The character is rendered entirely in the painting's art style—it HAS BECOME part of the artwork.

**Phase 3 - Emergency Exit (3-5s):** The character panics inside the painting. It runs toward the viewer, growing larger as it approaches the canvas surface from inside. The painting surface bulges outward dramatically. The character BURSTS out of the frame—**flying through the air in the real museum**, trailing splashes of paint in the painting's color palette. It tumbles across the polished marble floor, leaving colorful paint streaks. Lands in a dazed heap. Sits up, blinking. Its entire body is stained with the painting's colors—**[沾滿顏料："covered in Van Gogh's signature cobalt blue and cadmium yellow swirls, one starry spiral still spinning on its cheek" / "dripping with Monet's soft pinks and greens, a tiny painted lily stuck to its head" / "splattered with Hokusai's deep indigo and white foam patterns, a small woodblock-style fish flopping on its lap" / "wearing the pearl earring and a confused Renaissance expression that slowly fades to exhausted resignation"]**. Behind it, the painting has returned to normal—except the character's tiny footprints are now permanently painted into the scene. Museum alarm starts flashing red.

**Visual Style:** Photorealistic museum environment (marble floors, gallery lighting, ornate gold frames) contrasting with stylized painting interiors. The character's art style transforms seamlessly when entering the painting—becoming impasto brushstrokes in Van Gogh, soft watercolor in Monet, woodblock print in Hokusai. Paint splatter and color contamination rendered with satisfying fluid dynamics. Comedy derives from the mismatch between the sacred museum atmosphere and the chaotic paint-covered aftermath.

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format (TikTok/Reels optimized), dual color grading—warm museum gold (3500K) for real world, painting-specific palette for art world segments, dramatic depth of field shift when entering painting, fluid paint simulation for splatter effects, cloth/surface paint adhesion physics, sound design: hushed museum ambiance, magical ripple *bloop* on canvas touch, whooshing suction sound, painting-world sounds muffled and dreamy, explosive *POP* on exit, wet paint splat sounds, distant museum alarm beeping.

## Example

A whimsical 5-second video of **a tiny round Kirby** accidentally getting sucked into Van Gogh's Starry Night at an art museum, having a wild adventure inside, and popping back out covered in paint.

**Phase 1 - Museum Curiosity (0-1.5s):** Inside a grand art museum with polished marble floors and warm golden gallery lighting, a small pink Kirby stands before an enormous gilt-framed reproduction of Van Gogh's Starry Night. The painting towers above it—swirling cobalt skies, luminous yellow stars, the dark cypress tree reaching upward. Kirby tilts its round head, captivated. Other masterpieces line the walls in soft focus. Kirby slowly raises one stubby pink arm toward the thick impasto surface. Tiny red feet shuffle closer on the marble. The stubby hand touches the canvas—and a ripple spreads across the painted sky like a stone dropped in water. The swirling stars stutter in their orbits.

**Phase 2 - Sucked In (1.5-3s):** The Starry Night PULLS Kirby in. Kirby's blue eyes go wide as the canvas surface stretches like taffy around its round body. With a cartoonish *SCHLORP*, Kirby vanishes into the painting. The canvas settles. Inside the Starry Night world, Kirby tumbles down the rolling hills rendered entirely in Van Gogh's thick impasto brushstroke style—Kirby itself is now made of visible paint strokes, pink and red oils layered thickly. It rolls through the village, past the church steeple, and careens toward the massive cypress tree. Stars spiral around Kirby's head like cartoon dizziness, but they're Van Gogh's actual luminous yellow-white stars, leaving paint trails. The swirling sky pulls at Kirby's body, stretching its round form into momentary spirals. Kirby grabs onto the cypress tree, dangling from a branch made of dark green brushstrokes.

**Phase 3 - Emergency Exit (3-5s):** Kirby panics, lets go of the cypress, and runs directly toward the viewer. It grows larger as it approaches the canvas surface from inside, the painting's perspective warping. The canvas bulges outward in the museum—the swirling sky stretching into 3D space. Kirby EXPLODES out of the frame, soaring through the museum air trailing thick splatters of cobalt blue, cadmium yellow, and cerulean paint. It tumbles across the polished marble floor leaving a Jackson Pollock-style streak of Van Gogh colors. Lands in a dazed pile. Sits up, blinking. Its entire pink body is stained with Starry Night—cobalt blue swirls across its face, one luminous yellow star still slowly spinning on its left cheek, thick impasto texture visible on its surface. A tiny painted cypress branch is stuck to its head like a hat. Behind Kirby, the Starry Night has returned to normal—except there's a tiny Kirby-shaped gap in the hillside and two small pink footprints painted into the village path. The museum security alarm starts flashing red. Kirby looks at the camera with exhausted "I've made a terrible mistake" eyes.

**Visual Style:** Photorealistic museum with marble floors, track lighting, and ornate gilded frames. Kirby's art style seamlessly transforms when inside the painting—becoming thick impasto oil paint with visible brushstroke texture, palette knife marks, and Van Gogh's signature swirling energy. Paint splatter on exit rendered with satisfying fluid dynamics and realistic oil paint viscosity. Post-exit Kirby is a hilarious hybrid—3D glossy toy body covered in 2D painted textures that shouldn't physically exist on its surface.

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format, warm museum lighting (3500K) contrasting with Starry Night's cool cobalt palette (9000K), dramatic rack focus when entering painting, fluid paint simulation with oil-paint viscosity for splatter, impasto displacement mapping inside painting world, sound design: hushed gallery ambiance with distant footsteps, magical water-ripple *bloop*, dramatic suction whoosh, muffled dreamy Van Gogh world sounds, explosive canvas *POP*, wet oil paint *SPLAT SPLAT SPLAT*, Kirby's soft distressed squeak, museum alarm crescendo.
