### 萌寵認真工作中

小動物穿著小西裝認真打字、接電話，笨拙又可愛的模樣讓人忍不住笑出來。

---

## Prompt Template

A delightfully wholesome 5-second video of adorable animals "seriously working" at human jobs but in the most clumsy, endearing way possible. The scene features **[萌寵角色：chubby hamster / fluffy kitten / baby hedgehog / tiny bunny]** wearing **[工作裝扮：mini office suit and tiny glasses / chef hat and apron / doctor coat with stethoscope / barista apron]** attempting to **[工作任務：type on a keyboard with tiny paws / answer a desk phone / examine a patient / make coffee]**.

[笨拙可愛瞬間 with: 會讓人笑的失敗時刻 / "Hamster typing but the keyboard is too big, keeps sliding across the desk as it waddles after it, serious concentration face" / "Kitten trying to answer phone but keeps batting at the receiver like a toy, then gives up and sits on it looking proud" / "Hedgehog in doctor coat examining a teddy bear patient, gently poking it with nose, adjusting tiny glasses that keep slipping"]

Camera: Closeup shot at eye-level with the tiny worker, warm office/workplace lighting with soft bokeh background. The animal's expressions are captured in detail—focused eyes, little tongue sticking out in concentration, tiny paws struggling adorably.

Technical execution: Shot at 60fps for smooth capture of quick movements. Soft, pastel color grading with warm amber tones creates cozy atmosphere. Shallow depth of field (f/2.0) keeps focus on the animal while softly blurring the miniature workspace props. Natural lighting with practical desk lamp creates realistic office ambiance. Optional sound: tiny keyboard clicks, soft squeaks, gentle ambient office sounds.

The charm comes from the contrast: serious miniature work setup vs. the animal's innocent clumsiness. NOT cartoon—use real animals or hyper-realistic CGI animals in realistic miniature environments.
