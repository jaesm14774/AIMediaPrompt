## Example

A charming 5-second video of **Kirby** playing an adorable peek-a-boo game in a **cozy bedroom with soft white curtains and warm afternoon lighting**.

**Phase 1 - Hidden (0-1s):** Camera focuses on a gently swaying white curtain in a warm-lit bedroom. The beige wall and ceiling-mounted AC unit visible in the background. Subtle movement hints at something hiding behind the curtain. A tiny pink hand barely peeking out at the curtain edge. Soft ambient room sounds and gentle fabric rustling.

**Phase 2 - Shy Peek (1-3s):** The Kirby slowly emerges from behind the curtain, only showing the top half of its round head with big sparkling blue eyes looking left and right nervously. Its small hand twitch with curiosity. Expression shifts from cautious to playfully mischievous. Slight wobble in its body suggests contained excitement. Golden hour sunlight catches the character's glossy pink surface, creating a warm glow.

**Phase 3 - SURPRISE! (3-5s):** Kirby suddenly BURSTS out from behind the curtain with stubby arms spread wide, eyes squeezed shut in pure joy, mouth opened in the biggest triumphant smile! Camera shakes slightly from the dramatic entrance. The character bounces once on landing, body squishing adorably then popping back to shape. It strikes a proud pose with a satisfied smirk. Sparkles and light particles swirl around from the curtain movement. The curtain continues swaying dramatically from the explosive exit.

**Visual Style:** Warm domestic interior with soft beige walls, white flowing curtains catching afternoon sunlight, visible dust particles dancing in light beams. Kirby rendered in smooth cel-shaded animation style—glossy pink surface with subtle subsurface scattering, red-orange feet providing color contrast. Room filled with cozy realistic details.

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format (TikTok/Reels optimized), shallow depth of field creating dreamy bokeh on background elements, warm color grading (3500K), subtle motion blur on fast movements, playful bounce easing on all character movements, sound design featuring soft fabric rustling and a comedic "BOING" on the jump-scare moment.
