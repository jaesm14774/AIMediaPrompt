### 一杯珍奶引發的堵壁大亂鬥

外送員（IP角色）拎著一杯珍奶來到古厝堵壁前準備交單，結果整面牆的所有元素同時伸出來搶——牡丹花瓣當手抓、菱格紋翻開露出一堆小手、山水畫裡的文人探出半個身體扯外送袋、如意雲紋像觸手纏住杯子。一杯珍奶讓百年古蹟全面失控。

## Prompt Template

A chaotic hilarious 5-second video of **[外送員角色："a tiny Kirby delivery driver" / "a small Pikachu courier" / "a miniature Totoro delivery runner" / "a chibi Doraemon delivery bot"]** arriving at a traditional Southern Fujian temple wall relief (堵壁) to deliver ONE bubble tea — only for EVERY element of the ancient wall decoration to come alive and fight over it.

**Phase 1 - Delivery Arrival (0-1.5s):** A quiet traditional courtyard (三合院). Afternoon sunlight. A pristine ornate wall relief panel — the classic Southern Fujian temple decoration with layered borders of **glazed ceramic peony flowers (牡丹)**, **geometric diamond lattice (菱格紋)**, **gold-green-blue decorative trim**, and a central **classical Chinese landscape painting** with traditional scholarly figures. Everything is perfectly still, perfectly dignified, perfectly ancient. Then — a tiny **[外送員角色]** walks into frame from the side, wearing a miniature delivery uniform and helmet, carrying a single bubble tea cup in a delivery bag. It looks at a tiny phone screen showing the delivery address: **「古厝堵壁中央山水畫，左邊第二位文人，不要加冰」(Temple wall relief, center landscape panel, 2nd scholar from left, no ice)**. The character looks up at the wall. Looks at the phone. Looks at the wall again. Shrugs. Holds up the bubble tea toward the wall and calls out: **[喊單方式："「珍奶一杯！誰的？」(One boba! Who ordered?)" / "「外送到！請簽收！」(Delivery! Please sign!)" / rings a tiny bicycle bell *ding ding*"]**. A beat of absolute silence. The wall is still. Then — every single element on the wall **twitches simultaneously**.

**Phase 2 - Total Wall Chaos (1.5-3.5s):** The wall ERUPTS. Every decorative element comes alive AT ONCE, all reaching for the single bubble tea:

**Peony Attack (牡丹搶食):** The glazed ceramic peony flowers LUNGE — petals opening wide like grabbing hands, stems extending and stretching from the wall surface toward the bubble tea. **[牡丹動作："two peonies on opposite sides reach toward the cup simultaneously, their petal-fingers interlocking in a wrestling grip over the straw" / "one peony wraps its stem around the cup like a tentacle, another peony bites the stem to make it let go, ceramic petal fragments flying" / "a peony catches the cup but another peony slaps it away with a leaf — the cup goes airborne"]**.

**Lattice Hands (菱格小手):** The geometric diamond lattice pattern tiles **flip open like tiny trap doors** — and from EACH diamond cell, a tiny ceramic hand reaches out. **[菱格動作："dozens of miniature hands emerge simultaneously from the lattice grid, all grabbing toward the bubble tea, fingers wiggling frantically — it looks like a zombie horde of tiny elegant ceramic hands" / "the lattice hands form a bucket-brigade chain, passing the cup across the grid at high speed, but the last hand fumbles and the cup bounces off" / "the hands start fighting EACH OTHER, slapping neighboring hands away, some pulling other hands back into their cells"]**.

**Painting Figures Break Out (畫中人搶出):** The classical painted scholars in the center landscape **lean OUT of their scene** — upper bodies pushing through the painting surface into 3D, arms stretching for the cup. **[文人動作："the 'reading scholar' reaches out with his scroll still in one hand, using it like a fishing rod to hook the delivery bag — but the 'guqin player' grabs the scroll's other end, starting a tug-of-war that rips the painted scroll in half" / "the 'moon gazer' has the longest arms and nearly grabs the cup — but the other two scholars pile onto his back, all three half-out of the painting in a comedic pyramid" / "one scholar has completely exited the painting and is standing on the decorative trim like a ledge, desperately reaching — but his painted lower half is still flat in the painting, creating a hilariously stretched half-3D figure"]**.

**Cloud Tentacles (雲紋觸手):** The spiral cloud motifs (如意雲紋) in the corners **unwind and extend like tentacles** — **[雲紋動作："one spiral wraps around the bubble tea cup, another spiral wraps around the first spiral trying to pry it off, creating a spiral-on-spiral wrestling match" / "a cloud spiral gently lifts the cup away from everyone — momentarily elegant — then THREE other spirals lash out and grab it, creating a five-way tug-of-war" / "the cloud spirals try to be sneaky, slowly creeping along the wall surface toward the cup while everyone else fights, but get caught and yanked back by a peony stem"]**.

The tiny delivery character is **[外送員反應："standing below in absolute terror, pressed against the opposite wall, watching the century-old heritage art have a full meltdown over one drink — slowly putting on their helmet as ceramic debris rains down" / "frantically checking the order receipt again — 「是哪位啊！？」(WHICH ONE OF YOU ORDERED THIS!?)" / "trying to hold onto the cup but getting lifted off the ground by competing tentacles/hands/stems, dangling helplessly in the middle of the chaos"]**. The bubble tea cup is being yanked in four directions simultaneously — straw bending, lid popping, tapioca pearls threatening to spill.

**Phase 3 - Resolution & Aftermath (3.5-5s):** The chaos reaches a peak — all elements pulling the cup simultaneously — and **[結局："the cup EXPLODES — bubble tea splashes across the ENTIRE wall relief, tapioca pearls bouncing off ceramic peonies like tiny bouncy balls, brown milk tea dripping down the classical landscape painting, staining the scholars' white robes, filling the diamond lattice cells like tiny tea pools — everyone freezes in shock — the delivery character holds up the empty cup and the receipt: 「配送完成。好評求求了。」(Delivery complete. 5 stars please.)" / "the cup's lid pops off and the bubble tea RAINS down across the wall — tapioca pearls land in the geometric lattice cells like balls in a pachinko machine, bouncing from diamond to diamond — milk tea runs down the painted mountains like a new river in the landscape — one scholar catches a single tapioca pearl in his mouth, closes his eyes in bliss — totally worth it" / "the straw gets pulled out during the chaos — a single tapioca pearl sits on top of the straw — it rolls down in slow motion, everyone watches, it falls toward... the tiniest lattice hand, which catches it perfectly, pulls it into its diamond cell, and the tile SLAPS shut triumphantly — everyone else stares — RIOT ERUPTS AGAIN"]**. Final frame: the wall is a MESS — milk tea stains on priceless heritage art, tapioca pearls lodged in ceramic flowers, one scholar still licking tea off his painted sleeve. The delivery character walks away, dejected, one star rating appearing above its head. **A tiny sign has been posted on the wall: 「本堵壁暫停接單」(This wall relief is temporarily not accepting orders).**

**Visual Style:** Photorealistic Southern Fujian temple architecture — white plaster walls, wooden beam ceiling, brick floor. Museum-quality Koji pottery (交趾陶) decorative elements rendered with glossy ceramic glazes — but all animated with cartoon slapstick energy. The humor is the contrast between the SACREDNESS of heritage art and the CHAOS of modern food delivery culture. Every decorative element retains its traditional visual style while behaving with sitcom-level physicality. Bubble tea rendered photorealistically — visible tapioca pearls, realistic milk tea viscosity, satisfying liquid splatter physics. The final messy wall should look hilariously sacrilegious — priceless ancient art covered in boba.

**Technical specs:** 60fps, 4K, 9:16 vertical, warm afternoon (4500K), ceramic surface specular highlights, fluid dynamics for bubble tea splatter (milk tea viscosity, tapioca pearl bounce physics), ceramic petal/stem extension animation with material-accurate bending, lattice tile flip mechanics, painted-figure-to-3D transition rendering, sound design: quiet courtyard ambiance, tiny delivery bell *ding ding*, beat of silence, then EXPLOSION of activity — ceramic *creak-crack-stretch* sounds as elements come alive, dozens of tiny lattice tiles flipping *clack-clack-clack*, peony stems *creaking* as they extend, scholars grunting as they push out of painting, cloud spirals *whooshing*, the delivery character's tiny panicked squeaks, cup *straining* under multi-directional tension, climactic *POP-SPLASH* of cup exploding, tapioca pearls *bouncing* off ceramic surfaces (satisfying hollow *pok-pok-pok*), milk tea *dripping*, final deflated sigh from delivery character, comedic sad trombone *wah-wah*.

## Example

A chaotic hilarious 5-second video of **a tiny Kirby delivery driver** arriving at a traditional Southern Fujian temple wall relief (堵壁) to deliver ONE bubble tea — only for EVERY element of the ancient wall decoration to come alive and fight over it.

**Phase 1 - Delivery Arrival (0-1.5s):** A quiet traditional Southern Fujian courtyard (三合院). Warm afternoon sunlight on aged white plaster walls. A pristine ornate wall relief panel — glazed ceramic peony flowers (牡丹) in pink and purple, geometric diamond lattice (菱格紋) in deep teal, gold-green-blue trim bands, and a central classical landscape painting with three dignified scholarly figures. Everything ancient, sacred, perfectly still. A tiny pink Kirby waddles into frame from the right — wearing an oversized delivery driver helmet (green, with a logo), a miniature thermal delivery bag strapped to its back. One stubby arm holds a single bubble tea cup — brown milk tea with visible tapioca pearls, plastic lid, bent straw. Kirby checks a tiny phone: delivery address reads 「古厝堵壁中央山水畫，左邊第二位文人，不要加冰」. Kirby looks up at the imposing wall. Looks at phone. Back at wall. Shrugs. Holds the bubble tea up toward the panel and calls out with its tiny voice. *Ding ding* goes a miniature bicycle bell on its delivery bag. 「珍奶一杯——誰的？」 Complete silence. The wall is ancient. The wall is still. Then — the leftmost peony trembles. A lattice tile rattles. A painted scholar's eye twitches. Everything moves at once.

**Phase 2 - Total Wall Chaos (1.5-3.5s):** The wall DETONATES into chaos:

**Peonies:** Four glazed ceramic peony flowers LUNGE simultaneously — petals splitting open like hungry mouths, stems extending from the wall surface like arms, reaching for the cup. Two peonies on opposite sides grab the cup at the same time, their petal-fingers interlocking in a ceramic wrestling match over the straw, glazed fragments chipping off from the force. A third peony wraps its stem around the first peony's stem trying to pry it loose. The fourth peony, frustrated, just slaps everyone with a broad ceramic leaf — *SMACK* — the cup goes airborne.

**Lattice Hands:** The geometric diamond lattice tiles flip open in a cascading wave — *clack-clack-clack-clack* — and from EVERY diamond cell, a tiny ceramic hand emerges. Dozens of miniature glazed hands, all reaching upward, fingers wiggling frantically toward the airborne cup. It looks like a crowd at a concert. Some hands start fighting neighboring hands — slapping, pulling fingers, one hand grabs another hand's wrist and bends it back into its cell — tiny ceramic hand warfare across the entire lattice grid.

**Painted Scholars:** All three classical landscape figures LEAN OUT of their painting simultaneously — upper bodies pushing through the ceramic surface into 3D, painted robes rippling as they transition from flat to dimensional. The leftmost scholar (the one who ordered) stretches both arms out desperately — 「那是我的！我等了三個時辰！」(That's MINE! I waited three hours!). The center scholar grabs the first scholar's robe collar and pulls him back — 「你上次也說是你點的！」(You said that LAST time too!). The rightmost scholar has climbed completely out of the painting onto the decorative trim ledge — but his lower half is still flat and painted, creating a hilariously stretched half-3D half-2D figure, arms windmilling for the cup.

**Cloud Spirals:** The corner cloud motifs (如意雲紋) uncoil like springs — two spirals extending as elegant tentacles, weaving between the fighting peonies and lattice hands. One spiral WRAPS around the bubble tea cup with surprising grace — lifts it above the chaos — a moment of calm — then THREE other spirals lash out and grab it, creating a four-way spiral tug-of-war that makes the entire wall vibrate.

Tiny Kirby is pressed against the opposite courtyard wall, delivery helmet on, watching the entire centuries-old heritage art installation have a complete meltdown over one drink. Its expression cycles through: shock → fear → confused professional obligation. It weakly holds up the receipt: 「是...是哪位...？」 A ceramic peony petal shard *tinks* off its helmet.

**Phase 3 - Resolution & Aftermath (3.5-5s):** All elements pulling the cup in four directions simultaneously — the plastic lid POPS off — the cup SQUEEZES — bubble tea ERUPTS like a geyser. Brown milk tea SPLASHES across the ENTIRE wall relief: dripping down glazed peony petals (filling the glaze crevices like tiny tea rivers), pooling in every diamond lattice cell (each cell now a miniature boba cup), streaming down the landscape painting like a new waterfall in the mountain scene, staining the scholars' white robes brown. Tapioca pearls BOUNCE across the wall like pinballs — *pok-pok-pok-pok* — one pearl lands perfectly in a peony's center (it closes around it protectively), three pearls roll into lattice cells (the hands catch them and pull down like scoring goals), one pearl bounces off a scholar's forehead and lands in the painted stream. Everyone FREEZES. The scholars, half-out of their painting, dripping with milk tea. The peonies, petals sticky with brown liquid. The lattice hands, each clutching a single tapioca pearl like treasure. The cloud spirals, drooping and tea-stained. Total silence. Then — the center scholar raises the single tapioca pearl he caught, places it in his mouth, chews slowly, and closes his eyes with an expression of transcendent bliss. Worth it. Absolutely worth it. Kirby, covered in milk tea splatter, holds up the crumpled empty cup and the receipt — taps "delivery complete" on its tiny phone. A one-star rating animation appears above its head with the review: 「收件地址不存在。客戶太多位。」(Address doesn't exist. Too many recipients.) Final frame: the wall is a glorious mess — priceless Koji pottery art dripping with boba tea, a handwritten cardboard sign taped to the wall: 「本堵壁暫停接單 (This wall relief is temporarily not accepting deliveries)」. Kirby waddles away, shell-shocked, dragging its delivery bag.

**Visual Style:** Photorealistic Southern Fujian temple — white plaster, wooden beams, brick floor. Museum-quality Koji pottery with glossy ceramic glazes — animated with Looney Tunes slapstick energy. Sacred heritage art × food delivery chaos. Bubble tea rendered with satisfying photorealistic liquid physics — milk tea viscosity, pearl bounce dynamics, splatter patterns. The tea-stained final frame should be hilariously sacrilegious — priceless ancient art turned boba crime scene.

**Technical specs:** 60fps, 4K, 9:16 vertical, warm afternoon (4500K), ceramic glaze specular, fluid dynamics (milk tea viscosity + tapioca bounce), ceramic extension/bending animation, lattice tile flip cascade, painted-to-3D figure transition, sound design: quiet courtyard → bell *ding ding* → silence beat → ERUPTION — ceramic *stretch-creak*, lattice *clack-clack-clack* cascade, peony stems *groaning*, scholars *grunting* and yelling in classical Chinese, cloud spirals *whooshing*, Kirby panic squeaks, cup *creaking* under pressure, climactic *POP-SPLASH-SPLATTER*, tapioca *pok-pok-pok* bouncing, milk tea *drip-drip-drip*, silence beat, satisfied *mmm* from the scholar, one-star review *ding* notification, sad trombone *wah-wah-wah*, Kirby's defeated sigh.
