### 偶像誕生音樂節奏 - 蛋糕舞台演唱會
# Birthday Cake Concert Stage

> **系列 / Series：** 偶像誕生音樂節奏短影片系列 · 將 `[IP角色]` 替換為圓滾滾可愛角色（Kirby / Pikachu / Pusheen 等）。共用規格見同資料夾 `偶像誕生音樂節奏短影片系列.md`。

**概念 / Concept：** 角色爬上巨大分層蛋糕舞台，向麥克風深情演唱，蛋糕裝飾小角色們在旁應援歡呼。

---

## Prompt Template

A heartwarming 7-second video of **[IP角色]** performing a dramatic concert on a `[蛋糕舞台：tiered birthday cake stage / giant layered dessert platform / multi-level pastel cake]` under a dramatic spotlight.

**Scene 1 - The Stage Reveal (0-1.5s)**

Wide shot: magnificent `[蛋糕描述：2-tier cylindrical cake with star/dot patterns, gold polka dots on blue fondant, star decorations around base tier / pastel tiered cake with ribbon decorations]`.

- **蛋糕觀眾 / Cake audience（擇一）：**  
  - `"tiny miniature versions of [IP角色] around the base tier — they're actually an audience"`  
  - `"small figurines holding tiny glow sticks around the base"`  
- **背景 / Background：** `[演唱會背景：light blue sky with soft stars / gradient blue with twinkling points of light]`  
- Oversized boom mic descends. Crowd (tiny figures) starts buzzing

**Scene 2 - The Climb (1.5-2.5s)**

- **[IP角色]** at base, climbing to top tier — **爬升 / Climb（擇一）：**  
  - `"scaling the side with tiny arm-grabs, feet kicking, flopping onto top tier"`  
  - `"pulling itself over the edge with effort, one leg dangling"`  
  - `"bouncing up tier by tier in three quick jumps"`  
- Tiny audience REACT: `[觀眾反應："they all turn to watch, tiny arms raised" / "swaying side to side in unison" / "small cheer effect ripples through the crowd"]`

**Scene 3 - The Reach (2.5-4.5s)**

On top tier, reaching for hanging mic.

- **伸手 / Reach（擇一）：**  
  - `"arm fully extended upward, fingers barely grazing the mic — the stretch is everything"`  
  - `"tip-toeing, whole body elongated reaching for it"`  
  - `"jumping slightly to catch it, finally grasping on"`  
- Expression: effort → `[表情轉換：pure peaceful joy, eyes closing gently / soft emotional smile, head tilting back slightly]`  
- Stars glow brighter. Audience holds breath

**Scene 4 - The Song (4.5-6.0s)**

"Singing" — **姿態 / Sing pose（擇一）：**  
- `"mouth opens wide in an oval, head back, one arm raised — idol ballad pose"`  
- `"swaying gently, eyes closed, lost in the music"`  
- `"mouth opening/closing rhythmically, cheeks slightly puffed with each note"`  

**演唱背景 / Singing BG：** `[stars multiply and pulse with rhythm / light rays behind character / aurora-like color waves in sky]`. Tiny audience sways below.

**Scene 5 - Emotional Peak (6.0-7.0s)**

Slow pull back: full scene — small performer, adoring crowd, vast sky.

- **感動元素 / Emotional beat（擇一）：**  
  - `"single glowing star drifts down onto [IP角色]'s outstretched hand"`  
  - `"audience all raise tiny arms at once, synchronized wave"`  
  - `"light rays from behind intensify, beautiful silhouette"`  
- Hold wide shot. Optional: single tear on cheek in stage light

**Visual Style**

Contrast: cozy birthday cake vs dramatic concert. Tiny figurines = humor + heart. Warm blue concert light, golden star accents. Small character vs big sky = emotional scale.

**Technical specs**

9:16 vertical, 4K, 60fps. Camera: wide → medium → push-in on singing face → pull back for finale. Sound: upbeat intro → emotional ballad → soft sustained note. Tiny crowd ambiance.
