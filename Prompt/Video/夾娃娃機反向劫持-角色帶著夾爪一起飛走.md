### 夾娃娃機反向劫持——角色帶著夾爪一起飛走 (Claw Machine Reverse Heist — Character Flies Away WITH the Claw)

夾爪成功夾住了會飛的角色——但角色比夾爪更強。它張開翅膀/膨脹起來，連夾爪帶鋼索整組一起拽飛了。不是你夾我，是我帶你走。夾娃娃機史上最大的系統漏洞。

## Prompt Template

A hilarious and adorable 5-second video in soft kawaii illustration style — the claw machine's biggest system failure: it successfully grabs a **[在此填入會飛的角色：如 Kirby / baby owl / tiny dragon / flying hamster / 自訂會飛角色]**, but the character is STRONGER than the machine. Instead of being lifted by the claw, the character lifts the claw.

**Phase 1 — The Grab (0-1.5s):** Inside a pastel-colored claw machine. The mechanical claw descends from above and successfully clamps around the flying character's round body — a perfect grab, textbook form. The character is [被抓瞬間的反應 with: 被夾住時的可愛表情 / "initially startled — eyes popping open wide, cheeks squished by the claw's metal fingers pressing into its soft round body, a surprised 'oh!' expression with tiny sweat drops" / "completely unbothered — still half-asleep, eyes barely open, the claw squeezing its body only makes it look like a cute stress ball being gently pressed, yawning mid-grab" / "looking mildly annoyed — one eyebrow raised, giving the claw a deadpan 'really?' stare, arms crossed over its chest even as the claw squeezes"]. The claw's cable tenses as the machine tries to lift the character upward. Other plush toys around watch with tiny nervous expressions (subtle background detail).

**Phase 2 — The Power Reversal (1.5-3.5s):** The claw tries to lift — cable straining, motor whirring — but the character doesn't budge. Beat. Then the character's expression shifts to a confident little smirk. It [啟動飛行能力 with: 角色開始飛的方式 / "takes a deep breath and puffs up its body to twice its normal size, inflating like a balloon — the claw's grip actually tightens harder as the body expands, but now the character is buoyant and starting to RISE on its own power, pulling the claw upward instead" / "unfurls small hidden wings from behind its back — one dramatic wing-spread motion that sends a gust of wind through the machine, ruffling the other plushies' fur — then begins flapping with surprising power" / "closes its eyes in concentration, and its body begins to glow with a soft warm light — it levitates upward through pure willpower, completely ignoring the claw's feeble grip as if gravity simply doesn't apply"]. The claw's cable goes SLACK as the character rises ABOVE the claw's original position. The motor makes a confused grinding sound. The cable starts being pulled out of the machine's ceiling mechanism — the character is pulling the entire claw assembly upward.

**Phase 3 — The Escape & Punchline (3.5-5s):** The character, still firmly held by the claw (it doesn't even bother removing it), flies straight down toward the prize chute — [帶著夾爪一起飛的畫面 with: 離開的方式 / "dragging the claw, cable, and a chunk of the ceiling mechanism behind it like a trail — the metal claw dangles from its body like an oversized necklace as it squeezes through the exit flap, popping out the bottom with the claw still attached, sitting on the prize tray wearing the claw as a ridiculous hat" / "doing a victory lap inside the machine first — flying in a circle with the claw trailing behind like a kite tail, the cable wrapping around inside the machine in a spiral, before finally diving out the exit chute in one smooth motion, the claw clattering out after it" / "gently floating downward with the claw still gripping its body, treating the mechanical arm like a baby carrier — nestled comfortably in the claw's grip as both descend together through the exit, landing softly outside, then the character pats the claw fondly as if thanking it for the ride"]. **Visual Style:** Soft kawaii 2D animation style with pastel color palette — warm pink, cream, baby blue, lavender. Thick rounded outlines, characters with dot eyes and simple curved mouths. The mechanical claw is drawn with slightly harder/straighter lines to contrast with the soft round character. Sparkle and glow effects when the character activates its flying ability. The humor is gentle and wholesome — the character doesn't destroy the machine, it simply has a superpower that makes the claw irrelevant. The tone is "life hack discovered" not "machine defeated."

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format (Reels/TikTok/Shorts optimized), warm pastel color grading, camera follows the character's vertical movement — starts at claw level, follows down to the exit chute. ## Example

A hilarious and adorable 5-second video in soft kawaii illustration style — the claw machine's biggest system failure: it successfully grabs **Kirby**, but Kirby is STRONGER than the machine.

**Phase 1 — The Grab (0-1.5s):** Inside a pastel pink claw machine. The silver mechanical claw descends and successfully clamps around Kirby's round pink body — a perfect grab. Kirby is initially startled — big dot eyes popping open wide, round cheeks squished adorably by the claw's three metal fingers pressing into its soft body like squeezing a stress ball, a surprised 'oh!' expression with tiny sweat drops popping out. The claw's cable tenses as the machine tries to lift Kirby upward. Around Kirby, a teddy bear and a bunny plush watch with tiny worried bead eyes (subtle detail).

**Phase 2 — The Power Reversal (1.5-3.5s):** The claw tries to lift — cable straining, motor whirring with effort — but Kirby doesn't move an inch. Beat. Then Kirby's surprised expression slowly melts into a confident little smirk — the classic "I know something you don't" Kirby face. Kirby takes a deep breath — a BIG one — and puffs up its body to twice its normal size, inflating like a round pink balloon. The claw's metal fingers actually tighten as Kirby's body expands between them, but now Kirby is buoyant and starting to RISE on its own power. The claw's cable goes slack. Then starts being pulled UPWARD. The motor makes a confused grinding sound — it's designed to lift things, not to be lifted. Kirby floats higher, cheeks puffed out to maximum roundness, stubby arms out to the sides, pulling the entire claw assembly upward with effortless grace. The cable unspools from the ceiling mechanism. Inside the machine, the other plush toys' eyes follow Kirby upward in unison.

**Phase 3 — The Escape & Punchline (3.5-5s):** Kirby, still firmly held by the claw (doesn't even bother removing it), gently floats downward toward the prize chute — treating the mechanical arm like a baby carrier, nestled comfortably in the claw's grip as both descend together. Kirby squeezes through the exit flap with a soft *pop*, the claw clattering through after it. Final shot: Kirby sits on the prize tray outside the machine, the detached silver claw sitting next to it on the tray. Kirby gives the claw a warm, grateful little hug — wrapping both stubby pink arms around one of the claw's metal fingers — then looks up at the camera with the most innocent, proud sparkle-eyed smile. A tiny pink heart floats up between Kirby and the claw. It doesn't see the claw as an enemy — it's a new friend being brought home. Inside the machine, visible through the glass: a dangling empty cable where the claw used to be, the teddy bear and bunny staring in frozen shock, one small plush in the back slowly, solemnly clapping its tiny paw.

**Visual Style:** Soft kawaii 2D animation style with pastel color palette — warm pink, cream, baby blue, lavender. Thick rounded outlines, Kirby's signature pink with simple dot eyes and curved mouth. The silver claw is drawn with slightly harder lines to contrast with Kirby's soft roundness. Soft glow radiates from Kirby's body when it inflates. The humor is gentle — Kirby doesn't destroy anything, it simply makes the claw's job irrelevant through sheer natural ability. The final hug is the emotional punchline: this isn't a heist, it's a rescue — Kirby is "saving" the claw from its boring job.

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format (Reels/TikTok/Shorts optimized), warm pastel color grading with soft pink glow accents, camera follows Kirby's vertical movement — starts at grab level, pans down to exit. Sound design: mechanical whirr and strain of claw motor, Kirby's signature cute inhale as it puffs up, gentle balloon-float sound, cable tension and release, satisfying *pop* exiting the chute, metallic *clatter* of the claw landing on the tray, warm musical sting for the heart-float moment, one soft distant *clap clap clap* from inside the machine. 5 seconds total.
