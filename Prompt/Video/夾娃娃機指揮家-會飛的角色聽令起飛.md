### 夾娃娃機指揮家——會飛的角色聽令起飛 (Claw Machine Conductor — Flying Character Takes Off on Command)

誰說夾娃娃機一定要用夾爪？一隻手伸進投幣口的方向，拿著迷你指揮棒對著機器裡裝睡的會飛角色輕輕一揮——角色睜眼、起身、自己飛出出口。這是夾娃娃機最大的 bug：會飛的角色根本不需要被夾。

---

## Prompt Template

A heartwarming and hilarious 5-second video in soft kawaii illustration style — a **[在此填入會飛的角色：如 Kirby / Pikachu / baby dragon / flying squirrel / 自訂會飛角色]** lying inside a pastel-colored claw machine (UFO catcher), pretending to be a regular plush toy among other non-flying plushies.

**Phase 1 — The Setup (0-1.5s):** Close-up of the claw machine interior. The flying character lies on its back among a pile of ordinary plush toys, eyes closed, body limp and floppy like a stuffed animal, perfectly imitating a lifeless toy. [角色裝死狀態 with: 假裝是普通娃娃的樣子 / "round body completely relaxed and deflated-looking, arms and legs splayed out like a ragdoll, a tiny price tag clipped to one ear, blending in perfectly with the other real plush toys around it" / "curled up in a cozy ball among the other plushies, using a neighboring teddy bear as a pillow, a thin line of drool suggesting it's actually napping for real, not just pretending" / "lying stiff as a board with a frozen smile, eyes squeezed shut a little TOO tightly — clearly trying very hard to look 'not alive,' one eye occasionally cracking open to peek"]. The claw hangs motionless above, completely irrelevant to what's about to happen. Soft ambient glow inside the machine, gentle sparkle particles floating.

**Phase 2 — The Conductor's Command (1.5-3s):** A hand appears from outside the machine's glass — NOT reaching for the joystick, but holding a tiny conductor's baton (a miniature golden stick with a sparkly star tip). The hand gracefully waves the baton toward the machine in a single elegant conducting gesture — like a maestro cueing an orchestra. A trail of golden sparkle dust follows the baton's arc. [指揮動作 with: 指揮棒揮動方式 / "one smooth sweeping motion from left to right, like conducting the downbeat of a symphony — the sparkle trail forms a gentle arc that passes through the glass and reaches the character inside" / "a quick, playful flick of the wrist — tap-tap on the glass twice, then a dramatic upward sweep that sends a shower of tiny star particles raining down inside the machine" / "a slow, deliberate circular motion that draws a glowing spiral in the air — the spiral shrinks and floats through the glass like a magical summon circle hovering above the sleeping character"]. The sleeping character's ear/antenna/wing twitches once. A subtle musical note (♪) floats up from the baton's tip.

**Phase 3 — The Awakening & Takeoff (3-5s):** The character's eyes snap open — sparkle eyes full of energy. It sits up among the plush toys, shaking off its "toy disguise" like a dog shaking off water. The other real plush toys tumble aside. The character puffs up — [起飛動作 with: 角色的飛行方式 / "inflates its body like a balloon, cheeks puffing out comically, then gently floats upward off the pile — hovering past the useless claw which sways from the breeze, drifting toward the prize chute" / "spreads tiny hidden wings that were folded flat against its body, does one big flap that sends nearby plush toys flying in all directions, then swoops in a joyful spiral downward toward the exit" / "takes a deep breath and exhales a stream of air downward like a tiny jet engine, launching straight up, bonking its head on the machine ceiling with a comedic bounce, then recalibrating and gliding smoothly toward the exit chute"]. It flies/floats directly down into the prize chute and pops out the bottom flap with a satisfying *pop* — landing gently on the prize tray outside the machine, looking up at the conductor's hand with a happy, grateful expression. The baton taps once more — a tiny heart appears. The claw still hangs above, completely unused and confused (the claw sways slightly as if shrugging).

**Visual Style:** Soft kawaii 2D animation style with pastel color palette — pink, lavender, cream, baby blue. Thick rounded outlines, characters with dot eyes and simple curved mouths. The claw machine interior glows with warm ambient light. Sparkle effects follow the conductor's baton. The flying character's movement is floaty and gentle — not fast or aggressive, but dreamy and weightless like a soap bubble. The contrast between the mechanical claw (cold, metallic, unnecessary) and the character's natural flight ability (warm, organic, effortless) is the core visual joke.

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format (Reels/TikTok/Shorts optimized), soft warm color grading with golden sparkle accents, gentle camera movements — slight push-in during the baton wave, slight pull-out as the character flies. Sound design: soft ambient hum of the machine, magical chime when baton waves, gentle whoosh of tiny wings/inflation, satisfying *pop* when character exits the chute, warm musical resolution at the end. 5 seconds total.

## Example

A heartwarming and hilarious 5-second video in soft kawaii illustration style — **Kirby** lying inside a pastel pink claw machine, pretending to be a regular plush toy among other non-flying plushies.

**Phase 1 — The Setup (0-1.5s):** Close-up of the claw machine interior. Kirby lies on its back among a pile of ordinary plush toys (a teddy bear, a bunny, a cat), eyes closed, round pink body completely relaxed and deflated-looking, arms and legs splayed out like a ragdoll, a tiny price tag clipped to one foot, blending in perfectly with the other real plush toys around it. Kirby's body is suspiciously rounder and pinker than the other toys, but otherwise looks convincingly lifeless. The silver claw hangs motionless above — completely irrelevant to what's about to happen. Soft ambient glow inside the machine, gentle sparkle particles floating in the warm light.

**Phase 2 — The Conductor's Command (1.5-3s):** A hand appears from outside the machine's glass — NOT reaching for the joystick, but holding a tiny conductor's baton (a miniature golden stick with a sparkly star tip). The hand performs one smooth sweeping motion from left to right, like conducting the downbeat of a symphony — a trail of golden sparkle dust follows the baton's arc, passes through the glass as if it weren't there, and reaches sleeping Kirby inside. A subtle musical note (♪) floats up from the baton's tip. Inside the machine, the sparkle dust settles gently on Kirby's face. One of Kirby's stubby arms twitches. Then its cheek. Then a tiny smile begins to form at the corner of its mouth.

**Phase 3 — The Awakening & Takeoff (3-5s):** Kirby's eyes snap open — big sparkling dot eyes full of joyful energy. It sits up among the plush toys, shaking off its "toy disguise" — the price tag flies off, the neighboring teddy bear tumbles over. Kirby takes a big breath and inflates its body like a balloon, round cheeks puffing out even rounder than usual, then gently floats upward off the pile — hovering past the useless silver claw which sways from the breeze of Kirby's ascent. Kirby does a playful mid-air spin, then glides smoothly downward toward the prize chute, tucking its arms in and slipping through the exit flap with a satisfying soft *pop* — landing gently on the prize tray outside the machine with a tiny bounce. Kirby looks up at the conductor's hand with the happiest, most grateful round-eyed smile. The baton taps once more in the air — a tiny pink heart appears between the baton and Kirby. Above, through the glass, the mechanical claw still hangs motionless, swaying slightly as if shrugging in confusion — it was never needed. The ultimate claw machine bug: Kirby can fly.

**Visual Style:** Soft kawaii 2D animation style with pastel color palette — pink, lavender, cream, baby blue. Thick rounded outlines, Kirby with dot eyes and simple curved mouth. The claw machine interior glows with warm ambient light. Golden sparkle effects follow the conductor's baton. Kirby's flight is floaty and gentle — not fast or aggressive, but dreamy and weightless like a soap bubble rising. The contrast between the cold metallic claw (unnecessary) and Kirby's natural inflation flight (effortless, warm) is the core visual joke.

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format (Reels/TikTok/Shorts optimized), soft warm color grading with golden sparkle accents, gentle camera movements — slight push-in during the baton wave, slight pull-out as Kirby floats up. Sound design: soft ambient machine hum, magical chime when baton waves, Kirby's signature cute inhale sound as it puffs up, gentle whoosh as it floats, satisfying *pop* exiting the chute, warm musical resolution at the end. 5 seconds total.
