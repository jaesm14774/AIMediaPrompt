### 意外DJ公仔

小公仔在桌上無聊轉著杯墊/盤子，結果被誤認為在打碟！有人放上耳機、打開霓虹燈，整個房間瞬間變成派對現場。公仔：「我只是想轉著玩...」

## Prompt Template

A hilarious 5-second video of **[公仔角色：Psyduck / chubby hamster figure / round blob creature / tiny cat plushie]** accidentally becoming the hottest DJ in town while mindlessly spinning something in a **[場景：messy bedroom desk / kitchen counter / office cubicle / living room coffee table]**.

**Phase 1 - Innocent Spinning (0-1.5s):** A small **[公仔描述："round yellow duck with vacant stare" / "chubby creature with tiny paws" / "adorable blob with unfocused eyes"]** sits at a surface, mindlessly spinning a circular object—**[轉動物品："a drink coaster, pushing it in lazy circles" / "a small plate, making it rotate slowly" / "a CD/vinyl record that was just lying there" / "the lid of a jar, spinning it like a top"]**. Expression is completely zoned out, eyes unfocused, mouth slightly open in a peaceful 'o'. The spinning is purely fidgeting—a bored habit with no purpose. **[無聊動作："one paw lazily pushing the object around in circles" / "both hands on the edge, rotating it back and forth" / "head bobbing slightly, but only because it's sleepy, not to any beat"]**. Normal room lighting, mundane everyday environment. The figure has NO idea what this looks like.

**Phase 2 - The Setup (1.5-3.5s):** A hand enters frame and **[第一個改變："places a pair of oversized headphones on the figure's head—they're comically large but somehow fit" / "drops a pair of DJ-style headphones around its neck" / "slides one headphone cup onto one ear, tilted at a 'professional DJ' angle"]**. The figure **[困惑反應："freezes mid-spin, eyes slowly crossing to look up at the headphones" / "continues spinning but blinks in confusion—'what is this on my head?'" / "the spinning motion stutters but doesn't stop—muscle memory too strong"]**. Beat. Another hand enters—**[氛圍升級："flicks a switch and NEON LIGHTS suddenly illuminate the scene in purple and pink" / "places a small disco ball nearby, casting spinning light patterns" / "turns on RGB LED strips around the desk, transforming the mundane space" / "positions a fog machine that starts gently puffing"]**. The figure's spinning motion, now bathed in party lights, suddenly looks **[意外專業："incredibly intentional, like a sick beat drop is coming" / "like it's scratching a record with purpose" / "absolutely professional—the lighting changed EVERYTHING"]**. More hands appear—**[觀眾出現："people's phones enter frame, recording" / "someone holds up a lighter/phone flashlight, swaying" / "hands appear making 'raise the roof' gestures"]**.

**Phase 3 - Drop the Beat (3.5-5s):** The figure is now in FULL DJ MODE—**[DJ姿態："one hand on the 'turntable' (still just a coaster), other hand raised in the air" / "head bobbing dramatically to an implied beat" / "eyes closed in concentration, like feeling the music"]**. The room has completely transformed—neon lights pulsing, silhouettes of people dancing in background, the mundane object now looks like professional DJ equipment.

**THE DROP:** The figure suddenly **[高潮瞬間："SLAMS its hand down on the 'turntable' in a dramatic beat drop motion—everything goes slow-mo for a split second" / "raises both arms up and the lights EXPLODE in intensity" / "does a spin move, headphones flying off dramatically but landing perfectly back on" / "points at camera with intense energy—'THIS ONE'S FOR YOU'"]**. Confetti or light particles burst around the scene. The figure's vacant expression has transformed into **[最終表情："the most intense, focused 'DJ face' possible—eyes slightly closed, chin up, feeling it" / "a smug grin—it has NO idea what's happening but it's VIBING" / "still confused but somehow looking cool anyway—the ultimate 'fake it till you make it'" / "breaking the fourth wall with a knowing look—'I have no idea what I'm doing but I'm the best at it'"]**. **[結尾畫面："crowd goes wild (implied through raised hands and phone flashlights)" / "someone throws money at the 'DJ'" / "a sign appears: 'DJ DERP - NOW BOOKING'"]**.

**Visual Style:** Dramatic contrast between mundane Phase 1 (normal room lighting, everyday objects) and transformed Phase 2-3 (club-style neon lighting, dramatic shadows, party atmosphere). Figure rendered with glossy toy aesthetic but lit like a superstar DJ. The comedy comes from the SAME spinning motion looking completely different once the "vibe" is established. Use of silhouettes, light beams, and fog for club atmosphere while keeping the setting recognizably domestic.

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format (TikTok/Reels/Shorts optimized), dramatic lighting shift from neutral (5500K) to neon party colors (purple 380nm, pink, cyan), strobe effects synced to implied beat, shallow depth of field with dramatic bokeh from LED lights, dynamic camera movement (slight push-in during "drop"), sound design: ambient room silence → building electronic beat → BASS DROP with crowd cheering, the satisfying "record scratch" sound when figure touches the turntable.

## Example

A hilarious 5-second video of **Psyduck (the perpetually confused yellow duck Pokémon)** accidentally becoming the hottest underground DJ while mindlessly spinning a coaster on a **messy bedroom desk**.

**Phase 1 - Innocent Spinning (0-1.5s):** A small Psyduck figure sits at the edge of a cluttered desk—energy drink cans, scattered papers, a gaming mouse visible nearby. It's absentmindedly pushing a round drink coaster in lazy circles with one stubby hand. The classic vacant Psyduck stare is in full effect: round eyes completely unfocused, small bill hanging open, radiating maximum "I am thinking about nothing" energy. The spinning is purely a fidget—it's been doing this for who knows how long. Its head bobs slightly, but that's just because it's sleepy, definitely not to any rhythm. Normal bedroom lighting from a desk lamp. Just a duck. Spinning a coaster. Existing.

**Phase 2 - The Setup (1.5-3.5s):** A human hand reaches into frame and carefully places a pair of comically oversized DJ headphones on Psyduck's head. The headphones are huge compared to its round body—one cup covering almost half its face, the other tilted at that classic "one ear off" DJ angle. Psyduck freezes mid-spin. Its pupils slowly cross upward, trying to look at the thing now on its head. The spinning motion resumes—it can't help itself. Another hand enters and *click*—LED strip lights around the desk BURST to life in purple and hot pink. The entire vibe shifts INSTANTLY. That same coaster-spinning motion, now bathed in neon, looks absolutely INTENTIONAL. A third hand places a tiny disco ball on the desk—spinning light patterns cascade across Psyduck's glossy surface. Phone screens appear at the edges of frame, recording. Someone's hand enters making a "raise the roof" gesture. Psyduck's expression hasn't changed AT ALL—still the same vacant confusion—but somehow in this lighting, it looks like intense DJ focus.

**Phase 3 - Drop the Beat (3.5-5s):** The bedroom has been COMPLETELY TRANSFORMED. Neon lights pulse rhythmically. Fog (from a humidifier hastily repurposed) drifts across the desk. Silhouettes of people bob in the background. Psyduck sits in the center of this chaos—one hand still on the coaster (which now looks undeniably like a turntable), the other raised in the air. Its head is bobbing more dramatically now—still probably just sleepy, but the timing accidentally matches an implied beat.

**THE DROP:** Psyduck suddenly SLAMS its raised hand down onto the coaster. Time slows for a split second. The lights FLASH. In perfect slow-motion, Psyduck's perpetually confused expression somehow looks like the most intense DJ focus imaginable—eyes half-closed, chin slightly raised, feeling a beat that may or may not exist. Time resumes—BASS DROP—the lights explode in intensity, confetti particles burst from somewhere, phone flashlights wave in the background. Psyduck raises both stubby arms in triumph. The headphones, knocked slightly askew by the movement, somehow look even cooler now. A hand enters frame and drops bills on the desk. Another hand holds up a hastily written sign: "DJ PSYDUCK - NEXT SHOW: WHENEVER IT FEELS LIKE IT." Psyduck looks directly at camera. Still confused. Still vacant. But undeniably... legendary.

**Visual Style:** Intentionally jarring contrast between normal cluttered bedroom (Phase 1) and full club transformation (Phase 2-3) while staying in the same location. Psyduck's glossy yellow surface catches neon reflections beautifully. The headphones are perfectly oversized for comedy. Fog, light beams, and silhouettes create authentic club atmosphere in what is clearly still a bedroom. The coaster-turntable is never replaced with actual DJ equipment—the comedy relies on recognizing it's STILL just a coaster.

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format (TikTok/Reels optimized), dramatic three-stage lighting shift (warm desk lamp → neon explosion), purple/pink/cyan color palette for club atmosphere, strobe effects building to the "drop," dynamic camera with push-in during climax, shallow depth of field creating beautiful neon bokeh, sound design: quiet room ambiance with coaster spinning → building synth notes → MASSIVE bass drop with crowd reaction and air horn, satisfying vinyl scratch sound on the "drop."
