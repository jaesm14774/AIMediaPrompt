### 手繪塗鴉覺醒 - 筆記本角色逃脫記

某人在筆記本上隨手畫了一個簡單的角色塗鴉——結果塗鴉「活了過來」，從筆記本頁面上站起來，用鉛筆線條的身體在桌面上冒險，最後跳進旁邊的彩色漫畫書裡升級成全彩版本。

---

## Prompt Template

A magical 5-second video of a simple hand-drawn doodle of **[塗鴉角色："a stick-figure cat with wobbly lines" / "a crude circle-face creature with dot eyes" / "a childish sketch of a tiny warrior" / "a rough pencil doodle of a round bird"]** coming to life on a notebook page, climbing off the paper, exploring the real desktop world, and ultimately leaping into a colorful medium to transform.

**Phase 1 - Doodle Awakens (0-1.5s):** A real physical notebook lies open on a **[桌面環境："messy student desk with scattered erasers, pencil shavings, and a half-empty coffee cup" / "tidy home office desk with warm lamp light and a mechanical keyboard nearby" / "wooden café table with latte art and croissant crumbs" / "child's art desk covered in crayon marks and sticker residue"]**. On the notebook page, a simple pencil doodle sits among handwritten notes and math equations. The doodle is charmingly crude—wobbly lines, uneven proportions, endearingly amateur. A real pencil lies nearby, as if someone just drew it and walked away. The doodle's dot-eyes blink. One crude line-arm peels off the paper surface with a papery *crinkle*. The character sits up from the flat page, its 2D pencil-line body casting a tiny real shadow on the paper. It looks at its own hands—simple line drawings—with wonder. Looks around at the enormous 3D real world surrounding its notebook. Expression (conveyed through minimal dot-eyes and line-mouth) shifts from confusion to amazement.

**Phase 2 - Desktop Adventure (1.5-3.5s):** The pencil-line character **[桌面冒險："climbs over the notebook spiral binding like scaling a mountain, graphite smudging with each handhold" / "walks to the edge of the page and peers over the paper cliff at the desk surface far below, wobbling nervously" / "uses the nearby pencil as a bridge, walking across it like a tightrope, arms spread for balance" / "encounters a massive eraser and runs away in terror—it's a doodle's natural predator"]**. Everything maintains the pencil-sketch aesthetic—the character leaves faint graphite footprints on surfaces, its body smudges slightly when touching things. It interacts with real objects at its tiny scale: **[物品互動："sees its own reflection in the coffee cup surface and waves at itself" / "tries to lift an eraser crumb and fails comically, falling backward" / "stands in the warm circle of desk lamp light, basking in it like sunshine" / "discovers a colored pencil and touches it—the tip of its hand turns that color, startling it"]**. The character reaches the edge of the desk and spots something: a colorful **[目標媒介："manga volume lying open to a vibrant full-color page" / "watercolor painting drying on an easel nearby" / "tablet screen displaying a vivid digital illustration" / "pack of colored markers spilled open, creating a rainbow puddle of ink"]**.

**Phase 3 - Chromatic Transformation (3.5-5s):** The pencil doodle takes a running leap off the notebook, soaring through the air—its simple line-art body trailing graphite particles like a comet tail. It dives INTO the colorful medium—**[進入瞬間："crashing through the manga page surface like diving into water, ink ripples spreading across the printed page" / "splashing into the watercolor painting, wet paint swirling around its body" / "phasing through the tablet screen with a digital glitch effect, pixels scattering" / "belly-flopping into the marker ink puddle with a satisfying chromatic splash"]**. Inside, a transformation occurs: the crude pencil lines FILL with vibrant color, the wobbly proportions smooth into polished illustration quality, simple dot-eyes gain highlights and depth. The character emerges on the other side completely upgraded—**[升級後："now a fully-rendered manga character with speed lines, screentone shading, and dramatic pose" / "a beautiful watercolor painting with soft gradients, visible brush texture, and dreamy edges" / "a crisp digital illustration with cel-shading, clean lineart, and glowing effects" / "a vivid marker-art character with bold outlines, saturated flat colors, and graphic novel energy"]**. The character looks at its new colorful hands in absolute shock and delight. Behind it, on the original notebook page, there's now an empty silhouette where the doodle used to be—just eraser marks and a faint graphite ghost.

**Visual Style:** Hyper-realistic desktop photography as the environment, with the doodle character rendered in authentic pencil-sketch style—visible graphite texture, paper fiber interaction, natural line weight variation, occasional smudge marks. The transformation sequence bridges from raw pencil aesthetic to polished illustration medium with satisfying visual progression. Scale contrast is key—the tiny 2cm doodle character navigating enormous real-world desk objects creates inherent comedy and wonder. Depth of field emphasizes the miniature scale.

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format (TikTok/Reels optimized), warm practical desk lighting (3200K) with visible light source, extreme macro shallow depth of field (f/1.2 equivalent), graphite particle effects for character movement, paper texture displacement when character stands, chromatic aberration during transformation sequence, sound design: paper rustling and pencil scratching ambiance, tiny footstep sounds on paper, graphite scraping on notebook spiral, whooshing jump sound, satisfying *splash/phase* into color medium, orchestral swell during transformation, sparkle sounds as color fills in.

## Example

A magical 5-second video of a simple hand-drawn pencil doodle of **a crude round Kirby with wobbly lines** coming to life on a notebook page, climbing off the paper, exploring a student's desk, and ultimately leaping into an open manga volume to transform into a fully-colored character.

**Phase 1 - Doodle Awakens (0-1.5s):** A spiral-bound notebook lies open on a messy student desk—scattered erasers, pencil shavings, a half-empty iced coffee, crumpled sticky notes, and a mechanical pencil. On the ruled notebook page, among hastily scribbled math equations and doodled stars, sits a simple pencil sketch of Kirby. It's charmingly bad—the circle isn't quite round, the eyes are uneven dots, the feet are wobbly ovals, and there's an erased-and-redrawn arm visible underneath. A real mechanical pencil lies next to it. The doodle's dot-eyes blink. The crude line-mouth opens slightly. One wobbly arm peels up from the paper surface with a soft papery *crinkle*, graphite smudging at the lift point. Doodle-Kirby sits up from the flat page, its pencil-line body casting an impossibly tiny real shadow on the notebook's blue ruled lines. It examines its own graphite hands—simple curved lines—with wide dot-eyes. Looks up at the towering coffee cup, the massive mechanical pencil, the distant desk lamp sun. The expression (minimal dots and lines) conveys perfect amazement.

**Phase 2 - Desktop Adventure (1.5-3.5s):** Doodle-Kirby carefully climbs over the notebook's spiral binding like scaling a chrome mountain range, graphite smearing with each grip. It leaves tiny grey footprints on the binding. Reaches the desk surface—smooth laminate stretching in every direction like a beige desert. Kirby encounters the massive pink eraser block and PANICS—backing away in terror from the doodle's natural predator. The eraser looms like a monolith. Kirby runs, its wobbly pencil-line body bouncing with each step, leaving a faint graphite trail. It discovers a single red colored pencil lying on the desk and cautiously touches the tip—its stubby hand immediately floods with red color from the contact point. Kirby stares at its one red hand against its grey graphite body in shock. Shakes the hand frantically but the color won't come off. Then Kirby spots something across the desk: an open manga volume—*Pokémon Adventures*—lying face-up, its pages bursting with vivid full-color illustrations of Pokémon in dynamic action poses. Kirby's dot-eyes literally sparkle (graphite glitter). It wants IN.

**Phase 3 - Chromatic Transformation (3.5-5s):** Doodle-Kirby backs up to the notebook edge, takes a deep breath (its round body inflates slightly, stretching the pencil lines), and SPRINTS across the desk surface. Tiny graphite footprints stamp behind it in rapid succession. It reaches the manga page edge and LEAPS—soaring through the air with its stubby arms spread, crude pencil-line body trailing graphite particles like a grey comet. It crashes through the manga page surface like diving into water—printed ink ripples spread across the glossy page from the impact point, word bubbles wobbling. INSIDE the manga, a transformation erupts: the wobbly pencil lines SNAP into clean professional manga lineart, the uneven circle perfects into Kirby's iconic round shape, the dot-eyes gain beautiful highlights and the signature blue color, feet turn proper red-orange, the body fills with Kirby's perfect pink—cell-shaded with screentone shading and speed lines radiating outward. Manga-Kirby strikes a triumphant pose on the printed page, now PART of the comic—surrounded by speed lines and a dramatic impact starburst, expression reading pure joy with sparkling eyes and the biggest smile. Behind it, back on the notebook page visible in the background, there's just an empty Kirby-shaped silhouette with eraser marks and a lonely graphite smudge where it used to sit. The mechanical pencil rolls slightly, as if nodding approval.

**Visual Style:** Hyper-realistic student desk photography with authentic lived-in clutter. Doodle-Kirby in genuine amateur pencil-sketch style—visible HB graphite texture, paper fiber showing through, natural pressure variation in line weight, eraser ghost marks, occasional smudge. Transformation bridges from raw graphite sketch to polished full-color manga illustration with deeply satisfying visual progression. The extreme scale contrast (2cm sketch character vs. enormous desk objects) drives both comedy and wonder.

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format, warm desk lamp lighting (3200K tungsten) as primary illumination, extreme macro depth of field (f/1.2) keeping character sharp against massive blurred background objects, graphite particle system for movement trails, paper fiber displacement mapping when character stands/walks, printed ink fluid dynamics for manga page entry, clean cel-shade rendering for transformation result, sound design: paper texture ASMR ambiance, tiny scratchy graphite footsteps, metallic *ting* on spiral binding, comedic *whomp* running into eraser, sparkly transformation chime building from single note to full chord, manga-style *WHOOSH* speed-line sound, triumphant landing *THUMP* with comic impact star.
