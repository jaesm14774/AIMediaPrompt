### 極度認真大清潔突擊隊

穿著過規格清潔制服的角色從畫面四個角落同時彈出，以軍事作戰等級的嚴肅配置對付一粒有無辜睡臉的灰塵——任務完成後立刻發現角落還有另一粒，四人再度轉向鏡頭，宿命循環永不安息。

---

## Prompt Template

A comedic 5-second video featuring four versions of **[角色名稱：IP character / Kirby / 自訂圓潤角色]** dressed in **over-engineered professional cleaning uniforms**—each with a neon safety vest covered in official-looking badges, a headset communicator, and a utility belt loaded with absurdly specialized cleaning gadgets. The setting is an **almost completely clean, minimalist white room** with a single, microscopic speck of dust in the center of the floor.

**Phase 1 - Target Acquired (0-1s):** Extreme close-up on the microscopic dust speck. The camera pushes in slowly—then reveals the dust has a **tiny, sleeping face**: closed crescent eyes, a faint smile, utterly unaware. A dramatic red spotlight locks onto it. The room is otherwise immaculate, silent, pristine. One single drop of sweat falls in slow motion onto the spotless floor.

**Phase 2 - Squad Deploy (1-3s):** All four **[角色名稱]** simultaneously POP IN from each corner of the frame with a **[彈出動感：exaggerated cartoon bounce "BOING" from all four screen edges at once / rocket-propelled launch from off-screen with motion-blur trails / each corner character sliding in on wheeled mop-buckets like action heroes]**. Each carries a different absurdly oversized cleaning weapon: a backpack-mounted industrial vacuum cannon, a high-pressure foam launcher, a nano-fiber tornado mop the size of a helicopter rotor, and a UV sterilization bazooka. All four snap into aggressive action stances simultaneously. Their expressions: **dead serious**. Not a single smile. Eyes locked on the microscopic, innocently sleeping target with laser focus.

**Phase 3 - TOTAL OVERKILL (3-4.5s):** All four characters charge toward the center in synchronized formation, tools whirring and spinning at maximum capacity. The entire room fills with cleaning chaos—foam explosions, vacuum cyclones, spinning mop blades, UV light beams criss-crossing dramatically. The dust speck (and its sleeping face) vanishes in 0.3 seconds. The four characters skid to a halt in a perfect hero pose, surveying the spotless floor. One gives a single firm nod of professional satisfaction.

**Phase 4 - The Loop (4.5-5s):** One character slowly turns their head toward a far corner. A second spotlight quietly illuminates **another microscopic dust speck**—this one also has a sleeping face. Beat of silence. All four characters turn in unison to face the camera directly. Their expressions: **identical dead-serious intensity**, but now carrying the unmistakable weight of a destiny they can never escape. Slow zoom in on their faces. Cut to black.

**Visual Style:** Clean, minimal white background with subtle light gray floor shadow—zero environmental clutter, all focus on the characters. Characters rendered in bold 2D cartoon style with crisp outlines and saturated colors. Dust specks rendered with exaggerated micro-detail—tiny crescent sleep-eyes visible only on close-up shots, creating a rewatchable hidden-layer easter egg. Cleaning effects rendered as exaggerated cartoon physics—foam in perfect geometric blobs, vacuum trails as swirling vector spirals. Each character maintains identical stoic expression throughout the chaos.

**Technical specs:** 60fps smooth animation, 4K resolution, 9:16 vertical format (TikTok/Reels optimized), snappy timing with exaggerated anticipation frames on every pop-in, motion blur on fast cleaning equipment, push-in macro shot on sleeping dust face at Phase 1, dramatic slow-motion on final camera-facing moment (frames 4.5–5s), sound design featuring ominous alert siren, four simultaneous cartoon BOING impacts, equipment power-up whirs, a single satisfying "ding" when dust disappears, then dead silence before the loop reveal spotlight click.
